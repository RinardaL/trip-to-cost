7 Days in Nice & the French Riviera

Belle Époque promenades, a clifftop village 400 meters above the sea, and a full day in Monaco, a week based in one hotel that covers the best of the Côte d'Azur.

Ideal SeasonMay – Jun, Sep
Est. Budget$1,600-2,200
Duration7 Days
PaceModerate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Nice Côte d'Azur Airport

The airport sits right on the coast, just a short transfer from most Promenade des Anglais hotels.

🚕 ~20 min taxi from the airport 💵 ~€35 🕐 Plan around your flight time
Afternoon

Vieux Nice & Cours Saleya Market

Wander the pastel alleys of the old town, then browse the flower and produce stalls at Cours Saleya before they pack up for the day.

🚶 Walkable old town 💵 Free to browse 🕐 Before 1 PM, stalls close early
Evening

First sunset walk on the Promenade des Anglais

The Belle Époque hotel façades turn gold as the sun drops into the Baie des Anges, a fitting first look at the city.

🚶 Along the bay, flat and easy 💵 Free 🕐 Golden hour
Morning

Colline du Château viewpoint

Climb the switchback path, or take the small lift, up Castle Hill for the postcard view over the Baie des Anges and the old town rooftops.

🚶 ~15 min climb from Vieux Nice 💵 ~€2 for the lift 🕐 Right at 8 AM opening, before the haze
Afternoon

Beach afternoon on the Baie des Anges

Rent a chair at a private beach club, or spread a towel on a public stretch of Nice's smooth Riviera pebbles.

🚶 Steps from the Promenade 💵 ~€30 for a private beach chair 🕐 Early afternoon, before peak sun
Evening

Matisse Museum or Chagall Museum

The Matisse Museum sits in the Cimiez hills where the painter lived for decades; the Chagall Museum, closer to downtown, holds the largest public Chagall collection anywhere.

🚌 ~15 min bus to Cimiez 💵 ~€10 entry 🕐 Late afternoon, before the 5 PM close
Morning

Train & shuttle up to Èze village

Ride the coastal train to Èze-sur-Mer, then catch the local shuttle bus up the switchbacks to the perched village itself.

🚆 ~25 min train + 10 min shuttle 💵 ~€3 one-way 🕐 Before the day-trippers arrive
Afternoon

Jardin d'Èze exotic garden

Cacti and succulents frame a genuinely stunning 360-degree view from the ruins of the old château at the village summit.

🚶 Steep cobbled lanes, wear good shoes 💵 ~€8 garden entry 🕐 Midday, for the clearest views
Evening

Clifftop dinner in Èze or back in Nice

A handful of restaurants in the village serve dinner with a sea view that rivals anywhere on the coast, at a price to match.

🚆 Last shuttle back down to the station 💵 ~€75 dinner 🕐 Reserve a sunset table
Morning

Train to Monaco-Monte Carlo & the Prince's Palace

The regional train hugs the coastline the entire way. Arrive at the palace square in time for the Changing of the Guard.

🚆 ~20 min train from Nice-Ville 💵 ~€9 round trip 🕐 Right at 11:55 AM for the guard change
Afternoon

Oceanographic Museum of Monaco

Founded by Prince Albert I, the museum's shark lagoon and cliffside terrace are worth the entry fee on their own.

🚶 ~15 min downhill from the palace 💵 ~€32 entry 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Monte Carlo Casino exterior & Café de Paris

Even without a hand of cards, the Belle Époque façade lit up at dusk, plus a drink at Café de Paris across the square, makes for a classic Monaco evening.

🚶 Across Casino Square 💵 ~€80 dinner nearby 🕐 Blue hour, when the façade lights up
Morning

Antibes old town ramparts

Walk the sea-facing ramparts above the Mediterranean, where the old fortified town meets Europe's largest yachting harbor.

🚆 ~20 min train from Nice-Ville 💵 ~€9 round trip 🕐 Morning light along the sea wall
Afternoon

Picasso Museum, Antibes

Housed in the Château Grimaldi, where Picasso lived and worked for several prolific months in 1946.

🚶 In the old town, on the ramparts 💵 ~€8 entry 🕐 Early afternoon, fewer crowds
Evening

Saint-Paul de Vence art village

One of the Riviera's oldest hilltop villages, ringed by ramparts and galleries that once counted Matisse, Picasso, and Chagall as regulars.

🚗 ~35 min transfer from Antibes 💵 ~€50 round-trip taxi 🕐 Late afternoon light for the galleries
Morning

Train to Cannes & La Croisette

The famous waterfront boulevard, lined with palm trees, grand hotels, and the red-carpet steps of the Palais des Festivals.

🚆 ~35 min train from Nice-Ville 💵 ~€14 round trip 🕐 Mid-morning stroll
Afternoon

Le Suquet old town & Marché Forville

Climb the hill above the harbor to Cannes' original fishing village, then duck into the covered market for a Provençal lunch.

🚶 Uphill from the port 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Before 1 PM, market still full
Evening

Sunset drinks on La Croisette

Grab a terrace table facing the bay and watch the yachts and the light shift together.

🚶 Back along the bay 💵 ~€18 per drink 🕐 Golden hour over the Bay of Cannes
Morning

Bike ride along the Promenade des Anglais

Rent a bike right on the Promenade and ride its full flat length before the day's heat and crowds arrive.

🚲 Rental stand on the Promenade 💵 ~€15 half-day rental 🕐 Right after breakfast, quiet path
Afternoon

Last-minute shopping on Rue de France

A pedestrian shopping street a block back from the beach, good for a final coffee and a souvenir run.

🚶 Central Nice, walkable 💵 Varies 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Transfer to Nice Côte d'Azur Airport

The airport sits close enough to the center that even a late afternoon flight leaves room for a full last day.

🚕 ~20 min taxi 💵 ~€35 🕐 Leave 2 hrs before your flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service is already included by law (service compris), so tipping isn't obligatory anywhere on the coast.
  • Rounding up or leaving a few extra euros for good service is appreciated, not expected.
  • Monaco follows the same French tipping norms as Nice.

👗 Dress Code

  • Smart-casual is expected at Monte Carlo's private gaming rooms and upscale restaurants in the evening.
  • Cover shoulders and knees for church interiors in Vieux Nice and Èze.
  • Beachwear stays at the beach, so cover up before heading back into town.

🚆 Getting Around the Riviera

  • A single regional train line links Nice, Èze-sur-Mer, Monaco, Antibes, and Cannes along the coast, cheap and frequent.
  • Èze's train station sits well below the perched village itself, so budget time for the connecting shuttle bus or a steep uphill walk.
  • Book a private transfer for Saint-Paul de Vence, since it isn't directly served by the coastal rail line.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Pickpocketing occurs on crowded regional trains and along the Promenade des Anglais, especially in high season.
  • Many beach stretches are private, paid clubs with chair rental, clearly separate from the free public sections.
  • Jaywalking fines are enforced, particularly around Monaco's tightly monitored streets.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in French

  • Bonjour: hello
  • Merci beaucoup: thank you very much
  • L'addition, s'il vous plaît: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is used throughout, including Monaco, which uses it under a monetary agreement with France despite not being an EU member.
  • Because Monaco sits outside full EU membership, a few EU-wide consumer protections don't automatically extend there, so keep receipts for costly purchases.
  • Cards and contactless are accepted almost everywhere, including small cafes in Vieux Nice.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🚆

Take the regional TER trains

A single-digit-euro ticket between Riviera towns beats a private transfer many times over, and the coastal views are better too.

🍽️

Menu du jour lunches

Multi-course lunch specials at family-run bistros in Vieux Nice cost far less than the same restaurant's dinner menu.

🌄

Free viewpoints over paid excursions

Castle Hill and Rauba-Capeu rival any paid boat tour for panoramic views, at no cost at all.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌅

Rauba-Capeu at sunset

A quiet clifftop path just past Castle Hill with front-row Baie des Anges views and a fraction of the Promenade's crowds.

⚓

Villefranche-sur-Mer over Monaco's crowds

One short train stop away: a working fishing port with pastel buildings and none of the Monte Carlo price tags.

🌷

Cours Saleya after the flower market closes

By early evening, the same square turns into an open-air restaurant terrace favored by locals over tourists.

✈️ Flights

  • Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE) is France's second-busiest airport and sits right on the coast, just a few kilometers from the Promenade des Anglais.
  • The tram and taxis both run from the airport into central Nice in well under 30 minutes, no complicated transfer to plan.
  • Given the luxury budget on this trip, a private transfer is a reasonable upgrade over a taxi queue, especially with luggage for a full week.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Uber operates in Nice and along the Riviera, alongside local taxi services, so both are easy to book from a phone.
  • Nice, Monaco, Antibes, and Cannes are all linked by the coastal train, which is often faster and cheaper than a taxi between towns.
  • Saint-Paul de Vence isn't on the rail line, so a taxi or private transfer, like the one used on Day 5, is the practical way to reach it.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • The regional coastal train already connects Nice, Èze-sur-Mer, Monaco, Antibes, and Cannes directly, covering every day trip on this itinerary but one.
  • Parking in Monaco and central Nice is scarce and expensive, so a rental car adds cost and hassle without much benefit on the days that hug the coast.
  • Saint-Paul de Vence is the one stop not served by rail, but a single private transfer or taxi covers that gap without needing a car for the whole week.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Promenade des Anglais: the classic choice for this itinerary, with Belle Époque hotels steps from the beach and the Day 1 sunset walk.
  • Vieux Nice: puts Cours Saleya market and the old town alleys right outside the door, ideal for a slower morning routine.
  • Carré d'Or: Nice's upscale shopping district near the Promenade, a fitting base for a week built around fine dining and Monaco day trips.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • No boat or ferry legs are part of this 7-day itinerary, every day trip runs by train or private transfer.
  • A Riviera coastal boat tour from Nice to Villefranche-sur-Mer or along the Cap Ferrat coastline is a popular optional add-on that suits this trip's luxury pace.
  • Book it on a lighter day, such as the last morning in Nice, rather than swapping out one of the planned day trips.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Standard Schengen entry rules apply for the whole trip, including the Monaco stop, so non-EU visitors should confirm visa requirements before booking flights.
  • Monaco uses the euro and feels seamless to visit from France, though it isn't itself part of the EU or the Schengen area.
  • Travel insurance is worth it at this budget tier, given the museum tickets, private transfers, and Monaco excursion costs all add up if a day gets disrupted.
