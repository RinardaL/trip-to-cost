China Itineraries and Trip Costs

5 complete day-by-day trips in China, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 5 China itineraries: Shanghai, Zhangye, Zhangjiajie, Chongqing, Beijing. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $260 to $720 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Zhangye at about $85 a day, and the most expensive is Shanghai at about $120 a day.

Trips run from 4 to 5 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
