Iceland Itineraries and Trip Costs

3 complete day-by-day trips in Iceland, with the price per person up front.

Last updated: November 2026

TripToCost has 3 Iceland itineraries: Iceland, Vatnajökull, Iceland Ring Road. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $1,075 to $2,050 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Iceland Ring Road at about $190 a day, and the most expensive is Vatnajökull at about $415 a day.

Trips run from 4 to 7 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
