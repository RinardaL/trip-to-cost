💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Not obligatory: service (servizio) is often already included on the bill.
  • Round up or leave 5–10% at sit-down restaurants for great service.
  • No tipping expected for bars, gelato, or counter service.

👗 Dress Code

  • Cover shoulders and knees to enter the Duomo and other churches.
  • Swimwear is for the beach only. Cover up when walking through town.
  • Comfortable shoes matter more than style here: steps and cobblestones everywhere.

🚌 Transit Nuances

  • Buy SITA bus tickets at a tobacco shop (tabacchi) or bar before boarding: not sold on the bus.
  • Validate your ticket in the machine as soon as you board.
  • Buses fill fast in summer: arrive at the stop 15+ minutes early.

⚠️ Safety & Scam Warnings

  • Check for posted menu prices: some seafront spots quote inflated verbal prices to tourists.
  • Only use metered taxis, or agree on a fare before you get in.
  • Keep bags zipped and in front on crowded ferries and buses.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases

  • Buongiorno: good morning / hello
  • Grazie mille: thank you very much
  • Il conto, per favore: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • Cash is still preferred in small villages and family-run trattorias.
  • Many places have a minimum card amount (usually €10): carry euros for small purchases.
  • ATMs are common in Sorrento and Amalfi town, less so in smaller villages.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🛏️

Base in Sorrento, not Positano

Half the price for a room with the same ferry access to every town on the coast.

🎫

Buy SITA bus day-passes

Cheaper than individual tickets if you're hopping between three or more towns in a day.

⛴️

Ferries over buses when possible

Often faster and not much pricier than the bus, especially in summer traffic.

✈️ Flights

  • Naples International Airport (NAP) is the nearest major gateway, used for both arrival and departure on this itinerary.
  • The Curreri airport bus runs directly to Sorrento, or take a train via Naples Centrale if it's cheaper that day.
  • Book the Curreri bus or check timings ahead: departures thin out later in the day.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Uber has little to no presence on the Amalfi Coast; regular white taxis and hotel-arranged drivers fill the gap.
  • Always agree on a fare before getting in, or confirm the meter is running.
  • Taxis are a splurge here, so this budget itinerary sticks to SITA buses and ferries instead.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Not recommended for this itinerary: the SS163 coastal road is narrow, cliffside, and full of hairpin turns.
  • Parking in Positano, Amalfi, and Ravello is scarce, expensive, and often a long walk from the center.
  • SITA buses and ferries cover every stop on this plan for a fraction of a rental's cost and stress.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Sorrento: this itinerary's home base all week, roughly half the price of Positano with the same ferry access.
  • Positano: worth a splurge night if you want to wake up to the view instead of just visiting for sunset.
  • Amalfi town: a good mid-week alternative base, close to the Duomo and the Ravello bus.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Essential to this itinerary: ferries connect Sorrento to Positano, Amalfi town, and Capri on Days 1, 3, and 5.
  • Book the last return ferry early in peak season, since final crossings sell out fast.
  • Ferries are often faster than the bus and only a little more expensive, especially in summer traffic.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • EU/Schengen entry rules apply; non-EU visitors should confirm visa requirements before booking flights.
  • Travel insurance is worth it for the Path of the Gods hike and for ferry cancellations, which do happen in rough weather.
  • Buy SITA bus tickets at a tobacco shop before boarding, since they aren't sold on the bus itself.
