7 Days in Egypt: Cairo & a Nile Cruise from Luxor to Aswan

The Pyramids at dawn, the Grand Egyptian Museum's golden treasures, temple columns rising out of the desert, and four days drifting down the Nile between Luxor and Aswan.

Ideal SeasonOct – Apr
Est. Budget$1,200-1,700
Duration7 Days
PaceModerate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land at Cairo International Airport & check in

Most itineraries start with a visa-on-arrival stamp or a pre-booked e-visa, then a prearranged hotel transfer through Cairo's busy ring road traffic.

🚕 ~45 min hotel transfer 💵 ~$25 private transfer 🕐 Confirm check-in time
Afternoon

Orientation from Cairo Tower

The lotus-shaped tower on Gezira Island gives a 360° first look at the sprawling city, the curve of the Nile, and the Giza plateau on the hazy horizon.

🚕 ~20 min from downtown 💵 ~$8 entry 🕐 Late afternoon, before sunset
Evening

First dinner on the Nile

A Nile-side restaurant or a short dinner cruise eases in the trip gently, with felucca sails drifting past as the city lights come on.

🚶 walkable from most downtown hotels 💵 ~$20 dinner 🕐 After sunset
Morning

Great Pyramids of Giza & the Sphinx

Arrive right at opening, before the desert heat and tour buses build up. A licensed guide helps cut through the persistent camel-ride and "guide" touts at the gate.

🚕 ~30 min from downtown 💵 ~$15 entry 🕐 Right at 8 AM opening
Afternoon

Grand Egyptian Museum

Fully open since November 2025, the world's largest museum devoted to a single civilization sits a short drive from the pyramids, with book-ahead timed entry.

🚕 ~10 min from the plateau 💵 ~$30 entry 🕐 Book a timed slot in advance
Evening

Tutankhamun Galleries

All 5,398 objects from the boy king's tomb are displayed together for the first time since 1922, the gold mask as the unmissable centerpiece.

🚶 within the museum complex 💵 included in entry 🕐 Last two hours, once crowds thin
Morning

Citadel of Saladin & the Mosque of Muhammad Ali

The alabaster mosque crowning the medieval citadel gives sweeping views over the whole of Islamic Cairo's domes and minarets.

🚕 ~20 min from downtown 💵 ~$10 entry 🕐 Mid-morning
Afternoon

Khan el-Khalili bazaar

Spices, lanterns, silver, and papyrus fill a medieval trading quarter dating to the 14th century. Bargaining is expected here, not in fixed-price shops.

🚶 walkable old quarter 💵 Free to browse 🕐 Afternoon into early evening
Evening

Overnight sleeper train to Luxor

The Watania sleeper leaves Ramses Station in the evening and arrives in Luxor by morning, saving a hotel night and a domestic flight.

🚆 ~10-11 hr overnight sleeper 💵 ~$120 double cabin 🕐 Board by early evening
Morning

Arrive in Luxor & Karnak Temple

The train pulls in by morning; after checking into a hotel, the 134 towering columns of Karnak's Great Hypostyle Hall are an overwhelming first stop.

🚕 ~10 min from central Luxor 💵 ~$14 entry 🕐 Morning, before the midday heat
Afternoon

Rest & the Luxor Museum

A slower afternoon: an air-conditioned break at the compact, well-curated Luxor Museum, or simply resting before the evening heat breaks.

🚶 walkable from the Corniche 💵 ~$10 museum entry 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Luxor Temple after dark

Floodlit columns and statues of Ramesses II make Luxor Temple one of the few ancient sites genuinely better after sunset than by day.

🚶 central Luxor, on the Corniche 💵 ~$14 entry 🕐 After sunset
Morning

Valley of the Kings

A standard ticket covers three tombs; Tutankhamun's smaller tomb, where his mummy still rests, is a worthwhile separate add-on ticket.

🚕 ~20 min from the west bank ferry 💵 ~$16 entry, +$15 Tutankhamun's tomb 🕐 Early morning, before the heat
Afternoon

Hatshepsut's Temple & the Colossi of Memnon

The female pharaoh's terraced temple rises straight out of the cliff face; the two seated Colossi of Memnon are a quick stop on the way back.

🚕 ~10 min from the Valley of the Kings 💵 ~$9 entry 🕐 Late morning to midday
Evening

Board the Nile cruise & sail south

Check into the cabin, unpack once, and watch Luxor slide past from the sun deck as the ship casts off toward Esna and Edfu.

⛴️ boards in Luxor, sails overnight 💵 included in cruise package 🕐 Board by late afternoon
Morning

Temple of Horus, Edfu

A horse-drawn carriage from the dock covers the short ride into town, to what's considered the most complete surviving temple in Egypt.

🐎 ~10 min carriage from the dock 💵 ~$10 entry 🕐 Mid-morning shore excursion
Afternoon

Double Temple of Kom Ombo

Unusually dedicated to two gods side by side, Sobek the crocodile god and Horus, this riverside temple is often visited right at sunset from the ship.

🚶 docks directly at the site 💵 ~$9 entry 🕐 Late afternoon
Evening

Felucca sail toward Aswan

As the ship nears Aswan, traditional felucca sailboats glide past Elephantine Island, the Nile at its most scenic all trip.

⛵ ~1 hr optional felucca add-on 💵 ~$15 pp 🕐 Sunset
Morning

Philae Temple

Dedicated to Isis and relocated stone by stone to Agilkia Island in a UNESCO rescue project, Philae is reached only by a short motorboat crossing.

🚤 ~10 min boat crossing 💵 ~$14 entry, ~$6 boat 🕐 Morning, before the boat queue builds
Afternoon

Aswan High Dam

A quick stop at the dam that created Lake Nasser and reshaped modern Egypt, with views over the vast reservoir it holds back.

🚕 ~20 min from central Aswan 💵 ~$8 entry 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Nubian village visit & flight home

A brightly painted Nubian village on the Nile's west bank makes a colorful farewell before the short domestic flight back to Cairo for international departure.

✈️ ~1 hr 15 min flight to Cairo 💵 ~$70 domestic flight 🕐 3 hrs before international flight

💶 Tipping (Baksheesh) Etiquette

  • Tipping, known locally as baksheesh, is a routine, expected part of daily life, not an optional extra: small notes for bathroom attendants, porters, and site guards add up.
  • Restaurants: 10% is standard if service isn't already included on the bill.
  • Guides and cruise staff: budget a per-day tip, usually pooled and handed over at the end of a tour or cruise.

👗 Dress Code

  • Egypt is a conservative, majority-Muslim country: shoulders and knees covered are appreciated everywhere, essential inside mosques.
  • A headscarf is required for women entering active mosques; loose, breathable fabrics manage the desert sun better than shorts and tank tops anyway.
  • Sturdy, closed shoes matter more than modesty at the ancient sites themselves: temple floors and tomb steps are uneven stone.

🕌 Touting & Site Etiquette

  • Unsolicited "guides," camel handlers, and photo-op offers are common at Giza and other major sites: a polite, firm "la, shukran" (no, thank you) and continuing to walk works better than engaging.
  • Agree on any camel, horse, or felucca price in full before getting on, not after the ride has started.
  • A licensed Egyptologist guide for the big sites is genuinely useful for context and largely removes the touting friction.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Photography inside tombs and some museum galleries is restricted or requires a separate paid permit; check signage before shooting.
  • Desert sun is intense even in the cooler season: schedule outdoor sites for morning or late afternoon and carry water constantly.
  • Agree taxi fares upfront in Cairo and Luxor; metered rides are rare outside app-based rideshares.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Egyptian Arabic

  • As-salamu alaykum (السلام عليكم): hello, peace be upon you
  • Shukran (شكراً): thank you
  • Bikam? (بكام؟): how much?

💳 Money & Payments

  • The Egyptian pound (EGP) is the currency; carry small bills for tipping, bazaar purchases, and site bathrooms.
  • Cards are accepted at hotels, cruise ships, and larger restaurants, but cash is still king in markets and with drivers.
  • ATMs are common in Cairo, Luxor, and Aswan; withdraw larger amounts less often to avoid repeated fees.

Karnak at opening, not the sound and light show

The temple empty at 8 AM, sun raking sideways through the columns, beats the crowded evening show most travelers default to.

🏝️

Elephantine Island over central Aswan

A short local ferry ride reaches a quieter Nubian village and ancient ruins, away from the main Aswan corniche crowds.

A ridge a short ride behind the pyramids frames all three monuments in one shot, with far fewer people than the main viewpoints.

✈️ Flights

  • Cairo International Airport (CAI) is the main international gateway, roughly 45 minutes from downtown by taxi.
  • A short domestic flight (EgyptAir or Nile Air) connects Cairo and Luxor or Aswan in under 90 minutes, useful on the return leg.
  • Book domestic flights and the sleeper train well ahead in the busy December-February peak season.

🚆 Domestic Flight vs. Overnight Sleeper Train

  • The Watania sleeper train between Cairo and Luxor takes 10-11 hours overnight, combining transport and a night's accommodation.
  • A domestic flight covers the same route in about an hour but adds a hotel night and airport transfer time on each end.
  • Either works well; the sleeper is the more memorable, budget-friendly choice this itinerary follows southbound.

⛴️ Choosing a Nile Cruise Operator

  • Standard 4-5 star Nile cruise ships run fixed Luxor-Aswan routes with meals, shore excursions, and an Egyptologist guide included.
  • Check recent reviews for cabin condition and excursion quality specifically: ship age varies widely even at similar price points.
  • A private dahabiya sailboat is a slower, quieter, pricier alternative to the standard motorized cruise ships.

🕌 Guided vs. Independent Travel

  • Egypt's major sites are navigable independently, but a licensed guide adds real historical context and reduces friction with touts at Giza and the temples.
  • A private driver-guide for the Cairo days and a small-group Nile cruise for Luxor-Aswan is a comfortable middle ground between fully independent and a large fixed-group tour.
  • Either way, book skip-the-line tickets for the Grand Egyptian Museum and Valley of the Kings in advance.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Uber and Careem operate in Cairo, Luxor, and Aswan, and are generally more reliable and fairly priced than hailing a street taxi.
  • For a full day of sightseeing, a private driver booked through the hotel is often cheaper than several separate rides.
  • Always agree the fare before getting into a street taxi without a working meter.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Most nationalities can get an e-visa online in advance or a visa-on-arrival in cash at Cairo airport.
  • Travel insurance is worth it given the flights, overnight train, and boat transfers packed into one week.
  • Bottled or filtered water only, including for brushing teeth outside major hotels and cruise ships.
