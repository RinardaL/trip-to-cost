4 Days in Uludağ & Bursa: Skiing, Mosques & Thermal Baths

Bursa's Grand Mosque, Green Tomb and İskender kebap, the TeleferiK up to Uludağ, two days on the snow, then a thermal bath before the airport: a budget winter trip priced in lira.

4-day Uludağ & Bursa itinerary

How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Istanbul Airport & the coach to Bursa

Istanbul Airport (IST) is about 180 km from Bursa. İstanbul Seyahat runs a direct coach to Bursa bus terminal roughly every hour, taking about 2.5 hours and costing around 600 to 800 TL (check current). Book on obilet or at the airport desk. Arriving at Sabiha Gökçen instead? A Havaist coach to Bursa costs about 650 TL.

🚌 ~2.5 h by direct coach, then a taxi or city bus to the centre 💵 ~600-800 TL (about €11-14) by coach (check current) 🕐 Hourly departures, so plan around your landing time
Afternoon

The Grand Mosque & Koza Han

The Grand Mosque (Ulu Cami, completed 1399) has 20 domes and free entry; cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and avoid prayer times. Five minutes away, Koza Han is the 1491 silk-cocoon market, now a courtyard of tea gardens and silk shops. Check your hotel in first, then walk the bazaar lanes between the two.

🚶 ~5 min on foot between the two 💵 Both free, tea and silk scarves extra 🕐 Mosque outside prayer times, Koza Han until early evening
Evening

İskender kebap in the city centre

İskender kebap was created in Bursa in 1867: thin döner over pide, tomato sauce, melted butter and yoghurt. Central restaurants charge about 900 to 1,300 TL a portion (check current), and Kebapçı İskender, the original, is near Heykel square. Prices differ between places, so check the menu first.

🚶 Short walk or taxi in the centre 💵 ~900-1,300 TL a portion (about €16-23, check current) 🕐 Dinner from 7 PM
Morning

The Green Mosque & the Green Tomb

The Green Mosque (1424) and the Green Tomb of Sultan Mehmed I face each other on a hill east of the centre. Both are free, and a taxi or bus saves the uphill walk. Look for the turquoise tiles of the tomb and the green-tiled hall inside the mosque. Go before 10 AM for quiet.

🚕 Short taxi or bus ride from the centre 💵 Free entry, taxi extra 🕐 Before 10 AM, avoid prayer times
Afternoon

The TeleferiK cable car to the hotel zone

Take a taxi or bus (15A, 15B, 3C, 3İ or 3MU) to Teferrüç station. The cable car runs about 10:00 to 18:00 (check current) and takes about 32 minutes. A round trip is about 500 TL on the published tariff, and foreign visitors may pay more (check current). Wind can stop it, and weekend queues are long.

🚠 ~32 min from Teferrüç to the hotel zone 💵 ~500 TL round trip (about €9, check current) 🕐 Ride up by 3 PM, last service is early evening
Evening

Check-in, gear hire & a first night on Uludağ

Hire your kit tonight so the morning starts on snow. Skis or a board cost roughly 750 to 1,300 TL a day, with clothing extra (check current; prices come out between mid-November and December). The slopes are floodlit for night skiing, though hours are not published. Half-board at your hotel is often cheaper than eating out.

🚶 Everything in the hotel zone is within walking distance 💵 ~750-1,300 TL a day for a ski or board set (check current) 🕐 Shops are busiest 5 to 8 PM, so go early
Morning

Lift pass & the beginner slopes

The season runs from 18 December to 31 March. The 2026/27 day-pass prices were not yet on uludaginfo.com (check current); last season a one-day pass was about 1,700 TL online and 1,950 TL at the box office. Beginners should stay on the gentle platter-lift slopes near the hotels. Private lessons cost 3,000 to 4,000 TL an hour.

🎿 Lifts start beside the hotel zone 💵 Day pass ~1,700 TL online, last season (check current) 🕐 First lifts open mid-morning, buy online to skip the queue
Afternoon

Mid-mountain runs & lunch

After lunch, explore the wider slopes on the chairlifts. The area is mostly beginner and intermediate terrain with about 35 km of marked pistes, so strong skiers cover it in a day or two. Eat a late lunch at a hotel or café to avoid the rush. Wind can close upper lifts, so check the lift status first.

🚡 Chairlifts and platter lifts from the hotel area 💵 Mountain lunch costs more than in town (check current) 🕐 Late lunch after 1:30 PM, last runs before the lifts close
Evening

After-ski in the hotel zone

After-ski is low-key: hot salep or tea in a hotel café, a game of backgammon and an early dinner. Some hotels have a hamam, sauna or pool, but access prices vary (check current). Winterfest runs 17 to 24 January 2027 with extra events. Stay in the hotel zone after dark, since paths are icy.

🚶 Walk between hotels in the zone, taxis are scarce 💵 Hot drinks cheap, spa extra (check current) 🕐 Dinner from 7 PM, an early night before the last ski morning
Morning

Final ski morning & the ride down

Ski until about noon, then return your kit. A 3-hour ticket cost about 1,600 TL last season, and a 2-day pass about 3,200 TL online (check current), so decide on Day 3. Ride the TeleferiK down by mid-afternoon. Wind can close it, so check the status.

🚠 ~32 min from the hotel zone down to Teferrüç 💵 3-hour ticket ~1,600 TL last season (check current) 🕐 Return gear by noon, ride down before the late queue
Afternoon

Çekirge thermal baths

Çekirge, a short bus or taxi ride west of the centre, has used its hot springs since Byzantine times. The historic Eski Kaplıca and Yeni Kaplıca baths are the best-known, and many Çekirge hotels sell hourly thermal access. Entry prices are not published, so ask at the door (check current). Bring a towel and flip-flops.

🚕 ~15-20 min by taxi or bus from Bursa centre 💵 Public bath prices not published (check current) 🕐 Soak 1 to 2 hours, leave time to collect your bags
Evening

Coach back to Istanbul Airport

Take the İstanbul Seyahat coach from Bursa bus terminal to Istanbul Airport, about 2.5 hours for around 600 to 800 TL (check current), with up to 16 departures a day. Allow at least 3 hours before your flight. A private transfer costs roughly €120 to 180 (check current).

🚌 ~2.5 h by coach to Istanbul Airport 💵 ~600-800 TL by coach (check current) 🕐 Reach the airport at least 3 hours before departure

Uludağ & Bursa: what to know before you go

💶 Tipping, Tea & Service

  • Tipping is not compulsory in Turkey, but 5 to 10% is normal in a sit-down restaurant if no service charge is on the bill. Round up taxi fares and leave small change for cafés.
  • Tea (çay) is served free or very cheaply in many shops and bazaars. Accepting one is polite and does not oblige you to buy.
  • Ski instructors and hotel porters usually get a small tip in cash, so keep some small notes.

🍢 Eating & Drinking in Bursa

  • Bursa gave Turkey İskender kebap, and it is also known for Inegöl köfte, candied chestnuts (kestane şekeri) and peaches. Salep, a hot milky winter drink, is the mountain favourite.
  • Breakfast is a long spread of cheese, olives, eggs and tea. It is usually included in hotel rates, so eat well before a ski day.
  • Alcohol is sold in restaurants and shops, but many traditional kebab houses do not serve it. Drinking in the street is frowned upon.

🕌 Mosques, Hamams & Dress

  • Remove your shoes at the mosque door and cover shoulders and knees. Women should carry a light scarf, and everyone should avoid walking in front of people praying.
  • Mosques close to visitors during the five daily prayers, and especially around Friday midday prayers. Plan visits for other times.
  • Traditional hamams give you a wrap (peştemal) and are often split by sex. Bring a towel and flip-flops, and follow the staff instructions on how long to stay in the hot room.
  • In 2027 Ramadan falls roughly from early February to early March (check dates). Some cafés close at lunch, so be discreet when eating in public. Mountain hotels stay open.

⚠️ Safety, Scams & Etiquette Warnings

  • Check your government's official travel advice before you go, for example the UK FCDO or the US State Department. Stay on the marked pistes: off-piste terrain is not patrolled and avalanche risk is real after heavy snow.
  • Use official metered taxis or a taxi app and agree the fare before a long ride to the cable car. Ignore touts at the airport who offer a very cheap transfer.
  • Agree the hire price, deposit and return time in writing before you take ski gear, and photograph the kit. Buy tickets only at official windows or on the resort's own site.
  • Roads to the hotel zone often need snow chains, and the cable car can close in wind. Keep a plan B and insurance that covers skiing.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Turkish

  • "Merhaba" (hello), "Günaydın" (good morning), "İyi akşamlar" (good evening), "Hoşça kalın" (goodbye)
  • "Lütfen" (please), "Teşekkür ederim" (thank you), "Evet" / "Hayır" (yes / no), "Pardon" (excuse me)
  • "Hesap, lütfen" (the bill, please), "Ne kadar?" (how much?), "Kayak takımı kiralamak istiyorum" (I would like to rent a ski set)
  • "İngilizce biliyor musunuz?" (do you speak English?), "Teleferik açık mı?" (is the cable car open?), "Nerede...?" (where is...?)

💳 Money & Payments

  • Turkey uses the lira (TRY), at roughly 56 TL to the euro and 48 TL to the dollar at the time of writing. Inflation is high, so every price on this page is an estimate. Check current prices.
  • Cards and contactless work in most hotels, restaurants, shops and at the cable car, but carry cash for small bakeries, dolmuş and tips.
  • Withdraw lira from bank-branded ATMs and avoid exchange booths at the airport. If a terminal offers to charge you in euros or dollars, choose lira.

Insider tips for Uludağ & Bursa

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎟️

Buy the pass online, and not at the box office

Last season the one-day pass was about 1,700 TL online against 1,950 TL at the box office, and multi-day passes saved more. The 2026/27 day prices were not yet listed on uludaginfo.com, so check them before you buy.

🏨

Choose half-board and go midweek

Uludağ hotels sell half-board packages that cover dinner, which is the cheapest way to eat on the mountain. Prices and lift queues both jump at weekends and during the January school break, so ski Monday to Thursday if you can.

🧤

Bring your own goggles and gloves

Rental sets cover skis, boots and sticks, but clothing, goggles, helmets and gloves are charged on top. A cheap pair of goggles and gloves from home saves a few hundred lira over two days.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌇

Watch the sunset from the Tophane clock tower

The Tophane hill above the old town has the clock tower and gardens, and a free view of the city and the snow-covered Uludağ behind it. It is a calm place to end Day 1 or the last afternoon.

🏘️

Add Cumalıkızık if you have spare time

This Ottoman village, about 12 km east of the centre, has cobbled lanes and old wooden houses and is on the UNESCO list. A local bus runs from Bursa (check current), and Saturday and Sunday mornings are busiest.

🌰

Try Bursa's candied chestnuts

Kestane şekeri is a local sweet sold in small shops near the Grand Mosque. A small box makes a cheap gift and a good snack for the cable car ride.

Travel services for Uludağ & Bursa

✈️ Flights

  • Istanbul Airport (IST) is the main hub and has coaches direct to Bursa, about 180 km away. Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) is on the Asian side, and Havaist runs buses to Bursa from there for about 650 TL (check current).
  • Bursa Yenişehir (YEI) has limited flights, mostly domestic. The old BBBUS shuttle ended on 1 August 2026, and the airport link now runs as the 80/T line, so check the timetable and fare before you go.
  • Booking into IST and out of IST keeps the transfers simple, but compare both Istanbul airports, since fares can differ.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Use official taxis with a roof sign and a meter, or a taxi app. Ask the driver to start the meter, and note the plate for a long ride.
  • From Bursa, taxis and dolmuşes also run up to Uludağ when the cable car is closed, but drivers need snow chains. Agree a price first.
  • Private transfers from Istanbul Airport to Bursa are quoted from about €120 to 220 (check current). They suit a late arrival or a group of three or four.

🚇 Public Transport & Tickets

  • Bursa has city buses, the BursaRay metro and a tram line. Fares are low and change often (check current), and Bursakart or contactless can be used on many routes.
  • Buses 15A, 15B, 3C, 3İ and 3MU pass near the cable car station at Teferrüç, according to the cable car operator.
  • Official Bursa Teleferik hours are 10:00 to 18:00, with stations at Teferrüç, Kadıyayla, Sarıalan and the hotel zone (Oteller Bölgesi). Phone 444 6 345 for the live status.
  • Weekend and holiday queues for the cable car can run to hours, so ride midweek or early.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Uludağ hotel zone: staying by the slopes is the easiest option, but hotels cost more. Listings start around $95 a night in shoulder season and rise in peak weeks (check current). Many sell half-board.
  • Central Bursa near Heykel and the Grand Mosque: the cheapest base for Day 1 and walkable to the bazaar and İskender restaurants.
  • Çekirge: thermal hotels with spa access, a short ride from the centre, and a good last night if you want to soak.
  • The Uludağ hotel zone is the only place to ski for two days without a daily cable car trip, so stay there on nights 2 and 3.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • Turkey is outside EU roaming, so use an eSIM or a local prepaid SIM bought on arrival with your passport. Hotels and many cafés offer Wi-Fi.
  • Cards work at most places, but keep lira for dolmuş, small shops and tips.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs and avoid exchange booths. Say no if a terminal offers to charge in euros or dollars.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Check entry rules for your passport. Many nationalities enter visa-free or need an e-Visa, so use the official site, evisa.gov.tr, and your government's travel advice.
  • Check the ski season dates, which open on 18 December 2026 and close on 31 March, and look at the live snow and lift status in the week you travel.
  • Book the cable car and gear in advance if you travel on a weekend, a public holiday or during the school break.
  • You do not need a car. A car to Uludağ needs snow chains and parking is limited, so use the cable car, taxis or dolmuş.
