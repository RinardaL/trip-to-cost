7 Days in Thailand: Bangkok to the Andaman Coast

Golden temples and river life in Bangkok, then a short flight south to limestone islands, longtail boats, and some of the best snorkeling in Southeast Asia, all on a real backpacker-friendly budget.

💵 Tipping Etiquette

  • Not obligatory, but rounding up or leaving 10% at sit-down restaurants is appreciated.
  • Thai massage and spa staff appreciate a small tip handed directly to them, not left at the front desk.
  • No tipping expected at street food stalls or local markets.

🙏 Dress Code

  • The Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew enforce a strict dress code: shoulders and knees must be covered, no see-through fabric, and sandals with a back strap are safest.
  • Guards will turn away visitors in tank tops, shorts, or leggings; sarongs and cover-ups are sold just outside if you get caught out.
  • Most other temples are more relaxed, but pack a scarf or sarong anyway, it covers you in a pinch.

🛵 Getting Around

  • Tuk-tuks are fun for a short ride but agree the price before getting in, meters don't exist for them.
  • The Grab app is the region's answer to Uber: metered cars, motorbike taxis, and food delivery all in one place.
  • Domestic flights are the fastest way between Bangkok and the southern islands; book the Krabi legs early for the best fares.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Politely ignore anyone near the Grand Palace claiming it's "closed today" and offering to take you elsewhere: it's a well-worn tuk-tuk and gem-shop scam.
  • Thailand takes lese majeste seriously: never disrespect the monarchy or images of the King, including on banknotes.
  • Buddha images and monks are treated with real reverence: don't climb on statues, and women should avoid direct physical contact with monks.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Thai

  • Sawadee ka / khrap: hello (ka for women, khrap for men)
  • Khob khun ka / khrap: thank you
  • Tao rai? how much is this?

💳 Money & Payments

  • Thai baht (THB) is the currency; ATMs are everywhere in Bangkok and Krabi, less reliable on smaller islands.
  • Cash is still king at floating markets, street stalls, and longtail boat operators.
  • PromptPay QR codes are widely used in cities, but keep small bills handy for the islands.

Bamboo Island over Maya Bay

A quieter stop near Phi Phi with calmer, clearer water and a fraction of the crowds.

🛶

Amphawa over Damnoen Saduak

A more local, evening floating market popular with Thai weekenders rather than tour buses.

🌅

Nearly empty compared to the sunset crowds, and the early light on the spires is just as striking.

✈️ Flights

  • Suvarnabhumi (BKK) and Don Mueang (DMK) both serve Bangkok; check which one your domestic connection uses before booking a transfer.
  • The Bangkok to Krabi hop takes about 1h 10min and is the backbone of this itinerary's two-region structure.
  • Book the domestic legs a few weeks out: dry-season fares to the islands rise quickly closer to departure.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Grab is the reliable, transparent option in both Bangkok and Krabi/Ao Nang.
  • Street taxis in Bangkok sometimes "forget" to use the meter with tourists; insist on it or walk away.
  • Tuk-tuks are best treated as a novelty ride, not transport: agree a price up front.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Not needed and not recommended for this trip: Bangkok traffic is heavy, and the itinerary is built around a domestic flight to the islands rather than a long drive south.
  • Railay has no road access at all, so a rental car is useless once you reach the coast.
  • Grab, tuk-tuks, and longtail boats cover everything this itinerary needs.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Bangkok: stay riverside near Tha Tien or Charoen Krung, walking distance to the Grand Palace, Wat Pho, and the express boat piers.
  • Railay or Ao Nang: Railay puts you right on the cliffs with no daily boat commute; Ao Nang has more budget options and a short longtail ride over.
  • Book Railay a little further ahead in dry season: rooms are limited by the lack of road access.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Essential, not optional: both Railay and the Phi Phi Islands are reachable only by boat.
  • Longtails handle short hops like Ao Nang to Railay; speedboats cover the longer Phi Phi day trip.
  • Always confirm your return crossing time when booking, missing the last boat means an unplanned night wherever you are.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Many nationalities get visa-exempt entry to Thailand for up to 60 days; confirm the current rule for your passport before flying.
  • Travel insurance is worth it for the speedboat crossings and any water-sports plans alone.
  • Tap water isn't safe to drink anywhere on this route: stick to bottled or filtered water, including for brushing your teeth on the islands.
