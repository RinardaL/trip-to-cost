Winter Retreats

10 complete winter itineraries: Alpine and Balkan ski resorts, Arctic aurora, Japan in the cold season and city breaks, with prices per person.

Winter travel splits into three kinds of trip: snow and skiing, aurora and ice landscapes, and city breaks where the cold is part of the atmosphere. This page collects all three, each as a short day-by-day itinerary with the cost stated up front.

Prices are estimates per person for the whole stay, covering lodging, meals, local transport and activities. International flights are not included. Every guide has a winter packing checklist and notes on opening hours, which change in the cold months.

How to choose

  • Ski and mountain resorts: St. Moritz in Switzerland and Borovets in Bulgaria, the second a far cheaper option with a ski school for beginners. St. Moritz, Borovets
  • Aurora and ice: Abisko in Sweden, Iceland and Vatnajökull for northern lights, glaciers and ice caves. Abisko, Iceland, Vatnajökull
  • Japan in winter: Hakone and the Fuji Five Lakes, for Mount Fuji views, onsen and clear winter air. Hakone, Fuji Five Lakes
  • City breaks: Berlin and Vienna in market season, and Mardin in quiet, cheap south-eastern Turkey. Berlin, Vienna, Mardin

Frequently asked questions

How much does a winter trip cost?

Our 10 itineraries range from about $350 to $2,600 per person for the whole trip, excluding flights. Ski resorts and the Arctic are the most expensive.

Which winter trip is the cheapest?

Mardin ($350-500), Hakone ($460-740), Fuji Five Lakes ($480-720) are the three lowest on this list, per person and excluding flights.

Do I need to ski?

No. Most of these trips work without skiing. Borovets and St. Moritz are the ski-focused ones, and Borovets has a ski school for beginners.

Is it too cold to travel in winter?

Not with the right clothes. Every itinerary includes a packing checklist, and the day-by-day plans mention indoor options and shorter winter opening hours.

When is the best time for the northern lights?

Each itinerary lists its ideal season. The Arctic trips on this page are planned for the dark months, roughly from autumn to March.
