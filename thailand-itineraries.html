Thailand Itineraries and Trip Costs

3 complete day-by-day trips in Thailand, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 3 Thailand itineraries: Bangkok & Andaman Coast, Bangkok & Phuket, Chiang Mai. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $480 to $1,100 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Bangkok & Andaman Coast at about $135 a day, and the most expensive is Chiang Mai at about $150 a day.

Trips run from 4 to 7 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.

Frequently asked questions

Our 3 Thailand itineraries cost from $480 to $1,100 per person for the whole trip, which is about $135 to $150 a day. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities, and exclude international flights.

Which Thailand itinerary is the cheapest?

Chiang Mai ($480-720), Bangkok & Phuket ($750-1,050), Bangkok & Andaman Coast ($800-1,100) are the three lowest, per person and excluding flights.

Our Thailand itineraries run from 4 to 7 days, and the most common length is 4 days. Each page has a day picker with a price for every length from one day up to the full plan.
