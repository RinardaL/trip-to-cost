5 Days in Mykonos: Windmills, Beach Clubs & Delos

Whitewashed lanes tumbling toward Little Venice, a beach club day at Paradise, and a boat ride out to the sacred ruins of Delos: five days built around the Cyclades' most famous island.

Ideal SeasonJun – Sep
Est. Budget$1,100-1,500
Duration5 Days
PaceRelaxed to Active
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrive by ferry & check in

High-speed ferries from Piraeus dock right at the New Port; a short taxi or hotel shuttle covers the rest, since the port sits a couple of kilometers outside Chora.

⛴️ ~2h 30min high-speed ferry from Piraeus 💵 ~€60-90 one-way 🕐 Confirm check-in time
Afternoon

Wander Chora's alleys & boutique shopping

Cube-shaped whitewashed houses, blue balconies, and bougainvillea line lanes designed centuries ago to confuse pirates, now full of jewelry shops and small galleries.

🚶 walkable old town 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Mid-afternoon, before dinner crowds
Evening

Windmills at Kato Mili, then Little Venice

The island's seven iconic windmills line the ridge above Little Venice, the best spot on the island to watch the sun drop straight into the Aegean.

🚶 ~10 min from Chora 💵 Free, ~€16 sunset cocktail 🕐 Sunset
Morning

Water-taxi from Platis Gialos to Paradise Beach

Small caïque boats shuttle between the south-coast beaches all day; it's a scenic, quick alternative to the bus and the classic way locals island-hop the coast.

⛴️ ~15 min by water taxi 💵 ~€10-15 one-way 🕐 Depart mid-morning
Afternoon

Beach club day at Super Paradise

A short walk over the headland gets you to Super Paradise, the louder neighbor to Paradise Beach, with DJ sets, daybeds, and a full afternoon of Mykonos's beach scene.

🚶 ~15 min coastal path 💵 ~€70-120 sunbeds for two 🕐 Peak scene 1-5 PM
Evening

Sunset drinks before heading back to Chora

Beach bars keep the music going into the evening; catch the last water taxi or a pre-booked transfer back into town for dinner.

⛴️ ~20 min water taxi back to Chora 💵 ~€16 cocktail 🕐 Golden hour
Morning

Boat over to Delos

Excursion boats leave the old harbor a few times a morning; sailings are limited and occasionally cancelled in strong Meltemi winds, so book the earliest slot you can.

⛴️ ~30 min boat crossing 💵 ~€25-35 round trip 🕐 Depart by 9 AM
Afternoon

Terrace of the Lions & the sacred site

A UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the ancient world's most important sanctuaries, with the marble Lions, the Sacred Lake, and mosaic-floored merchant houses to explore. Bring water and a hat: there's almost no shade.

🚶 uneven ancient paths, wear real shoes 💵 ~€12 site entry 🕐 Midday, allow 2-3 hours
Evening

Back to Mykonos for a Little Venice dinner

After a dusty afternoon among the ruins, a table right over the water in Little Venice is the easiest way to unwind, with the waves sometimes reaching the windows.

⛴️ ~30 min boat back to Chora 💵 ~€45-60 dinner for two 🕐 Reserve ahead in peak season
Morning

Panagia Paraportiani, five churches in one

Built up over centuries into one sculptural, sun-bleached mass, this is the most photographed church on the island, right at the edge of Little Venice.

🚶 ~5 min from Chora's center 💵 Free 🕐 Early morning, softest light
Afternoon

A calmer afternoon at Ornos Beach

Sheltered and family-friendly, Ornos trades the beach-club noise for a quiet swim, a few relaxed taverna sunbeds, and shallow water close to town.

🚌 ~10 min by local bus 💵 ~€2 bus fare 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Sunset dinner in Little Venice

Balconies built directly over the water make this Mykonos's signature sunset spot; arrive early for a front-row table before the crowds gather.

🚶 walkable from Ornos with a taxi/bus back 💵 ~€50-65 dinner for two 🕐 Sunset
Morning

One last quiet swim

Beach clubs open late morning, so an early swim at Super Paradise or Paradise means having the water almost to yourself before packing up.

⛴️ ~15 min water taxi 💵 Free public sand, sunbeds extra 🕐 Early morning
Afternoon

Last-minute shopping in Chora

Small boutiques and jewelers around Matoyianni Street make for an easy last afternoon before heading to the airport or port.

🚶 pedestrian shopping lanes 💵 ~€25 lunch 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Airport transfer & departure

Given the island's well-known taxi shortage, arrange a transfer or catch the public bus well ahead of your flight rather than counting on hailing a cab.

🚌 ~20 min by bus from Chora 💵 ~€2 bus, ~€25 taxi if available 🕐 2.5 hrs before flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Not obligatory: round up or leave 5-10% at sit-down restaurants and beach clubs.
  • No tipping expected at cafes, bakeries, or takeaway souvlaki spots.
  • Beach club sunbed and waiter service in peak season often includes a service charge; check the bill.

👗 Dress Code

  • Cover shoulders and knees when visiting Panagia Paraportiani or other churches.
  • Mykonos has a genuine dress-to-impress evening scene around Little Venice and the beach clubs; pack at least one elevated outfit.
  • Chora's lanes are smooth, worn marble, so bring sandals or shoes with real grip, not just flip-flops.

🚌 Getting Around

  • Public buses run from Fabrika Square to the main beaches and the airport, and are far cheaper than a taxi.
  • Caïque water taxis link the south-coast beaches (Platis Gialos, Paradise, Super Paradise, Agrari) all day in season.
  • Quad bikes and small ATVs are a popular way to reach beaches independently, given how few taxis the island has.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Mykonos has a well-documented taxi shortage; don't count on flagging one at the port or airport without a wait.
  • Strong summer Meltemi winds can delay or cancel the Delos boat; build in a buffer day if possible.
  • Reserve beach club sunbeds and popular restaurant tables in advance during July and August.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Greek

  • Yassas (Γεια σας): hello, formal
  • Efharisto (Ευχαριστώ): thank you
  • Ton logariasmo, parakalo (Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ): the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • Euro is the currency; cards are widely accepted in Chora and at most beach clubs.
  • Smaller tavernas, water taxis, and beach vendors sometimes prefer cash, so carry some euros.
  • ATMs are common around Chora and Fabrika Square; expect lines in peak season.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

📅

Go in late June or September

Same beaches and weather as peak summer, with noticeably lower hotel and beach-club prices and far fewer crowds.

🏖️

Free beach sections exist at every beach

Bring your own towel and skip the sunbed rental; you only pay for the section with waiter service if you want it.

🚌

Public buses over taxis

With so few licensed taxis on the island, the bus network is often faster as well as far cheaper for getting to the beaches.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🏝️

Agios Sostis or Fokos Beach

Undeveloped, no sunbeds, no music: a quiet alternative to the party beaches on the island's north side.

🏘️

Ano Mera village

Inland and largely bypassed by tourists, with genuinely local tavernas around its shaded main square.

🌅

Windmills at sunrise, not sunset

The famous photo spot is nearly empty at dawn, before the tour groups and cruise crowds arrive for golden hour.

✈️ Flights

  • Mykonos National Airport (JMK) sits about 4 km from Chora, roughly a 10-15 minute drive.
  • Flights from Athens take about 40 minutes and run frequently in summer; some European cities also add seasonal direct flights.
  • The high-speed ferry from Piraeus is a scenic, often cheaper alternative to flying, at around 2.5-4 hours depending on the boat.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Mykonos is notorious for having very few licensed taxis for the whole island, so expect long waits at the port and airport in peak season.
  • Ride-share apps have limited coverage here compared to Athens; don't rely on one being available on short notice.
  • The public bus from Fabrika Square is frequently the more reliable option for beaches and the airport alike.

🛵 ATVs & Rental Cars

  • Renting a quad bike or small car is one of the most popular ways to get around independently, given the taxi shortage.
  • Roads are narrow and can get busy near the beaches in July and August; drive cautiously and book ahead in peak season.
  • An International Driving Permit is recommended alongside your home license for non-EU visitors.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Chora: walkable to Little Venice, the windmills, and the restaurant scene, ideal for a car-free trip.
  • Platis Gialos or Psarou: beach-forward stays close to the water-taxi hub for Paradise and Super Paradise.
  • Ornos: a calmer, more family-friendly base with easy bus access into Chora.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Essential for this itinerary: the Delos day trip runs by excursion boat from the old harbor in Chora.
  • Sailings are limited and weather-dependent, so book ahead and keep a flexible day nearby in case of cancellation.
  • Caïque water taxis are the easiest way to hop between the south-coast beaches without a car.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • EU/Schengen entry rules apply; non-EU visitors should confirm visa requirements before booking flights.
  • Travel insurance is worth it given how often Meltemi winds affect the Delos ferry schedule.
  • Book beach club sunbeds and sought-after dinner tables in advance for July and August travel.
