7 Days on Route 66: Neon Motels, Painted Desert & the Santa Monica Pier

The western segment of Route 66, Albuquerque to Santa Monica, because all 2,400 miles from Chicago will not fit in a week: neon, Petrified Forest, burros, the Mojave and the pier.

7-day Route 66, USA (Albuquerque to Santa Monica) itinerary

How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at ABQ, the Rental Car Center & the first drive

Albuquerque Sunport (ABQ) is about 5 miles from downtown. Take the free shuttle from the commercial lane outside the first level to the Rental Car Center at 3400 University Blvd SE (open 24 hours, shuttles about every 10 minutes). Photograph every scratch, confirm it is an automatic and that LAX is the drop-off, then head to Old Town.

🚗 ~7 mi, 15-20 min from the Rental Car Center to Old Town 💵 Shuttle free, hotel parking varies (check current) 🕐 Plan around your landing time, then collect the car
Afternoon

Old Town Albuquerque & San Felipe de Neri

Old Town is Albuquerque's original plaza, with adobe shops, galleries and the church of San Felipe de Neri on the square. It is free to wander. Use a pay lot nearby (check current rates) rather than circling the narrow streets. The city sits at about 5,300 ft (1,600 m), so drink water and wear a hat.

🚗 Park once, then ~1 h on foot around the plaza 💵 Free to walk, parking in a pay lot (check current) 🕐 Mid-afternoon, with shops open until about 5 or 6 PM
Evening

Central Avenue neon, Nob Hill & green chile

Central Avenue became Route 66 through Albuquerque in 1937, and old motel and diner signs still glow after dark. Drive about 5 miles east from Old Town to the Nob Hill district for neon, street parking and dinner. Try a New Mexican plate with red or green chile (ask for "Christmas" to get both), around $12 to $20.

🚗 ~5 mi, 15 min from Old Town to Nob Hill 💵 Dinner ~$12-20, parking varies (check current) 🕐 After dark, when the neon is lit
Morning

Albuquerque to Gallup & the El Rancho Hotel

I-40 West covers 135 miles to Gallup in about 2 hours 10 minutes. Fuel is cheaper in New Mexico than in Arizona or California (see the car costs below), so fill up first. In Gallup, the 1937 El Rancho Hotel on Route 66 (1000 E Highway 66) has a Western lobby worth a quick look.

🚗 ~135 mi, 2 h 10 min on I-40 West 💵 Free to look; fuel ~$3.70 a gallon in New Mexico (Nov 2026) 🕐 Leave by 8 AM to reach the park with 3 hours of daylight
Afternoon

Petrified Forest National Park & the Painted Desert

Enter at I-40 exit 311, the park's north entrance. The 28-mile road takes about an hour without stops: Painted Desert Inn, the Route 66 Studebaker, Puerco Pueblo, Blue Mesa and Crystal Forest. Entry is $25 per vehicle for 7 days (nps.gov). The park keeps Arizona time all year and is open roughly 8 AM to 5 PM (check current).

🚗 ~70 mi, 1 h 10 min to exit 311, then 28 mi through the park 💵 $25 per vehicle, 7 days (nps.gov) 🕐 Arrive by 1 PM Arizona time; the park shuts around 5 PM
Evening

Holbrook & the Wigwam Motel

Leave by the south gate and follow US 180 for 19 miles to Holbrook, about 25 minutes. Sleep in a concrete teepee at the Wigwam Motel on old Route 66. A room was around $120 in a September 2026 news report (check current). It is small, so book months ahead. Chain motels in town start around $70.

🚗 ~19 mi, 25 min from the south gate to Holbrook 💵 Wigwam Motel ~$120, chain motels from ~$70 (check current) 🕐 Arrive before dark, when the teepees are best photographed
Morning

Winslow: Standin' on the Corner & La Posada

From Holbrook, I-40 West reaches Winslow in about 35 minutes (33 miles). Standin' on the Corner Park, at Kinsley Avenue and 2nd Street, honours the Eagles song with a statue, a mural and a painted shield, in a public park. La Posada, a 1930 Harvey House hotel, is a short walk away. Ask about visiting if you are not staying.

🚗 ~33 mi, 35 min on I-40 West 💵 Public park, coffee ~$4-6 (check current) 🕐 Mid-morning, before tour buses and the midday glare
Afternoon

Flagstaff: the depot & historic Route 66

Winslow to Flagstaff is 57 miles, about 55 minutes on I-40. Park near the 1926 depot, now the visitor centre, and stroll Route 66 and Santa Fe Avenue for murals, bookshops and cafés. At about 7,000 ft (2,130 m) Flagstaff stays cool even in spring, and snow is possible from late autumn to April. Check az511.gov before leaving.

🚗 ~57 mi, 55 min on I-40 West 💵 Free to walk, lunch ~$12-18, parking varies 🕐 Early afternoon, then leave by 4 PM
Evening

Williams: the Route 66 main street

Williams is 33 miles west of Flagstaff, about 35 minutes. It was the last Route 66 town bypassed by I-40, in 1984, and its main street still has neon motels, diners and the Grand Canyon Railway depot. The South Rim is about 60 miles north and not part of this plan. Nights are cold at 6,800 ft.

🚗 ~33 mi, 35 min on I-40 West 💵 Motel ~$90-150, dinner ~$15-25 (check current) 🕐 Arrive by 5 PM and walk the strip at dusk
Morning

Seligman & Delgadillo's Snow Cap

Fill up in Williams or Seligman: fuel on the 87-mile road to Kingman is not guaranteed, so do not count on it. I-40 West takes about 45 minutes to Seligman (44 miles), where the Route 66 revival began in 1987. Stop at Delgadillo's Snow Cap Drive-In for a cheap burger and the jokes; hours are seasonal (check current).

🚗 ~44 mi, 45 min on I-40 West 💵 Burger and shake ~$10-15 (check current) 🕐 Arrive by 9:30 AM, before the tour buses
Afternoon

Hackberry General Store & Kingman

From Seligman, Historic Route 66 runs 87 miles to Kingman, paved and quiet, through Peach Springs (Hualapai land), Truxton and Valentine. Hackberry General Store, about 47 miles along, is a free stop of old signs and vintage pumps. In Kingman, the Arizona Route 66 Museum in the Powerhouse costs about $10 (check hours).

🚗 ~87 mi, 1 h 50 min on Historic Route 66 💵 Store free, Kingman museum ~$10 (check current) 🕐 Hackberry before 1 PM, since hours vary (check current)
Evening

Oatman, the burros & Needles

From Kingman, Oatman Road climbs Sitgreaves Pass: 28 miles, about 50 minutes, with hairpins, narrow lanes and no guardrails in places. Wild burros roam Oatman's main street; the BLM says do not approach, feed or touch them. Arrive by about 4 PM, then drive about 41 miles to Needles, California, for the night.

🚗 ~28 mi, 50 min to Oatman, then ~41 mi, 1 h to Needles 💵 Free to walk, motel in Needles ~$80-120 (check current) 🕐 Reach Oatman by 4 PM, before most shops close
Morning

Roy's Motel & Cafe in Amboy

Fill up in Needles first: the only fuel on the way is Fenner and Amboy. Take I-40 West about 65 miles to the Kelbaker Road exit, south 10 miles, then National Trails Highway about 8 miles to Roy's in Amboy. The gas station and shop are open; the motel was closed at last report. Carry water.

🚗 ~83 mi, 1 h 30 min via I-40 and Kelbaker Road 💵 Free to photograph, fuel is pricey (check current) 🕐 Leave Needles by 8 AM to beat the desert heat
Afternoon

Bagdad Cafe, Newberry Springs

The old road west of Amboy has reported bridge closures, so go back to I-40 and drive about 75 miles to Newberry Springs, roughly 1 hour 20 minutes. Fuel is limited between Ludlow and Newberry Springs. The Bagdad Cafe, made famous by a late-1980s film, is mainly a photo stop, as food service has been patchy.

🚗 ~75 mi, 1 h 20 min via Kelbaker Road and I-40 💵 Drink and souvenirs ~$5-15 (check current) 🕐 Early afternoon, since opening hours vary
Evening

Barstow: Casa del Desierto & the Mother Road Museum

Barstow is 22 miles further, about 25 minutes. Casa del Desierto, the 1911 Harvey House beside the railway, holds the Route 66 Mother Road Museum, free (donations welcome) and reported open Friday to Sunday, around 10 AM to 4 PM (check current). Motels cost about $80 to $120, and trains pass at night, so ask for a quiet room.

🚗 ~22 mi, 25 min on I-40 West 💵 Museum free, motel ~$80-120 (check current) 🕐 Reach the museum before 4 PM, if it is open
Morning

Cajon Pass & the Wigwam Motel, San Bernardino

I-15 South climbs through Cajon Pass and drops into the San Bernardino Valley, about 80 miles and 1 hour 20 minutes. The Wigwam Motel, 2728 E Foothill Blvd, is a row of concrete teepees on the old Route 66 alignment. Ask at the office before walking the grounds. Leave before 8 AM to beat Los Angeles traffic.

🚗 ~80 mi, 1 h 20 min via I-15 and Cajon Pass 💵 Free to photograph from outside 🕐 Mid-morning, then keep moving to avoid the rush hour
Afternoon

Old Pasadena & the Colorado Street Bridge

Foothill Boulevard and then I-210 West lead to Pasadena in about 55 minutes (45 miles). Old Pasadena on Colorado Boulevard has cafés and murals. The 1913 Colorado Street Bridge, a concrete arch over the Arroyo Seco, can be seen from the road or from the park below. Public garages charge by the hour (check current).

🚗 ~45 mi, 55 min via Foothill Blvd and I-210 💵 Free sights, parking by the hour (check current), lunch ~$15-20 🕐 Early afternoon, then leave by 3:30 PM
Evening

Santa Monica Pier & the End of the Trail

Pasadena to Santa Monica is about 27 miles: 45 minutes off-peak, up to 1.5 hours at rush hour. The Route 66 "End of the Trail" sign stands at the pier entrance, at Ocean and Colorado Avenues. Pier parking is roughly $4 an hour (check current), and hotel parking often adds a nightly fee, so ask when you book.

🚗 ~27 mi, 45 min to 1 h 30 min depending on traffic 💵 Pier free, parking ~$4 an hour (check current) 🕐 Sunset, with the pier lights coming on
Morning

Santa Monica State Beach & Palisades Park

Walk Santa Monica State Beach and the bluff-top Palisades Park, both free, before the crowds arrive. Ocean Avenue has cafés where breakfast runs about $12 to $20. Leave the hotel in time for the airport run: the 10-mile drive to the car return can take 30 to 60 minutes in traffic, so allow extra on a weekday morning.

🚶 No driving until the airport run, ~15 min on foot from the pier 💵 Free, breakfast ~$12-20 🕐 Early morning, with calm light and no crowds
Afternoon

Return the car at the LAX Rental Car Center

Fill the tank a few miles from LAX, since stations near the airport often charge more. The LAX Rental Car Center is at 5251 W 98th Street, next to the 405. Photograph the car, get a closed receipt, and check any one-way fee on it. Shuttles or the people mover (check current) run to the terminals.

🚗 ~10 mi, 30-60 min from Santa Monica to the Rental Car Center 💵 Fuel to refill ~$5.40-5.60 a gallon in California (Nov 2026) 🕐 Return the car at least 3.5 hours before an international flight
Evening

LAX departures

Plan to be at the terminal about 3 hours before an international flight and 2 hours before a domestic one. Many international airlines use the Tom Bradley International Terminal. If your flight leaves in the morning, return the car the evening before and take a rideshare from Santa Monica instead. Keep your passport and boarding pass in your day bag.

🚌 Shuttle or people mover from the Rental Car Center (check current) 💵 Rideshare from Santa Monica varies (check current) 🕐 At the terminal 3 hours before an international flight

Route 66, USA (Albuquerque to Santa Monica): what to know before you go

💵 Tipping, Taxes & Resort Fees

  • Prices in shops, restaurants and hotels are shown before sales and lodging tax, which is added at the register or on the bill. Expect the total to be noticeably higher than the menu or room price.
  • Tipping is expected in restaurants: about 15 to 20 percent of the pre-tax bill when you are served at the table, and a few dollars at counters and for bar drinks. Tip hotel housekeepers a few dollars a night.
  • Some hotels add a nightly "resort" or parking fee, especially in Santa Monica, so check the final price before you book.

🌶️ Diners, Chile & Road Food

  • In New Mexico you will be asked "red or green?" for chile sauce. Say "Christmas" for both. Green is usually milder, but heat varies, so ask.
  • Diners often seat you or tell you to sit anywhere. Coffee refills are common, and water arrives free. Check the menu board before ordering at drive-ins such as the Snow Cap.
  • Alcohol must stay out of reach in the car, and open containers are illegal for drivers and passengers in all three states.
  • In the desert, buy water and snacks when you see a shop, as the next one may be an hour away.

🚗 Driving Etiquette on the Mother Road

  • Drive on the right. Keep right except to pass, and use the pull-outs on slow historic roads if cars build up behind you.
  • At four-way stop signs the first car to arrive goes first. Right turns on a red light are allowed after a full stop unless a sign says otherwise.
  • Move over or slow down for stopped police, ambulances and tow trucks. Handheld phone use is banned in Arizona and California, so use hands-free and check each state's rules with its motor vehicle department.
  • Slow down in towns such as Oatman and Seligman, where people and burros share the street.

⚠️ Heat, Floods, Wildlife & Safety Warnings

  • Desert heat can be dangerous. Needles has averaged near 109 F (43 C) in July highs, and the Mojave National Preserve advises at least 1 gallon of water per person per day. Never leave children or pets in a parked car.
  • In the monsoon season, roughly July to September, never drive into a flooded wash. Check forecasts, as Arizona law can make drivers who ignore flood barricades pay for their own rescue (check current).
  • Wild burros can bite and kick, and the BLM says not to approach or feed them. Cattle and wildlife also cross rural roads at dusk, so slow down after dark.
  • Do not leave bags or devices visible in a parked car in Albuquerque, Barstow or Los Angeles; break-ins happen. For official advice, check travel.state.gov or your own government's travel advice for the USA.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in American English

  • "Regular, please" or "Fill it up" at the pump (gas = petrol, regular = standard unleaded), "Do I pay inside or at the pump?"
  • "Can I get the check, please?" (the bill), "To go" (takeaway), "Is tax included?" (usually not)
  • "Which exit?", "I-40 West" (the road and direction), "rest area" (service stop), "shoulder" (hard shoulder)
  • "Yá'át'ééh" (hello in Navajo) and a smile are welcome on Navajo land, where it is polite to ask before photographing people.

💳 Money & Payments

  • The US dollar is the only currency. Cards and contactless work nearly everywhere, but carry $40 to $60 in small notes for tips, burro-town shops and cash-only diners.
  • Many pumps ask for a US ZIP code. A foreign card may be refused there, so pay inside first or ask for pre-pay at the counter.
  • Use bank ATMs. Expect a fee of a few dollars from the ATM operator plus your own bank's fee, and always choose to be charged in dollars.

Insider tips for Route 66, USA (Albuquerque to Santa Monica)

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

⛽

Fill up in Arizona, not California

In mid-November 2026 California regular was about $1.50 a gallon above Arizona (AAA). Fill up in Kingman before Needles, then top up only as needed at Roy's or Ludlow, where desert prices are high.

🎟️

One park fee for the whole car

Petrified Forest costs $25 per vehicle, covering everyone in it for 7 days, so splitting the car halves it. The $80 America the Beautiful pass ($250 for non-US residents) only pays off if you add the Grand Canyon or other parks, so check nps.gov first.

🛒

Shop for a cooler lunch

Fill a small cooler at a supermarket in Albuquerque with water, fruit and sandwiches, and refill it in Flagstaff or Kingman. It saves roughly $10 to $15 per person on days with long gaps between towns.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌋

Climb Amboy Crater early in the cool season

The BLM trail leads to the crater rim near Roy's, but temperatures rise quickly by early afternoon. In the cooler months, start early, carry at least 1 litre of water per person and download maps, because there is little or no mobile signal.

☄️

Add Meteor Crater from Winslow

Meteor Crater is 6 miles off I-40 at exit 233. Adult admission was $29 for ages 14 to 59 on the official site (check current), and it is open about 8 AM to 5 PM. It adds around 1.5 to 2 hours to Day 3.

🚗

Spot the Studebaker on old Route 66

Petrified Forest is the only national park unit with a stretch of Historic Route 66 inside it. Look for the Studebaker monument beside the old alignment, about 4 miles past Painted Desert Inn, and stop for a photo on the way south.

Travel services for Route 66, USA (Albuquerque to Santa Monica)

✈️ Flights

  • Fly into Albuquerque Sunport (ABQ) and out of Los Angeles (LAX), booked as an open-jaw or multi-city ticket rather than two single returns.
  • The Sunport has a free shuttle to a rental car centre (see Day 1), but individual rental counters can have different hours (check current).
  • LAX is a long way from the Santa Monica pier by traffic, so keep a buffer for the car return and the shuttle to the terminals.
  • When you compare car hire, also price the reverse direction (LAX to ABQ), as one-way fees can differ (check current).

🚗 Car Hire

  • The minimum rental age is 21 at Enterprise, with a young-renter fee of about $25 a day (check current). Bring a full licence, a passport and a credit card in the driver's name for the deposit hold.
  • Choose an automatic. They are standard in the US, and manuals are rare and not worth the effort on this route.
  • Ask about the one-way fee before you book. Avis says most locations charge none, National typically charges no drop fee between airports, and Alamo may add one and shows it when booking.
  • Insurance traps: loss damage waivers and extra liability add up at the counter, so check what your card and travel insurance cover first.
  • Return the car with the same fuel level (usually full), and keep the fuel receipt in case of a dispute.

🛣️ Driving Rules & Roads

  • Drive on the right. Interstate speed limits are up to 75 mph in New Mexico and Arizona and up to 70 mph on rural California freeways, but always follow the posted signs.
  • Check closures before each driving day: nmroads.com (New Mexico), az511.gov (Arizona), quickmap.dot.ca.gov (California) and San Bernardino County Public Works for National Trails Highway (Route 66) closures near Amboy and Essex.
  • Time zones: Arizona does not use daylight saving time, but the Navajo Nation does. In summer, New Mexico and Navajo land are 1 hour ahead of the rest of Arizona and of California. In winter, Arizona and New Mexico share a time and California is 1 hour behind.
  • Fuel gaps: Seligman to Kingman (87 miles), Needles to Amboy (about 83 miles) and Ludlow to Newberry Springs (about 32 miles) have little or no fuel. Fill up before each, and never start a desert stretch below half a tank.
  • The Oatman Road has hairpins, steep drops and no guardrails in places, and burros on the road. Some old Route 66 sections are closed, damaged, or unpaved, such as parts of the Jericho Gap in Arizona and the National Trails Highway near Essex.
  • Winter snow can close I-40 around Flagstaff and Williams, so check az511.gov in the cold months.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Albuquerque (night 1): near Nob Hill or Old Town for Route 66 neon, about $85-130 a night (check current).
  • Holbrook (night 2): the Wigwam Motel is about $120, chain motels start around $70. Book the Wigwam months ahead.
  • Williams (night 3): Route 66 motels and inns, about $90-150 (check current), cold at 6,800 ft.
  • Needles (night 4): basic motels about $80-120 (check current), hot in summer, so ask for working air conditioning.
  • Barstow (night 5): chain motels about $80-120 (check current). Santa Monica (night 6): about $200-320 a night plus parking (check current), so book early.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • US eSIMs or prepaid SIMs work well. Check coverage on your phone plan, as signal is patchy on Route 66 between Seligman and Kingman and in the Mojave.
  • Download offline maps for New Mexico, Arizona and California before you leave Albuquerque.
  • There are no tolls on this route, but some Los Angeles freeways have ExpressLanes that need a transponder, so stay in the general lanes unless you check the rental company's toll rules.
  • Pay at the pump with a card, or prepay inside. Keep $40 to $60 in small notes for tips and cash-only shops.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Entry: many visitors need an ESTA or a visa. Check eligibility and apply at esta.cbp.dhs.gov (the fee is about $40, check current) or on travel.state.gov.
  • Take travel insurance with medical cover. US healthcare is expensive, and make sure it covers car hire excess if you want it.
  • Carry in the car: at least 1 gallon of water per person per day, snacks, a phone charger, a hat, sunscreen and a basic first-aid kit.
  • Declare fruit, vegetables and plants at the California agricultural inspection station on I-40 east of Needles (CDFA), and keep the hire agreement handy.

The Route 66 route, distances and driving times

This is the western segment of Route 66, not the whole road: the full route runs about 2,400 miles from Chicago to Santa Monica and needs two weeks or more, so a 7-day trip starts in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and ends at the Santa Monica Pier. The distance is about 805 miles on I-40 alone and about 930 miles with the detours used here: the Petrified Forest park road, the long Seligman to Kingman stretch, Oatman and Amboy. The route runs west, the traditional direction, with three longer driving days (Day 2, Day 4 and Day 5), and keeps the stretches that are still best preserved. The Grand Canyon is left out, as it adds a full day.

Is 7 days enough for the western half of Route 66?

Yes, for a fast but rewarding trip: about 930 miles, with three days of 4 to 4.5 hours of driving. It works if you accept short stops and skip the Grand Canyon. With 9 or 10 days you could add the South Rim, Meteor Crater and a lazy day in Kingman or Needles.

Can you do this route without a car, and why west?

Not practically: Seligman, Hackberry, Oatman and Amboy have no public transport, though guided tours exist (check current). West is the traditional direction, and it ends at the Santa Monica sign and LAX. The cost is afternoon sun in your eyes on the long westbound stretches, so keep sunglasses handy.

Does my credit card cover the rental car?

Some cards include collision damage cover, but liability is usually not included, and rules differ by card and country. Read the benefit guide, ask the rental company what it requires before you decline its cover, and photograph the car at pick-up and return (check current).
