Bulgaria Itineraries and Trip Costs

3 complete day-by-day trips in Bulgaria, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 3 Bulgaria itineraries: Sofia, Bansko, Borovets. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $360 to $700 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Sofia at about $115 a day, and the most expensive is Borovets at about $150 a day.

Trips run from 4 to 4 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
