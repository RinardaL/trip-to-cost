How the estimate is calculated

Every itinerary on TripToCost has a cost range per person for each trip length, from 1 day to the full trip. Budget uses the low end of that range, Comfortable the high end and Middle the midpoint. The total multiplies the per-person figure by the number of travellers. The estimate covers lodging, meals, local transport and activities, and leaves out international flights. Cost calculator FAQ

How does the trip cost calculator work?

It uses the cost range printed on each itinerary: a low figure for a careful budget and a high figure for a comfortable version, for every trip length from 1 day up to the full itinerary. Budget uses the low figure, Middle uses the midpoint and Comfortable uses the high figure. The total is that per-person figure multiplied by the number of travellers.

What does the estimate include?

Lodging, mid-range meals, local transport and activities per person. International flights are not included, because they depend on where you start.

Why is the price per day higher on short trips?

Getting there and the first night cost the same whether you stay one day or seven, so shorter trips cost less in total but more per day.

Does the total change for two people sharing a room?

A shared room usually lowers the lodging cost per person, but our figures do not split lodging out, so the calculator simply multiplies the per-person estimate. For two or more travellers, treat the total as a cautious upper estimate.
