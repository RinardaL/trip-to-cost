6 Days of Hidden Gems in Albania: Krujë, Valbona-Theth & the Blue Eye

A self-drive loop across a country most of Europe still hasn't discovered: Skanderbeg's hilltop castle and old bazaar in Krujë, a fjord-like ferry into the Accursed Mountains, the legendary Valbona-Theth trek, the impossibly blue Syri i Kaltër spring, Gjirokastër's Ottoman old town, and a final stretch of coastline at Palasë on the Albanian Riviera.

Ideal SeasonJun – Sep
Est. Budget$550-750
Duration6 Days
PaceActive
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land at Tirana International Airport & pick up the rental car

Nënë Tereza Airport sits about 17 km northwest of central Tirana, and picking up a rental car here on arrival is the easiest way to start a route that spans both the northern Alps and the southern coast in one loop.

🚗 ~25 min to central Tirana 💵 ~€30-40/day rental, book ahead 🕐 Arrivals cluster midday, expect a short queue at the counter
Afternoon

Skanderbeg Castle & Museum in Krujë

Krujë sits on a steep hillside about 35 km north of Tirana. The fortress where Gjergj Kastrioti Skanderbeg, Albania's national hero, held off Ottoman sieges in the 15th century now houses a museum devoted to his life, with sweeping views over the plain toward the Adriatic.

🚗 ~45 min drive from Tirana 💵 ~500 Lek museum entry 🕐 Aim for a mid-afternoon arrival, before the tour buses clear out
Evening

Browsing the Old Bazaar & a first Albanian dinner

Just below the castle walls, Krujë's restored Old Bazaar is the country's best-known traditional market, lined with stalls selling filigree silver, hand-woven rugs, and hammered copperware. A nearby restaurant serving qofte and byrek is a good first taste of Albanian home cooking before the drive back to Tirana for the night.

🚶 A short walk down from the castle 💵 ~800 Lek dinner 🕐 Late afternoon into early evening, then drive back to Tirana
Morning

Drive to Koman & board the Lake Komani ferry

From Tirana, the road runs north through Shkodër to the small port of Koman, where a public ferry threads through steep, pine-covered canyon walls on a reservoir often compared to a Nordic fjord. It's routinely rated one of the most scenic short boat rides in Europe.

🚗 ~2.5 hr drive from Tirana to Koman 💵 ~1,500-2,000 Lek ferry fare 🕐 The public ferry departs around 9 AM, don't miss it, the next one is hours later
Afternoon

Fierzë to Valbonë by shared 4x4 or furgon

The ferry docks at Fierzë, where minivans and 4x4s wait to run the final, unpaved stretch up the Valbona river valley. The road is rough, but the scenery, jagged limestone walls closing in on both sides, sets the tone for the two trekking days ahead.

🚙 ~1 hr on an unpaved valley road 💵 ~500 Lek shared transfer 🕐 Transfers time themselves around the ferry's arrival
Evening

Settle into a Valbonë guesthouse

Valbona Valley National Park has no hotels in the conventional sense, just family-run guesthouses serving home-cooked mountain dinners. Book ahead in summer: the handful of beds fill up fast before trekking days.

🏠 Family guesthouse, shared or private rooms 💵 ~2,500-3,500 Lek for dinner, bed & breakfast 🕐 Early night, the trek starts near sunrise
Morning

Climb to Qafa e Valbonës, the Valbona Pass

The trail leaves the valley floor and climbs steadily through beech forest and open scree to the 1,800 m pass, the high point separating Valbonë from Theth and one of the most-photographed vistas in the Bjeshkët e Namuna, Albania's "Accursed Mountains."

🥾 ~3-4 hr climb, ~1,000 m gain 💵 Free, guide optional (~€25-40/group) 🕐 Start by 7-8 AM to clear the pass before afternoon cloud builds
Afternoon

Descend into the Theth valley

The far side drops through pine forest and pasture into Theth, a valley long isolated by the mountains and known for its 19th-century lock-in tower, tied to the old Kanun code of blood-feud justice, and its small stone church. The full crossing runs about 17-19 km, roughly 6-8 hours end to end.

🥾 ~3 hr descent into Theth 💵 Free, within Theth National Park 🕐 Keep a buffer, the descent is longer than it looks on the map
Evening

Guesthouse dinner in Theth

A hot meal and a cold beer at a Theth guesthouse is well earned after the crossing. If your schedule allows a spare day here, the short walks to Grunas Waterfall and the smaller "Blue Eye of Theth" pool are worthwhile add-ons before continuing south.

🏠 Family guesthouse, home-cooked dinner 💵 ~2,500-3,500 Lek for dinner, bed & breakfast 🕐 Early night after a long trekking day
Morning

Furgon from Theth back to Shkodër

There is no shortcut from the Alps to the far south: the honest route backtracks first. A shared furgon winds down the improved but still switchbacking mountain road to Shkodër, where a stop at hilltop Rozafa Castle is an easy way to break up the ride.

🚌 ~2.5-3 hr furgon from Theth 💵 ~500 Lek furgon fare 🕐 Furgons usually leave Theth by 8 AM
Afternoon

On to Tirana, then south toward Gjirokastër

Realistically, connecting the two ends of this trip routes back through Tirana: about 1.5-2 hours from Shkodër, then a further 3.5-4 hours south to Gjirokastër via Durrës, Fier, and Tepelenë. It's a genuinely long day, closer to 8 hours of total transfers, so treat it as a travel day rather than a sightseeing one, and consider an overnight break in Tirana if the ferry or furgon connections run late.

🚗 ~3.5-4 hr drive, Tirana to Gjirokastër 💵 Rental car already paid 🕐 Depart Tirana by early afternoon at the latest
Evening

Arrive in Gjirokastër, dinner over the old town

Gjirokastër's stone-roofed skyline comes into view well before the road climbs into it. Check in, then find a terrace restaurant looking out over the Ottoman old town and the castle silhouette for a well-earned, unhurried dinner.

🚗 Arrival in the early evening 💵 ~1,000 Lek dinner 🕐 A slow evening after a long transfer day
Morning

Syri i Kaltër, the Blue Eye spring

About 55 km (roughly an hour) from Gjirokastër, this karst spring pushes cold water up from a channel plunging over 50 m deep, and the result is a saturated, almost unreal blue that photographs barely do justice to. A wooden platform lets you look straight down into it; swimming is allowed in the cooler outflow pool downstream, not the spring itself.

🚗 ~1 hr drive from Gjirokastër 💵 ~200 Lek entry 🕐 Arrive before 10 AM to beat the tour groups from Saranda
Afternoon

Wander Gjirokastër's UNESCO old town

Back in Gjirokastër, the "City of Stone" earns its UNESCO listing with steep cobbled lanes and grey-roofed Ottoman mansions, the restored Zekate House among the finest. It's also the birthplace of writer Ismail Kadare, whose novels are set in these same stone streets.

🚶 Walkable old town, steep in places 💵 ~300-500 Lek per house museum 🕐 Mid-to-late afternoon
Evening

Gjirokastër Castle at golden hour

The hilltop castle, one of the largest in the Balkans, holds a small arms museum and the wreck of a US Air Force jet forced down during the Cold War. Climb up for sunset, when the stone roofs below turn a deep amber.

🚶 Short uphill walk from the old town 💵 ~400 Lek entry 🕐 An hour before sunset for the best light
Morning

Drive over Llogara Pass to the coast

The road from Gjirokastër climbs through Tepelenë and into Llogara National Park, cresting the Ceraunian range at over 1,000 m before switchbacking down toward the sea. The viewpoint near the top looks straight down the Riviera coastline, with Corfu visible across the water on a clear day.

🚗 ~2 hr drive from Gjirokastër to the pass 💵 Free, no toll 🕐 Depart by mid-morning, the descent has heavy switchbacks
Afternoon

Palasë Beach on the Albanian Riviera

Quieter than nearby Dhërmi or Himarë, Palasë's long pebble-and-sand shoreline sits inside Llogara National Park, backed by mountains that drop almost straight into the Ionian Sea. The water is clear enough to see well past waist depth, a fitting final stop after six days of mountains and history.

🏖️ Free public beach, sunbeds ~500-800 Lek 💵 ~1,000-1,500 Lek for a beachside lunch 🕐 Early-to-mid afternoon for the calmest water
Evening

Drive back to Tirana & return the rental car

The coastal road and inland highway back to Tirana take roughly 3.5-4 hours from Palasë, so budget the whole evening for the return leg, especially if flying out the next morning rather than the same night.

🚗 ~3.5-4 hr drive back to Tirana 💵 Rental car already paid 🕐 Leave Palasë by mid-afternoon for a same-day flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Tipping isn't obligatory in Albania, but rounding up or leaving about 10% at sit-down restaurants is a welcome, increasingly common gesture.
  • Guesthouse hosts in Valbonë and Theth don't expect tips on top of the set dinner-and-bed rate, though it's appreciated for a memorable stay.
  • Furgon and taxi drivers rarely expect a tip; rounding to the nearest note is plenty.

👗 Dress Code & Village Etiquette

  • Rural mountain villages like Valbonë and Theth are conservative; modest, covered clothing is appreciated when you're off the trail, not just at the church in Theth.
  • Layer for temperature swings: a warm morning at Palasë has nothing to do with a cold pre-dawn start on the Valbona Pass two days earlier.
  • Coastal towns on the Riviera are casual and beachwear-friendly; save the modesty for the mountain villages and old towns.

🤝 Besa: Albania's Code of Honor

  • Besa, roughly "the keeping of a promise," is a centuries-old concept from the Kanun customary code and remains central to how Albanians see hospitality and trust.
  • In practice it shows up as remarkably warm, genuine hospitality, especially in mountain guesthouses, where a host's word is treated as absolute.
  • Accepting an offered coffee or raki is a small but real way to honor that hospitality; a firm but polite decline is fine too.

⚠️ Safety Warnings

  • The Qafa e Valbonës pass is genuinely closed by snow outside roughly June through September/October; conditions in early June can still be marginal, so check locally before committing to the crossing.
  • Mountain weather on the pass changes fast, start early, carry a rain layer, and be ready to turn back if cloud or storms roll in.
  • Llogara Pass's switchbacks and the Theth-Shkodër road both demand cautious, unhurried driving, especially in early summer traffic or wet conditions.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Albanian

  • Mirëdita: good day/hello
  • Faleminderit: thank you
  • Sa kushton?: how much does it cost?

💳 Money & Payments

  • Albania uses the Lek (ALL); cards work in Tirana, Gjirokastër, and Riviera resorts, but cash is essential in Valbonë, Theth, and most rural stops.
  • There are no ATMs in Valbonë or Theth, withdraw enough Lek in Tirana or Shkodër before heading into the mountains.
  • Albania remains noticeably cheaper than Croatia or Greece, though Riviera prices climb in July and August.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

⛴️

Take the public Komani ferry, not a private speedboat

Tour operators sell a faster private speedboat crossing at several times the price; the slower public ferry covers the same jaw-dropping scenery for a fraction of the cost.

🏠

Book guesthouses directly, skip the markup

Valbonë and Theth guesthouses often charge less when booked by phone or through local Facebook groups than through third-party booking sites.

🥪

Eat where the furgon drivers eat

Roadside lokal restaurants along the Tirana-Gjirokastër drive serve the same qofte and grilled meat as tourist-facing spots for noticeably less.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

💧

The Blue Eye of Theth, a quieter cousin of Syri i Kaltër

If a rest day in Theth allows it, this smaller mountain pool sees a fraction of the crowds that gather at the more famous southern spring.

🏛️

Zekate House over the crowded castle courtyard

Gjirokastër's restored Ottoman mansion gives a more intimate sense of the old town's stone architecture than the busier castle grounds.

🌅

Palasë over Dhërmi for a quieter beach day

A short drive from the busier resort strip at Dhërmi, Palasë keeps the same striking water color with noticeably fewer sunbeds and beach bars.

✈️ Flights

  • Tirana International Airport "Nënë Tereza" (TIA) is Albania's only major international gateway and the natural start and end point for this loop.
  • No domestic flights connect the north and south, the transfer on Day 4 genuinely has to be done by road.
  • Book arrival and departure with a buffer day if possible, given how tightly this route's transfers are timed.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • A rental car is the only practical way to link Krujë, the Riviera, and the drive segments of this route on a 6-day schedule.
  • Confirm the car is comfortable with the unpaved Fierzë-Valbonë road, or plan to park it in Fierzë and use local transfers instead.
  • Roads have improved significantly in recent years but still run slower than distances suggest, especially over Llogara Pass and through the mountains near Theth.

⛴️ The Komani Lake Ferry

  • The public ferry between Koman and Fierzë runs on a set daily schedule, typically once each direction; missing it means a multi-hour wait or an expensive private alternative.
  • Confirm the current departure time locally or with your guesthouse before Day 2, schedules shift by season.
  • Cars can be left at the Koman dock in a paid lot if you'd rather return the same way after Theth, though this itinerary continues south instead.

🏠 Guesthouses in Valbonë & Theth

  • Neither valley has hotels in the usual sense; family-run guesthouses with home-cooked dinners are the standard, and often the highlight, of the stay.
  • Rooms fill up in July and August, book at least a few weeks ahead for peak summer dates.
  • Rates typically bundle dinner, bed, and breakfast together and are usually settled in cash.

🚕 Taxis & Furgons

  • Furgons, shared minivans that leave once full, are the backbone of budget travel between towns, including the Theth-Shkodër leg on Day 4.
  • Ride-hailing apps work in Tirana but have little to no coverage once you're north of Shkodër or south of Vlorë.
  • Hotel-arranged taxis are the most reliable option in Gjirokastër and along the Riviera if you're without the rental car for a stretch.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Albania isn't in the Schengen area, so double-check passport and entry requirements separately even if arriving from a Schengen country.
  • Travel insurance covering trekking and rural medical evacuation is worth adding given the Day 3 mountain crossing.
  • This is squarely a warm-season itinerary: outside June-September, the Valbona Pass is typically closed and guesthouses in Valbonë and Theth largely shut down.
