From idea to itinerary

Four steps. Zero spreadsheets.

Here's exactly what you get with every TripToCost guide, start to finish.

1

Pick a trip that matches how you travel

Browse by style (budget, culture, luxury, road trip, wellness, hidden gems) or search a destination directly. Every itinerary states its real budget tier and pace upfront.

2

Follow the day-by-day plan

Each of the 7 days is broken into morning, afternoon, and evening blocks, with realistic travel times between stops, not just a list of attractions with no sense of order.

3

Prep with the Culture and Packing tabs

Local etiquette, tipping norms, safety notes, and a packing checklist tuned to your destination's actual season and terrain, so you can check items off as you pack.

4

Travel with the Insider Tricks in your pocket

Money-saving hacks and off-the-beaten-path spots for each trip, so you're not just seeing what every other visitor sees.
