4 Days in Vienna: Christmas Markets, Palaces & Coffeehouses

Palaces, Klimt, coffeehouse cake and hot punch under the Rathaus lights: four days in Vienna in Christmas market season, with a plan that works in other months too.

Ideal SeasonNov – Dec (Christmas Markets)
Est. Budget$850-1,150
Duration4 Days
PaceModerate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Vienna Airport & into the centre

The main S-Bahn line from Vienna Airport (VIE) is closed until October 2027, so premium buses replace the City Airport Train to Wien Mitte (about 21 minutes, from around €7). Railjet trains reach Wien Hauptbahnhof in about 15 minutes for roughly €5.50. Airport Lines buses cost €11 one way, so check the ÖBB and airport sites.

🚆 ~15-25 min by Railjet or CAT bus 💵 ~€5.50 Railjet, from ~€7 CAT bus, €11 airport bus (check current) 🕐 Plan around your landing time, then drop your bags at the hotel
Afternoon

Stephansdom, the Graben & Kärntner Straße

Stephansdom dominates Stephansplatz. The sightseeing visit has set hours (Monday to Saturday 9:00 to 11:30 and 13:00 to 16:30) and an €8 fee, and services can limit access. The south tower climb is 343 steps, and the north tower has a lift, each €8. Then walk the Graben to the Kärntner Straße.

🚶 Stephansplatz is on U1 and U3, the rest is pedestrian 💵 Free to walk, €8 cathedral visit, €8 per tower 🕐 Late afternoon, allow 2-3 hours with a tower climb
Evening

Rathausplatz Christkindlmarkt & your first punch

The Wiener Christkindlmarkt fills the square before the City Hall (Rathaus) from 13 November to 26 December in 2026, daily 10:00 to 22:00 (24 December until 18:30). Try Punsch, Glühwein or Maroni (roasted chestnuts). Drinks come in a deposit mug: in 2024 punch ran from about €5 to €8.50 with a €5 deposit.

🚋 Tram 1, 2 or D along the Ring, or ~25 min on foot from Stephansplatz 💵 Free entry, ~€5-8.50 per punch plus a ~€5 mug deposit (2024 prices) 🕐 After dark for the lights, weekdays are quieter than weekend evenings
Morning

Hofburg: Sisi Museum & Imperial Apartments

The Sisi Museum and Imperial Apartments in the Hofburg cover court life under Franz Joseph and Empress Elisabeth. The redesigned Sisi Museum is due to reopen on 19 November 2026, with reduced prices and shorter hours until then, so confirm what is open. Book a timed ticket through the official shop, as the palace warns about fake resellers.

🚶 ~10 min walk from Stephansplatz via the Graben and Kohlmarkt 💵 ~€15-20 Sisi Museum day ticket (reduced until 18 Nov, check current) 🕐 Arrive at opening, allow 2-3 hours
Afternoon

Coffeehouse culture: Demel & Café Sacher

Demel on Kohlmarkt is open daily 10:00 to 19:00 and does not take reservations. Café Sacher, beside the Opera, serves the Original Sacher-Torte. Café Central closed in March 2026 for renovation, with a pop-up called Decentral on the Freyung, so check whether it has reopened. Order a melange and take your time.

🚶 Kohlmarkt is ~5 min from the Hofburg, Café Sacher ~10 min 💵 ~€4-7 per melange, ~€7-11 per slice of cake (check current) 🕐 Mid-afternoon, off-peak hours are calmer
Evening

Spittelberg Christmas market

Spittelberg is a quarter of Biedermeier houses and narrow lanes behind the MuseumsQuartier, and its market leans toward crafts as well as punch. The 2026 dates are 13 November to 23 December, Monday to Friday 14:00 to 21:30 and weekends and holidays from 11:00. Mug deposits differ from market to market, so ask at the stall.

🚶 ~15-20 min walk from the Hofburg via the MuseumsQuartier 💵 Free entry, ~€5 per punch plus a mug deposit (check current) 🕐 Weekday stalls open from 2:00 PM, weekends are busier
Morning

Schönbrunn Palace & the Gloriette

Schönbrunn opens daily from 8:30 (check winter closing times), and tickets are timed and sold through the official shop. The State Apartments ticket (€30, about 40 minutes with audio guide) is the short visit, and the Palace Ticket (€42, about 75 minutes) the longer one. The gardens are free; check whether the Gloriette terrace is open in winter.

🚇 U4 to Schönbrunn, ~15 min from Karlsplatz 💵 €42 Palace Ticket or €30 State Apartments, gardens free (check current) 🕐 Arrive at the 8:30 opening, allow 3 hours with the gardens
Afternoon

Schönbrunn Christmas market & the Naschmarkt

The palace courtyard hosts a Christmas market with more than 90 stalls, ice skating and Eisstock (Austrian curling), listed for 6 November to 6 January, daily 10:00 to 21:00. Then ride the U4 to Kettenbrückengasse for the Naschmarkt. Stalls are open Monday to Saturday (to 21:00 on weekdays, 18:00 on Saturday).

🚇 U4 from Schönbrunn to Kettenbrückengasse, ~10 min 💵 Free to browse, pay per dish or stall 🕐 Early afternoon at the palace, then the market before it closes
Evening

State Opera on a standing ticket

The Vienna State Opera sells standing places at a separate box office, with prices in recent seasons from roughly €4 to €18. Check the Staatsoper's standing-room page for the current sale time, since the box office has opened about 80 minutes before curtain. Dress smartly. Schönbrunn Palace Concerts run daily at 8:30 PM in the Orangery.

🚶 Karlsplatz station (U1, U2, U4) is beside the Opera, ~15 min walk from the Naschmarkt 💵 Standing room roughly €4-18 (check current) 🕐 Queue well before curtain, latecomers may wait for a break
Morning

Upper Belvedere & Klimt's The Kiss

The Upper Belvedere holds Gustav Klimt's The Kiss among its Klimt masterpieces. A timed ticket is required: it costs €23 per adult and opens daily 9:00 to 18:00 (check current), and a 2-in-1 day ticket with the Lower Belvedere is €32. Book online and choose the first slot of the day, since the Klimt rooms get busy by late morning.

🚋 Tram D to Schloss Belvedere, or a ~20 min walk from Karlsplatz 💵 €23 Upper Belvedere, €32 with the Lower Belvedere 🕐 Book the 9:00 slot, allow 2 hours
Afternoon

Karlskirche & the Ringstrasse by tram

Karlskirche is a baroque church with a green copper dome. Entry is €9.50 and includes the panoramic terrace, so check whether the terrace lift is running. Then ride the Ringstrasse by tram: lines 1 and 2 (and D and 71) pass the State Opera, Hofburg, Parliament and City Hall, but each covers only part of the Ring.

🚶🚋 ~15 min walk from Belvedere, then a Ring tram near the Opera 💵 €9.50 Karlskirche, tram included in a normal ticket 🕐 Afternoon, allow 1 hour at the church and 30-40 min on the tram
Evening

Riesenrad & the transfer to the airport

The Wiener Riesenrad in the Prater rises nearly 65 m over the rooftops, and tickets can be booked online to skip the queue. Adult tickets have been around €15, so check current prices. From Praterstern on U1, the VAB 5 bus runs directly to the airport (new in September 2026). Skip the wheel if your flight is early.

🚇 U1 to Praterstern, then the VAB 5 airport bus 💵 ~€15 Riesenrad, €11 airport bus (check current) 🕐 Leave central Vienna at least 3 hrs before an international flight

💶 Tipping & Coffeehouse Etiquette

  • Service is rarely included in the bill. Round up or add about 5-10% at restaurants and cafés, and say the total you want to pay as you hand over the money rather than leaving coins on the table.
  • In a coffeehouse you can stay as long as you like over one coffee, and waiters do not bring the bill until you ask. A glass of water traditionally comes with the coffee, though some well-known cafés now charge for water, so check the menu.
  • Greet staff when you enter a shop or café with "Grüß Gott" or "Guten Tag", and say "Auf Wiedersehen" when you leave.

🍷 Market Punch, Mugs & Hours

  • Punch and Glühwein come in a deposit mug (Häferl). Deposits vary by market and year, roughly €2 to €5 in recent seasons, and the mug designs change each year. Return the mug at the market where you bought it for the refund, or keep it as a souvenir.
  • Most big markets open late morning and close at 21:00 or 22:00, and some end before Christmas: Rathausplatz runs to 26 December, while Spittelberg, Am Hof and Karlsplatz stop on 23 December. Hours on 24 December are shorter (check current).
  • Carry some coins and small notes, since individual stalls may not take cards or may set a minimum.

🚋 Trams, Tickets & Sunday Closures

  • Paper tickets must be validated (stamped) before your first ride, at the blue validators at station entrances or inside trams and buses. Tickets bought in the Wiener Linien app are activated digitally. Riding without a valid ticket brings an on-the-spot fine of about €135 (check current).
  • Most shops, including supermarkets, are closed on Sundays and public holidays. Small shops in train stations and at the airport are usually open, as are markets, restaurants, cafés and museums.
  • Vienna runs trams, buses, the U-Bahn and S-Bahn on one fare system, so one ticket covers all of them within the city zone.

⚠️ Safety, Dress & Etiquette Warnings

  • Vienna is generally safe for visitors, but keep your bag zipped and in front of you in crowded markets and on the U-Bahn. Buy concert and opera tickets only from the venue's own website or box office, not from costumed street sellers.
  • Stephansdom and Karlskirche are active churches. Cover your shoulders, keep your voice down, and expect limited access during services.
  • Markets are outdoors, so dress in warm layers, a hat and gloves. The State Opera asks guests to dress elegantly, so pack one smarter outfit for the opera or a concert.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in German

  • Grüß Gott: hello
  • Danke: thank you
  • Zahlen, bitte: the bill, please
  • Einen Punsch, bitte: a punch, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • Austria uses the euro, and contactless cards are accepted in most restaurants, shops and museums. Keep some cash for market stalls, small cafés and tips.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs and avoid standalone currency-exchange booths, which tend to offer weaker rates. Ask your card issuer about foreign-transaction fees before you travel.
  • The $850-1,150 budget per person covers about three nights in a mid-range hotel (rates rise in market season), meals, local transport, entry fees and one standing opera ticket, but not international flights.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎟️

Count your rides before buying a pass

A single ticket costs about €3, a 24 Stunden WIEN ticket €9.70 and a 7 Tage WIEN ticket €25.20 (2026 fares, which rise each January). Four 24-hour tickets would cost €38.80, so a 7-day ticket is cheaper for this trip if you ride daily, and the old 48- and 72-hour tickets are no longer sold. The Vienna City Card adds sight discounts, but they are small (about €1.50 off the Upper Belvedere), so it only pays off if you use many.

🎭

Stand at the State Opera

Standing places are the cheapest way into the house, roughly €4 to €18 in recent seasons depending on the area. Check the Staatsoper's standing-room page for the sale time, and dress warmly in case you queue.

🍷

Get your mug deposit back

The deposit is often €4 to €5 per cup, so across several evenings it adds up. Return each mug at the market where you bought it, or keep one as a souvenir and treat the deposit as its price.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🕯️

Smaller markets for a calmer evening

Spittelberg, Am Hof and the Altwiener Christkindlmarkt on the Freyung all run from mid-November to 23 December and are smaller than Rathausplatz, with more craft stalls.

⏰

Rathausplatz at opening time

The Rathausplatz market opens at 10:00 daily, so a weekday late-morning visit avoids the evening crowds. Come back after dark on another day for the lights.

🛍️

The Saturday flea market

If Day 3 falls on a Saturday, swap the order: go to the Naschmarkt flea market first (it runs roughly 6:30 AM to 2:00-3:00 PM, check current) and Schönbrunn afterwards. On a Sunday the Naschmarkt stalls are closed, though its restaurants stay open.

✈️ Flights

  • Vienna International Airport (VIE) is in Schwechat, southeast of the centre. Check which terminal your airline uses. The airport lies outside the Vienna core zone, so a city ticket does not cover the whole trip, and the Railjet fare is split into an airport part and a city part.
  • The S-Bahn main line is closed until October 2027. The CAT is replaced by premium buses between Wien Mitte (Marxergasse 1b) and Terminal 3, roughly every 12 to 15 minutes from about 6:10 AM from the airport, and the S7 runs to Wien St. Marx. Railjet, Intercity and REX7 trains still run to Hauptbahnhof.
  • Vienna Airport Lines buses run to Hauptbahnhof and Westbahnhof (VAB 1), Schwedenplatz (VAB 2), Donauzentrum (VAB 3), Karlsplatz (VAB 4) and Praterstern (VAB 5), at €11 one way. Check all routes and times before travelling.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Uber and Bolt both operate in Vienna, and Freenow books licensed taxis. The main taxi companies are 40100, 31300 and 60160, with apps and phone numbers using the same digits.
  • A fixed fare from an address in Vienna to the airport has been quoted at about €36 to €45. Agree the price before you set off, and note that licensed taxis must accept cards.
  • Taxis are rarely needed in the centre, where trams, the U-Bahn and walking cover most of this itinerary.

🚇 Public Transport & Tickets

  • Wiener Linien runs the U-Bahn, trams and buses. In 2026 a single ticket is about €3, 24 Stunden WIEN is €9.70 and 7 Tage WIEN is €25.20, with higher fares announced for 2027. The 48- and 72-hour tickets ended on 1 January 2026.
  • The WienMobil app sells tickets and plans routes. A Tap+Ride pilot lets visitors pay with a contactless card, so check whether it is still running.
  • The Vienna City Card includes transport and sight discounts (24 hours €19, 72 hours €37 in the Tourist Board's 2026 brochure). Stamp paper tickets before your first ride.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Innere Stadt (1st district): walkable to Stephansdom, the Graben, the Hofburg and the Rathausplatz market, but the most expensive base.
  • Karlsplatz and Wieden: on U1, U2 and U4, close to the Opera, the Naschmarkt and Karlskirche, and a short tram ride from Belvedere.
  • Mariahilf and Neubau: the MuseumsQuartier and Spittelberg on foot, with U2 and U3 nearby.
  • Near Hauptbahnhof or Landstraße: close to Belvedere and about 15 minutes by Railjet to the airport. Book early for market season.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • Austria is in the EU, so EU SIM holders roam at home rates. Other visitors can use an eSIM or local prepaid SIM, and many cafés offer Wi-Fi.
  • Most places take contactless cards, but keep some cash for market stalls and small purchases.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs, and avoid standalone exchange booths.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Check your passport's Schengen rules before booking, including whether ETIAS applies on your travel dates (it was planned to start in the last quarter of 2026).
  • Book timed tickets for the Hofburg, Schönbrunn and Belvedere ahead in market season. Many sights and all markets have shorter hours on 24 and 25 December.
  • Car rental is not needed for this itinerary. Trams, the U-Bahn and walking cover it, and the airport links above handle both transfers.
