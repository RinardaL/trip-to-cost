Summer in Winter: Warm Places for the Cold Months

16 itineraries for escaping the cold: hot beaches, mild sunny cities and a southern-hemisphere summer, with prices per person.

Last updated: November 2026

Each itinerary is a complete short trip with the price up front: lodging, meals, local transport and activities are included in the estimate, international flights are not. Be honest about the weather when you choose, and read the season line on each page.

What winter sun covers, and which trip fits you

These sixteen trips share one idea: go somewhere warm, or at least mild, while the northern hemisphere is cold. They are not all beach holidays. Nine are beach and coastal trips, two are modern cities, two are historic cities and three are nature trips.

For the lowest daily cost, look at Luang Prabang at $85 per day, Alicante at $95, then Gran Canaria and Sharm el-Sheikh at $100. If you want a longer stay with several stops, Bangkok & Andaman Coast, Bangkok & Phuket, Canary Islands and Cairo run six or seven days. At the other end, Waitomo works out at $260 per day over only three days, and Socotra Island at $245 per day over five.

Travellers who would rather sightsee than sunbathe can choose Singapore or Dubai for a modern city, or Cairo and Luang Prabang for temples and old towns. Socotra Island is a remote island trip: its page says nearly every visitor arrives on a seasonal or charter flight via Abu Dhabi and travels with a local guide. It belongs to Yemen, so read your government's travel advice before you book.

Choosing the right month for the sun

Warm does not mean the same thing everywhere. The mainland Spanish trips, Málaga and Alicante, are listed year-round with mild winters and their best months in spring and autumn, so expect pleasant days rather than hot ones in January. Málaga's January highs average about 16 to 17°C. The Canary Islands trips (Canary Islands, Tenerife and Gran Canaria) are also listed year-round, with October to April, or November to April for Gran Canaria, as the months the pages recommend for winter sun.

Asia follows a dry season. Bangkok & Andaman Coast lists November to March, and Bangkok & Phuket and Luang Prabang November to February, the cool dry months in northern Laos. Singapore is listed year-round, and February is typically its driest month. Cairo and Sharm el-Sheikh list October to April, and Dubai November to March, which keeps you out of the summer heat, when Luxor and Aswan regularly pass 40°C.

The Caribbean and southern hemisphere trips follow their own logic. Curaçao is year-round and driest from February to June, San Juan lists December to April, and Waitomo lists November to March because those are summer months in New Zealand.

Combining trips and keeping the bill down

Several trips overlap, so choose rather than stack them. Bangkok & Andaman Coast and Bangkok & Phuket both start in Bangkok and both include a Phi Phi Islands day trip, one continuing to Railay and the Andaman coast, the other to Phuket. The Canary Islands work the same way: Canary Islands covers Tenerife and Lanzarote, so Tenerife overlaps with its Tenerife days, and both visit Mount Teide, while Gran Canaria is a separate island.

Cairo and Sharm el-Sheikh show two very different sides of one country, history on the Nile and reef snorkelling on the Red Sea, so they work best as separate trips.

To keep costs down, favour the Spanish short breaks: Gran Canaria at $300-500 per person, Málaga at $300-520 and Alicante at $270-480, or Luang Prabang at $270-410. The trip estimates exclude international flights, so compare fares before you compare itineraries, especially for the long-haul trips.

Frequently asked questions

Where is warm in winter?

For real heat choose Thailand, Singapore, Curaçao, San Juan, Dubai or Sharm el-Sheikh. For mild sunny weather choose the Canary Islands, Málaga, Alicante or Cairo.

Which winter sun trip is the cheapest?

Luang Prabang ($270-410), Alicante ($270-480), Sharm el-Sheikh ($300-500) are the three lowest on this list, per person and excluding flights.

Can you swim in Málaga or Alicante in winter?

The sea is cold for most swimmers, so plan walks, sights and tapas rather than beach days. For warm-water swimming choose Thailand, Curaçao, San Juan or Sharm el-Sheikh.

How much does a winter sun trip cost?

Our 16 itineraries range from about $270 to $1,600 per person for the whole trip, excluding flights.

What is the best month to go?

Each itinerary lists its ideal season. Most of these are at their best from about November to March, while Curaçao and Singapore work all year and Waitomo peaks in the southern summer.

Which winter sun trips work as a short break?

The four-day trips are the easiest to fit in: Singapore, Luang Prabang, Dubai, Sharm el-Sheikh, Curaçao, San Juan, Gran Canaria, Málaga and Alicante. Waitomo is the only three-day trip. If you have a full week, Thailand, the Canary Islands and Egypt are the seven-day plans.

Which Canary Islands trip should I pick?

Pick the seven-day Canary Islands trip ($1,200-1,600 per person) for Tenerife plus Lanzarote, Tenerife alone ($740-1,150 per person) for a resort coast, Mount Teide and theme parks in five days, or Gran Canaria ($300-500 per person) for four days around Las Palmas, the mountains and the Maspalomas dunes. They overlap, so book one.
