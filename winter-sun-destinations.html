Summer in Winter: Warm Places for the Cold Months

15 itineraries for escaping the cold: hot beaches, mild sunny cities and a southern-hemisphere summer, with prices per person.

Between roughly November and March these places are warm or pleasantly mild while much of Europe and North America is cold. Some are properly hot, some are sunny and mild rather than hot, and one has its summer in the southern hemisphere.

Each itinerary is a complete short trip with the price up front: lodging, meals, local transport and activities are included in the estimate, international flights are not. Be honest about the weather when you choose, and read the season line on each page.

Frequently asked questions

Where is warm in winter?

For real heat choose Thailand, Singapore, Curaçao, San Juan, Dubai or Sharm el-Sheikh. For mild sunny weather choose the Canary Islands, Málaga, Alicante or Cairo.

Which winter sun trip is the cheapest?

Luang Prabang ($270-410), Alicante ($270-480), Sharm el-Sheikh ($300-500) are the three lowest on this list, per person and excluding flights.

Can you swim in Málaga or Alicante in winter?

The sea is cold for most swimmers, so plan walks, sights and tapas rather than beach days. For warm-water swimming choose Thailand, Curaçao, San Juan or Sharm el-Sheikh.

How much does a winter sun trip cost?

Our 15 itineraries range from about $270 to $1,600 per person for the whole trip, excluding flights.

What is the best month to go?

Each itinerary lists its ideal season. Most of these are at their best from about November to March, while Curaçao and Singapore work all year and Waitomo peaks in the southern summer.
