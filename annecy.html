4 Days in Annecy: Canals, Lake Swims & Alpine Paragliding

France's cleanest alpine lake wraps around a medieval old town of flower-lined canals and a castle-like island palace, then opens onto four unhurried summer days of lake swims, a lakeshore bike ride, a hilltop château, and a tandem paraglide over water so clear you can see the bottom from a thousand feet up.

Ideal SeasonMay – Sep
Est. Budget$700-950/person
Duration4 Days
PaceRelaxed to Moderate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Train into Annecy from Geneva or Lyon

Annecy sits on a direct rail line from Geneva, with Lyon a straightforward TGV-plus-regional connection away, so most trips start with a scenic ride past farmland and foothills before the lake comes into view.

🚆 ~1h45 direct train from Geneva, or ~2 hrs from Lyon Part-Dieu with one change 💵 ~€24 from Geneva, ~€35-50 from Lyon 🕐 Trains run roughly hourly, no need to book far ahead outside peak summer
Afternoon

The Thiou canals and the Palais de l'Île

A short loop through the Vieille Ville follows the Thiou canal past geranium-covered bridges to the Palais de l'Île, a slender 12th-century building that has served as a lordly residence, a mint, a courthouse, and a prison, and is now widely considered France's most photographed building.

🚶 Flat walk through the old town, no transport needed 💵 Free to view from outside, ~€4 for the Palais de l'Île museum 🕐 Afternoon light on the canals, an early look beats the midday crowds
Evening

Jardins de l'Europe and the Pont des Amours

A lakeside park of towering plane and sequoia trees leads to the Pont des Amours, a small footbridge over a lily-covered canal where the lake, the mountains, and the old town's rooftops all line up for a first sunset.

🚶 ~5 min walk from the old town along the lakefront 💵 Free 🕐 An hour before sunset, when the light over the lake is best
Morning

A swim at the Plage de l'Impérial

Fed by mountain streams and dredged clean for decades, Lake Annecy is regularly ranked among Europe's cleanest, and the lawns and shallow entry at the Plage de l'Impérial make for an easy first swim before the day heats up.

🚶 ~15 min walk or short bus from the old town 💵 Free public lakefront access, a small fee at some guarded sections in peak season 🕐 Morning, before the beach and lake traffic fill up
Midday

A cruise on the Lac d'Annecy Express

A guided boat trip from the old port circles past Château de Menthon and the eastern shore's villages, giving a water-level view of the mountains ringing the lake that the shoreline paths can't quite match.

🚤 ~1 hr guided cruise departing from the old port 💵 ~€17-20 per adult 🕐 Boats run regularly through the day, late-morning departures are usually less crowded
Evening

A Savoyard dinner in the old town

Tartiflette and fondue savoyarde both trace back to this corner of the Alps, and after a day on the water a table along the canals with melted reblochon or a shared cheese pot is the natural way to end it.

🍽️ Sit-down dinner in the old town 💵 ~€20-30 per person for a tartiflette or fondue set menu 🕐 Book ahead in summer, canal-side tables fill quickly
Morning

Riding the Voie Verte lakeside greenway

One of France's oldest cycling greenways traces roughly 42 km around the lake on a mostly car-free path, and a rented bike makes it easy to cover the flat southern stretch toward Sévrier and Saint-Jorioz at an easy pace.

🚲 Bike rental in town, ride a section of the lakeside loop 💵 ~€20-25 per day for a rental bike 🕐 Morning, before the path gets busy with the midday crowd
Afternoon

Château d'Annecy and the Musée-Château

The hilltop castle that once housed the Counts of Geneva now holds a regional museum covering Savoyard art, local wildlife, and the lake's history, with a courtyard terrace that looks straight down over the old town's rooftops.

🚶 ~10 min uphill walk from the old town 💵 ~€6 museum entry 🕐 Afternoon, the terrace view is good in any light
Evening

An excursion to Menthon-Saint-Bernard

A short hop around the eastern shore reaches Menthon-Saint-Bernard, where a turreted lakeside château with a storybook silhouette overlooks the water, a scenic stop for a walk or a lakefront dinner before heading back.

🚗 ~20 min drive or local bus around the east shore 💵 Bus ~€2 with the local network, a taxi runs higher 🕐 Early evening, for a lake-view dinner
Morning

Tandem paragliding from Col de la Forclaz

The launch sites above Talloires and Menthon-Saint-Bernard are among the best-known paragliding spots in the Alps, and a tandem flight with a certified pilot circles out over the lake with the whole basin and the Vieille Ville laid out below.

🚗 ~30 min drive up to the Col de la Forclaz launch site 💵 ~€90-120 for a tandem flight 🕐 Book ahead, morning flights usually have the calmest air
Midday

Panoramic views from Le Semnoz

Known locally as the balcony of Annecy, this road-accessible mountain above town strings together viewpoints over the entire lake basin, and on a clear day the horizon stretches as far as Mont Blanc.

🚗 ~20 min drive from the col, or from Annecy directly 💵 Free, small parking fee in peak season 🕐 Midday, when visibility toward Mont Blanc is usually best
Afternoon

Train back toward Geneva or Lyon

The same direct rail line from Day 1 runs in reverse, with onward connections to Geneva Airport or Lyon departing regularly through the afternoon.

🚆 ~1h45 train back to Geneva, or ~2 hrs to Lyon Part-Dieu 💵 ~€24 to Geneva 🕐 Leave at least 3 hrs before an international flight from Geneva

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • France doesn't require tipping since service is included by law, but rounding up or adding about 5 to 10% at sit-down restaurants is appreciated.
  • Rounding to the nearest euro is standard for cafes and taxis.
  • Boat cruise crews and paragliding pilots don't expect tips, though it's welcome for a great flight.

👗 Dress Code

  • Lake Annecy stays cool even in summer, a rash guard or wetsuit top is worth considering for longer swims.
  • Sturdy shoes matter for the château's stone stairs and the cycling path, sandals aren't ideal on Days 3 or 4.
  • Old-town restaurants are casual to smart-casual, there's no strict dress code for dinner.

🚴 Getting Around Annecy

  • The old town is compact and entirely walkable, most hotels sit within a few minutes of the canals.
  • The lakeside greenway makes cycling the easiest way to cover longer distances around the lake.
  • Local buses connect central Annecy to Talloires, Menthon-Saint-Bernard, and the base of the mountain roads.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Annecy draws heavy summer crowds around the old town and lakefront at midday, an early start helps on Days 1 and 2.
  • Swim only in designated areas, boat traffic on the lake picks up by mid-morning.
  • Book the Col de la Forclaz paragliding flight ahead in summer, good-weather slots sell out days in advance.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in French

  • Bonjour: hello/good day
  • Merci beaucoup: thank you very much
  • L'addition, s'il vous plaît: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • France uses the euro, and cards are accepted almost everywhere, including small cafes.
  • Carry some cash for the market, bike rental deposits, and small lakeside kiosks.
  • Annecy is a popular summer resort town, so lodging and dining prices run higher than in nearby Haute-Savoie towns.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🚲

Rent bikes for a half-day

Most shops offer a cheaper half-day rate, plenty of time to ride the flat southern stretch of the greenway without paying for a full day you won't use.

🪂

Book morning paragliding slots

Early flights are often priced the same as midday ones but come with calmer air and shorter waits at the launch site.

🧺

Picnic from the covered market

Annecy's market stalls near the old town beat paying tourist-menu prices for a simple lakeside lunch.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🏛️

The Musée-Château's regional collection

Quieter than the old town streets below, with Savoyard art and lake history that make a good rainy-hour filler.

🚴

Duingt's quieter west-shore lakefront

A short ride or drive around the lake gives the same mountain backdrop without Annecy's midday crowds.

🏞️

Gorges du Fier as a side trip

A short drive from town reaches a dramatic river canyon with a suspended footbridge, worthwhile if you have extra time.

✈️ Flights

  • Geneva Airport (GVA) is the closest gateway, with a direct train taking roughly 1h45 to Annecy.
  • Lyon-Saint Exupéry (LYS) also works, with a TGV plus regional connection to Annecy.
  • Book the Geneva train with some buffer time, regional trains run on a fixed schedule with modest frequency in shoulder season.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Taxis can be booked by phone or through your hotel for transfers to Talloires or Menthon-Saint-Bernard.
  • Ride-hailing apps operate in central Annecy but have limited coverage once you're around the lake's east shore.
  • A taxi is the easiest way up to the Col de la Forclaz if you're not driving yourself.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • A car isn't necessary for the old town and lakeside days, but it helps for reaching Col de la Forclaz and Semnoz independently.
  • Parking in central Annecy is limited and pricey in summer, most visitors park at the edge of downtown or use the train.
  • A rental also opens up side trips to Talloires, Menthon-Saint-Bernard, and the Gorges du Fier at your own pace.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • In the Vieille Ville: puts the canals, Palais de l'Île, and restaurants within a short walk, though rooms book up early in summer.
  • Along the lakefront near Jardins de l'Europe: quieter, with easy access to the cycling path and beaches.
  • In Talloires or Menthon-Saint-Bernard: a calmer, more scenic base for travelers with a car, a short drive from central Annecy.

🚤 Boat Trips

  • Guided cruises depart regularly from the old port in summer, an easy way to see Château de Menthon and the eastern shore from the water.
  • Small electric boats and pedal boats can be rented at the lakefront for an hour or two without a license.
  • Paddleboard and kayak rentals are widely available along the Plage de l'Impérial and other lakefront beaches.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • France is in the Schengen area and uses the euro, standard Schengen visa rules apply for most visitors.
  • Travel insurance covering paragliding and cycling is worth adding given the Day 3 and Day 4 activities.
  • Annecy is walkable for most visitors, though the château and old-town lanes include some stairs and cobblestones.
