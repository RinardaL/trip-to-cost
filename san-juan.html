4 Days in San Juan: Old San Juan, the Forts & El Yunque

Blue cobblestones and Spanish forts in Old San Juan, a rainforest day at El Yunque and Luquillo beach, then Condado and the Santurce murals: four warm winter days.

Ideal SeasonDec – Apr
Est. Budget$630-980
Duration4 Days
PaceModerate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Luis Muñoz Marín (SJU) & the transfer

Luis Muñoz Marín International (SJU) is in Carolina, 25 to 40 minutes from Old San Juan depending on traffic. Official taxis charge zone flat fares: roughly $21 to Old San Juan and $17 to Condado, plus small bag and airport extras. Uber and Lyft often cost less. Confirm the fare at the taxi kiosk (check current).

🚕 ~25-40 min by taxi or Uber to Old San Juan 💵 ~$21-25 taxi to Old San Juan, ~$17-20 to Condado (check current) 🕐 Plan around your landing time, then drop your bags
Afternoon

Plaza de Armas, the cathedral & the blue cobblestones

Old San Juan is a compact grid of narrow streets paved with blue-grey cobblestones. Start at the Plaza de Armas, then walk up Calle del Cristo to the cathedral and the small Capilla del Cristo. Wear shoes with grip, because the stones get slick after rain. Streets and squares are free, and lunch costs about $12 to $20.

🚶 ~10 min on foot between the plaza and the cathedral 💵 Streets and plazas free, ~$12-20 for lunch 🕐 Mid-afternoon, once your bags are dropped
Evening

Paseo de la Princesa & sunset on the sea wall

The Paseo de la Princesa is a wide, tree-lined promenade along the old city wall, with street vendors and a fountain at the far end. It takes about 20 minutes and is free. Come at sunset, then eat a few streets inland, where mains cost about $20 to $30. Book ahead on Fridays and Saturdays.

🚶 ~20 min on foot along the wall 💵 Free walk, ~$20-30 for dinner 🕐 Around sunset, which is near 6 PM in winter
Morning

Castillo San Felipe del Morro

El Morro guards the harbour mouth at the north-west tip of Old San Juan. The National Park Service ticket costs $10 for ages 16 and over, children are free, and it covers both forts for 24 hours. The park takes cards only. Hours are about 9 AM to 6 PM in winter (check current), so arrive at opening.

🚶 ~15 min on foot from the plaza, or a free trolley (check current) 💵 $10 per adult, kids free, both forts, cards only (check current) 🕐 Arrive at opening, around 9 AM, to beat the heat and the cruise crowds
Afternoon

Castillo San Cristóbal & Calle Fortaleza

San Cristóbal faces the Atlantic and is the larger fort, with tunnels and long views. Your morning ticket covers it, and it is about a 15-minute walk from El Morro. Then stroll Calle Fortaleza for shops, cafés and the governor's residence at the far end. Allow about 2 hours for the fort.

🚶 ~15 min on foot between the forts 💵 Included in the fort ticket, ~$10-15 for lunch 🕐 After lunch, once the midday sun passes
Evening

Casa Bacardí by ferry, then mofongo

The Cataño ferry leaves from Pier 2 and takes about 12 minutes, at about 50 cents each way. Casa Bacardí tours cost around $45 for ages 18 and over (check current), and the bar and views are open to everyone. Prefer to skip it? Try a rum flight in Old San Juan. Dinner is mofongo, about $18 to $28.

⛴️ ~12 min by ferry from Pier 2, then a short shuttle or Uber 💵 ~$45 tour (check current), ~$18-28 for mofongo 🕐 Late afternoon start, and check the last ferry back
Morning

El Portal, PR-191 & La Coca Falls

El Yunque is about an hour east of San Juan by rental car or tour. The Forest Service says the PR-191 corridor needs no reservation, but check its alerts page, as La Mina trail has been closed for repairs. El Portal visitor centre charges $8 for ages 16 and over. La Coca Falls drops beside the road.

🚗 ~1 hour by car, or a guided tour from San Juan 💵 $8 El Portal, car $40-100 a day (check current) 🕐 Leave San Juan by 7:30 AM, before the clouds and the tour buses
Afternoon

Luquillo Beach (Balneario La Monserrate)

Luquillo Beach is about 20 minutes from El Portal. A reef offshore keeps the water calm and shallow, and winter sea temperatures sit near 26 degrees Celsius (79 degrees Fahrenheit). The public beach has lifeguards and a small parking charge (check current). Take a rain jacket, as showers can pass over quickly.

🚗 ~20 min by car from El Portal 💵 Free beach, small parking fee (check current) 🕐 Early afternoon, with sunscreen and a hat
Evening

The Luquillo kiosks & the drive back

Los Kioskos de Luquillo are a strip of food stalls and bars on PR-3, beside the beach. Try alcapurrias and bacalaítos at about $2 to $4 each, fried seafood and a piña colada (check current prices). Bars stay open late on Fridays and Saturdays. The drive back takes about 50 minutes, so skip the rum if you are driving.

🚗 ~50 min back to San Juan by car 💵 ~$15-25 for a snack dinner (check current) 🕐 Dinner from 6 PM, then leave before the late traffic
Morning

Ocean Park & Condado beaches

Ocean Park is a local beach a short walk from Condado, and both are free and public. It faces Atlantic waves, so swim near lifeguards where possible, watch the flags and avoid rip currents. A beach chair costs about $10 to $20 (check current). From Old San Juan, an Uber costs roughly $10 to $15.

🚕 ~15 min by Uber from Old San Juan 💵 Free beach, ~$10-20 for a chair (check current) 🕐 Morning, with lower UV and calmer water
Afternoon

Santurce street art on Calle Cerra

Santurce is the arts district south of Condado, and Calle Cerra has some of the densest mural walls in San Juan, with more on Calle Loíza. Walking is free, and the work changes often. Take an Uber between streets and stay in busy daytime areas. Lunch at La Placita costs about $10 to $18.

🚕 ~10 min by Uber from Condado 💵 Murals free, ~$10-18 for lunch 🕐 Afternoon, in daylight
Evening

Departure from SJU (and the bio bay option)

Leave for SJU at least 3 hours before an international flight and 2 hours before one to the US mainland. A taxi or Uber costs about $17 to $25. The bioluminescent bay at Laguna Grande, Fajardo, is a night tour, usually $65 to $125, with no guaranteed glow, so add it only if you fly the next day.

🚕 ~15-30 min by taxi or Uber to SJU 💵 ~$17-25 transfer, bio bay $65-125 optional (check current) 🕐 Leave 2 to 3 hours before your flight

💵 Tipping & Payments

  • Tipping follows US norms. Leave 15 to 20 percent at restaurants unless a service charge is already on the bill, $1 to $2 per drink at a bar, and $1 to $2 per bag for hotel porters.
  • For taxi drivers, round up or add about 10 to 15 percent. Tour guides and drivers on day trips usually receive $5 to $10 per person.
  • Cards and contactless work almost everywhere, but small kiosks, beach vendors and some taxis prefer cash. Keep $20 to $40 in small bills.

🍽️ Puerto Rican Food & Drink

  • Mofongo is mashed fried plantain with garlic, often served with pork, chicken or shrimp. Alcapurrias and bacalaítos are fried snacks, and tostones are twice-fried plantain slices.
  • The piña colada is claimed by San Juan, and rum is the local drink, with Medalla as the everyday beer. Coquito, a coconut and rum drink, is a Christmas season speciality.
  • Order a mallorca, a sweet, powdered bread, for breakfast, and a cafecito (small black coffee) afterwards. Lunch is the main meal, and many cafés run a plato del día at a lower price.

🎄 Winter Weather & the Holiday Season

  • December to April is the dry season, with highs near 28 to 29 degrees Celsius (82 to 85 degrees Fahrenheit) and sea temperatures of about 26 to 27 degrees Celsius. Showers still happen, especially around El Yunque.
  • Las Navidades run from late November to the San Sebastián Street Festival in Old San Juan, which is set for 21 to 24 January 2027 and draws about a million people. Three Kings Day on 6 January is a public holiday.
  • Hotels and flights cost more at Christmas, New Year and the festival, so book early or travel in late January to March. Hurricane season runs from June to November, so winter is the safer window.

⚠️ Safety, Heat & Hurricane Warnings

  • The sun is strong all year round. Use SPF 30 or higher, drink water often, and rest in the shade at midday, especially on the forts, which have little cover.
  • Rip currents are common on Atlantic beaches, including Ocean Park. Swim at beaches with lifeguards, follow the warning flags and do not swim alone.
  • Petty theft happens in tourist areas. Never leave bags or phones in a parked rental car, and avoid walking alone at night in quiet streets or in La Perla, below the Old San Juan wall.
  • Hurricane season is June to November. Outside winter, check the National Hurricane Center and buy travel insurance that covers storms. Non-US visitors should also read their own government travel advice.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Spanish

  • "Buenos días" (good morning), "Buenas tardes" (good afternoon), "Buenas noches" (good evening or night)
  • "Por favor" (please), "Gracias" (thank you), "De nada" (you're welcome), "Con permiso" (excuse me)
  • "Una mesa para dos, por favor" (a table for two, please), "La cuenta, por favor" (the bill, please)
  • "¿Habla inglés?" (do you speak English?), "¿Dónde está...?" (where is...?), "¿Cuánto cuesta?" (how much is it?)

💳 Money & Payments

  • Puerto Rico uses the US dollar. Cards work in most hotels, restaurants and shops, and the San Juan National Historic Site takes cards only.
  • Use bank ATMs inside branches or malls to avoid fees, and decline dynamic currency conversion when a terminal offers your home currency. A sales tax of 11.5 percent is added to most purchases.
  • Hotels add room taxes and often resort fees on top of the listed rate, so check the final price. Rental cars carry a toll transponder fee, so ask at the desk.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎟️

One fort ticket covers both

The $10 National Park Service ticket works for both El Morro and San Cristóbal for 24 hours, and children 15 and under are free. An annual America the Beautiful pass ($80) also gets you in, so check whether you already hold one (check current).

⛴️

Use the 50-cent ferry

The Cataño ferry costs about 50 cents each way and gives free views of the old city skyline from the water. It is the cheapest way to reach Casa Bacardí, and a rideshare costs far more (check current).

🍟

Eat fritters, not tourist menus

Alcapurrias, bacalaítos and tostones at kiosks cost a few dollars each, against $25 to $30 for a main course on the main squares of Old San Juan. Eat the big meal at lunch, when plato del día specials are cheaper.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌅

Walk the sea wall at sunrise

Before 8 AM, the Paseo de la Princesa and the lawn in front of El Morro are quiet, and the light on the stone is warm. It is the best time for photos before cruise passengers arrive.

🪦

Visit the seaside cemetery

The Santa María Magdalena de Pazzis cemetery sits on the shore beside El Morro and is free to enter. It is a calm place with sea views and a short walk from the fort (check current hours).

✨

The bioluminescent bay, honestly

Laguna Grande in Fajardo is about an hour from San Juan. Night kayak tours cost $65 to $125, and the glow depends on the moon and water, so it is not guaranteed. It is optional and needs a spare evening.

✈️ Flights

  • Luis Muñoz Marín International (SJU) is the main airport, in Carolina, about 15 minutes from Isla Verde and 25 to 40 minutes from Old San Juan. It has direct flights from many US cities and some from Europe and Latin America.
  • Flying from the US mainland counts as a domestic flight for US citizens, with no passport needed beyond a REAL ID or a passport.
  • Fares peak around Christmas, New Year and the San Sebastián festival, so book early or fly in late January to March.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Official taxis from SJU use flat zone fares: roughly $12 to Isla Verde, $17 to Condado and Ocean Park and $21 to Old San Juan, with small extras for bags and the airport fee. Confirm the fare at the kiosk (check current).
  • Uber and Lyft work in San Juan, and are often $5 to $10 cheaper than the taxi flat rate.
  • A rideshare between Old San Juan, Condado and Santurce usually costs $10 to $15 for a short trip.

🚌 Public Transport & Tickets

  • AMA buses cover San Juan, but routes and times are hard to follow. Tren Urbano does not reach Old San Juan, so most visitors walk, take a rideshare or use the free trolleys when they run (check current).
  • The Cataño ferry runs from Pier 2 in Old San Juan to Cataño in about 12 minutes, at about 50 cents each way. Check the timetable and the last return.
  • For El Yunque, rent a car for one day or book a guided tour. Book cars ahead in winter, parking is limited in Old San Juan, and tolls use the AutoExpreso transponder.
  • You do not need a car for Old San Juan, Condado or Santurce.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Old San Juan: the most atmospheric and walkable base for the forts and restaurants, but pricier and noisy on weekends.
  • Condado: beachfront hotels, restaurants and shops, 15 minutes from Old San Juan by rideshare.
  • Ocean Park: quieter, with guesthouses and a local beach, and a good value for a relaxed stay.
  • Santurce: murals, cafés and nightlife, with lower prices, but use rideshares at night.
  • Isla Verde: resort strip close to the airport, handy for an early flight.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • US carriers roam as at home. Other visitors can use an eSIM or a prepaid US SIM, and most hotels and cafés offer Wi-Fi.
  • Cards work in most places, but keep cash for kiosks, beach vendors and tips.
  • Use bank ATMs and avoid standalone machines with high fees.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • US citizens do not need a passport to enter, but need REAL ID or a passport for flights. Other visitors need the same documents as for the US mainland (passport and a visa or ESTA), so check before booking.
  • Check El Yunque's official alerts page before you leave, since trails and fees may change.
  • Hurricane season runs from June to November. Winter is the dry season, but showers still occur.
  • Many sights have winter hours, and some close on Christmas Day and New Year's Day, so check before you plan.
