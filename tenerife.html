5 Days in Tenerife: Resort Beaches, Water Parks & Mount Teide by Starlight

One island, in depth: Costa Adeje's resort beaches and a whale-watching boat off the southwest coast, the slides of Siam Park and the parrots of Loro Parque, Puerto de la Cruz's volcanic-rock lido, La Orotava's balconied old town, and a night spent stargazing under Mount Teide's Starlight-certified sky.

Ideal SeasonMar – May, Oct – Nov
Est. Budget$700-950
Duration5 Days
PaceModerate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land at Tenerife South & transfer to Costa Adeje

Almost every international flight lands at Tenerife South (Reina Sofía), a short hop down the TF-1 motorway from the Costa Adeje resort strip that anchors this itinerary.

🚗 ~20 min transfer to Costa Adeje 💵 ~€25 taxi, or ~€3 by bus 🕐 Plan around your flight's landing time
Afternoon

Playa del Duque

Costa Adeje's flagship beach is Blue Flag-rated and unusually calm for the Atlantic, its golden imported sand backed by a palm-lined promenade of cafes and upscale hotels.

🚶 Walkable from most Costa Adeje hotels 💵 Free, sun lounger rental ~€8 🕐 A relaxed first afternoon
Evening

Dinner at the Los Cristianos harbourfront

A short drive along the coast to this working fishing harbour and ferry port, where a palm-shaded promenade of seafood restaurants makes an easy first Canarian dinner.

🚗 ~10 min from Costa Adeje 💵 ~€22 dinner 🕐 Evening, ~8 PM
Morning

Arrive early at Siam Park

Through the White Elephant gate lies a Thai-themed water world repeatedly ranked the world's best, from the near-vertical Tower of Power to the shark-tunnel lazy river.

🚶 Walkable from most Costa Adeje hotels 💵 ~€45 day ticket 🕐 Right at opening, 10 AM
Afternoon

Wave Palace & Siam Beach

Wind down at the park's artificial wave pool or its sheltered white-sand beach, both looking out over the same Adeje coastline the rest of the itinerary follows.

🏖️ Inside the park grounds 💵 Included in day ticket 🕐 Early afternoon, before the crowds thin the shade
Evening

Dinner & the strip at Playa de las Américas

Next door to Costa Adeje, this is the liveliest stretch of Tenerife's south coast after dark, with a boulevard of restaurants, bars and street performers.

🚗 ~10 min drive or a coastal walk 💵 ~€25 dinner 🕐 Evening, ~8:30 PM
Morning

Loro Parque's parrots, penguins & orcas

Opened in 1972 around one of the world's largest parrot collections, the park has grown into a wider zoo with a walk-through penguinarium and marine shows.

🚗 ~45 min drive north from Costa Adeje 💵 ~€40 entry 🕐 Morning, arrive for the first shows
Afternoon

Lago Martiánez

Since Puerto de la Cruz's own beaches are mostly black volcanic sand and rough surf, César Manrique built this artificial lagoon and lido, ringed with palms and volcanic rock.

🚶 Central Puerto de la Cruz 💵 ~€8 entry 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

La Orotava's balconied old town

A short drive uphill to this well-preserved colonial town, where 17th-century mansions like Casa de los Balcones show off the carved wooden balconies the north is known for.

🚗 ~15 min from Puerto de la Cruz 💵 Free to wander, ~€4 house entry 🕐 Early evening, before dinner
Morning

Drive up through La Orotava valley to Llano de Ucanca

The northern road climbs through pine forest and laurel woodland into the UNESCO-listed caldera, opening onto the vast pale plain of Llano de Ucanca from the La Ruleta viewpoint.

🚗 ~1h drive from Puerto de la Cruz 💵 ~€35 car rental for the day 🕐 Depart mid-morning, before the coach tours
Afternoon

Cable car toward Teide's summit

At 3,715 metres, Teide is Spain's highest point, and the cable car covers most of the climb in eight minutes, weather and wind permitting.

🚡 8 min cable car ride 💵 ~€40 return ticket 🕐 Book the early-afternoon slot in advance
Evening

Stargazing near the Teide Observatory

The park's altitude, dry air and distance from city lights earned it Starlight Reserve status; a guided astrophotography session near the observatory rivals any planetarium.

🚗 Stay within the park for dark skies 💵 ~€38 guided stargazing tour 🕐 After sunset, ~9 PM
Morning

Whale & dolphin watching boat trip

The channel between Tenerife and La Gomera holds a resident pod of short-finned pilot whales visible almost year-round, spotted on eco-focused no-chase boat trips from Los Cristianos.

⛴️ ~2.5h boat trip from Los Cristianos 💵 ~€45 per person 🕐 Morning departure, book ahead
Afternoon

Tandem paragliding above Costa Adeje

A minibus runs up to a launch point above the resort strip for a 20-minute tandem flight back down toward the coast, no experience or licence required.

🪂 ~20 min tandem flight 💵 ~€90 per person 🕐 Early afternoon, weather dependent
Evening

Return the car & fly home from Tenerife South

Build in buffer time for the drive back down the TF-1 and rental drop-off, Tenerife South sits close to Costa Adeje but can back up in late-afternoon traffic.

🚗 ~20 min to Tenerife South Airport 💵 Included in car rental 🕐 Evening, allow 2.5h before departure

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service is usually included, so tipping isn't the custom the way it is in the US.
  • Rounding up the bill or leaving a euro or two at restaurants is a nice gesture, not an obligation.
  • Boat crews, paragliding pilots and park guides appreciate a small tip if the experience ran well.

👒 Dress Code

  • Casual, beach-ready clothing works almost everywhere in the resort south, this is one of Europe's most relaxed dress cultures.
  • Bring a proper warm layer for Teide's summit and evening stargazing, temperatures there drop well below what the coast feels like.
  • Swimwear is fine on the beach promenade in Costa Adeje, but cover up before heading into restaurants or La Orotava's churches.

🚗 Getting Around

  • A rental car makes this itinerary far easier, the TF-1 motorway runs the south coast and the TF-5 covers the north, with Teide's mountain road connecting the two.
  • Public buses (guaguas) link the main resort towns and Puerto de la Cruz cheaply, though schedules thin out on Sundays.
  • A modern tram connects Santa Cruz and La Laguna in the northeast, useful if you add an extra day in the capital area.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Mount Teide's altitude can bring on mild altitude symptoms, headaches or shortness of breath, especially if you go straight up by cable car.
  • Resort beaches in the south are generally calm, but check flags before swimming at north-coast beaches, where Atlantic swell and rip currents are stronger.
  • Sun strength is stronger than it feels here year-round thanks to the altitude and Atlantic breeze, sunscreen matters even on cloudy days.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Spanish

  • Buenos dias: good morning/hello
  • Muchas gracias: thank you very much
  • La cuenta, por favor: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is the currency, and the Canary Islands have a reduced local tax (IGIC) instead of mainland Spain's VAT, so some prices run slightly lower.
  • Contactless cards are accepted almost everywhere, including water parks, boat trips and park entry kiosks.
  • Keep some cash for sun-lounger rentals, small guachinche restaurants, and market stalls in La Orotava.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎢

Book Siam Park and Loro Parque as a combo ticket online

The two-park combined ticket costs meaningfully less than paying for each entry separately at the gate.

🚡

Book the Teide cable car online, weeks ahead

Tickets are capped daily and sell out fast in high season, walk-up prices and availability are both worse.

🍽️

Eat at a guachinche away from the resort strip

These small, often unmarked family eateries inland from Costa Adeje serve the same Canarian classics for a fraction of resort prices.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🏖️

Playa de la Enramada over the main Costa Adeje beaches

A quieter cove just south of Playa del Duque with the same calm water and a fraction of the loungers.

🐦

Loro Parque's early morning slot

Arriving right at opening gets you the penguinarium and parrot show building largely to yourself before the tour buses land.

🐋

Choose a no-chase, eco-certified whale-watching operator

Several boats from Los Cristianos follow strict distance rules around the resident pilot whale pod, better for the animals and usually a calmer ride too.

✈️ Flights

  • Tenerife South (TFS) handles nearly all international arrivals and sits closest to Costa Adeje, where this itinerary is based.
  • Tenerife North (TFN) is smaller and mostly serves mainland Spain and inter-island routes, less convenient for a south-coast trip.
  • Book flights and the Teide cable car in the same window, both fill up fastest around the same high-season dates.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Metered taxis are reliable throughout the south, and ride-hailing apps operate in Costa Adeje and Playa de las Américas.
  • Confirm a fixed fare for longer transfers to Teide or Loro Parque if you skip the rental car.
  • The airport taxi rank posts a fixed-fare table to the main resort areas.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Highly recommended, this itinerary crosses the whole island, from Costa Adeje's beaches to the north-coast old towns and Teide's summit road.
  • The TF-1 (south coast) and TF-5 (north coast) motorways are fast and well maintained; the mountain road linking them through Teide National Park is narrower and winding.
  • Book Teide-area parking and cable car tickets together, both sell out at the same times in peak season.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Costa Adeje or Playa de las Américas put you closest to the beaches, Siam Park, and the boat and paragliding departures covered on Days 1, 2 and 5.
  • Puerto de la Cruz is a good alternative base if you'd rather sleep near Loro Parque and La Orotava and day-trip south for the water park and boat trip.
  • Either base works for the Teide day, the mountain road is a similar drive time from both coasts.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Whale and dolphin-watching boats depart regularly from Los Cristianos and Puerto Colón on the southwest coast, book a morning slot for calmer water.
  • Los Cristianos is also the main ferry terminal for connections to La Gomera, La Palma and El Hierro, worth knowing if you want to extend the trip to a second island.
  • A direct ferry to the other Canary Islands from the north coast isn't practical, most routes run from the south.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • The Canary Islands are part of Spain, so standard Schengen entry rules apply for most visitors.
  • Travel insurance is worth it given the pre-booked Teide cable car, Siam Park, and boat trip slots, most are non-refundable.
  • Pharmacies (farmacias) are easy to find in every resort town for altitude, sun, or motion-sickness complaints.
