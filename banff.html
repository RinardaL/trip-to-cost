💵 Tipping Etiquette

  • Standard North American tipping: 15–20% at sit-down restaurants.
  • Spa services typically expect 15–20% as well.
  • No tipping required at cafes or counter service.

🧥 Dress Code

  • Casual outdoor layers everywhere: even summer mornings can be cold at altitude.
  • Smart-casual is expected for Fairmont dining rooms in the evening.
  • Swimwear for hotel pools and hot springs.

🐻 Wildlife & Trail Safety

  • Carry bear spray on any hike and know how to use it.
  • Never approach elk, especially during fall rutting season: they're more dangerous than they look.
  • Store all food in a vehicle or bear-proof locker, never in a tent.

⚠️ Safety Warnings

  • Moraine Lake Road often closes to private vehicles in peak season, so check shuttle schedules ahead.
  • Mountain weather changes fast, so pack layers even on a sunny forecast.
  • Altitude (Banff sits around 1,400m) can cause mild symptoms for the first day or two.

🗣️ Good to Know

  • Canada is bilingual (English/French), though Alberta is overwhelmingly English-speaking.
  • Parks Canada Discovery Pass: the annual pass covering park entry fees.
  • Roam Transit: the local bus network connecting Banff and Lake Louise.

💳 Money & Payments

  • Canadian dollars (CAD): cards accepted virtually everywhere.
  • Contactless/tap payment is the norm, even for small purchases.
  • Park entry fees can be paid online in advance to skip the gate line.

✈️ Flights

  • Calgary International Airport (YYC) is the gateway, about 1.5 hours from Banff by road.
  • A private shuttle or rental car covers the transfer directly through the foothills into the Rockies.
  • Confirm your shuttle pickup time ahead, connections can be tight after a long-haul arrival.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • This is a rental-car and shuttle itinerary, so taxis matter only within Banff townsite itself.
  • Ride-hailing apps have limited coverage in the mountains; a taxi is more reliable for a short in-town hop.
  • Roam Transit's local bus covers Banff Avenue and Lake Louise if you'd rather skip taxis entirely.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Essential for reaching Lake Louise, the Icefields Parkway, and Emerald Lake, none practical without one.
  • Pick up at the Calgary airport counters and book ahead, mountain-route vehicles get scarce in summer.
  • Roads are well-paved but watch for sudden weather changes and wildlife on the road at dusk.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Banff townsite: central for Banff Avenue, the Upper Hot Springs, and the Banff Gondola.
  • Lake Louise: worth a night if you want to canoe or catch the lakeshore at sunrise without the extra drive.
  • Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise or Banff Springs, if the trip's luxury budget allows, both already feature in the day plans.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • No ferry legs here, Banff is landlocked in the Rockies.
  • Canoeing stands in as the "boat trip": rent one right at the docks on Lake Louise or Emerald Lake.
  • Lake Minnewanka also runs a scenic boat cruise, a quieter alternative to the lake canoe outings.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Grab the Parks Canada Discovery Pass if entering more than one park, it covers entry fees for the whole week.
  • Carry bear spray on any hike, and know how to use it before you're on trail.
  • Altitude (Banff sits around 1,400m) can cause mild symptoms for a day or two, so pace the first day easy.
