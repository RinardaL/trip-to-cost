What links these seven trips, and how do I choose?

These itineraries run from Tallinn and Riga in the Baltics, through Poland, the Czech Republic and Hungary, to Slovenia. Six are four-day trips and Budapest takes five, so they are easy to compare, and the choice comes down to season, mood and daily spend. Six are historic old town trips, with Zakopane as the mountain exception. Two itineraries add a day away from the base city: Lake Bled from Ljubljana and Auschwitz-Birkenau from Krakow.

First-timers who want a classic old town have two obvious options in Prague and Budapest, both built around the Christmas markets, and the Budapest itinerary adds the Széchenyi thermal baths in winter. Krakow suits history-minded travellers: day three goes entirely to the Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial and Museum, and the last morning to the Wieliczka Salt Mine. Zakopane swaps museums for a cable car to Kasprowy Wierch, the Morskie Oko lake hike and a valley walk.

Budget travellers should compare the daily figures. Riga works out at $110 per day, with a total of $350-540 per person, the lowest on the hub. Tallinn and Budapest sit at $140, Prague and Zakopane at $150 and Ljubljana at $155, and that itinerary includes the Lake Bled day trip.

When is the best time to visit each one?

The seasons split the list clearly. Prague is a November to December trip, timed for the Christmas markets. Budapest shares that window but also lists April to June and September to October, which makes it the most flexible of the seven. Krakow is listed for April to June and September.

Summer is the other half. Tallinn, Riga and Ljubljana are all listed for May to September, and Zakopane runs from June to September, which suits its hiking and cable car days. If you can only travel in December, the choice narrows to Prague and Budapest. For July or August, four trips are listed: Tallinn, Riga, Ljubljana and Zakopane. Outside the listed months, check the itinerary before you commit, since several days are built around seasonal markets or mountain trails.

How do I combine trips and keep costs down?

Pair cities that share a season. Prague and Budapest both peak for Christmas markets, and Riga and Tallinn are both Baltic capitals with the same May to September window. The Zakopane page has you arrive from Krakow by bus or train, so the two pair naturally on a map, although their listed seasons differ. Each page is a self-contained itinerary, so add the totals together and plan the journey between cities yourself.

To spend less, look at an open-jaw flight, arriving in one city and leaving from another, so you never backtrack. Pick dates inside the months an itinerary lists. Markets also help: the Great Market Hall in Budapest, the Central Market in Riga and the market in Ljubljana are all part of their itineraries.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a trip to Central and Eastern Europe cost?

Our 7 Central & Eastern Europe itineraries cost from $350 to $800 per person for the whole trip, which is about $110 to $155 a day. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities, and exclude international flights.

Which Central & Eastern Europe itinerary is the cheapest?

Riga ($350-540), Tallinn ($440-670), Krakow ($450-700) are the three lowest, per person and excluding flights.

How many days do I need in Central and Eastern Europe?

Our Central & Eastern Europe itineraries run from 4 to 5 days, and the most common length is 4 days. Each page has a day picker with a price for every length from one day up to the full plan.

When is the best time to visit Central and Eastern Europe?

It depends on the trip, and each itinerary lists its ideal season. For example: Budapest, Nov – Dec (Christmas Markets), Apr – Jun, Sep – Oct; Prague, Nov – Dec (Christmas Markets); Krakow, Apr – Jun, Sep. The table above shows the ideal season for every Central & Eastern Europe trip.

Can I visit Prague and Budapest on one trip?

Yes. Both itineraries share a November to December window, so they work back to back. Treat each page as its own block, add the two totals together, and budget the journey between the cities separately, because the pages do not cover that leg. Check current timetables before you book.

Which trip is best if I want hiking rather than museums?

Zakopane. Its four days are a cable car to Kasprowy Wierch, the hike to the lake at Morskie Oko and a flat walk through Dolina Chochołowska, with Krupówki Street on arrival day. It is listed for June to September, so plan around those months rather than winter.
