4 Days in Luxembourg City: Christmas Markets & the Old Quarter

Winterlights chalets on Place d'Armes, the cliff-top ramparts of the old quarter, Mudam on Kirchberg and a day trip to Vianden Castle, all on free trams, buses and trains.

Ideal SeasonLate Nov – Dec (Christmas Markets), May – Sep
Est. Budget$400-640
Duration4 Days
PaceRelaxed
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Findel & the tram into town

Luxembourg Airport (LUX) is about 6 km from the centre. Tram line 1 starts at the terminal and runs through Kirchberg to Hamilius and the Gare in roughly 30 to 35 minutes (check the journey planner). It is free, as are all buses, trams and second-class trains, but carry ID in case an inspector asks. Bus 16 is an alternative.

🚋 ~30-35 min by tram line 1 to Hamilius 💵 Free (second-class travel) 🕐 Plan around your landing time, then drop your bags
Afternoon

Place d'Armes & the Lëtzebuerger Chrëschtmaart

Place d'Armes is the main square of the upper town, 4 minutes on foot from the Hamilius tram stop, and the traditional market fills it. Winterlights runs from 20 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, but each market has its own end date, so check. Hours were 11:00 to 21:00 in 2025, later on weekends.

🚶 ~4 min on foot from Hamilius 💵 Free entry, ~€4-6 for a hot drink (check current) 🕐 Weekday afternoons are calmer than weekend evenings
Evening

The Wantermaart, the Ferris wheel & the Pétrusse view

Walk about 8 minutes to Place de la Constitution, where the Wantermaart has chalets, a giant pyramid and a Ferris wheel above the Pétrusse valley. The Gëlle Fra war memorial stands beside it. Wandering is free and ride prices vary (check). Evenings are often 0 to 4°C, so keep gloves handy, and eat dinner from a stall or nearby.

🚶 ~8 min on foot from Place d'Armes 💵 Free to wander, ~€15-25 for a market dinner (check current) 🕐 After dark for the lights, markets close around 21:00-22:00
Morning

Chemin de la Corniche & the Bock Casemates

The Chemin de la Corniche is a free rampart walk above the Alzette valley, part of the UNESCO-listed old quarter, leading to the Bock promontory. The Bock Casemates tunnels cost about €11 (check current) on seasonal hours, so they may be closed during the markets. Wear grippy shoes: wet stone is slippery. Allow about 1.5 hours.

🚶 ~10 min on foot from Place d'Armes 💵 Corniche free, Casemates ~€11 when open (check current) 🕐 Daylight hours only, sunset is around 16:30 in December
Afternoon

The Grund & the Pfaffenthal lift

Walk down into the Grund, the old lower town beside the Alzette, with cafés and Neimënster abbey. Follow the river about 20 minutes to Pfaffenthal, then take the free panoramic lift up to the upper town (check it is running, as lifts are sometimes serviced). Handrails help: steps can ice over in winter. Allow 2 hours.

🚶 ~20 min along the river, then the lift 💵 Free, ~€4-5 for a coffee stop 🕐 Finish before dusk, when the paths get dark
Evening

Notre-Dame Cathedral & dinner in the old town

Notre-Dame Cathedral, a 17th-century Jesuit church with a Baroque portal, is free and about 5 minutes from Place d'Armes. Opening hours vary and it closes for services, so check before going. Then eat a few streets away from the main squares, where menus cost less. Try Judd mat Gaardebounen, smoked pork collar with broad beans, at about €20-25.

🚶 ~5 min on foot from Place d'Armes 💵 Cathedral free, ~€25-35 for dinner 🕐 Daytime for the cathedral, dinner from about 19:00
Morning

Mudam & the Philharmonie on Kirchberg

Tram line 1 reaches Philharmonie-Mudam in about 10 minutes from Hamilius. Mudam, the modern art museum, costs €10 and is free on Wednesdays from 18:00 to 21:00 and for under-21s. It opens 10:00 to 18:00 (until 21:00 on Wednesdays), closed Mondays and 25 December. Walk past the Philharmonie and Fort Thüngen next door.

🚋 ~10 min by tram from Hamilius 💵 Mudam €10, free Wed 18:00-21:00 🕐 10:00 opening, closed Mondays, check 24 December hours
Afternoon

The City Museum & the Grand Ducal Palace

Back in the old town, the Lëtzebuerg City Museum costs about €5 and is a dry way to learn the story of the fortress, usually closed on Mondays (check). The Grand Ducal Palace is a few minutes away: admire the facade and the guard outside, as inside visits run mainly on guided summer tours (check current).

🚋 ~10 min tram back, then ~8 min on foot 💵 City Museum ~€5 (check current), palace exterior free 🕐 Museums close around 18:00, shorter on 24 and 31 December
Evening

Place Guillaume II & the Niklosmaart

Place Guillaume II, known as the Knuedler, is the square outside the Hôtel de Ville, 3 minutes from Place d'Armes. It has hosted the children's Winterkids market in recent seasons (check the 2026 programme). For the Niklosmaart, a Saint Nicholas market on Place de Paris, take the tram two stops from Hamilius towards the Gare. Dinner afterwards.

🚶 ~3 min from Place d'Armes, then ~2 stops by tram 💵 Free entry, ~€25-35 for dinner 🕐 Evenings, markets close around 21:00-22:00
Morning

To Vianden by train & bus 570

Take a train from Luxembourg Gare to Ettelbruck, about 30 minutes with departures every 30 minutes, then bus 570 to Vianden, about 25 minutes. Both are free, so expect about 1 hour 15 to 1 hour 30 each way (check mobiliteit.lu). Stroll Vianden's main street by the Our river before the castle opens. Leave bags at your hotel.

🚆 ~30 min train + ~25 min bus 570 💵 Free (second class) 🕐 Leave by about 08:30 to reach the castle at opening
Afternoon

Vianden Castle

Vianden Castle costs €13 (students €7, children 6 to 12 €4) and is covered by the LuxembourgPass. Listed hours were about 10:00 to 17:00 Tuesday to Saturday and 10:00 to 16:00 on Sunday, closed Monday (check current, including holidays). Allow 1.5 hours. The rooms are unheated, so wear warm layers.

🚶 ~10-15 min uphill on foot from the bus stop 💵 ~€13 adult, ~€7 student (check current) 🕐 Closed Mondays, last entry 30 min before closing
Evening

Return & departure from Findel

Leave Vianden by about 16:00 in winter to catch bus 570 and the train to Luxembourg Gare, then take tram line 1 to the airport, about 35 minutes and free (check). Aim to arrive 2 hours before your flight. Trams run until about 00:30, but a morning flight is safer from a hotel near the line.

🚋 ~35 min by tram from the Gare to the airport 💵 Free (second-class travel) 🕐 Arrive at the airport about 2 hours before departure

💶 Tipping & Service

  • Service is normally included in restaurant prices, so tipping is a bonus, not a rule. Rounding up the bill or leaving €1 to €2 for good service is plenty.
  • In a taxi, rounding up to the next euro is usual. At market stalls and for a coffee at the bar, nobody expects a tip.
  • Check the bill for a separate service line before adding more, and ask to pay at the table: waiters bring a card terminal to you.

🍷 Market Food & Drink

  • Try Gromperekichelcher (crisp potato fritters, often with apple sauce), Bouneschlupp (green bean and bacon soup) and Quetschentaart (plum tart). Judd mat Gaardebounen is the national main course: smoked pork collar with broad beans.
  • Glühwein is served in a mug that carries a deposit, usually a few euros (about €5 at one guide, check at the stall). Return the mug at a stall to get the money back, or keep it as a souvenir.
  • Crémant de Luxembourg is the local sparkling wine from the Moselle, a cheaper alternative to champagne. Look for it by the glass, and check whether each stall takes cards.

🗣️ Three Languages & Winter Hours

  • Luxembourgish, French and German are all official, and English is widely spoken in hotels, restaurants and markets. Menus are usually in French, and staff switch language easily.
  • Most shops close on Sundays outside a few Advent openings, and many close on 25 December and 1 January. Check the days before, as markets, museums and the tram may use reduced hours.
  • Daylight is short in December, with sunset around 16:30. Plan sights for daytime and markets for the evening.

⚠️ Safety, Scams & Winter Warnings

  • Luxembourg is generally safe, but pickpockets work crowded markets, the station and trams. Keep phones and wallets in zipped pockets, especially on weekend evenings.
  • The area around Gare Centrale is livelier and rougher at night than the old town. Keep valuables out of sight and choose well-lit routes back.
  • Steps and cobbles in the Grund, the Corniche and Pfaffenthal can ice over or turn slippery in rain. Wear shoes with grip and hold the handrails.
  • Free transport does not cover first-class train seats or cross-border trips, and inspectors can ask for ID. For emergencies call 112 (police 113), and check your government's travel advice before you go.

💬 Crucial Phrases in Luxembourgish & French

  • "Moien" (hello, informal), "Gudde Moien" (good morning), "Gudden Owend" (good evening), "Äddi" (goodbye)
  • "Wann ech gelift" (please), "Merci" (thank you), "Pardon" (excuse me), "Jo" (yes), "Neen" (no)
  • French: "Bonjour" (hello), "S'il vous plaît" (please), "Merci beaucoup" (thank you very much), "Parlez-vous anglais ?" (do you speak English?)
  • "Wéi vill kascht dat ?" (how much is it?), "Prost !" (cheers), "L'addition, s'il vous plaît" (the bill, please)

💳 Money & Payments

  • Luxembourg uses the euro. Cards and contactless work in nearly all hotels, shops and restaurants, and phone payments are common, but carry some cash for small market stalls.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs and avoid standalone exchange booths. If a terminal offers to charge you in your home currency, choose euros.
  • Luxembourg is expensive. A coffee costs about €3 to €4 and a main course €20 to €30, so lunch menus and market food stretch a budget (check current prices).

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🚋

Ride everything for free

Trams, buses and second-class trains cost nothing, including from the airport and to Vianden. Skip taxis and day passes. Carry ID, as inspectors can ask, and avoid first class, which is not free.

🍽️

Make lunch your main meal

Luxembourg is expensive, but a plat du jour at lunch often costs well under dinner prices, usually about €15 to €20 (check current). Pair it with a market snack in the evening.

🎟️

Count your entries before buying a pass

Mudam, the City Museum and Vianden together come to about €28, and the Bock Casemates add about €11 when open. A LuxembourgPass runs roughly €23 to €39 by length (check current), so add up your list first.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌅

Walk the Corniche at dusk

The rampart walk looks best in the low winter light, with the Grund and the old fortifications below. Go before the paths darken, around 15:30 to 16:00 in December.

🎨

Use Mudam's free Wednesday evening

Entry is free from 18:00 to 21:00 on Wednesdays. If Day 3 falls on a Wednesday, visit Mudam in the evening instead and save €10, then take the city sights in the morning.

🍇

Swap Vianden for the Moselle

If you prefer wine to castles, the Moselle towns such as Remich and Grevenmacher are reachable by bus or train. Check cellar opening times first, as some close or shorten hours in winter.

✈️ Flights

  • Luxembourg Airport (LUX) at Findel is about 6 km from the centre, with a tram stop at the terminal. Luxair is the home carrier, and other airlines serve major European cities.
  • The tram to the centre takes roughly 30 to 35 minutes and is free. Buses 16 and 29 also run to the city (check current routes).
  • Some visitors fly to Brussels or Frankfurt and take a train, which can be cheaper than flying into Luxembourg. Brussels to Luxembourg takes about 3 hours, so compare times and fares.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Taxis are expensive and rarely needed, because the tram runs from the airport and covers most of this itinerary. Use only licensed taxis with a meter, and ask for an estimate.
  • Taxi and rideshare apps operate in Luxembourg City, but coverage and prices change, so check the apps before relying on them.
  • For a very early flight, book a taxi the evening before, since public transport starts around 04:00 (check current).

🚇 Public Transport & Tickets

  • Since 1 March 2020, buses, trams and second-class trains are free for everyone, including tourists. First-class train seats and cross-border trips still cost money, and night buses have their own rules (check mobiliteit.lu).
  • Carry ID, since an inspector can ask, and plan trips with the mobiliteit.lu app or website.
  • Tram line 1 links Findel, Kirchberg, Hamilius and the Gare. Hamilius is the stop for Place d'Armes, and Philharmonie-Mudam serves Day 3.
  • The Pfaffenthal panoramic lift and the Pfaffenthal-Kirchberg funicular are free (check they are running).

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Old town (near Place d'Armes): the most walkable base for the markets, but the priciest in December. Book early.
  • Gare district: cheaper and well connected by tram, but busier and rougher at night.
  • Kirchberg: modern business hotels beside the tram, often cheaper at weekends and a quiet evening base.
  • Grund and Pfaffenthal: picturesque and quiet, but expect steps and uphill walks to the old town.
  • Hostels and budget chains cost far less than central hotels, and staying outside the city is cheaper but needs a paid cross-border train.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • Luxembourg is in the EU, so EU SIM holders roam at home rates. Other visitors can use an eSIM or a local prepaid SIM, and many cafés, hotels and public spaces offer Wi-Fi.
  • Cards and contactless work almost everywhere, but keep a little cash for small stalls and markets.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs and avoid standalone exchange booths.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Check your passport's Schengen rules before booking, including whether ETIAS applies on your travel dates (it was planned to start in the last quarter of 2026).
  • Confirm each market's opening dates and the Winterlights 2026 programme before you go, and check 24 December, 25 December and 1 January closures.
  • The LuxembourgPass gives free or discounted entry to many sights across the country, but compare its price with your list of paid entries.
  • Most shops close on Sundays outside a few Advent openings, so buy gifts on Saturday or a weekday.
