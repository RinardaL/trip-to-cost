Austria Itineraries and Trip Costs

5 complete day-by-day trips in Austria, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 5 Austria itineraries: Austrian Alps, Hallstatt, Salzburg, Vienna, Schladming. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $480 to $1,300 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Schladming at about $185 a day, and the most expensive is Austrian Alps at about $215 a day.

Trips run from 3 to 5 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
