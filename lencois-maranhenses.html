4 Days at Lençóis Maranhenses: White Dunes, Blue Lagoons & the Preguiças River

A vast sea of white sand dunes in northeastern Brazil that fills with clear rainwater lagoons after the rainy season, best seen just after they've reached their fullest. Four days based in the river town of Barreirinhas is enough to cover the classic 4x4 dune circuit to Lagoa Azul and Lagoa Bonita, catch a sunset from a high dune ridge, and take the Rio Preguiças all the way to the small fishing village of Atins.

Ideal SeasonJun – Sep
Est. Budget$700-950
Duration4 Days
PaceRelaxed
How many days do you have?

Morning

Fly into São Luís, then transfer to Barreirinhas

There's no airport near the dunes, so nearly every trip starts by flying into São Luís, the state capital, then making the roughly 3.5 to 4 hour drive east to Barreirinhas, the small river town that's the main gateway to the park.

🚗 ~3.5-4 hr transfer from São Luís (MA) 💵 ~$45-70 shared van or ~$150 private 🕐 Book a transfer around a midday flight landing
Afternoon

Check in and walk out to the edge of the dune field

Most guesthouses sit within walking distance of where the town ends and the dunes begin, so once you've checked in, an easy stroll out to the first ridge gives a preview of the white sand sea you'll be exploring properly over the next two days.

🚶 ~15-20 min walk from most pousadas 💵 Free, no ticket needed for the edge trail 🕐 Mid-to-late afternoon, once the heat eases
Evening

First taste of Maranhão cuisine in town

Barreirinhas has a small strip of riverside restaurants serving Maranhão specialties, grilled shrimp, fresh river and coastal fish, and rice dishes cooked with local cuisandeira greens, a good introduction before the longer days ahead.

🚶 A short walk along the riverfront 💵 ~$12-18 dinner with a drink 🕐 Early evening, most kitchens open by 6:30 PM
Morning

4x4 dune-buggy tour from Barreirinhas

An open-sided 4x4 picks you up early and bounces along a sandy track deep into the dune field, a rougher, more exhilarating ride than it sounds, with the white dunes rolling out in every direction once the last scrub trees disappear.

🚙 ~45-60 min ride to the main lagoon circuit 💵 ~$35-55 per person, shared tour 🕐 Depart by 8 AM, before the midday heat
Midday

Swimming in Lagoa Azul

The Blue Lagoon is the park's signature swim spot, rainwater pooled between dunes and filtered clean by the sand until it turns a startling turquoise, warm, shallow, and calm enough to float in for an hour without wanting to leave.

🏊 A short walk from the buggy drop-off 💵 Included in the tour price 🕐 Late morning into early afternoon
Evening

Sunset at Lagoa Bonita

A second lagoon, larger and framed by a taller dune ridge that's become the park's classic sunset viewpoint, most tours time the last stop here so the light turns the sand gold and pink just as it drops behind the dunes.

🚙 The tour's final stop before returning to town 💵 Included in the tour price 🕐 Late afternoon into sunset
Morning

Downriver by boat to Vassouras

A wooden riverboat leaves Barreirinhas and follows the Rio Preguiças toward the coast, stopping first at the settlement of Vassouras, where a short walk leads into the Pequenos Lençóis, a miniature version of the main dune field, for fresh coconut water and a chance to spot capuchin monkeys in the mangroves.

⛴️ ~45 min downriver from Barreirinhas 💵 ~$30-40 per person, shared boat 🕐 Depart by 9 AM
Midday

Mandacaru lighthouse

A quick stop at the village of Mandacaru brings you to a Brazilian Navy-run lighthouse dating to 1940, worth the climb for the view over the river bend, plus homemade ice cream churned from local fruit sold right at its base.

⛴️ A short hop further downriver 💵 Small entry donation, ~$1-2 🕐 Late morning
Afternoon

Atins, where the river meets the ocean

The boat's final stop is Atins, a sandy, laid-back fishing village that's also become a magnet for kitesurfers thanks to its steady wind, with sand streets, simple seafood shacks, and a beach that stretches out toward the dunes.

⛴️ ~20 min further to the river mouth 💵 Included in the boat tour 🕐 Early to late afternoon, return boat by 5 PM
Morning

A free morning along the river

Keep this one unscheduled, a slow riverside walk or a last coffee overlooking the Preguiças, with a decent chance of spotting capuchin monkeys in the trees along the bank, is a fitting last stretch before the long ride out.

🚶 Around Barreirinhas itself 💵 Free 🕐 Before checkout, late morning
Midday

Transfer back to São Luís

Retrace the same road west, the same 3.5 to 4 hour drive as the arrival, timed to reach São Luís with enough buffer before an evening or next-morning flight home.

🚗 ~3.5-4 hr transfer to São Luís (MA) 💵 ~$45-70 shared van or ~$150 private 🕐 Depart Barreirinhas by early afternoon
Afternoon

A last look at São Luís before flying out

If your flight leaves late enough, São Luís's UNESCO-listed historic center, known for its tiled colonial facades, is worth a short walk before heading to the airport for the trip home.

🚗 Airport is ~15 min from the historic center 💵 Free to walk around 🕐 Leave at least 3 hrs before an international flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Tipping isn't mandatory in Brazil, but leaving about 10 percent at sit-down restaurants is standard and often already added as a "taxa de serviço" on the bill.
  • 4x4 and boat tour drivers or guides appreciate a small tip, roughly $3-5 per person, especially if they've gone out of their way with wildlife spotting or photos.
  • Rounding up is fine for casual drinks and snacks at riverside kiosks.

👗 Dress Code

  • Swimwear under light, quick-dry clothing is the default for most of the trip, you'll be in and out of lagoons and off-road vehicles all day.
  • The equatorial sun is intense and reflects hard off white sand, a wide-brim hat and UPF shirt matter more here than sunscreen alone.
  • Sandals with a heel strap handle both hot sand and shallow water far better than flip-flops, which tend to get left behind in soft dunes.

🚶 Getting Around

  • Barreirinhas itself is small and entirely walkable, most pousadas, restaurants, and tour operators sit within a few blocks of the river.
  • Inside the park, the only way in is by licensed 4x4 or on foot from a drop-off point, private cars can't drive the dune tracks themselves.
  • The Rio Preguiças toward Vassouras, Mandacaru, and Atins is covered by shared or private riverboat, there's no road that reaches Atins directly.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • The lagoons are seasonal and genuinely dry up outside the Jun-Sep window, always confirm current water levels with your tour operator before booking swim-focused days.
  • The sun and heat are the real hazard here, not the terrain, carry more water than feels necessary and avoid the midday hours without shade.
  • The small riverside communities the boat tours pass through are people's homes, not attractions, ask before photographing residents and keep noise down.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Brazilian Portuguese

  • Bom dia / Boa tarde: good morning / good afternoon
  • Muito obrigado(a): thank you very much
  • A conta, por favor: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • Brazil uses the real (R$), and Barreirinhas is remote enough that cash is still king for tours, small restaurants, and the riverside villages.
  • Cards are accepted at larger pousadas and some tour agencies in town, but connectivity can be patchy, so don't rely on it.
  • ATMs exist in Barreirinhas but are limited, withdraw what you'll need before heading out, especially before Day 3's river villages.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🚙

Join a shared 4x4 or boat instead of booking private

Shared tours cost a fraction of a private buggy or boat and still reach every major stop, private only makes sense for a group of four or more splitting the cost.

🥥

Skip pricier drinks at the lagoon kiosks

Small stalls at Lagoa Azul and Lagoa Bonita sell coconut water and snacks at a markup, a cheap gas-station stop in Barreirinhas before departure covers the same ground.

🏨

Book a pousada instead of a resort-style hotel

Family-run guesthouses in Barreirinhas run well below the handful of larger hotels and often include a better, more local breakfast.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌊

Stay a night in Atins instead of a day trip

Most visitors treat Atins as a quick boat stop, but its guesthouses and kitesurf scene make it a worthwhile overnight add-on if your schedule allows a fifth day.

🐒

Watch the mangroves near Vassouras

The short walk between the boat landing and the Pequenos Lençóis passes mangrove trees where capuchin monkeys are regularly spotted, most passengers walk straight past without looking up.

🌅

Ask for the early sunrise buggy option

A handful of operators run a sunrise version of the dune tour, far fewer people, softer light, and cooler temperatures than the standard afternoon departure.

✈️ Flights

  • São Luís Airport (SLZ) is the only realistic gateway, with connections through major Brazilian hubs like São Paulo, Brasília, and Fortaleza.
  • There's no airport near Barreirinhas itself, budget the full transfer time on both ends of the trip rather than trying to fly in closer.
  • Because this route involves at least one domestic connection for most international travelers, book flights well ahead and build in a buffer day if arriving from overseas.

🚕 Transfers & Shuttles

  • Shared vans between São Luís and Barreirinhas are the standard, cheapest option, arranged through your pousada or a local agency in advance.
  • Private transfers cost more but run on your own schedule, worth it if your flight lands late or you're traveling with a group.
  • Rideshare apps have essentially no coverage this far from São Luís, don't count on hailing anything once you're in Barreirinhas.

🚙 4x4 & Boat Tour Booking

  • Book the dune-buggy and river boat tours through a licensed local operator, either ahead online or once you've arrived in Barreirinhas, same-day booking is usually fine outside peak weeks.
  • Only registered vehicles and drivers are permitted inside the national park, always confirm an operator is licensed before paying.
  • This is a genuinely remote, logistically involved trip, plan the flight, transfer, and tour bookings together well ahead rather than assuming everything can be arranged on arrival.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Barreirinhas, near the river: the practical base for this itinerary, with the widest choice of pousadas and easy access to both tour types.
  • Atins, further out: a quieter, more remote alternative if you're extending the trip, better suited to travelers comfortable with fewer amenities.
  • There's no lodging inside the park boundary itself, everything is based in the surrounding river towns.

🎟️ Park Access & Timing

  • There's no single gate or ticket booth for the park, access is arranged entirely through your 4x4 or boat tour operator.
  • Lagoon levels genuinely change year to year, if you're traveling right at the edge of the June-September window, ask your operator for current conditions before finalizing dates.
  • Tours can pause or reroute after unusually heavy rain even in the dry season, build a spare half-day into longer trips if possible.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Most nationalities need only a valid passport for short tourist stays in Brazil, but requirements vary, check current visa rules well ahead of booking flights.
  • Cell signal is unreliable past Barreirinhas, let people know you may be hard to reach on Day 3's river route.
  • Travel insurance covering remote-area evacuation is worth the extra cost given how far this trip sits from major hospitals.
