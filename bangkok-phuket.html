6 Days in Thailand: Bangkok & Phuket Island

Two days threading Bangkok's canals, silk heritage, and a Muay Thai stadium, then a short flight south to Phuket Island itself: Patong's beaches, the Big Buddha, Old Town's Sino-Portuguese lanes, Phang Nga Bay's James Bond Island, and a Phi Phi day trip.

Ideal SeasonNov – Feb
Est. Budget$750-1,050
Duration6 Days
PaceActive
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land at Suvarnabhumi & transfer to Sukhumvit

A pre-booked transfer skips the taxi queue; the giant mythological guardian statues in the terminal are worth a look before you leave.

🚗 ~40 min to central Bangkok 💵 ~$12 🕐 Confirm pickup time
Afternoon

Longtail boat through Thonburi's khlongs to Khlong Bang Luang

A quieter, more local alternative to the big-name floating markets, reached by threading a longtail boat through the canals on the river's west bank.

🛶 longtail canal tour, ~2 hrs 💵 ~$18 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Dinner cruise on the Chao Phraya

A slow, lit-up loop past riverside temples and skyscrapers is a relaxed way to land in the city after a long flight.

⛴️ riverboat, pickup from your pier 💵 ~$45 🕐 Boards ~7 PM
Morning

Jim Thompson House

A canal-side teak house turned museum, telling the story of the American who revived Thailand's silk industry and vanished without a trace in 1967.

🚇 BTS to National Stadium 💵 ~$8 🕐 Opens 10 AM
Afternoon

Hands-on Thai cooking class

A market run followed by a few classic dishes at the wok, a practical souvenir that beats another t-shirt.

🚕 Grab to the cooking school 💵 ~$35 🕐 Afternoon, ~3 hrs
Evening

Fight night at Rajadamnern Stadium

The world's oldest Muay Thai stadium, and one of the most electric evenings Bangkok has to offer, live drums included.

🚕 Grab, ~20 min from Sukhumvit 💵 ~$30 🕐 Bouts start ~6:30 PM
Morning

Morning flight Bangkok to Phuket

Roughly 90 minutes in the air lands you on a genuinely different island, not the Krabi mainland: Phuket has its own airport, its own beaches, and its own pace.

✈️ ~1h 30min flight 💵 ~$50 🕐 Morning departure
Afternoon

The Big Buddha & Wat Chalong

A 45-metre marble statue on Nakkerd Hill with panoramic island views, paired with Phuket's most revered temple on the way back down.

🚕 taxi/Grab from the airport 💵 ~$15 (transport) 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Sunset on Patong Beach & Bangla Road

Check in, watch the sun drop over Phuket's busiest beach, then wander Bangla Road's neon strip for dinner and people-watching.

🚶 beachfront 💵 ~$12 (dinner) 🕐 Sunset, ~6:15 PM
Morning

Thalang Road & Soi Rommanee

Pastel-painted Sino-Portuguese shophouses built by 19th-century tin-mining fortunes, now filled with cafes, murals, and boutique shops.

🚶 walkable old-town grid 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Mid-morning, before the heat
Afternoon

Karon or Kata Beach

Both quieter and cleaner than Patong, with Kata's viewpoint over three bays a short, worthwhile detour if you have a scooter or songthaew.

🚐 songthaew from Old Town, ~30 min 💵 ~$1.50 (songthaew) 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Fresh seafood at Rawai

Pick your own catch at the seafront market stalls and have it grilled to order at the tables right behind them.

🚕 Grab, ~20 min from Karon 💵 ~$20 🕐 Evening, ~7 PM
Morning

Boat tour into Phang Nga Bay

Sheer limestone towers rise straight out of jade-green water; a full-day tour is the standard way in, with hotel pickup included.

🚐 minivan + longtail, hotel pickup 💵 ~$55 (full day) 🕐 Pickup ~8 AM
Afternoon

James Bond Island & Koh Panyee's floating village

The famous Koh Tapu sea stack from The Man with the Golden Gun, followed by lunch in a Muslim fishing village built entirely on stilts.

🚤 longtail between stops 💵 included above 🕐 Midday
Evening

Sunset at Laem Phromthep

Phuket's southernmost cape and its most famous sunset viewpoint, with a small lighthouse and a steady stream of locals arriving for the show.

🚕 Grab, ~30 min from Patong 💵 Free 🕐 Sunset, ~6:15 PM
Morning

Speedboat from Phuket to Phi Phi

A longer crossing than from Krabi, but it means seeing Phi Phi as a single well-organized day trip rather than needing to overnight there.

🚤 ~1h 30min by speedboat 💵 ~$50 (incl. park fee) 🕐 Earliest boat, ~7:30 AM
Afternoon

Phi Phi viewpoint hike & Long Beach snorkel

A steep 20-minute climb for the postcard shot of Phi Phi Don's twin bays, then a swim off Long Beach away from the Maya Bay crowds.

🚶 viewpoint hike, ~20 min up 💵 ~$1 (viewpoint entry) 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Return to Phuket & transfer to the airport

Confirm your return speedboat time when booking, then head straight from the pier to Phuket International for the flight home.

🚗 ~40 min pier to airport 💵 ~$18 🕐 Evening, allow 3h before departure

💵 Tipping Etiquette

  • Not obligatory, but rounding up or leaving 10% at sit-down restaurants is appreciated, in both Bangkok and Phuket.
  • Longtail and speedboat crews, and cooking-class instructors, appreciate a small tip handed over directly at the end.
  • No tipping expected at street food stalls, songthaews, or local markets.

🙏 Temple Etiquette & the Wai

  • Shoulders and knees must be covered at Wat Chalong and other active temples; sarongs are sold or loaned at the entrance if you get caught out.
  • Remove your shoes before entering any temple building, and never point your feet toward a Buddha image while seated.
  • The wai, palms pressed together at chest height with a slight bow, is the standard greeting; return one if offered, but you needn't initiate it with staff or service workers.

🛵 Getting Around

  • Bangkok: the Grab app covers metered cars and motorbike taxis; tuk-tuks are a fun novelty but agree the fare before getting in.
  • Phuket: songthaews (shared pickup-truck taxis) run fixed routes from Phuket Town for a flat fare; metered taxis and Grab are thinner on the ground than in Bangkok.
  • The Bangkok–Phuket domestic flight is the fastest way to connect the two halves of this trip; book it a few weeks ahead in high season.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Rental scooters are everywhere in Phuket, but Thai insurance rarely covers foreign riders without a valid motorcycle license; check your travel insurance's fine print before renting.
  • Patong's flag system on the beach is worth heeding: red flags mean dangerous currents, especially outside the November–February dry season.
  • Buddha images are treated with real reverence everywhere in Thailand: don't climb on statues, and women should avoid direct physical contact with monks.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Thai

  • Sawadee ka / khrap: hello (ka for women, khrap for men)
  • Khob khun ka / khrap: thank you
  • Tao rai? how much is this?

💳 Money & Payments

  • Thai baht (THB) is the currency; ATMs are plentiful in both Bangkok and Phuket, though each withdrawal carries a foreign-card fee.
  • Cash is still expected at Rawai's seafood stalls, songthaews, and longtail boat operators.
  • PromptPay QR codes are widely used in Bangkok cafes and shops; keep small baht notes handy for the islands.

A short longtail ride from Patong's south end reaches a cove with none of the jet skis or noise.

🎨

Old Phuket Town closes Thalang Road to traffic every Sunday evening for street food, live music, and local crafts.

Afternoon light on the karst towers is softer, and the morning tour crowds have already headed back.

✈️ Flights

  • Suvarnabhumi (BKK) and Don Mueang (DMK) both serve Bangkok; most Phuket routes fly out of Suvarnabhumi, but double-check before booking a transfer.
  • The Bangkok to Phuket hop takes about 1h 30min and is the backbone of this itinerary's two-region structure.
  • Book the domestic leg a few weeks out: dry-season fares to Phuket rise quickly closer to departure.

🚕 Taxis, Tuk-Tuks & Songthaews

  • Grab is the reliable, transparent option in Bangkok; in Phuket it's available but with fewer drivers, especially outside Patong.
  • Phuket's songthaews run fixed routes from Phuket Town to each major beach for a flat, posted fare, cash only.
  • Tuk-tuks in both cities are best treated as a novelty ride, not transport: agree a price up front.

🛵 Scooters & Rental Cars

  • Not needed in Bangkok, where traffic and transit make a rental car pointless.
  • In Phuket, a rental scooter or car gives real freedom between beaches and viewpoints, but insurance often requires a valid motorcycle license for scooters.
  • Songthaews and Grab cover this itinerary comfortably without renting anything.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Bangkok: Sukhumvit or riverside near the BTS puts you close to the Jim Thompson House, the stadium, and the express boat piers.
  • Phuket: Patong for nightlife and beach energy, Kata or Karon for a quieter beach base, or Old Town for character and easy day-trip logistics.
  • Book Phang Nga Bay and Phi Phi tours a day or two ahead in high season; the best-reviewed operators sell out first.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Essential on the Phuket half: Phang Nga Bay and the Phi Phi Islands are both reachable only by boat.
  • Longtails handle short hops like the Thonburi khlongs and inter-island stops; speedboats cover the longer Phi Phi day trip.
  • Always confirm your return crossing time when booking, missing the last boat means an unplanned night wherever you are.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Many nationalities get visa-exempt entry to Thailand for up to 60 days; confirm the current rule for your passport before flying.
  • Travel insurance is worth it for the speedboat crossings and any scooter or water-sports plans alone.
  • Tap water isn't safe to drink anywhere on this route: stick to bottled or filtered water, including for brushing your teeth on the islands.
