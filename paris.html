💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service is included by law (service compris): tipping is not expected.
  • Rounding up a euro or two for great service is a nice gesture, not required.
  • No tipping expected at cafes or bakeries.

👗 Dress Code

  • Parisians dress smart-casual: athletic wear is uncommon outside the gym.
  • Cover shoulders and knees to enter Notre-Dame or Sacré-Cœur.
  • Comfortable shoes matter more than style: expect a lot of walking.

🚇 Transit Nuances

  • A carnet of 10 Metro tickets (or a Navigo Easy card) is cheaper than singles.
  • Validate your ticket before boarding: inspectors do check.
  • The Metro runs late but starts to thin out after midnight.

⚠️ Safety & Scam Warnings

  • Pickpockets are common near the Eiffel Tower and on Metro Line 1, so keep bags zipped and in front.
  • Ignore "petition" or "found ring" scams near major monuments.
  • Use official taxi ranks or a rideshare app rather than unlicensed drivers.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases

  • Bonjour: hello (always lead with this before asking anything)
  • Merci: thank you
  • L'addition, s'il vous plaît: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • Cards and contactless payment are accepted almost everywhere.
  • Small bakeries and markets sometimes have a minimum card amount.
  • ATMs are widely available; avoid currency exchange kiosks near tourist sites.

✈️ Flights

  • Charles de Gaulle (CDG) is the main international gateway; the RER B train runs straight into central Paris in about an hour.
  • Orly (ORY) is closer to the city but serves fewer long-haul routes, so check which one your flight actually lands at.
  • Book RER tickets or a Navigo Easy card in advance to skip the airport ticket line.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Uber works well and reliably throughout Paris, alongside the local G7 taxi app.
  • Official taxi ranks near stations and monuments are safer than street hails, especially at night.
  • Fares are metered, service is included, so no tip is expected beyond rounding up.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Not needed for this itinerary: the Metro reaches every stop on this plan, from the Eiffel Tower to Montmartre.
  • Parking is expensive and hard to find, and central Paris has a low-emission zone that restricts older vehicles.
  • A car adds stress with no benefit for a compact, walkable weekend like this one.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Le Marais: this itinerary's Day 1 check-in area, central, walkable, and close to Day 2's dinner spot.
  • 7th arrondissement: near the Eiffel Tower and Champ de Mars for an easy Day 1 afternoon.
  • Latin Quarter: convenient for Notre-Dame, Sainte-Chapelle, and the Rue Mouffetard market street on Day 3.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Not required for getting around: the Metro handles all transport on this itinerary.
  • The Day 1 evening Seine river cruise is a sightseeing add-on already built into this plan, not a transit option, so book it ahead for sunset.
  • No other boat legs are part of this weekend.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • EU/Schengen entry rules apply; non-EU visitors should confirm visa requirements before booking flights.
  • The Paris Museum Pass pays for itself after about three sites, including the Louvre and Sainte-Chapelle on Day 2.
  • Travel insurance is worth it mainly for the Louvre and summit tickets booked in advance, since a missed slot isn't refundable.
