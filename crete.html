Beach & Coastal Minoan Crete 6 Days

6 Days in Crete, Greece: Knossos, the Samaria Gorge & Balos Lagoon

Three days based in Heraklion for Crete's Minoan palaces and the windmill-studded Lasithi Plateau, then west to Chania for one of Europe's longest gorges and a lagoon of impossibly turquoise water. Six days across the island's mountains, coastline, and 4,000 years of history.

Ideal SeasonMay – Jun, Sep
Est. Budget$850-1,150
Duration6 Days
PaceActive
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land in Heraklion & transfer to the old town

Most international flights land at Heraklion's Nikos Kazantzakis Airport, a short ride from the old town where this itinerary both begins and ends.

🚗 ~15 min transfer to the old town 💵 ~€15 taxi, or ~€2 by bus 🕐 Plan around your flight's landing time
Afternoon

Koules Fortress & the Venetian Harbor

This squat 16th-century fortress guards the mouth of Heraklion's harbor, its thick walls and vaulted chambers a reminder of over two centuries of Venetian rule.

🚶 Walkable from the old town 💵 ~€4 entry 🕐 Mid-afternoon, before the sunset crowds
Evening

Lion Square & a first Cretan dinner

Morosini Fountain's carved lion heads mark the heart of the old town, ringed by cafes and narrow lanes leading to a first dinner of dakos and grilled meat.

🚶 Compact, walkable old town 💵 ~€20 dinner 🕐 Evening, ~8 PM
Morning

Explore the Palace of Knossos

Sir Arthur Evans's early-20th-century excavation and partial reconstruction turned this Bronze Age palace complex into Crete's most visited site, the sprawling foundation behind the later Greek myth of the Labyrinth and the Minotaur.

🚗 ~20 min from Heraklion 💵 ~€15 entry 🕐 Arrive at opening, 8 AM, before the tour buses
Afternoon

Heraklion Archaeological Museum

The finest Minoan artifacts anywhere left the palace for this museum: the still-undeciphered Phaistos Disc, gold jewelry, and frescoes of bull-leapers and saffron-gatherers lifted straight from Knossos's walls.

🚶 ~10 min walk from the old harbor 💵 ~€10 entry 🕐 Allow at least 90 minutes
Evening

Dakos & raki in the old town

A proper Cretan dinner starts with dakos, a barley rusk piled with tomato and mizithra cheese, and ends with a complimentary glass of raki, the island's grape-skin spirit, poured on the house.

🚶 Old town taverna 💵 ~€25 dinner 🕐 Evening, ~8 PM
Morning

Drive up to the Lasithi Plateau

The road climbs through pine-covered passes into a wide, fertile plain over 800 metres up, still dotted with the white-sailed stone windmills that once pumped water for its orchards.

🚗 ~1h drive from Heraklion 💵 ~€30 car rental for the day 🕐 Morning, before the midday haze
Afternoon

Psychro Cave, mythical birthplace of Zeus

Greek myth places the birth of Zeus in this stalactite-hung cave above the plateau, a steep climb to a chamber where archaeologists have also uncovered troves of Minoan-era votive offerings.

🥾 ~15 min uphill walk from the car park 💵 ~€6 entry 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

A village taverna on the plateau

Small villages ring the plateau's edge, and their tavernas serve simpler, heartier food than the coast: slow-cooked lamb, wild greens, and potatoes grown right in the plateau's soil.

🚗 Return to Heraklion after dinner, ~1h 💵 ~€20 dinner 🕐 Early evening, before the drive back
Morning

Rethymno's Venetian harbor

Halfway between Heraklion and Chania, Rethymno's old town packs minarets, Venetian loggias, and a horseshoe harbor into a few walkable blocks, worth the stop even on a transfer day.

🚗 ~1h drive from Heraklion 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Mid-morning
Afternoon

The Fortezza, Rethymno's Venetian fortress

This hilltop fortress was built to hold the entire town against pirate raids, and its walls now frame views over the old town's terracotta roofs to the Cretan Sea.

🚶 ~10 min walk uphill from the harbor 💵 ~€4 entry 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Arrival in Chania at sunset

The drive continues west to Chania, this itinerary's second base, in time to watch the light turn gold over the Venetian harbor and its Egyptian-built lighthouse.

🚗 ~1h drive from Rethymno 💵 ~€25 dinner 🕐 Evening, sunset around 8-8:30 PM in season
Morning

Descend from Xyloskalo into the gorge

A pre-dawn transfer reaches the Omalos Plateau in time for opening, where a steep stone stairway, the Xyloskalo, drops hikers through pine forest and into the start of a 16-kilometre trail.

🚗 ~1h drive from Chania to Omalos 💵 ~€5 park entry 🕐 Start by 7-8 AM
Afternoon

Through the Iron Gates

The gorge narrows to just a few metres wide at the Sideroportes, the Iron Gates, where limestone cliffs rise nearly 300 metres on either side of the dry riverbed trail.

🥾 ~5-6h total hiking time 💵 Included in park entry 🕐 Midday, the trail's narrowest stretch
Evening

Agia Roumeli & the boat back

The trail ends at the coastal village of Agia Roumeli, reachable only on foot or by sea, where a ferry runs along the coast to Hora Sfakion for the bus back to Chania.

⛴️ ~45 min coastal ferry 💵 ~€12 ferry, ~€10 bus 🕐 Late afternoon, plan for a late return
Morning

Boat to Balos Lagoon

A boat from Kissamos rounds the Gramvousa peninsula to reach Balos, a shallow lagoon where turquoise water meets sand tinted pink by crushed shell, backed by one of the wildest stretches of coast in Crete.

⛴️ ~1h boat from Kissamos, ~1h drive from Chania 💵 ~€35 boat trip 🕐 Morning departure, ~10 AM
Afternoon

Beach time & the return crossing

There's little to do at Balos but swim, sunbathe, and admire the view back toward the Venetian fortress ruins on Gramvousa island before the boat returns to Kissamos.

🏖️ ~3h at the lagoon 💵 Included in the boat trip 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Transfer to the airport & departure

Allow extra time for the drive back across the island to Heraklion if your flight departs from there, or check in closer to Chania if your return routes through its smaller airport.

🚗 ~2.5h to Heraklion, or ~30 min to Chania Airport 💵 ~€60-90 taxi to Heraklion 🕐 Evening, allow 2.5h before departure

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service isn't automatically included everywhere, rounding up or adding 5-10% for a good sit-down meal is appreciated, not required.
  • Raki is often poured for free at the end of a meal, a customary gesture rather than something to tip extra for.
  • Guides for the Samaria Gorge transfer or the Balos boat appreciate a small tip if the day went well.

👒 Dress Code

  • Casual clothing works for most of the trip, but bring sturdy hiking shoes and moisture-wicking layers for the Samaria Gorge.
  • Cover shoulders and knees for churches and monasteries around the Lasithi villages.
  • Many shops and restaurants close for a few hours in the early afternoon, plan the day around the siesta rather than against it.

🚗 Getting Around

  • A rental car is close to essential, Knossos, the Lasithi Plateau, and the transfer to Chania all sit outside town.
  • Long-distance KTEL buses connect Heraklion, Rethymno, and Chania cheaply and often, a backup if you skip the car.
  • Book the Samaria Gorge transfer and the Balos boat a day or two ahead in peak season, both routinely sell out.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • The Samaria Gorge descent is longer and harder on the knees than most expect, trekking poles help, and the trail closes in high winds or after heavy rain.
  • Afternoon sun on Balos and the drive to Elafonissi is intense with almost no shade, bring your own.
  • Roads on the Lasithi Plateau and up to Omalos are narrow and winding, drive them in daylight.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Greek

  • Kalimera: good morning/hello
  • Efharisto poli: thank you very much
  • Ton logariasmo, parakalo: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is the currency, and contactless cards are accepted in most restaurants and shops, even in smaller towns.
  • Keep cash for the Samaria Gorge park entry, Psychro Cave tickets, and small village tavernas.
  • A local tourist tax is added to hotel bills nightly, usually a few euros depending on the category.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🚐

Book the Samaria Gorge minibus directly with a local operator

Going straight through a Chania-based transfer company rather than a hotel desk typically runs noticeably cheaper for the same seat.

🍽️

Eat where the KTEL buses stop

Small village kafeneia near the bus routes serve the same Cretan classics as the resort tavernas, for a fraction of the price.

🚗

Rent the car for the whole trip, not day by day

One-way and short-term rentals both carry a premium, a single six-day booking from Heraklion works out cheaper overall.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🥾

Aradena Gorge over Samaria in shoulder season

A deeper, quieter canyon near Chania with its own suspension bridge, and a fraction of the crowds of its famous neighbor.

🏔️

The Amari Valley over the busier Lasithi routes

The same mountain-village scenery and stone architecture, with almost no tour buses on the roads.

🏖️

Sfakia's south coast beaches

Reachable only by the same small boat that serves Samaria Gorge hikers, these coves stay empty even at the height of August.

✈️ Flights

  • Heraklion's Nikos Kazantzakis Airport (HER) handles most international and domestic arrivals, with Chania (CHQ) a smaller alternative in the island's west.
  • Flights from Athens take about 45 minutes and run frequently in summer, often cheaper the further ahead you book.
  • International charter and budget routes into Heraklion expand significantly from May through September.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Metered taxis are reliable in Heraklion and Chania, agree a fixed fare in advance for longer transfers like the Samaria Gorge minibus.
  • Ride-hailing apps have limited coverage outside the two main cities, don't count on them in the mountain villages.
  • Airport taxi ranks post fixed fares to the main hotel districts.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Close to essential for this itinerary, Knossos, the Lasithi Plateau, and the coast between Heraklion and Chania all sit well outside town.
  • Book with unlimited mileage given the distances covered across six days.
  • Mountain roads on the Lasithi Plateau and near Omalos are narrow with sheer drops in places, drive them slowly and in daylight.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Heraklion's old town: the closest base for Knossos, the museum, and the Lasithi Plateau day trip (Days 1-3).
  • Chania's old town: the most atmospheric base for the Samaria Gorge transfer and the Balos boat departure (Days 4-6).
  • Book Samaria Gorge transfers alongside your Chania hotel, both sell out on the same peak-season dates.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • The Balos Lagoon boat from Kissamos is the easiest way in, the alternative dirt-track drive and cliff path down is rough and exposed.
  • The Agia Roumeli to Hora Sfakion ferry is the only way out of the Samaria Gorge without hiking back the way you came.
  • Ferries connect Heraklion with Santorini and the other Cyclades islands if you want to extend the trip beyond Crete.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Crete is part of Greece, so standard Schengen entry rules apply for most visitors.
  • The Samaria Gorge only opens 1 May to 31 October, weather-dependent, and closes in high winds or after heavy rain, so check conditions before booking.
  • A direct overnight ferry from Piraeus (Athens) to Heraklion takes about 9-10 hours, a scenic but slower alternative to the 45-minute flight.
