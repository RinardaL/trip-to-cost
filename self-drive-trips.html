Sixteen trips planned around a hire car

Every itinerary here assumes you collect a hire car at the airport and drive the route yourself. Five are named routes written in our newest format, with a day-by-day route table and a car-cost table: Iceland Ring Road, Pacific Coast Highway, North Coast 500, Wild Atlantic Way and Route 66. The other eleven are regional loops that need a car to reach their stops: Iceland, Scottish Highlands, Tuscany, Puglia, Algarve, Utah, Bavaria, Valensole, Bardenas Reales, Krujë and Lofoten Islands.

Length is the first choice. The shortest loops take 4 days (Scottish Highlands, Algarve, Valensole, Bardenas Reales), the regional trips take 5 to 7, and the named routes take 6 or 7 days. Bardenas Reales is the cheapest on the page at $430-640 per person and Lofoten Islands the dearest at $1,600-2,200.

What the car adds to the price

On the five newest pages the total already includes each person's half of the car for two travellers, and a separate table splits it into hire, fuel, tolls and parking, and the one-way fee. The share per person is $425-670 for Iceland Ring Road, $275-460 for Pacific Coast Highway, $245-405 for North Coast 500, $325-515 for Wild Atlantic Way and $280-525 for Route 66. A third or fourth traveller lowers the share, because the car costs the same for one person or four.

Three of the five are one-way trips, so check the one-way fee before you book: Pacific Coast Highway runs from San Francisco to Los Angeles, Wild Atlantic Way from Cork to Shannon and Route 66 from Albuquerque to Santa Monica. The older regional pages mention car hire in their travel notes but do not split the car out as a separate cost, and we are bringing them to the same format.

When to go and what to check before you drive

The named routes are written for the shoulder seasons. Pacific Coast Highway suggests late September to mid-October, Route 66 mid-April to May or late September to October because the desert is extremely hot in summer, and North Coast 500 and Wild Atlantic Way late May to June or September. Iceland Ring Road is planned for June or September, when cars and beds cost less than in July and August.

Road conditions change, so each page names the official source to open before you set off: Caltrans for Highway 1 in California, road.is and SafeTravel for Iceland, Traffic Scotland and Highland Council for the North Coast 500, and the state transport departments for Route 66. Treat every price on these pages as a range and check current rates, because car hire moves with the season and the booking date.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a self-drive trip cost?

Our 16 itineraries range from about $430 to $2,200 per person for the whole trip, depending on the route and the number of days. The five newest pages include each person's half of the car for two travellers; the older ones treat the car less consistently, so check the page.

Which self-drive trip is the cheapest?

Bardenas Reales ($430-640), Scottish Highlands ($450-680), Krujë ($550-750) are the three lowest on this list, per person and excluding flights.

How many days do I need?

The loops here run from 4 to 7 days. Each page has a day picker with a price for every trip length from one day up to the full plan.

Which side of the road do I drive on?

On the right in Iceland, the USA and mainland Europe, and on the left in Scotland and Ireland. The Scottish and Irish pages explain single-track roads and passing places, which are the main thing to learn before the North Coast 500 and the Wild Atlantic Way.

Is it cheaper to share the car?

Yes. Hire, fuel and the one-way fee cost the same for one traveller or for four, so the share per person falls as you add travellers. The five newest pages assume two people sharing one car, and the car-cost table shows the whole-car and per-person figures.
