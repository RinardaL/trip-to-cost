6 Days in Puglia: A Self-Drive Road Trip Through Italy's Heel

Whitewashed hill towns, UNESCO trulli, Baroque Lecce stone, and turquoise coves carved into limestone cliffs, a heel-of-Italy road trip built around a Valle d'Itria trullo stay, a Lecce city base, and easy coastal drives between towns.

Ideal SeasonMay – Jun, Sep
Est. Budget$1,000-1,350
Duration6 Days
PaceModerate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land in Bari & pick up the rental car

Bari Karol Wojtyła Airport sits about 15 minutes from the city center, with rental counters right in the arrivals hall. Reserve an automatic ahead of time, since most Italian fleet cars are manual.

🚗 ~15 min drive into central Bari 💵 ~€50/day rental 🕐 Book the automatic weeks ahead
Afternoon

Watch orecchiette made by hand in Bari Vecchia

On Via Arco Basso and the surrounding lanes of the old town, local nonne set up folding tables each morning and hand-roll orecchiette right on the street, selling it fresh by the kilo.

🚶 Walkable maze of alleys in the old town 💵 ~€6/kg fresh orecchiette 🕐 Before lunch, while the pasta-makers are out
Evening

Sunset walk & seafood on the Lungomare

Bari's Lungomare curves along the Adriatic past the Norman-Swabian Castle, one of Italy's longest waterfront promenades and the spot locals take their evening passeggiata.

🚶 ~15 min walk from the old town 💵 ~€35 seafood dinner 🕐 Golden hour along the water
Morning

Lama Monachile cove & the cliffside old town

Polignano's old town perches directly on limestone cliffs, with the small Lama Monachile beach wedged into a cove below a Roman bridge. Arrive early to see it before the beach chairs fill in.

🚗 ~40 min drive from Bari along the coast 💵 Free to visit, ~€15 beach umbrella rental 🕐 Before 10 AM, ahead of the crowds
Afternoon

Cliffside walk to the diving platform & lunch over the sea

Every summer Polignano hosts a stop on the Red Bull Cliff Diving World Series from the cliffs above Lama Monachile. Even without the event, the platform and the view down into the cove are worth the walk.

🚶 Short walk along the cliffside path 💵 ~€25 lunch on a sea-view terrace 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Drive to the Valle d'Itria & check into a trullo stay

Base yourself in or near Alberobello for the next two nights. Many of the old trulli have been converted into small guesthouses, so you can actually sleep inside one of Puglia's conical stone houses.

🚗 ~40 min drive inland to Alberobello 💵 ~€130/night trullo stay 🕐 Check-in from 4 PM
Morning

Rione Monti & Rione Aia Piccola, Alberobello's UNESCO trulli

Alberobello's roughly 1,500 trulli, whitewashed cone-roofed stone houses built without mortar, earned UNESCO status in 1996. Rione Monti holds the souvenir shops; the quieter Rione Aia Piccola across town is still mostly residential.

🚶 Walkable historic center 💵 ~€5 Trullo Sovrano museum entry 🕐 Before 9:30 AM, ahead of the tour buses
Afternoon

Locorotondo's circular old town

Named for the round layout of its historic center, Locorotondo's whitewashed streets and gabled "cummerse" roofs make it one of Italy's officially recognized "most beautiful villages," with almost none of Alberobello's crowds.

🚗 ~15 min drive from Alberobello 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Mid-afternoon
Evening

Masseria dinner experience in the Valle d'Itria

A fortified countryside masseria turned agriturismo serves a multi-course dinner built around what the farm grows: burrata, taralli, orecchiette, and grilled meats, often at a long shared table among the olive trees.

🚗 ~15-20 min drive into the countryside 💵 ~€45 multi-course dinner 🕐 Book a table for sunset
Morning

Ostuni's whitewashed hilltop center & Cattedrale

Ostuni's old town is lime-washed white from top to bottom, a tradition that once helped fight the plague and now keeps the stone cool. Wander up to the 15th-century Cattedrale at the summit for a view over the countryside to the sea.

🚗 ~40 min drive from the Valle d'Itria 💵 Free to wander, ~€3 Cattedrale entry 🕐 Mid-morning
Afternoon

Torre Guaceto nature reserve beach & snorkeling

This protected marine reserve near Carovigno pairs a sandy, dune-backed beach with clear water good for snorkeling, a quieter alternative to Puglia's more built-up beach clubs.

🚗 ~25 min drive from Ostuni 💵 ~€3 reserve access, snorkel gear extra 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Drive to Lecce & an evening passeggiata

Check into Lecce for the next two nights, then join the evening passeggiata around a floodlit Piazza Duomo, when the Lecce-stone facades glow gold under the streetlights.

🚗 ~1 hr 15 min drive to Lecce 💵 ~€40 dinner in the centro storico 🕐 After dark, when the piazza lights come on
Morning

Piazza del Duomo & Lecce's Baroque stone facades

Soft, honey-colored Lecce stone was easy to carve, which is why the city's 17th-century builders went so ornate. The enclosed Piazza del Duomo, ringed by the cathedral, bell tower, and bishop's palace, is the best introduction.

🚶 Walkable historic center 💵 Free to enter the piazza 🕐 Mid-morning
Afternoon

Basilica di Santa Croce & the Roman amphitheater

The Basilica di Santa Croce's facade is Lecce Baroque at its most extravagant, covered in carved vines, animals, and figures. A short walk away, the 2nd-century Roman amphitheater sits sunken below street level in Piazza Sant'Oronzo.

🚶 ~10 min walk across the old town 💵 Free to view the amphitheater 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Aperitivo in Piazza Sant'Oronzo

Puglia's aperitivo hour is unhurried: a spritz or a glass of Salento wine with a plate of small bites at a table in the piazza, watching the passeggiata circle the Roman ruins.

🚶 Central piazza in Lecce 💵 ~€10 aperitivo 🕐 7-8 PM
Morning

Otranto's Aragonese Castle & the cathedral's mosaic floor

Italy's easternmost town, Otranto pairs a 15th-century Aragonese castle, the inspiration for Horace Walpole's Gothic novel, with a cathedral holding a vast 12th-century mosaic floor depicting the Tree of Life.

🚗 ~45 min drive from Lecce 💵 ~€9 castle entry, ~€2 cathedral donation 🕐 Mid-morning
Afternoon

Beach time at Torre dell'Orso

Twin coves separated by a rock stack locals call "Le Due Sorelle" (the two sisters) give Torre dell'Orso some of the Salento coast's clearest, most turquoise water, backed by low pine-covered cliffs.

🚗 ~20 min drive from Otranto 💵 ~€15 beach umbrella rental, or free on the public sand 🕐 Early afternoon, before the drive back
Evening

Return the rental car & fly out of Bari

Budget close to two hours for the drive back across the peninsula to Bari Karol Wojtyła Airport, longer with a stop for one last granita along the way.

🚗 ~1 hr 50 min drive back to Bari 💵 Rental already paid 🕐 Arrive 2 hrs before your flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service is generally included (servizio compreso) in Puglia as elsewhere in Italy, so tipping isn't obligatory anywhere on this route.
  • Rounding up or leaving a few euros for great service at a trattoria is appreciated, not expected.
  • At a masseria dinner, a small tip for staff who cooked and served a long multi-course meal is a nice gesture, not a requirement.

👗 Dress Code

  • Cover shoulders and knees to enter the Basilica di Santa Croce, Lecce's Duomo, and Otranto's cathedral.
  • Beachwear stays at the beach: swimsuits and bare chests aren't worn in town centers, even in the summer heat.
  • Pack light, breathable fabrics; Puglia's summer heat is dry but intense, especially on the sun-exposed coast.

🚗 Getting Around: Self-Drive & Limited Transit

  • Public transit barely connects Alberobello, Locorotondo, Ostuni, and the Salento coast, so a rental car is close to essential for this route.
  • Lecce and a few other centers restrict cars in the historic core; park in a lot just outside the old walls and walk in.
  • Roads between towns are well-paved but narrow through the countryside; a GPS with offline maps downloaded helps where signal drops.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • The sanpietrini and worn limestone streets in old towns like Ostuni and Alberobello get slippery, so skip flip-flops for exploring.
  • Midday sun in late spring and early autumn is still intense; plan beach and driving time around it rather than through the hottest hours.
  • Polignano's cliffs and rocky beach entries have no lifeguard in most coves, so check conditions before swimming.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Italian

  • Buongiorno: good morning/hello
  • Grazie mille: thank you very much
  • Il conto, per favore: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is used throughout Italy, and cards are widely accepted, including most masserie and agriturismi.
  • Keep some cash for small-town market stalls, beach umbrella rentals, and parking meters that skip card readers.
  • Autostrada tolls on the drive south from Bari are usually cash or card at the booth.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🅿️

Park outside the historic centers

Lots just outside the old walls of Lecce and Ostuni cost far less than anything inside, and avoid restricted-zone fines entirely.

🍝

Pranzo fisso & aperitivo hour

Fixed-price lunch menus and a modest aperitivo spread cost a fraction of a full dinner, and the food is just as good.

🏡

Masseria over hotel

A countryside masseria stay is often cheaper per night than a comparable town hotel, and usually includes a home-cooked breakfast.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🏘️

Locorotondo & Cisternino over crowded Alberobello

If Alberobello's lanes feel overrun by midday, these two Valle d'Itria towns offer the same whitewashed charm with far fewer tour buses.

🏖️

Punta Prosciutto over Porto Cesareo

This stretch of the Salento coast has the same shallow turquoise water with a fraction of the beach-club crowds.

🥩

Cisternino's fornello pronto

Butcher shops here grill the meat you pick out right on the spot, a Valle d'Itria tradition worth the short detour from Locorotondo.

✈️ Flights

  • Bari Karol Wojtyła Airport is the main gateway for this route, with direct flights from several European hubs.
  • Brindisi Airport sits closer to Lecce and the Salento coast, a useful alternate if you want to flip the route.
  • Both airports have rental counters right in the terminal, so you can be on the road within the hour.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • This is a self-drive road trip, so taxis and rideshare are a minor part of the plan at best.
  • Uber has limited coverage outside Bari and Lecce; local radio taxis are the fallback in smaller towns.
  • A taxi is useful for a masseria dinner where you'd rather not worry about driving back after the wine.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • The essential service for this itinerary: no train or bus line links Alberobello, Locorotondo, and the Salento coast the way a car does.
  • Reserve an automatic transmission well ahead of time, since most Italian rental fleets default to manual.
  • Parking is restricted in the historic centers of Lecce and Ostuni; park in a lot just outside the walls and walk in.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • A trullo stay near Alberobello, as used on Day 2, puts you close to Locorotondo and Ostuni for easy day trips.
  • Lecce works as a second base for Days 4-6, covering the Baroque city and the Salento coast beaches.
  • A masseria stay anywhere in the Valle d'Itria adds a countryside night without breaking the route.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Boat excursions along the Polignano coastline show the cliffside town and its sea caves from the water, a different angle than the clifftop view.
  • Snorkeling boat trips run out of Torre Guaceto's marine reserve during the summer season.
  • Otranto has ferry connections toward Albania and Greece, though that's outside the scope of this 6-day loop.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Standard Schengen entry rules apply, so non-EU visitors should confirm visa requirements before booking flights.
  • Non-EU drivers should carry an International Driving Permit alongside their home license for the rental car.
  • Travel insurance is worth adding at this budget tier, given the rental car and multiple town-to-town legs all add up if a day gets disrupted.
