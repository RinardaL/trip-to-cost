7 Days on the Pacific Coast Highway: Big Sur, Hearst Castle & Malibu

Seven days down Highway 1 from San Francisco to Los Angeles: Carmel, Bixby Bridge, McWay Falls, Hearst Castle and Malibu, with the car cost and what to check before Big Sur.

Ideal SeasonLate September to mid-October (second best: late April to May)
Est. Budget$1325-1810
Duration7 Days
PaceModerate

Last updated: November 2026

7-day Pacific Coast Highway, California itinerary

How many days do you have?

Morning

SFO, car pick-up & the road to Half Moon Bay

The rental companies at San Francisco airport (SFO) are in the Rental Car Center, reached by the free AirTrain. Photograph every side of the car before leaving, and confirm the fuel level and the one-way return at Los Angeles. Follow signs to I-280, then CA-92 west to Half Moon Bay, where Highway 1 begins.

🚗 ~45 km, 40 min to Half Moon Bay 💵 AirTrain free, car hire in the car costs below 🕐 Collect the car by midday to keep the afternoon free
Afternoon

Pigeon Point Lighthouse on Highway 1

Half Moon Bay to Pigeon Point is about 50 minutes south on Highway 1, past farm stands and beaches. The lighthouse stands on a clifftop and its tower is closed to visitors, so this is a grounds-and-views stop; confirm on parks.ca.gov. Fog is common on this stretch, so keep a jacket in the car and drive slowly.

🚗 ~45 km, 50 min from Half Moon Bay 💵 Grounds free, parking fee unconfirmed (check current) 🕐 Late afternoon light, once the fog has lifted
Evening

Santa Cruz Wharf & Beach Boardwalk

From Pigeon Point it is about 50 minutes to Santa Cruz. The wharf is a long wooden pier of fish restaurants with sea lions underneath, and the Beach Boardwalk is a short walk away. Downtown and beach car parks are paid and fill up on summer weekends. Buy fish and chips to eat on the pier, then check in.

🚗 ~45 km, 50 min from Pigeon Point 💵 Parking and dinner ~$15-25 (check current) 🕐 Early evening, before the wharf crowds
Morning

West Cliff Drive & Natural Bridges State Beach

Start with West Cliff Drive, a clifftop road and path above the surf, then continue to Natural Bridges State Beach, where a sea arch stands off the shore. The day-use fee is about $10 per vehicle (check current). Tide pools are best at low tide, and monarch butterflies roost in the grove from roughly October to February.

🚗 ~5 km, 10 min from downtown Santa Cruz 💵 ~$10 per vehicle (check current) 🕐 Morning, check the tide table for the pools
Afternoon

Monterey, Cannery Row & the Aquarium

Monterey is about an hour south on Highway 1. Park in a garage near Cannery Row and walk to the Monterey Bay Aquarium, where adult tickets are about $60 to $65 (check current) and are best booked online. Allow two to three hours inside. Street parking along the waterfront is time-limited, so a garage saves tickets.

🚗 ~70 km, 1 h from Santa Cruz 💵 Aquarium ~$60-65 (check current), garage parking extra 🕐 Arrive soon after opening to beat the midday crowd
Evening

17-Mile Drive, the Lone Cypress & Carmel Beach

Pebble Beach charges a gate fee of $12.50 per vehicle (official site, check current), refundable against a $35 spend at its restaurants. Stop at the Lone Cypress, then leave by the Carmel gate and watch the sunset from Carmel Beach, which is free. The gates are open sunrise to sunset, so start in the late afternoon.

🚗 ~27 km loop plus ~6 km to Carmel, 1 h with stops 💵 $12.50 gate fee per vehicle (check current), beach free 🕐 Enter in late afternoon, before the gates close at sunset
Morning

Bixby Bridge & the start of Big Sur

Fill the tank in Carmel: stations in Big Sur are few and dear. Bixby Bridge is about 20 km south. Monterey County moved in 2026 to limit roadside parking near it, so use a signed turnout, follow the signs and never stop in the lane. Check Caltrans QuickMap before you set off, and download offline maps.

🚗 ~21 km, 30 min from Carmel 💵 Free, fill up first (CA average ~$5.76/gal, Nov 2026) 🕐 Early morning, before the fog and the tour buses
Afternoon

McWay Falls at Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park

Day use at Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park is $10 per car (parks.ca.gov), and the small lot fills up quickly; vehicles over 22 ft cannot park. The waterfall is seen from a short overlook trail, and the beach is closed. State Parks posted fire-related closures here in 2026, so check its alerts page first.

🚗 ~45 km, 1 h from Bixby Bridge 💵 $10 per vehicle, day use 🕐 Early afternoon, the park is open 8 AM to sunset
Evening

Piedras Blancas elephant seals & San Simeon

From McWay it is about 1 h 30 to the Piedras Blancas elephant seal viewpoints north of San Simeon. Gorda has the only fuel for a long stretch, and its pump was reported near $10 a gallon in 2026, so arrive with fuel. Viewing is free from the roadside boardwalk; stay on it. Sleep in Cambria or San Simeon.

🚗 ~95 km, 1 h 30 from McWay Falls 💵 Seal viewing free, motel ~$150-250 (check current) 🕐 Last two hours of daylight
Morning

Hearst Castle tour

Book on hearstcastle.org, up to 60 days ahead. The Grand Rooms Tour is $35 adult and $18 child (5 to 12), including a $5.50 non-refundable fee, per the official page in November 2026. Tours start by bus from the visitor centre, most from 9 AM. The castle is closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day.

🚗 ~15 km, 15 min from Cambria 💵 $35 adult, $18 child (5-12), Grand Rooms Tour 🕐 First tours, around 9 AM, booked ahead
Afternoon

Morro Rock & Morro Bay

Morro Bay is about 50 minutes south on Highway 1 via Cayucos. Morro Rock is a volcanic plug at the harbour mouth, and you can drive to its base car park; climbing it is prohibited (check the State Parks notice). Look for sea otters and harbour seals in the bay. Cayucos Pier is a free stop on the way.

🚗 ~50 km, 50 min from Hearst Castle 💵 Rock car park free (check current) 🕐 Afternoon, when morning fog has burned off
Evening

Morro Bay Embarcadero & dinner

Walk the Embarcadero, the waterfront boardwalk of fish shops, kayak rentals and small restaurants facing the Rock. Evening fog often rolls in off the sea, which makes the harbour quiet and cool, so bring a layer. Order local fish, or buy smoked fish from a dock stall. Motels here are cheaper than in Carmel or Santa Barbara.

🚶 Short walk from the Rock car park, or ~5 min by car 💵 Dinner ~$25-40, motel ~$150-250 (check current) 🕐 Sunset, with a warm layer
Morning

Pismo Beach Pier

Pismo Beach is about 40 minutes south of Morro Bay on Highway 1 and US-101. The pier is free to walk, and clam chowder in a bread bowl is the local order. Street parking near the pier is metered or time-limited, so use a paid lot. The Monarch Butterfly Grove has free viewing from roughly late October to February.

🚗 ~40 km, 40 min from Morro Bay 💵 Pier free, parking ~$5-15 (check current) 🕐 Morning, before the beach fills
Afternoon

Refugio State Beach on the Gaviota Coast

US-101 leaves the freeway feel near Gaviota and runs along an undeveloped stretch of coast. Refugio State Beach, west of Santa Barbara, has palm-lined sand and a state park car park; fees are charged per vehicle (check parks.ca.gov). There is little fuel on this road, so top up in Pismo or Buellton first.

🚗 ~120 km, 1 h 30 from Pismo Beach 💵 Day-use fee per vehicle (check current) 🕐 Mid-afternoon, time for a beach walk
Evening

Stearns Wharf & State Street, Santa Barbara

Santa Barbara is about 35 minutes east of Refugio. Stearns Wharf is a free wooden pier with seafood stalls, and State Street's restaurants are a short walk. The wharf car park charges by the hour with validation from some businesses (check current). Book your hotel ahead: central rooms are among the dearest on this trip.

🚗 ~35 km, 35 min from Refugio 💵 Wharf free, parking ~$10-20, dinner ~$25-45 (check current) 🕐 Sunset, then dinner from about 7 PM
Morning

Old Mission Santa Barbara & the Courthouse tower

Old Mission Santa Barbara, founded in 1786, has twin towers and a garden, and is about 3 km from the centre; entry is a small fee (check current). Then climb the County Courthouse clock tower downtown for free, with views across red roofs to the sea. Check the opening hours, and park in a city lot.

🚗 ~5 km, 10 min between the sights 💵 Mission entry ~$15 (check current), tower free 🕐 Morning, mission and tower open from about 9-10 AM
Afternoon

El Matador State Beach, Malibu

Take US-101 south, then CA-1 past Point Mugu to El Matador State Beach in Malibu. A steep path leads down from a small clifftop car park, and roadside spaces have strict signs, so read them to avoid a ticket. Wear shoes with grip, and keep clear of wet rocks and large waves. Allow about 1.5 hours.

🚗 ~110 km, 1 h 20 from Santa Barbara 💵 Parking varies, read the signs (check current) 🕐 Mid-afternoon, with an hour to spare for the tide
Evening

Malibu Pier & sunset, then Santa Monica

Malibu Pier is about 30 minutes east on the Pacific Coast Highway and is free to walk; the pay car park beside it fills at sunset. Eat at a pier restaurant or save money and continue to Santa Monica, about 25 minutes on, for dinner. Ask your hotel in advance what overnight parking costs.

🚗 ~35 km to the pier, then ~25 km to Santa Monica, 1 h in total 💵 Pier free, parking ~$10-20, hotel parking extra (check current) 🕐 Sunset, then dinner in Santa Monica
Morning

Santa Monica Pier

Santa Monica Pier is free to walk, with a small amusement park whose rides are priced separately. A sign near the entrance marks the end of Route 66, a good place to end this road trip. Park in a beach or city car park, as street parking is short-stay. Mornings are cooler and calmer than afternoons.

🚗 ~5 km, 10 min from a Santa Monica hotel 💵 Pier free, parking ~$15-30 (check current) 🕐 Morning, before the crowds
Afternoon

Venice Beach Boardwalk

The Venice boardwalk is about 4 km south of the pier, a 10-minute drive or a flat 40-minute walk along the beach path. Expect street performers, skate and surf shops and murals. Beach car parks charge a flat fee and street spaces are scarce, so go early. Keep bags with you and nothing visible in the car.

🚗 ~6 km, 15 min, or walk ~4 km on the beach path 💵 Parking ~$15-30 (check current), boardwalk free 🕐 Early afternoon, then leave in good time
Evening

Refuel, return the car & fly home from LAX

Refuel near LAX and keep the receipt, since companies charge a premium if the tank is not full. Return the car to the hire company's lot and take its shuttle to the terminal; allow 45 minutes. Plan to reach the terminal at least 3 hours before an international flight, and check the car for damage with the agent.

🚗 ~20 km, 25-45 min depending on traffic 💵 Fuel receipt, plus any one-way fee already in the booking 🕐 Arrive at LAX at least 3 hours before an international flight

Pacific Coast Highway, California: what to know before you go

💵 Tipping, Taxes & Prices

  • Tip 18 to 20 per cent at sit-down restaurants, about $1 to $2 per drink at a bar and a few dollars for hotel housekeeping or bag handling. Counter service tips are optional.
  • Menu and shelf prices in the US exclude sales tax, which is added at the till, so the bill is higher than the price shown.
  • Hotels add occupancy taxes at checkout, and some add parking or resort fees. Check the total before you book.

🦞 Eating & Drinking on the Coast

  • Try clam chowder in a bread bowl at Santa Cruz, Monterey or Pismo, fish tacos along the whole coast and Santa Maria-style tri-tip barbecue on the Central Coast.
  • The legal drinking age is 21, and an open container of alcohol must not be in the passenger area of a car, so keep bottles in the boot.
  • Wineries in the Santa Barbara and Paso Robles areas charge for tastings, so decide who is driving. Drink-driving limits apply to every driver.

🚗 Driving Etiquette on Highway 1

  • Use the marked turnouts to let faster traffic pass. California law requires slow vehicles with a queue behind them to pull over, so check the DMV driver handbook for the exact rule.
  • Never stop in the travel lane or park on the road for a photo. Use signed vista points, and expect fines at places such as Bixby Bridge.
  • Give cyclists room, and keep a safe distance when passing them. Stop for pedestrians in marked crosswalks.
  • Turn on headlights in fog, and keep to the speed limit on narrow, cliff-edge bends.

⚠️ Road Closures, Break-ins & Cliff Warnings

  • Highway 1 in Big Sur has had long closures after landslides and fires. Check Caltrans QuickMap (quickmap.dot.ca.gov) or call 1-800-427-7623 the morning you drive, and again at lunch. Do not rely on an old map or app.
  • Car break-ins happen at busy viewpoints and beach car parks. Take valuables with you and leave nothing visible in the car, as local police advise.
  • Cliffs, sneaker waves and slippery rocks are risks at McWay Falls, El Matador and other beaches. Follow the posted signs and fences, and stay off wet rocks.
  • Keep your distance from elephant seals and sea lions. Cell signal is limited in Big Sur, so tell someone your plan and download offline maps.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in American English

  • "Gas" (petrol), "freeway" (motorway), "turnout" or "vista point" (a pull-in on the road), "trunk" (boot)
  • "Where can I park?", "Is this lot paid?", "How much for the day?"
  • "Could we get the check, please?" (the bill), "Is service included?" (is the tip added?)
  • "Fill it up, regular" (a full tank of standard fuel), "Do you take cards?"

💳 Money & Payments

  • The US dollar is the currency. Cards and contactless work almost everywhere, including at fuel pumps, car parks and most small shops.
  • Many car parks and some state park lots use pay stations or apps, and a few still take cash, so carry a few dollar bills and coins.
  • Use bank ATMs inside branches and decline dynamic currency conversion. Choose to pay in dollars.

Insider tips for Pacific Coast Highway, California

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🛏️

Sleep in the cheaper towns

Seaside, Marina, Cambria, Morro Bay and Oxnard-area motels are usually well below Carmel, Santa Barbara or Santa Monica prices. Use them as bases, and book the dear towns for one night only.

⛽

Fill up before the gaps, not in them

Fuel in Big Sur and at Gorda can cost far more than in Carmel or Cambria. Top up in Carmel, and again in Cambria or San Simeon, and compare prices on an app before you pay.

🍞

Make lunch a picnic

Supermarkets in Santa Cruz, Carmel, Morro Bay and Santa Barbara sell sandwiches, fruit and drinks. Eat at a vista point or beach and spend the money on one good dinner a day.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌅

Drive the Big Sur stretch before 10 AM

Morning light and quieter turnouts make the cliff road easier to enjoy, and you reach the viewpoints before the fog and the tour buses. Leave Carmel early on Day 3.

🦦

Add a kayak or otter-spotting stop at Morro Bay

The bay is sheltered, and otters and seals are often visible from the Embarcadero. Kayak rentals are an easy way to see them close, and fog tends to lift by midday.

🏛️

Climb the Courthouse tower for free

Santa Barbara County Courthouse has a free clock tower with a view over red roofs to the sea. It is a calmer alternative to paid viewpoints, and it is worth checking the hours before you go.

Travel services for Pacific Coast Highway, California

✈️ Flights

  • Fly into San Francisco (SFO) and out of Los Angeles (LAX): this open-jaw route saves two driving days back to the start.
  • Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC) are alternatives for the start, and Burbank (BUR) or Long Beach (LGB) are alternatives for the finish.
  • Compare the open-jaw fare with a return to one city, because the one-way car fee can cancel the saving.
  • For a flight out from LAX, plan to reach the terminal at least 3 hours before an international departure.

🚙 Car Hire

  • Renters under 25 usually pay a daily young-driver fee. Hertz states a minimum age of 20 in California; check each company.
  • Visitors can drive on a valid home-country licence; carry an International Driving Permit as a translation if your licence is not in English, and check with the hire company.
  • Expect a one-way fee for SFO to LAX unless the company waives it; compare total prices across brands (check current).
  • Compact cars are enough. Automatics are standard in the US, so manual cars are rare and not cheaper.
  • Hire companies often hold a deposit on your card. Read the fuel policy: return full or pay a premium.
  • Check whether your credit card covers collision damage before buying the desk upgrade.

🛣️ Driving Rules & Roads

  • Drive on the right. Speed limits are in miles per hour, and on Highway 1 they are often posted well below the statewide 55 mph for two-lane roads, so follow the signs.
  • Highway 1 in Big Sur has had long closures from landslides and fires, including the Mud Creek slide in 2017, Regent's Slide in 2024 and fire closures in August to September 2026 (Caltrans). Check Caltrans QuickMap the morning you drive, and call 1-800-427-7623.
  • Caltrans listed construction one-way traffic control near Point Sur when this page was researched in November 2026, so allow extra time and keep to the signs.
  • Fuel is scarce through Big Sur, with a few high-priced stations, so fill up in Carmel and again at Cambria or San Simeon.
  • Fog on the coast can cut visibility, so use headlights and slow down. Never stop in the lane for a photo.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Overnight stops: Santa Cruz, Carmel or Monterey, Cambria or San Simeon, Morro Bay, Santa Barbara and Santa Monica.
  • Typical room prices are about $150-250 in Santa Cruz, Cambria and Morro Bay, and $200-350 in Carmel, Santa Barbara and Santa Monica (check current).
  • Seaside and Marina near Monterey, and Oxnard near Malibu, are cheaper bases than the famous towns.
  • Book 2 to 4 months ahead for September and October, and earlier for Big Sur and Carmel.
  • Campers can reserve state park sites, such as Pfeiffer Big Sur, on ReserveCalifornia; the official page says sites fill about six months ahead.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • Get a US eSIM or SIM before you travel, because signal is poor or absent through much of Big Sur.
  • Download offline maps for the whole route; do not rely on live navigation south of Carmel.
  • Car parks mostly use pay stations or apps (ParkMobile or Passport in some cities), so check the signs before you leave the car.
  • There are no tolls on this route, so parking is the main payment to plan for.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Check entry rules on the official US government sites: travel.state.gov for visas, and the official ESTA site if you are eligible for the Visa Waiver Program.
  • Buy travel insurance that covers medical costs, which are high in the US, and car-hire excess.
  • Reserve the Hearst Castle tour, and any state park campground, as far ahead as you can.
  • In the car: water, snacks, a warm layer, a charger, a paper map copy and a torch.

The Pacific Coast Highway route, distances and driving times

This one-way drive starts at San Francisco airport (SFO) and ends at Los Angeles airport (LAX), about 825 km (513 mi) over seven days, on Highway 1 and US-101. Driving south puts the ocean on your right, the passenger side, and puts you in the ocean-side lane, which makes turning into the turnouts easier.

Is 7 days enough for the Pacific Coast Highway from San Francisco to Los Angeles?

Yes, if you accept short stops. The drive is about 825 km, mostly in 1 to 3 hour legs, with the longest day through Big Sur. Five days is possible if you skip Santa Cruz and Pismo, but you lose the slow, enjoyable stops that make the drive worthwhile.

Which direction is better, San Francisco to Los Angeles or the reverse?

Southbound is usually better. In the US the driver sits on the left, so the ocean is on the passenger side southbound, not the driver side. The benefit is that you drive in the ocean-side lane and turn right into turnouts, instead of crossing traffic. Compare one-way fees for both directions.

Does my credit card cover the rental car?

Some cards cover collision damage on the rental if you decline the company's cover and pay with that card, but terms, exclusions and liability cover vary. Ask your card issuer for the written benefit, check it covers California, and photograph the car at pick-up and return.
