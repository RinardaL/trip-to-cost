7 Days in the Canary Islands: Tenerife's Volcanoes to Lanzarote's Lava Fields

Four days on Tenerife around Spain's highest peak, then Lanzarote's lava fields and César Manrique's volcanic architecture. Sunshine all year.

Ideal SeasonYear-round (Oct – Apr best)
Est. Budget$1,200-1,600
Duration7 Days
PaceRelaxed to Moderate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land at Tenerife South & transfer north

Tenerife South is about 80 km from La Laguna, roughly an hour by car on the TF-1 and TF-5 motorways. Bus 110 reaches Santa Cruz in about an hour, and a tram or bus covers the rest. A taxi to La Laguna is nearer €90 to €110 (check current). Collect a rental at the airport if you want the car for Day 2.

🚗 ~1h transfer north 💵 ~€90-110 taxi, or bus 110 to Santa Cruz (check current) 🕐 Plan around your flight's landing time
Afternoon

San Cristóbal de La Laguna's old town

La Laguna's grid of streets, one of the first planned colonial towns, became a model for cities in the Americas and earned its UNESCO listing in 1999. The flat old town takes about two hours to cover. Climb the Iglesia de la Concepción bell tower for rooftop views (small fee, check current), and expect shops to close for a long lunch.

🚶 Compact, walkable old town 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Mid-afternoon, before shops close for siesta
Evening

Santa Cruz de Tenerife by night

Tram line 1 links La Laguna with the harbour in roughly half an hour, so check the last tram before settling into dinner. Walk the flat seafront from Plaza de España to Calatrava's Auditorio de Tenerife, lit at night. Order papas arrugadas, small salty wrinkled potatoes, with red and green mojo sauce.

🚌 ~30 min tram or bus from La Laguna 💵 ~€20 dinner 🕐 Evening, ~8 PM
Morning

Drive up into Teide National Park

From La Laguna the TF-24 forest road climbs through pines, often above the clouds, before dropping into the Las Cañadas caldera at about 2,100 m. Entry to the national park is free. Park near the Roques de García and walk the easy 3.5 km loop, about two hours, and bring a jacket because it is far colder than the coast.

🚗 ~1h drive from La Laguna 💵 ~€30 car rental for the day 🕐 Depart early, before the midday coach tours
Afternoon

Cable car toward Mount Teide's summit

The official return fare is currently about €43 for non-resident adults (check current), and the upper station sits at roughly 3,555 m. Buy online for a fixed slot, as the last ascent is around 4 PM from late October. Wind often halts it. Reaching the 3,718 m summit needs a separate permit booked well ahead through Tenerife ON.

🚡 8 min cable car ride 💵 ~€43 return ticket 🕐 Book the midday slot in advance
Evening

Stargazing under a Starlight-certified sky

Guided telescope sessions typically last about three hours and start around dusk, from about €35. Temperatures above 2,000 m often drop towards freezing outside summer, so bring a warm coat and gloves. Clouds or wind can end a session, so check refund terms. Decide who drives before you start, because the winding road back is dark.

🚗 Stay within the park for dark skies 💵 ~€35 guided stargazing tour 🕐 After sunset, ~9 PM
Morning

Hike the laurisilva forest of Anaga

Start at the Cruz del Carmen visitor centre on the TF-12, where parking is free but can fill by mid-morning. The laurel forest belongs to a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, and its trails are damp and slippery, so wear shoes with grip and carry a rain layer. Mist thickens through the day, so an early start improves your odds of views.

🥾 ~2h round trip from Cruz del Carmen 💵 Free, park entry included 🕐 Morning, before the cloud burns off
Afternoon

Garachico's volcanic rock pools

The 1706 eruption of Trevejo buried the harbour, and the cooled lava now shapes the El Caletón pools. Entry is free, but the rock edges are sharp, so wear water shoes. Heavy Atlantic swell can make the pools unsafe, and water-quality closures have happened, so check the flags and local notices before swimming. Street parking in town is limited.

🚗 ~40 min drive to the north coast 💵 Free to swim 🕐 Early afternoon, calmest water
Evening

Dinner in Puerto de la Cruz

Restaurants in Puerto de la Cruz usually start serving dinner around 7:30 PM. Look for local fish such as vieja or cherne near the old fishing quarter. César Manrique's seawater lido, Lago Martiánez, is worth a stroll along the front. The drive back to La Laguna takes 30 to 40 minutes on the TF-5, so plan who drives.

🚗 ~35 min from Garachico 💵 ~€25 dinner 🕐 Evening, ~7:30 PM
Morning

Playa de las Teresitas

Las Teresitas is about 1.5 km long, with golden sand shipped from the Sahara in the 1970s and a breakwater that keeps the water calm. Loungers cost roughly €5, and weekends bring local families. San Andrés village behind the beach has fish restaurants. Swim only when the flag is green, and leave time for the airport.

🚗 ~15 min from Santa Cruz 💵 Free, sun lounger rental ~€5 🕐 A relaxed late morning
Afternoon

Return the car & head to the airport

Most rental companies have a desk at Tenerife North Airport, but check your contract for the agreed drop-off point and fuel policy. Refuel before returning the car and photograph it as proof of condition. Confirm which Tenerife airport your inter-island flight uses, and carry photo ID, since domestic flights still check it.

🚗 ~40 min to Tenerife North Airport 💵 Included in car rental 🕐 Mid-afternoon, allow 2h before your flight
Evening

Short flight to Lanzarote

Binter Canarias flies from Tenerife North to Lanzarote in under an hour, and tickets are usually cheapest when booked early (check current). These are domestic flights, so there is no passport control, but carry ID. Pick up a rental car at Lanzarote airport, then drive about 35 to 40 minutes south to Playa Blanca.

✈️ ~40 min inter-island flight 💵 ~€60 🕐 Evening departure, arrive in time for dinner
Morning

Ruta de los Volcanes coach tour

The official CACT site now sells Montañas del Fuego tickets online only, for a fixed time slot, at about €30 for adults (check current). Parking and the bus tour are included. The park runs from 9 AM to 3:45 PM and the visit takes around 90 minutes. Arrive 15 minutes before your slot to validate the ticket.

🚌 ~35 min guided coach route 💵 ~€30 (Timanfaya, online slot, check current) 🕐 Arrive at opening, 9 AM
Afternoon

Geyser demonstration & a camel ride

At the Islote de Hilario, staff pour water into a borehole and steam bursts out within seconds, and the demonstration is covered by your park ticket. The Echadero de los Camellos is a short drive away on the LZ-67 and the ride is brief, costing about €12 to €15 (check current). Confirm its days and hours first.

🐫 Camel ride at Echadero de los Camellos 💵 ~€15 camel ride 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Dinner grilled over geothermal heat at El Diablo

César Manrique designed the grill so food cooks over a geothermal pit at the Islote de Hilario. The restaurant serves lunch only, roughly noon to 4 PM (check current), and arriving early helps because seats are hard to reserve. As the park closes in the afternoon, plan this as a late lunch and have dinner in Playa Blanca or Yaiza.

🚗 On site at the park's edge 💵 ~€30 lunch 🕐 Lunch only, ~12-4 PM (check current)
Morning

Jameos del Agua

The lava tube from the Corona volcano holds a lagoon where tiny albino crabs live, found nowhere else but Lanzarote. The official ticket now starts at €17 (check current), the site opens 10 AM to 6 PM, and a visit takes one to two hours. The drive from Playa Blanca is nearer an hour than half an hour.

🚗 ~1h from Playa Blanca 💵 ~€17 entry (check current) 🕐 Right at opening, before tour buses arrive
Afternoon

Mirador del Río or the Jardín de Cactus

Mirador del Río and the Jardín de Cactus each cost about €9 on the official site (check current) and open 10 AM to 5 PM. Allow 45 minutes at the viewpoint and an hour at the garden. They sit roughly 25 to 30 minutes apart, and mist often hides La Graciosa, so pick the garden if cloud hangs low.

🚗 ~25-30 min between the two sites 💵 ~€9 entry each 🕐 Mid-afternoon
Evening

Wine tasting in La Geria

Vines here grow in pits dug into volcanic ash, each sheltered by a crescent wall that catches the night dew, so no irrigation is needed. Malvasía volcánica is the signature white, and a tasting flight usually includes dry and sweet styles. Many bodegas stop tastings around 6 PM (check current), and Spain's limit is 0.5 g/l, so nominate a driver.

🚗 ~20 min drive through the vineyards 💵 ~€15 tasting flight 🕐 Early evening, before dinner
Morning

Playa Blanca or the Papagayo coves

The road to Papagayo is unpaved for a few kilometres, and some rental contracts exclude such tracks, so check your insurance. The €3 toll is per car (check current). Playa Papagayo, Caleta del Congrio and Playa de la Cera differ in crowds and wind. Facilities are limited, so bring water and shade, and arrive early for parking.

🚗 ~15 min from Playa Blanca marina 💵 ~€3 access road toll 🕐 Morning, before the day-trip boats arrive
Afternoon

Last-minute shopping & lunch at the marina

Marina Rubicón has cafés, small shops and a short walk to the old town. Buy wine for the hold bag, because liquids over 100 ml cannot go in cabin baggage, and choose olivine jewellery from a shop with marked prices. Do not take lava stones or sand from the parks and beaches, because removal is prohibited.

🚶 Walkable marina and old town 💵 Varies 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Transfer to Lanzarote Airport

Playa Blanca to Lanzarote Airport is about 35 km via the LZ-2 and takes 35 to 40 minutes. Taxis run roughly €35 to €45 (check current). Refuel before returning a rental car, and allow the full time at the airport, since bag drop typically closes 45 to 60 minutes before departure and connections via Madrid are tight.

🚗 ~35 min to Lanzarote Airport (ACE) 💵 ~€35 taxi 🕐 Evening, allow 2.5h before departure

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service is usually included, so tipping isn't the custom the way it is in the US.
  • Rounding up the bill or leaving a euro or two at restaurants is a nice gesture, not an obligation.
  • Tour guides for Timanfaya or Teide excursions appreciate a small tip if the tour ran well.

👒 Dress Code

  • Casual and beach-ready clothing works almost everywhere, this is one of Europe's most relaxed dress cultures.
  • Bring a proper layer for Teide's summit and evening stargazing, temperatures there drop well below what the coast feels like.
  • Swimwear is fine on the beach promenade in resort towns, but cover up before heading into restaurants or old-town churches.

🚗 Getting Around

  • A rental car is the easiest way to cover both Tenerife's north coast and Lanzarote's spread-out volcanic sites.
  • Public buses (guaguas) connect the main towns on both islands cheaply, though schedules thin out on Sundays.
  • Inter-island flights run frequently and take under an hour, book them a few weeks ahead in high season for the best fares.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Mount Teide's altitude can bring on mild altitude symptoms, headaches or shortness of breath, especially if you go straight up by cable car.
  • Stay on marked paths in Timanfaya, the ground in parts of the park is still hot just centimetres below the surface.
  • Sun strength is stronger than it feels here year-round thanks to the altitude and Atlantic breeze, sunscreen matters even on cloudy days.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Spanish

  • Buenos dias: good morning/hello
  • Muchas gracias: thank you very much
  • La cuenta, por favor: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is the currency, and the Canary Islands have a reduced local tax (IGIC) instead of mainland Spain's VAT, so some prices run slightly lower.
  • Contactless cards are accepted almost everywhere, including small cafes and park entry kiosks.
  • Keep some cash for camel rides, access-road tolls, and rural market stalls.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🚡

Book the Teide cable car online, weeks ahead

Tickets are capped daily and sell out fast in high season, walk-up prices and availability are both worse.

✈️

Book the inter-island flight early

Binter and CanaryFly fares climb quickly closer to departure, especially around winter-sun season.

🍽️

Eat where the guaguas stop

Small local guachinches (informal Tenerife eateries) near bus routes serve the same Canarian classics for a fraction of resort prices.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌲

Sendero de los Sentidos over the main Anaga trail

A short, quieter laurel-forest path built around touch and smell, with a fraction of the crowds of the popular ridgeline routes.

🌋

La Geria's back roads over the main wine route

Smaller family bodegas along the quieter lanes pour the same volcanic-soil wines without a tour bus in sight.

🐋

Whale and dolphin watching off the southwest coast

The channel between Tenerife and La Gomera holds a resident pod of pilot whales visible almost year-round.

✈️ Flights

  • Tenerife South (TFS) handles most international arrivals, while Tenerife North (TFN) is closer to La Laguna and better placed for this itinerary's start.
  • Lanzarote Airport (ACE) sits a short drive from Playa Blanca and Puerto del Carmen.
  • The Tenerife to Lanzarote hop takes under an hour on Binter Canarias or CanaryFly, book a few weeks out for the best fares.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Metered taxis are reliable on both islands, and ride-hailing apps operate in the larger towns like Santa Cruz and Puerto del Carmen.
  • Confirm a fixed fare for longer transfers to Teide or Timanfaya if you skip the rental car.
  • Airport taxi ranks on both islands post fixed-fare tables to the main resort areas.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Highly recommended for this itinerary, both islands' key sights (Teide, Anaga, Timanfaya, La Geria) sit well outside the main towns.
  • Book separate rentals on each island rather than trying to bring one across on the inter-island flight.
  • Roads are well maintained, but the Teide ascent and Anaga's ridgeline lanes are narrow and winding, drive them in daylight.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Tenerife: La Laguna or Puerto de la Cruz put you closest to Teide, Anaga, and the north coast covered on Days 1 to 4.
  • Lanzarote: Playa Blanca is the most convenient base for Timanfaya, La Geria, and the Day 7 beach morning at Papagayo.
  • Book Teide cable car tickets alongside your Tenerife hotel, both sell out at the same times in peak season.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • A direct ferry between Tenerife and Lanzarote isn't practical, most routes connect through Gran Canaria and take most of a day, which is why this itinerary uses a short flight instead.
  • Once on Lanzarote, a boat crossing to the nature reserve island of La Graciosa makes a good half-day add-on if you have spare time.
  • Whale and dolphin-watching boats depart regularly from Tenerife's southwest coast if you want to extend Day 4's beach morning.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • The Canary Islands are part of Spain, so standard Schengen entry rules apply for most visitors.
  • Travel insurance is worth it given the pre-booked Teide cable car and Timanfaya tour slots, most are non-refundable.
  • Pharmacies (farmacias) are easy to find in every town for altitude or sun-related complaints.
