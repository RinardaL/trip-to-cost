4 Days in Salzburg: Old Town, Hohensalzburg Fortress & Wolfgangsee

Wrought-iron shop signs over a cobbled shopping lane, a hilltop fortress reached by funicular, the palace gardens where the Von Trapp children sang, and a glassy Alpine lake an hour beyond the city limits, four unhurried days in Mozart's hometown at the edge of the Austrian Alps.

Ideal SeasonApr – Jun, Sep
Est. Budget$750-1,000/person
Duration4 Days
PaceModerate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival & check-in near the Altstadt

Salzburg Airport W. A. Mozart sits just 3 km from the center, and bus 2 reaches the Altstadt in about 20 minutes, so most visitors are dropping bags well before lunch.

🚌 ~20 min bus from the airport 💵 ~€3 🕐 Plan around your flight's landing time
Afternoon

Getreidegasse's iron signs & Mozart's Birthplace

Salzburg's oldest shopping street bans modern signage, so bakeries and boutiques still hang ornate wrought-iron guild emblems, and the canary-yellow house at Getreidegasse 9 where Mozart was born sits right in the middle of it.

🚶 Central, walkable from most Altstadt hotels 💵 ~€13 Mozart's Birthplace entry 🕐 Early afternoon, before the lane fills with tour groups
Evening

Residenzplatz & a first Austrian dinner

Wander past the Salzburg Cathedral's dome to Residenzplatz, where Central Europe's largest Baroque fountain anchors the square, then settle in for Wiener schnitzel and a local Stiegl beer in the surrounding lanes.

🚶 On Residenzplatz itself 💵 Free to wander, ~€16 dinner 🕐 After 6 PM, once the day heat fades
Morning

Festungsbahn funicular to Hohensalzburg Fortress

A three-minute funicular ride climbs to one of Europe's largest fully preserved castles, and the fortress's state rooms, ramparts, and marionette museum are easiest to see before the courtyards fill with day-trippers.

🚡 Festungsbahn from Festungsgasse 💵 ~€17 funicular + fortress combo 🕐 Right at the 9 AM opening
Afternoon

St. Peter's Cemetery & catacombs

Down from the fortress, St. Peter's Cemetery's wrought-iron grave markers sit beneath the Mönchsberg cliff face, with rock-cut catacombs carved into the rock above that have sheltered monks since the early Christian era.

🚶 ~10 min downhill walk from the fortress 💵 Free cemetery, ~€2 catacombs 🕐 Early afternoon, between fortress and dinner
Evening

Augustiner Bräustübl monastery brewery

Fill your own stoneware mug straight from the wooden barrel at this centuries-old monastery brewery in Mülln, then carry it out to the chestnut-shaded garden for pretzels and self-serve deli stalls, a proper Salzburg evening.

🚶 ~15 min walk or bus 8 from the Altstadt 💵 ~€5 per liter mug, ~€10 food stalls 🕐 After 6 PM, when the garden fills up
Morning

Mirabell Palace & Gardens

The steps around the Pegasus Fountain are the actual Do-Re-Mi steps from The Sound of Music, and the dwarf statues and rose gardens beyond are free to wander with the fortress framed perfectly in the background.

🚶 ~10 min walk from the Altstadt, across the river 💵 Free to enter the gardens 🕐 Mid-morning, before the tour buses arrive
Afternoon

Mozart's Residence on Makartplatz

The Mozart family's second, larger home holds original instruments and family letters, and it's a quieter, less crowded counterpart to the Birthplace museum a bridge away on Getreidegasse.

🚶 On Makartplatz, near Mirabell Gardens 💵 ~€13, or ~€20 combined with the Birthplace 🕐 Early afternoon, after the gardens
Evening

Hellbrunn Palace's trick fountains

A 17th-century archbishop built these Wasserspiele purely to soak unsuspecting guests, and the guided tour still triggers hidden jets from grottoes and stone tables, a genuinely fun, un-stuffy way to close out the day.

🚌 Bus 25 from the Altstadt, ~20 min 💵 ~€15 palace & trick fountains 🕐 Late afternoon into early evening, before closing
Morning

Bus & boat to Wolfgangsee

A regional bus threads through the hills to the lake in about an hour, then a short ferry crossing over Wolfgangsee's glassy water, a quieter, less-visited cousin of nearby Hallstatt, delivers you straight to the village.

🚌⛴️ ~1 hr bus + short lake ferry 💵 ~€14 combined ticket 🕐 Early morning, to make the most of the day
Afternoon

St. Wolfgang village & the White Horse Inn

The famous lakeside White Horse Inn, the setting of a beloved Austrian operetta, anchors a village of pilgrimage-church spires and pastel houses, with a lakeside table well worth the wait for a trout lunch.

🚶 Compact village, walkable in an hour 💵 ~€18 lakeside lunch 🕐 Midday into early afternoon
Evening

Return to Salzburg & departure

The same bus and ferry combination runs back into the city by early evening, leaving enough time to collect luggage and reach either Salzburg Hauptbahnhof or the airport for an onward journey.

🚌⛴️ ~1 hr back to the Altstadt 💵 Included in the day's combined ticket 🕐 Leave at least 2 hrs before an international flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Tipping isn't obligatory, but rounding up or adding 5-10% at restaurants is standard and expected.
  • State the total out loud when paying (e.g. "make it 20") rather than leaving coins on the table.
  • At the Augustiner Bräustübl and other self-serve beer halls, tipping isn't customary since you serve yourself.

👗 Dress Code

  • Salzburg Cathedral and St. Peter's expect shoulders and knees covered, as with most European churches.
  • Evening classical concerts (a Salzburg staple) lean smart-casual to formal; a jacket or dress is safe, though jeans are tolerated at less formal venues.
  • Sturdy, comfortable shoes matter on Days 1, 2, and 3, given the Altstadt's cobblestones and the fortress's inclines.

🚋 Getting Around Salzburg

  • The Altstadt, Mirabell Gardens, and the fortress base are close enough to walk between; buses cover Hellbrunn and Mülln.
  • A single bus ticket costs about €3, while a 24-hour ticket (~€6) pays off on Day 3's multi-stop route.
  • The Salzburg Card (see Travel Services) bundles transit with entry to most sights on this itinerary.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Book Hohensalzburg Fortress and Hellbrunn tickets online ahead in peak season (Jun-Aug); on-site lines can run over an hour.
  • Pickpocketing is rare but not unheard of on the crowded Getreidegasse and around the funicular queue.
  • Jaywalking is frowned upon and can draw a fine, cross at marked crossings even on quiet Altstadt streets.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in German

  • Grüß Gott: hello (the Austrian greeting, more common than "Guten Tag")
  • Danke schön: thank you very much
  • Die Rechnung, bitte: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • Austria uses the euro, and contactless cards are accepted almost everywhere, including buses and small cafes.
  • The Augustiner Bräustübl and some market stalls are cash-preferred, so carry some small bills.
  • ATMs are common throughout the Altstadt; avoid standalone currency-exchange booths, which offer weaker rates than bank ATMs.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎟️

Get the Salzburg Card if you're hitting 3+ sights a day

It bundles the fortress, Hellbrunn, Mozart's Birthplace and Residence, and public transit into one pass, often cheaper than paying for each separately.

🍺

Fill your own mug at the Augustiner Bräustübl

Self-serve monastery beer and bring-your-own-style deli stalls cost a fraction of an Altstadt restaurant tab for the same evening out.

🎫

Combine the two Mozart museums

A joint ticket for the Birthplace and the Residence costs noticeably less than buying entry to each on its own.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌳

Mönchsberg cliff walk instead of the fortress queue

A free hillside path along the Mönchsberg ridge offers Altstadt and fortress views with a fraction of the crowds waiting for the funicular.

🏞️

Wolfgangsee over Hallstatt for a quieter lake day

Wolfgangsee sees far fewer tour buses than its famous neighbor, with the same Salzkammergut scenery and a genuinely relaxed village pace.

🕯️

Getreidegasse at breakfast hour

Before 9 AM, the wrought-iron signs and arcaded courtyards are nearly empty, a completely different lane than the one you'll see by noon.

✈️ Flights

  • Salzburg Airport W. A. Mozart (SZG) sits just 3 km from the center, the closest major airport of any city on this route.
  • Munich Airport, about 2 hours away by train and bus, is a common alternative with more long-haul connections.
  • Bus line 2 runs from the airport terminal directly into the Altstadt in roughly 20 minutes.

🚆 Trains from Munich & Vienna

  • Munich to Salzburg takes about 1.5 hours by direct train, making Salzburg an easy add-on to a Bavaria trip.
  • Vienna to Salzburg runs around 2.5 hours on the fast Railjet service, with frequent daily departures.
  • Salzburg Hauptbahnhof sits a 15-minute walk or short bus ride from the Altstadt.

🎫 The Salzburg Card

  • A 24, 48, or 72-hour card covers public transit plus entry to Hohensalzburg Fortress, Hellbrunn, and most museums on this itinerary.
  • It's worth pricing out against paying per sight, especially on Days 2 and 3 when you're covering the most ground.
  • Cards are sold at the tourist information office, the airport, and most hotel front desks.

🚡 Funicular vs. Hiking to the Fortress

  • The Festungsbahn funicular takes about 3 minutes and includes fortress admission in its combo ticket.
  • A signed footpath also climbs to the fortress in 15-20 minutes for travelers who'd rather walk and skip the funicular fee.
  • Either way, budget at least 2 hours inside once you're up, the state rooms and ramparts take time to see properly.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Altstadt: puts Getreidegasse, the Cathedral, and Day 1's evening within a five-minute walk.
  • Neustadt (near Mirabell): a quieter base close to Mirabell Gardens and Mozart's Residence, an easy bridge crossing to the Altstadt.
  • Near the Hauptbahnhof: convenient for Day 4's train or bus connections onward.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Standard Schengen entry rules apply, so non-EU visitors should confirm visa requirements before booking flights.
  • The euro (EUR) is the local currency, shared with the rest of the eurozone, unlike Prague or Budapest.
  • Travel insurance is a smart add-on given the pre-booked fortress and concert tickets, which are usually non-refundable.
