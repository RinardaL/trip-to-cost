France Itineraries and Trip Costs

10 complete day-by-day trips in France, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 10 France itineraries: Paris, Nice, Strasbourg, Chamonix, Bordeaux, Annecy, Colmar, Valensole, Megève, Mont Saint-Michel. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $480 to $1,950 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Strasbourg at about $190 a day, and the most expensive is Megève at about $400 a day.

Trips run from 3 to 7 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
