5 Days in Madeira, Portugal

Laurel forest levadas, a hike above the clouds between Madeira's highest peaks, and volcanic pools carved into the north coast: five days built around Portugal's dramatic Atlantic island.

Ideal SeasonApr – Oct
Est. Budget$800-1,100
Duration5 Days
PaceActive
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Madeira Airport & transfer to Funchal

Officially Cristiano Ronaldo Madeira International Airport, its short runway is famously built out over the ocean on stilts. Funchal is a quick drive from the terminal.

🚕 ~20 min taxi from the airport 💵 ~€25 🕐 Plan around your flight time
Afternoon

Check in & wander the Zona Velha

Funchal's old town alleys are lined with painted doors, each one a small art project. Ease into the pace before the busier days ahead.

🚶 Walkable in central Funchal 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Mid-afternoon, easy first walk
Evening

First dinner & a poncha tasting

Poncha, Madeira's honey-and-citrus rum drink, started as a fisherman's remedy and is now the island's signature cocktail. Pair it with fresh tuna or espetada skewers.

🚶 Old town restaurants, walkable 💵 ~€25 dinner 🕐 Evening, around 7 to 8 PM
Morning

Mercado dos Lavradores

Madeira's Workers' Market overflows with tropical fruit, flowers, and the catch of the day, including the strange-looking espada (black scabbardfish).

🚶 Walkable in central Funchal 💵 Free to browse, ~€5 fruit tasting 🕐 Early morning, freshest stalls
Afternoon

Cable car to Monte & the Tropical Garden

The gondola climbs almost 600 meters above Funchal's rooftops in under 15 minutes. At the top, Monte Palace Tropical Garden's koi ponds and Asian pavilions are one of the island's best botanical stops.

🚡 ~15 min cable car from Funchal 💵 ~€15 one-way cable car, ~€12.50 garden entry 🕐 Midday, clearest valley views
Evening

Wicker toboggan ride down to Livramento

Two drivers in straw hats steer a wicker sled down the steep streets, a tradition dating back to the 1850s. It's touristy, and everyone should do it once.

🛷 ~10 min toboggan run 💵 ~€30 per sledge (2 people) 🕐 Late afternoon, before the last run
Morning

Transfer to Rabaçal

A scenic drive through mountain tunnels to the trailhead for Levada das 25 Fontes, deep in Madeira's UNESCO-listed laurissilva forest.

🚗 ~50 min drive from Funchal 💵 ~€40 return taxi or tour transfer 🕐 Depart mid-morning
Afternoon

Levada das 25 Fontes hike

The flat, narrow path hugs the water channel through moss-draped laurel forest, ending at a pool fed by two dozen small waterfalls.

🥾 ~3 hr round trip, ~6 km 💵 Free (transfer cost covers access) 🕐 Midday, allow the full afternoon
Evening

Return via Ribeira Brava

A quieter fishing town on the south coast, worth an easy stop and a seafood dinner on the way back to Funchal.

🚗 ~30 min back to Funchal 💵 ~€25 dinner 🕐 Early evening
Morning

Pre-dawn drive to Pico do Arieiro

Madeira's third-highest peak sits at 1,818 meters, and clouds tend to roll in by mid-morning, so an early start matters more here than almost anywhere else on the trip.

🚗 ~45 min drive from Funchal 💵 ~€35 private transfer 🕐 Leave before sunrise
Afternoon

PR1 trail to Pico Ruivo

A dramatic ridge path through hand-cut tunnels and exposed rock ledges connects Madeira's third-highest and highest peaks. Fog and narrow sections make it demanding, not just scenic.

🥾 ~4 hr one-way, ~7 km 💵 Free 🕐 Start by 8 AM, before the clouds
Evening

Pickup at Achada do Teixeira & return to Funchal

Arrange a driver to meet the trail's other end rather than doubling back over the same terrain. A quiet dinner is enough after a full day at altitude.

🚗 ~50 min back to Funchal 💵 ~€35 return transfer 🕐 Confirm pickup time in advance
Morning

Cabo Girão skywalk

A glass-floored platform juts out from Europe's highest sea cliff at 580 meters, with banana terraces clinging to the slope directly below.

🚗 ~20 min drive from Funchal 💵 ~€5 skywalk parking/entry 🕐 Early morning, before tour buses arrive
Afternoon

Porto Moniz natural swimming pools

Volcanic rock pools on the island's northwest tip, filled and refreshed by the Atlantic. Grab lunch at one of the seafront restaurants overlooking the pools.

🚗 ~1 hr drive from Cabo Girão 💵 ~€3 pool entry, ~€20 lunch 🕐 Midday, warmest for swimming
Evening

Transfer to Madeira Airport & departure

The drive back to the airport retraces the coast road, with one last look at the cliffs before the short runway comes into view.

🚗 ~50 min to the airport 💵 ~€45 return transfer 🕐 Leave 2 to 3 hrs before your flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Not obligatory: rounding up or leaving 5 to 10% at sit-down restaurants is appreciated for good service.
  • No tipping expected at cafés, bars, or counter service.
  • Tour and transfer drivers welcome a small tip, but it's not required.

👗 Dress Code

  • Casual, layered clothing works for most of the trip, mild coastal weather during the day, cooler evenings.
  • Proper hiking footwear, broken in and with good grip, is essential for the levada and mountain trails, not optional sneakers.
  • Pack a warm layer even in summer: temperatures at Pico do Arieiro and Pico Ruivo run well below Funchal's.

🚗 Getting Around

  • Madeira's terrain is steep and mountainous, laced with tunnels, so a rental car gives the most flexibility for reaching trailheads and the north coast.
  • Tour buses and shared minivans cover the popular sights and the Pico hike without the stress of mountain driving.
  • Funchal itself is easily walkable, so a car mainly matters for day trips outside the city.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Fog can roll into Pico do Arieiro and Pico Ruivo within minutes, cutting visibility on narrow, exposed sections of the trail.
  • Levada paths run alongside open water channels with no railing in places, so stay on the marked path, especially after rain.
  • Check trail conditions before setting out: sections of both the levada and mountain routes close after heavy rain or landslides.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Portuguese

  • Bom dia: good morning / hello
  • Obrigado / Obrigada: thank you (said by men / women respectively)
  • A conta, por favor: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is used throughout, the same as mainland Portugal.
  • Cards and contactless are accepted almost everywhere in Funchal and at tourist sites.
  • Carry some cash for small cafés, market stalls, and the toboggan run.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🚗

Rent a car for the whole trip

One multi-day rental beats stacking individual taxis and tours once you're covering the mountains and the north coast.

🍽️

Eat at an adega or local tasca

Espetada (beef skewers grilled over wood) at a family-run spot costs a fraction of the marina-front restaurants in Funchal.

🎫

Book the cable car and toboggan together

Combined tickets for the Monte cable car and the wicker toboggan run cost less than buying each separately at the counter.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌲

Fanal Forest

Ancient, wind-twisted laurel trees wrapped in mist, a quieter alternative to the more visited Rabaçal trails.

🥾

Ponta de São Lourenço

The island's arid, dramatic eastern tip looks nothing like the green interior, and most visitors skip it entirely.

🎨

Câmara de Lobos

A working fishing village near Cabo Girão where Winston Churchill once set up his easel to paint the harbor.

✈️ Flights

  • Cristiano Ronaldo Madeira International Airport (FNC) is famous for its short cliffside runway, extended out over the ocean on massive concrete stilts.
  • The airport sits about 20 minutes from central Funchal, an easy taxi or transfer regardless of arrival time.
  • Crosswinds occasionally delay landings, so build a little buffer into tight connections.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Uber and other rideshare apps have a limited presence on the island, mostly around Funchal, so don't count on one for the mountain or north coast days.
  • Regular taxis are easy to hail in Funchal and can be booked in advance for early starts like the Pico do Arieiro hike.
  • Agree a fare or confirm the meter before longer trips outside the city.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Genuinely essential for this itinerary: the levada trailhead, the mountain peaks, and the north coast are all spread across the island, well beyond easy taxi range.
  • Roads climb through frequent tunnels and tight switchbacks, so drive cautiously, use headlights in tunnels, and expect slower average speeds than the map suggests.
  • Book automatic transmission ahead if you need it: manual is far more common in the rental fleet.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Funchal old town (Zona Velha): puts Day 1's evening stroll and dinner right outside the door, with an authentic, walkable base.
  • The hotel zone (Lido): larger resorts with sea-facing pools, a short taxi or bus from the old town's restaurants.
  • Either base works well for the day trips on Days 2 through 5, since transfers start from central Funchal either way.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • No ferry or boat leg is part of this 5-day itinerary; every day trip runs by road.
  • A whale and dolphin watching boat tour out of Funchal marina is a popular optional add-on, with sightings common in the deep waters just offshore.
  • Slot it into a lighter morning rather than swapping out the hike or the levada walk.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Madeira is Portuguese and EU territory, so standard Schengen entry rules apply for the whole trip.
  • Travel insurance is worth it given the amount of hiking on Days 3 and 4, particularly the exposed ridge trail to Pico Ruivo.
  • Weather and temperature at Pico do Arieiro can differ sharply from Funchal at sea level: check the mountain forecast the night before, not just the coastal one.
