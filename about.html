Our story

We got tired of 20 browser tabs too.

TripToCost exists because planning a great trip shouldn't take longer than the trip itself.

Why we built this

Every trip we planned went the same way: one tab for "things to do," another for restaurant recommendations, a third for a packing list someone posted in 2019, and a fourth for a forum thread arguing about tipping etiquette. By the time we'd stitched it all together, we'd spent more hours researching than we would actually spend on the ground.

So we started building the guide we wished existed: one place with a real day-by-day plan, the cultural context to navigate a new place with confidence, a packing list tuned to the actual season and terrain, and the local tricks that usually only come from someone who's already been.

What we believe

A good itinerary isn't a list of top-10 attractions. It's a rhythm: when to go, how long to stay, how to get to the next spot, and what to do when you're tired and just want dinner nearby. That's what every TripToCost guide is built around.

What guides every itinerary

Built on four principles.

🗺️

Actually walkable

Every day is checked for realistic timing and travel distance: no itinerary that looks good on paper but falls apart in real traffic.

🌍

Locally informed

Culture and etiquette notes come from people who've actually lived or traveled extensively in each destination.

🎒

Season-specific

Packing lists are tuned to the actual climate and terrain of your trip dates, not a generic one-size-fits-all list.

💬

Honest about cost

Every guide states a real budget tier upfront: no surprise costs buried three days into the trip.
