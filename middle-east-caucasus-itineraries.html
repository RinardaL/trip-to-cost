What do these eight trips have in common?

This hub runs from Turkey and the Red Sea to the Gulf, the Caucasus and the Arabian Sea. Four trips are in Turkey, and the others are in the United Arab Emirates, Egypt, Georgia and the Yemeni island of Socotra. Each is a fixed plan of four or five days, so the real differences are style, season and cost per day.

Cost per day is the quickest filter. Sharm el-Sheikh is lowest at $100, then Tbilisi & Gudauri and Mardin at $105 and Uludağ & Bursa at $110. Dubai is $145, Istanbul is $165 and Cappadocia is $180, where day two includes a sunrise balloon. Socotra Island is the outlier at $245 a day, because it is a guided camping trip with a driver and cook.

Snorkellers should look at Sharm el-Sheikh, skiers at Tbilisi & Gudauri or Uludağ & Bursa, and anyone wanting a stone old town at Mardin. Socotra Island is for travellers who want remote nature and accept a higher bill.

When is the best time to go?

The region splits into a warm-weather group and a snow group. Dubai and Mardin run from November to March, while Sharm el-Sheikh and Socotra Island run from October to April. Summer is the reason for the gaps: Dubai's average highs reach about 42°C in July and August, and Sharm el-Sheikh's about 35 to 38°C.

Skiing is the opposite case. Tbilisi & Gudauri and Uludağ & Bursa both run from December to March, and each pairs the slopes with a city: Tbilisi's sulphur baths in one, Bursa's mosques and Çekirge baths in the other. The Georgia plan visits Kazbegi only if the road is open, and the trip page says to ski instead if it is shut, so keep that last day flexible.

Cappadocia and Istanbul share milder windows, April to June and September to October. Those months suit long days on foot, from the Göreme valleys to the bazaars. July and August fall outside every window in this hub, so pick a trip by its own window rather than by your holiday dates.

How can you combine trips and keep costs down?

The Turkey trips are the easiest to join, since they share a country. Istanbul and Cappadocia have the same season, so they fit one itinerary without a clash. Dubai and Socotra Island both include November to March in their windows, but check Socotra's current flight routing first, because it has changed recently.

The lowest totals are Sharm el-Sheikh at $300-500 per person and Tbilisi & Gudauri at $300-520 per person. In Tbilisi, the trip page advises booking weekend rooms ahead at the sulphur baths, where private rooms are charged per room, not per person. A fixed daily route also stops you drifting into unplanned taxi rides and tours.

Compare totals, not only daily figures. Cappadocia at $600-850 per person and Istanbul at $520-780 per person look close, yet the first is built around a sunrise balloon and the second around walkable sights. Decide which activity you would pay for before you decide on the trip.

How do you get to Socotra?

Socotra is reached by air, with no overland route through the mainland. Flights are seasonal and the routing has changed recently: Air Arabia's Abu Dhabi service was disrupted in January 2026, and Yemenia has announced a link via Jeddah. Confirm the current route with your operator before booking. The trip page says a licensed local guide, driver and cook meet the group on landing.

Which trip suits a first visit to this region?

Istanbul and Dubai are the most straightforward, because both are city plans with a clear daily route. Istanbul adds Byzantine and Ottoman history, bazaars and a crossing to Asia, while Dubai adds modern towers, old souks and a desert safari. Choose by the kind of sightseeing you prefer.
