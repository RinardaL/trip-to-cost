💵 Tipping Etiquette

  • Standard US tipping applies at sit-down restaurants: 15–20% of the bill.
  • No tipping at park entrances, visitor centers, or shuttle drivers.
  • Tour guides (rafting, jeep trips): 10–15% is customary.

🥾 Dress Code

  • No formal dress rules: practical hiking layers are the norm everywhere.
  • Desert temperature swings hard between day and night: pack layers, not just one outfit.
  • Closed-toe shoes required on most maintained trails; sandals are fine in town only.

🎟️ Park Rules & Permits

  • Angels Landing requires a permit lottery: apply online the day before your hike.
  • Arches and Zion use timed-entry reservations in peak season (Apr–Oct): book weeks ahead.
  • Pets must be leashed and are barred from most backcountry trails.

⚠️ Safety Warnings

  • Flash flood risk in slot canyons and The Narrows: always check the forecast before entering.
  • Carry more water than feels necessary: desert heat dehydrates faster than it feels like.
  • Cell service is spotty between parks: download offline maps before you drive.

🗣️ Trail Talk

  • Trailhead: where a hike officially starts
  • Switchback: a zigzagging turn climbing a steep grade
  • Slot canyon: a narrow, deep canyon carved by water, beautiful but flood-prone

💳 Money & Payments

  • Cards accepted almost everywhere, including most park gift shops.
  • Keep some cash for small-town gas stations off the main highways.
  • Gas up in Springdale, Moab, or bigger towns: stations between parks are sparse and pricier.

✈️ Flights

  • Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) in Las Vegas is the nearest major airport for this route.
  • Pick up your rental car right at the airport and drive straight to Springdale, about 2.5 hours away.
  • Book the rental weeks ahead of peak season, SUVs sell out fast and airport counters run out first.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • This is a rental-car road trip, so taxis and rideshare barely factor in.
  • Uber and Lyft cover short in-town trips in Springdale and Moab, not park-to-park travel.
  • Skip rideshare between parks entirely, coverage is unreliable once you're off the highway.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Non-negotiable for this itinerary: no public transit connects Zion, Bryce, Arches, and Canyonlands.
  • Pick up at the Las Vegas airport rental counters and book early, since SUVs sell out in peak season.
  • Watch for flash flood warnings and sudden weather shifts on the desert highways between parks.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Springdale: right at Zion's entrance, walkable to the shuttle stop and the gear rental shops for the Narrows.
  • Bryce Canyon area: close to the Rim Trail and Sunrise/Sunset Points for the early starts this itinerary needs.
  • Moab: home base for both Arches and Canyonlands, with Main Street dinners and bike rentals for the last morning.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • No ferry or boat leg is needed, this itinerary is entirely inland desert.
  • The closest thing is the optional half-day Colorado River rafting trip out of Moab on day 6.
  • Book the rafting trip ahead if you want it, slots fill up fast during peak season.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Get the $80 America the Beautiful annual pass if visiting 3+ parks, cheaper than paying each $30-35 entrance fee separately.
  • Apply for the Angels Landing permit lottery the day before you plan to hike it.
  • Download offline maps before you leave cell coverage, service is spotty between parks.
