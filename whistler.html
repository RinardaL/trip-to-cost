4 Days in Whistler: Skiing, Peak 2 Peak & Budget Tips

From Vancouver airport up the Sea to Sky Highway to two big mountains: four winter days with advance lift tickets, packed lunches, the Peak 2 Peak Gondola and a Lost Lake morning.

Est. Budget$750-1000

4-day Whistler itinerary

How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at YVR & the Sea to Sky bus

Vancouver airport (YVR) is roughly 130 km from Whistler. YVR Skylynx buses leave from the arrivals area: the Express takes about 2 hours 15 minutes and was listed at C$70 one way (about US$50) for winter 2026/27, with slower standard buses. Epic Rides runs from Burrard Station for C$33.50 (check current).

🚌 ~2 h 15 min by Express bus to Whistler 💵 ~C$70 Skylynx Express, ~C$33.50 Epic Rides from downtown (check current) 🕐 Book a bus that leaves 90 minutes or more after landing
Afternoon

Check-in, Whistler Village & a grocery shop

The Express stops at the Whistler Gateway Loop by the Visitor Centre, a short walk from the Village. Drop your bags, then stroll the car-free Village Stroll. Buy supplies at Fresh St. Market in the Village or IGA at Marketplace: packed lunches avoid on-mountain meals that can reach C$30 (check current).

🚶 Short walk from the bus stop into the Village 💵 ~C$15-25 for groceries for a lunch and snacks 🕐 Shop before the lifts open on Day 2
Evening

Olympic Plaza & a cheap first dinner

Olympic Plaza holds the Vancouver 2010 rings and is free to visit. Take a photo, then eat cheaply: a grocery-store meal or a slice of pizza costs far less than a sit-down restaurant, where mains often run C$25-35 (about US$18-25, check current). Sleep early, since lifts open around 9:00 AM.

🚶 ~5 min on foot from the Village Stroll 💵 Plaza free, ~C$10-35 for dinner 🕐 After dark, then an early night
Morning

Rentals & the Whistler Village Gondola

Book rentals online first: Evo's Village Sports lists about C$80 online and C$100 walk-in per day for skis or a board (check current). Lifts were planned to open at 9:00 AM, so collect gear early. Daily lift tickets are capped and the window price peaks at C$295 (check current), so buy online ahead, then ride the Village Gondola up.

🚡 Whistler Village Gondola from the base up Whistler Mountain 💵 Rental ~C$80-100 per day, lift ticket ~C$187-295 (check current) 🕐 Lifts planned from ~9:00 AM, open until ~3:30 PM (check current)
Afternoon

Roundhouse Lodge & the alpine

The Roundhouse Lodge sits at mid-mountain, with views over the valley and the start of the Peak 2 Peak Gondola. Eat the lunch you packed, or pay lodge prices. Stay on marked runs and check terrain and lift status at whistlerblackcomb.com, because upper lifts close in poor weather. Beginners should stay on the green runs below the lodge.

⛷️ On skis or board within the ski area 💵 Included in the lift ticket, packed lunch free 🕐 Lunch from about 11:30 AM to avoid the rush
Evening

Creekside, après & the bus back

Ski or ride down to Creekside, a quieter base than the Village, with a market and a few cheaper bars. Then take Whistler Transit back to the Village: a single ride was C$2.75 and a DayPASS C$5.50 (check current), and contactless payment works on the fixed bus routes. Eat a simple pasta dinner from the groceries you bought.

🚌 Whistler Transit from Creekside to the Village 💵 ~C$2.75 per ride, DayPASS ~C$5.50 (check current) 🕐 After the lifts close, around 3:30 to 4:00 PM
Morning

Blackcomb Mountain from the Upper Village

Blackcomb is reached from the Upper Village, about 10 minutes on foot from the Village Stroll. Ride the gondola up and check the trail map and conditions on the sign at the top. Blackcomb has long, wide runs, so early morning is good for groomers. Wear layers and sunscreen, since the sun is strong on snow.

🚶 ~10 min on foot to the Upper Village, then a gondola up 💵 Included in the lift ticket 🕐 Start with the first lifts at about 9:00 AM (check current)
Afternoon

The Peak 2 Peak Gondola

The Peak 2 Peak links Blackcomb and Whistler Mountain. The ride is about 11 minutes, covers 4.4 km and hangs up to 436 m above the valley floor. It is included with a winter lift ticket. Non-skiers can buy a separate sightseeing ticket (check current price). Sit by a window for the best views.

🚡 ~11 min between mountains on the gondola 💵 Included with a lift ticket, sightseeing ticket extra (check current) 🕐 Early afternoon, after your morning runs
Evening

Après-ski & the Village at night

The Village is lively after the lifts close. Skip the expensive terrace bars and watch the lights on the Village Stroll, which is free. Ask restaurants about early-evening specials or happy hours, which are often posted on the door (check current). Pasta or soup from the market makes a cheap dinner. Check the next day's forecast before bed.

🚶 On foot in the Village 💵 Free to walk, ~C$10-35 for a meal 🕐 From about 4:00 PM
Morning

Lost Lake snowshoe & Nordic trails

Lost Lake Park is a short walk from the Village. In winter its groomed trails are for snowshoeing and cross-country skiing with a ticket or pass, usually sold at the PassivHaus, and walkers are not allowed on them (check current). For a free alternative, walk the multi-use Valley Trail from the Village instead.

🚶 Short walk from the Village to the PassivHaus 💵 Trail ticket and rentals extra (check current), Valley Trail free 🕐 Trails usually open mid-December to March (check current)
Afternoon

Last lunch, souvenirs & bag storage

Check out and ask your accommodation to store your bags. Buy a last sandwich from Fresh St. Market and browse the Village shops for gifts. Check the bus timetable and book your seat early, because winter buses can sell out around weekends. Keep a warm layer in your day bag for waiting outside.

🚶 On foot around the Village 💵 ~C$10-15 for lunch 🕐 Be back at the bus stop 15 minutes before departure
Evening

Bus to YVR & departure

Take a Skylynx bus back to YVR (about 2 h 15 min by Express, up to 3 hours on standard) and allow for winter delays on Highway 99; check DriveBC that day. For an international flight, reach YVR 4 hours before departure. Epic Rides plus the Canada Line is cheaper.

🚌 ~2 h 15 min by Express, ~3 h by standard bus 💵 ~C$70 Express one way (check current) 🕐 Arrive at YVR 4 hours or more before an international flight

Whistler: what to know before you go

💵 Tipping, Taxes & Service

  • Tipping is expected in Canada: 15 to 20 percent in restaurants and bars, and C$1-2 per drink at a bar. Ski-school instructors, rental techs and bus drivers are tipped less often, but a few dollars is welcome.
  • Menu and shelf prices do not include tax. GST (5 percent) and provincial tax are added at the till, and hotels add further accommodation taxes, so budget a little above the listed price.
  • Service is table-based in restaurants: wait to be seated unless a sign says otherwise. Asking for tap water is free and normal.

🥞 Eating & Drinking in the Mountains

  • Typical comfort food includes poutine (fries, cheese curds and gravy), burgers, chowder and pizza. Craft beer is popular, and many pubs have happy-hour deals in the late afternoon (check current).
  • The legal drinking age in British Columbia is 19, and bars and shops often ask for photo ID. Alcohol is sold at BC Liquor Stores and some private stores.
  • On the mountain, lodges are busy at midday. Eating at 11:00 or after 1:30 saves queueing, and packing a sandwich saves a lot of money.
  • Tap water in Whistler is safe to drink, so carry a refillable bottle.

⛷️ Mountain Etiquette & the Skier Code

  • Follow the Skier Responsibility Code: people downhill have right of way, stop only where you can be seen, and look uphill before you start or merge onto a run.
  • Obey slow zones, closed-run signs and ropes. Closed terrain is closed for a reason, including avalanche risk and snow-making.
  • Lift lines have a merge system: wait your turn and keep your tips together. Carry your skis in one hand on gondolas.
  • Collect your lift ticket or load your pass before the lifts open, because queues are usually longest just before opening.

⚠️ Safety, Scams & Mountain Warnings

  • Stay inside the marked ski area. Avalanches occur outside boundaries, and backcountry terrain needs a guide, training and gear. Check the Avalanche Canada forecast, and never duck ropes or gates.
  • Buy lift tickets only from whistlerblackcomb.com or the resort ticket window. Third-party sellers and resale posts can be fake or cancelled.
  • Winter weather on Highway 99 can close or slow the road. Check DriveBC before you travel, and note that winter tyres or chains are required on this route in the cold season (check current).
  • Check your government's travel advice before you go: travel.gc.ca for Canadians, and the equivalent pages from the US State Department, the UK FCDO or your own country. In an emergency call 911.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Canadian English

  • "Is this the Express bus to Whistler?" and "Which stop is the Village?"
  • "Do you have a youth, child or two-day lift ticket?" and "Where do I pick up my ticket?"
  • "Which lifts are open?" and "Is this a green, blue or black run?" (green is easy, blue is intermediate, black is difficult)
  • "Can I pay by tap?" and "Is tax included?"

💳 Money & Payments

  • Canada uses the Canadian dollar (C$). The rate was roughly C$1 to US$0.72 when this page was written (check current), so a C$70 bus fare is about US$50.
  • Cards and contactless work almost everywhere in Whistler, including buses, lodges and small shops. Keep a little cash for tips and farmers' stalls.
  • Use bank ATMs rather than standalone machines in tourist areas, and choose to be charged in Canadian dollars to avoid poor conversion rates.

Insider tips for Whistler

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎟️

Buy lift tickets early

For 2026/27 the 1-day adult Day Pass was listed from about C$187, against a window price near C$295 at peak (check current). Prices rise on set dates, and daily sales are capped, so buy online as early as you can.

🥪

Pack lunches & self-cater

An on-mountain burger combo can reach about C$30. A sandwich from Fresh St. Market or IGA, plus a refillable bottle, saves C$20 or more a day. Hostels and many budget rooms have kitchens.

🛏️

Stay outside the Village

Hostels such as HI Whistler (Cheakamus Crossing) and Whistler Lodge, or lodging in Function Junction or Squamish, cost much less than Village hotels. Use Whistler Transit (C$2.75 a ride, check current), and in Squamish the Skylynx bus to Whistler is about an hour.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🥾

Walk the Valley Trail

The Valley Trail is a free, mostly flat multi-use path that links lakes and neighbourhoods, and it stays open in winter (check current). Use it for a free morning, or for a walk with a dog, which is not allowed on the Lost Lake ski trails.

🌲

Add Squamish if you have time

If you stay in Squamish or break your return journey there, Shannon Falls is free to visit and the Stawamus Chief rises above town. Check the Sea to Sky Gondola winter hours and prices first.

📅

Go midweek

Weekends and holiday weeks are busier, with fuller buses and higher lodging prices. If your dates are flexible, ski Tuesday to Thursday and avoid Christmas, New Year and mid-February.

Travel services for Whistler

✈️ Flights

  • Vancouver International (YVR) is the gateway, about 130 km from Whistler, and has direct flights from many cities in Europe, Asia, Australia and North America.
  • Allow at least 3 hours for international departures. The Canada Line connects YVR with downtown Vancouver in about 25 minutes (check current fares).
  • Check the Whistler Blackcomb opening dates: the 2026/27 target was November 20, and terrain opens gradually.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Taxis and private transfers are available from YVR, but the trip to Whistler costs far more than the bus. Compare costs with a group of three or four.
  • In Whistler, taxis and limited ridesharing operate (check current), and the Village is easily walkable on foot.
  • Whistler Transit buses are far cheaper for getting between the Village, Creekside and the Upper Village.

🚇 Public Transport & Tickets

  • YVR Skylynx runs buses from the arrivals area: the Express takes about 2 h 15 min and was listed at C$70 one way in winter (check current). Standard buses take about 3 hours.
  • Epic Rides runs from Burrard Station in downtown Vancouver, listing C$33.50 one way and C$44 round trip, with skis and luggage free (check current). Pacific Coach also serves Vancouver and Whistler (check current).
  • Whistler Transit costs about C$2.75 a ride or C$5.50 for a DayPASS (check current), and contactless payment works.
  • Lift tickets are sold at whistlerblackcomb.com and the ticket windows. Winter lifts normally open at about 9:00 AM.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Whistler Village: the most convenient base, with the gondolas on your doorstep, but the highest prices in winter.
  • Creekside: quieter, with the Creekside Gondola nearby and a few cheaper options, plus a regular bus to the Village.
  • Cheakamus Crossing and Function Junction: further from the lifts, but HI Whistler and some apartments offer cheaper beds. Check bus times before you book (check current).
  • Squamish: much cheaper lodging, about an hour from Whistler on the Skylynx bus or by car. Best if you are comfortable with a daily commute.
  • Book early for December to March, and check for the cancellation terms.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • Free Wi-Fi is common in lodges and cafés. Check roaming costs, or use an eSIM or a Canadian prepaid SIM, since coverage is good in the Village.
  • Cards and contactless are accepted almost everywhere, including buses.
  • Download offline maps and the Whistler Blackcomb app before you go up the mountain.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Check entry rules before booking: visa-exempt visitors flying in need an eTA, and US citizens do not (check canada.ca).
  • Buy travel and ski insurance. Mountain rescue and medical treatment are expensive in Canada.
  • You do not need a car. If you drive, winter tyres or chains are required on Highway 99, and the Village parking is expensive.
  • Check the Whistler Blackcomb website and the DriveBC site on the day for lift status, snow reports and road closures.
