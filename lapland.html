5 Days in Finnish Lapland: Christmas in Rovaniemi & the Arctic Circle

Five days at Santa Claus Village and the Arctic Circle: Santa, reindeer and husky sleds, a snow hotel and aurora hunts, with Finnish Christmas traditions.

Ideal SeasonDec – Mar (Christmas & Aurora)
Est. Budget$1,800-2,400
Duration5 Days
PaceActive
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Rovaniemi Airport & transfer to your hotel

Rovaniemi's small Arctic airport sits just outside town, about 4 km from Santa Claus Village. From November to March the private Santa's & Airport Express serves the airport, the city centre and the village, and many hotels run shuttles (check current).

✈️ ~10 min transfer from Rovaniemi Airport 💵 ~€25 🕐 Plan around your flight, daylight is only about 2 to 4 hours in December
Afternoon

Crossing the Arctic Circle & meeting Santa at Santa Claus Village

Santa Claus Village sits on the Arctic Circle, about 8 km from the city centre. Meeting Santa is free of charge at Christmas House (the elves offer photos for a fee, check current), cards posted at Santa Claus' Main Post Office get an Arctic Circle stamp, and certificates for crossing the Arctic Circle are sold at several village shops. Queues can build in the Christmas weeks, so go early in the day.

🚌 City bus 8 from central Rovaniemi runs year-round (~8 km) 💵 Free entry and free to meet Santa; photos, certificates and cards cost extra 🕐 Santa Claus Office listed 8 AM-8 PM daily from 30 Nov 2026 (check current)
Evening

First Northern Lights hunting tour

A guided minibus runs you out past the town's light pollution into genuinely dark countryside for your first shot at the aurora. In the weeks around Christmas the nights are at their longest, which gives you the most hours to try.

🚐 Guided minibus into the dark-sky countryside 💵 ~€120 per person 🕐 9 PM to 1 AM, entirely weather and solar-activity dependent, never guaranteed
Morning

Hotel pickup & husky farm briefing

A short safety briefing and a chance to meet the dogs before you're handed the sled's reins for real. Christmas-week slots are limited, so reserve yours when you book flights (check current availability).

🚐 ~20 min transfer, included in the safari price 💵 Included in the safari price 🕐 Depart by 9 AM to make the most of the brief daylight
Midday

Husky sledding safari through the snowy forest

You drive your own sled behind a team of six to eight huskies along a forest route, by far the most anticipated day of the trip for most visitors. In December the spruces are heavy with snow and the low sun colors the sky pink and blue for much of the run.

🛷 Self-driven team, ~20 km forest route 💵 ~€230 per person 🕐 Late morning start, timed to the day's short real daylight window
Evening

Campfire dinner at the husky farm

Warm up with hot berry juice and a dinner cooked over an open fire in a lakeside kota. Ask whether the farm also serves glögi, the hot spiced Finnish Christmas drink, or gingerbread (piparkakut) in December. Cooking over an open fire, even pancakes in a pan, is a normal winter outing in Finland.

🔥 On-site, lakeside forest kota 💵 ~€35 for the campfire dinner 🕐 Dusk, which falls in mid-afternoon in December
Morning

Reindeer farm visit & sleigh ride

Meet a working reindeer herd, hand-feed lichen, and take a short sleigh ride pulled at a reindeer's unhurried pace.

🚐 ~25 min transfer from Rovaniemi 💵 ~€90 per person 🕐 Late morning, once there's enough light to see the herd clearly
Afternoon

Snowmobile safari

Drive your own snowmobile in a guided convoy across frozen fields and forest trails, an entirely different pace from the huskies.

🛵 Self-drive snowmobile, guided convoy 💵 ~€160 per person (two-seater) 🕐 Early afternoon, before the light fades around 3 PM this far north
Evening

Reindeer-herder storytelling dinner

A kota dinner with a local reindeer herder sharing herding traditions and stories passed down through generations. Reindeer stew is a common Lapland dinner, and in December operators may add glögi or gingerbread (check what is included).

🍽️ Traditional kota or forest hut 💵 ~€55 with dinner 🕐 From 6 PM onward
Morning

Transfer to the Arctic SnowHotel & ice room tour

Walk through rooms carved entirely from snow and ice, rebuilt fresh each winter, including an Ice Restaurant, an Ice Bar serving drinks in ice glasses, and an Ice Chapel. The hotel is in Lehtojärvi and is listed to open on 15 December in the 2026 season (check current), so on an earlier-December trip swap this day for another activity, such as the SantaPark theme park (check its dates).

🚐 ~30 min from Santa Claus Village (Lehtojärvi) 💵 ~€35 self-guided entry (check current) 🕐 Listed 10 AM-8 PM daily from 15 Dec (check current)
Afternoon

Snow sauna & ice chapel

The hotel's snow sauna is a bookable extra, and the Ice Chapel inside the hotel is worth a look even if you skip the sauna. Book the sauna ahead, since it is an add-on, not part of the entry ticket.

🚶 On-site at the snow hotel 💵 Snow sauna is an optional extra, price varies (check current) 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Second Northern Lights hunting tour

A second attempt from a different fell viewpoint further out, since the aurora rewards persistence more than any single night's forecast.

🚐 Guided minibus to a fell viewpoint 💵 ~€130 per person 🕐 10 PM to 2 AM, only runs if skies are clear, entirely weather dependent
Morning

Relaxed breakfast & optional lake ice swim

No early alarm today. A hole cut in the lake ice offers an optional, invigorating dip for anyone who wants the full Finnish experience. If your trip crosses 24 to 26 December, many shops and restaurants are closed, so confirm breakfast and check-out arrangements with your hotel.

🚶 On-site at your hotel or cabin 💵 Free, ~€20 for an optional ice swim (check current) 🕐 Flexible late morning, no early alarm needed
Midday

Traditional Finnish sauna experience

Sauna culture is a genuine national institution in Finland, not a spa add-on, so treat this as one of the trip's essential stops rather than an afterthought. Many Finns count the Christmas Eve sauna among the holiday's most important traditions, so a December sauna here is a taste of the real thing.

🚶 Hotel sauna or a public sauna in town 💵 ~€40 at a public sauna, often included at hotels 🕐 Late morning to early afternoon, before checkout
Afternoon

Transfer to Rovaniemi Airport for departure

The airport transfer is short, but build in buffer time anyway, Arctic winter weather can occasionally slow the roads. Over 24 to 26 December, flights, buses and trains may run reduced schedules, so check your airline and transfer in advance.

🚐 ~10 min transfer to the airport 💵 ~€25 🕐 Leave at least 2 hrs before an international flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service is already included in restaurant prices, so tipping isn't expected the way it is in the US.
  • Rounding up the bill or leaving a euro or two for good service is a nice gesture, not a requirement.
  • Guides on husky, snowmobile, and Northern Lights tours don't expect tips, though it's welcome for exceptional service.

🧥 Dress Code

  • Layering is not optional here: a moisture-wicking base layer, an insulating mid-layer, and a windproof outer shell are the minimum. December in Rovaniemi is typically around -6C to -12C, but cold spells can bring it well below -20C.
  • Cotton holds moisture and chills you fast at these temperatures, stick to wool or synthetic base layers instead.
  • Swimwear rules vary by sauna: tourist saunas and mixed groups often expect it, while traditional single-sex saunas are often used without. Ask when you book, and pack a swimsuit for the sauna and the optional ice swim.

🚐 Getting Around Rovaniemi

  • Rovaniemi's town center is small and walkable, but the husky farm, reindeer farm, snowmobile safari, and snow hotel all sit well outside it.
  • Nearly every activity on this itinerary is booked as an organized tour with hotel pickup and drop-off included, so a rental car isn't necessary.
  • Santa Claus Village is about 8 km from the centre: city bus 8 runs there year-round, and from November to March the private Santa's & Airport Express also serves the village, centre and airport (check current).
  • A handful of local taxis serve the town itself, useful for short hops between the center and outlying hotels.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Frostbite is a real risk on exposed skin in under 15 minutes at the coldest temperatures, keep the face and hands covered on every outdoor excursion.
  • Daylight is short: about 2 hours of direct sun around the winter solstice and about 3 hours 40 minutes on 1 December, so book outdoor excursions for the middle of the day.
  • Sauna etiquette is a serious Finnish cultural institution: shower first, follow the venue's swimwear rules, and keep voices low and unhurried.
  • Snowmobile and husky trails can be genuinely dangerous off the marked route, always follow the guide's line exactly.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Finnish

  • Hyvää päivää: good day, hello
  • Kiitos paljon: thank you very much
  • Lasku, kiitos: the bill, please
  • Hyvää joulua: Merry Christmas
  • Joulupukki: Santa Claus (literally the Christmas goat)

🎄 Christmas in Finland: What to Expect

  • Christmas Eve (24 December) is the main day. Many shops close around noon and stay closed on Christmas Day, and long-distance trains and buses wind down on the evening of the 24th (older Yle guidance, check current).
  • Many families take a sauna on Christmas Eve afternoon, eat ham, casseroles and rice porridge, and Santa (Joulupukki) traditionally visits homes that evening.
  • At noon on 24 December the Declaration of Christmas Peace is read from the balcony of Brinkkala Mansion in Turku's Old Great Square and broadcast nationally. Yle traces the reading to the 1320s. Turku is in southern Finland, so you would watch it on TV or online.
  • Santa Claus Village stays open on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day (Christmas House is listed 8 AM-10 PM on both days in 2026), but check tour operators, restaurants and your transfers for 24 to 26 December.

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is the currency, and contactless cards are accepted almost everywhere, including tour operators and small cafes.
  • Carry a little cash for tips or small souvenir stalls in Santa Claus Village, though it's rarely required.
  • ATMs are common in central Rovaniemi but scarce once you're out at the farms or the snow hotel, withdraw before heading out for the day.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🐕

Book husky and snowmobile safaris direct with the farm

Going straight to the operator instead of through a hotel concierge cuts out a booking markup that can be significant on these higher-priced excursions.

🏨

Stay central for most nights, splurge for one

A central Rovaniemi hotel covers the tour pickups just as well as a wilderness lodge, saving the splurge for the one night at the snow hotel or a glass-roof cabin. Book early if your dates include Christmas week.

🧣

Bring base layers from home

Renting full arctic wear in Rovaniemi is convenient but priced for tourists, packing your own thermal layers cuts that cost considerably.

🎅

Meet Santa for free, skip the extras

Entry to meet Santa at Christmas House is free, so budget only for the elves' photo (a paid extra, check current) if you want one, and for certificates and cards if you want those (check current).

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🌌

Ounasvaara fell over Santa Claus Village crowds

Skip the busier Northern Lights meeting points near the village and head to this fell just outside town for darker skies and far fewer people.

🏛️

Arktikum museum's riverside walk at dusk

A quiet, free way to take in the Arctic light and river ice before committing to a paid evening excursion.

🕐

Ask for an early or late husky slot

Midday tour groups tend to be the largest, an early morning or late-day run is often smaller, calmer, and easier to enjoy at your own pace.

🎄

Visit Santa Claus Village early or after dark

Queues build through the Christmas weeks, so arrive when the Santa Claus Office opens (listed 8 AM from 30 November in 2026) or in the evening, when the village is lit up (check current).

✉️

Post your cards before you leave the village

A mailbox outside Santa Claus' Main Post Office stamps outgoing mail with the Arctic Circle stamp. Delivery times are not guaranteed, so post early and check current postal cut-off dates.

✈️ Flights

  • Rovaniemi Airport (RVN) is a small Arctic gateway with direct seasonal flights from Helsinki and several major European hubs during winter high season.
  • Christmas and New Year are the hardest weeks to book: flights, hotels and excursions sell out early, and some Rovaniemi bookings are non-refundable, so check cancellation terms.
  • Helsinki connections run multiple times a day, a reliable backup if a direct flight from your home city isn't available.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Rideshare apps have very limited presence in a small Arctic town like Rovaniemi, don't count on summoning one at short notice.
  • A handful of local taxi companies operate in town and can be booked by phone, useful for the airport transfer or a short in-town trip. A taxi from the centre to Santa Claus Village is about 8 km, and city bus 8 also runs there year-round.
  • For anything beyond the town center, an organized tour with transport included is the realistic option, not a taxi.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Renting is possible, and some travelers do it, but winter driving conditions, ice, extreme cold, and very short daylight, make it a genuine challenge for anyone unused to Arctic roads.
  • Most visitors book organized tours instead, which include experienced local drivers and transport as part of the package.
  • If you do rent, insist on a vehicle fitted with proper winter tires and check the return policy for icy-road incidents.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Central Rovaniemi: convenient for Day 1's arrival and easy pickups for the Day 2 husky and Day 3 reindeer and snowmobile tours.
  • Arctic SnowHotel: the Day 4 highlight, listed to open on 15 December in 2026 and run to 31 March (check current), and worth a night inside its ice-carved rooms for the novelty alone.
  • Glass-roof cabins in the surrounding forest: a quieter base for the Day 5 wind-down, with aurora views possible right from the bed.
  • Book early for Christmas week, when many hotels and packages are close to full and some bookings are non-refundable (check the terms).

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • This itinerary has no boat or ferry component: Lapland's winter landscape is snow and ice, not open water.
  • The frozen rivers and lakes around Rovaniemi are used for snowmobiles and ice fishing, not boats, during the winter season.
  • If open water appeals, that's a summer Lapland activity, worth considering on a return trip in a different season.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Standard Schengen entry rules apply, so non-EU visitors should confirm visa requirements before booking flights.
  • Travel insurance matters more here than on a typical city break, given the genuinely extreme cold and the remote, activity-heavy days that are hard to reschedule if weather or injury intervenes.
  • Download an aurora forecasting app before you arrive, it makes the difference between guessing and actually knowing when to head out for the night.
  • Expect many shops, restaurants and public transport services to close or run reduced hours on 24 to 26 December, even though Santa Claus Village itself stays open, so confirm transfers and meals in advance.
