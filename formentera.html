4 Days in Formentera: Ses Illetes, Cala Saona & Cap de Barbaria

Fly to Ibiza, ferry to La Savina, then ride between Ses Illetes, Llevant, Cala Saona, Migjorn and the Cap de Barbaria lighthouse: four relaxed beach days.

4-day Formentera itinerary

How many days do you have?

Morning

Ibiza Airport, the Bus to the Port & the Ferry

Formentera has no airport, so fly to Ibiza (IBZ). The Alsa airport bus to the town and port costs about €3.60 and takes 20 to 30 minutes (check current), while a taxi to the port is roughly €20 to €25. Baleària, Trasmapi and Formentera Lines fast ferries reach La Savina in about 30 minutes, around €40 to €56 return.

🚌 Airport bus to the port, then ~30 min by fast ferry to La Savina 💵 Bus ~€3.60, ferry ~€40-56 return (check current) 🕐 Book the ferry ahead in July and August
Afternoon

La Savina: Hiring a Bike or Scooter

Rental shops stand within a few minutes' walk of the ferry at La Savina. Bicycles cost roughly €8 to €12 a day, e-bikes a little more and scooters about €25 to €40 (check current). Helmets are compulsory on scooters, and 125 cc models usually need a licence held for two to three years plus a card deposit of around €200.

🚶 Rental shops a few minutes on foot from the ferry 💵 Bike ~€8-12, scooter ~€25-40 a day (check current) 🕐 Book summer scooters ahead, as rental fleets are capped
Evening

Sant Francesc & a First Dinner

Sant Francesc, the island capital, is about 4 km from La Savina, an easy ride or a trip on the L2 bus. The whitewashed fortified church stands on the main square, ringed by cafés. Walk the side streets for tapas and try peix sec, sun-dried fish. Mains cost about €15 to €25 (check current), so a supermarket picnic saves money.

🚲 ~4 km, about 15-20 min by bike, or the L2 bus 💵 ~€15-25 for dinner 🕐 Early evening, when the square cools down
Morning

Ses Illetes Beach

Ses Illetes is a narrow white-sand spit at the island's northern tip, inside the Ses Salines natural park and about 6 km from La Savina. Ride in for free: cars pay €5 to €7 and motorbikes €3 to €5 (2026 rates, check current), and car access is limited once the car parks fill. Arrive before 10 AM.

🚲 ~6 km from La Savina, about 30 min by bike 💵 Free on foot or bike, car €5-7, moto €3-5 (2026, check current) 🕐 Before 10 AM, vehicle control runs about 9 AM to 8 PM in summer
Afternoon

Llevant Beach & the Trucadors Spit

Llevant is the east side of the same Trucadors spit, with a more open sea and fine sand. The Cavall d'en Borràs and Llevant car parks share the Illetes access control and fill early in summer. Walk north to look across the shallow channel to Espalmador island. Bring food, as beach bars charge high prices.

🚲 A short walk or ride along the spit from Illetes 💵 Free beach, ~€5-8 for a supermarket picnic 🕐 Midday, but bring a parasol: there is little shade
Evening

Cala Saona & the Sunset

Cala Saona is a small sandy cove between red cliffs on the west coast, facing the sunset. It is roughly 12 to 14 km from Ses Illetes via Sant Francesc, so a scooter helps. The free car park near the hotel fills early. Walk to the boathouses, have a drink at the kiosk and bring a light.

🛵 ~12-14 km via Sant Francesc, about 25 min by scooter 💵 Free beach, allow ~€10 for a drink and snack 🕐 Arrive an hour before sunset (about 9 PM in June, 8 PM in late September)
Morning

Platja de Migjorn

Migjorn is a straight beach of about 5 km on the south coast, reached by boardwalks over the dunes. It is free, with a few beach bars (chiringuitos) along the sand and a quieter feel than Ses Illetes. Lock your bike at the access points and keep nothing valuable on the sand. Nudism is common, so choose your spot accordingly.

🚲 Roughly 8-10 km from La Savina via Sant Francesc (check the map) 💵 Free, ~€10-15 for a beach-bar lunch (check current) 🕐 Morning, before the bars and sunshades fill
Afternoon

Es Caló de Sant Agustí

Es Caló de Sant Agustí is a small fishing hamlet on the south coast, with wooden boat sheds, whitewashed houses and a rocky cove for swimming. The L2 bus stops here. Wear water shoes for the rocks, and order peix sec or grilled fish at a local restaurant for roughly €20 to €30 (check current).

🚲 ~10 km from Sant Francesc by bike, or the L2 bus 💵 Swim free, fish lunch ~€20-30 (check current) 🕐 Lunch from about 1:30 PM, then a swim
Evening

Es Pujols Seafront & Dinner

Es Pujols is the island's main resort, on the north-east coast, with a sandy beach, a seafront and plenty of restaurants. It is about 5 km from La Savina by bike or on the L1 bus, which cost about €2.25 each way in 2024 (check current). Compare set menus on the side streets before you sit down.

🚲 Roughly 8 km from Es Caló, about 35 min by bike 💵 ~€20-30 for dinner 🕐 Sunset stroll, dinner from about 8 PM
Morning

Cap de Barbaria Lighthouse

The Cap de Barbaria lighthouse stands on the cliffs of the south-west tip, about 10 km from Sant Francesc. From 15 May to 15 October the last stretch is access-controlled: park before the barrier at the tanca d'Allà Dins (60 car and 100 motorbike places) and go on by foot or bike. Bring water, as there is no shade.

🚲 ~10-12 km from Sant Francesc, the last stretch on foot or by bike 💵 Free on foot or bike (check vehicle fee) 🕐 Go early for cooler air, as sunset is busy
Afternoon

Salt Pans & a Last Swim near La Savina

Return towards La Savina along the road beside the Ses Salines salt pans, where the water can turn pink in warm weather and wading birds feed in the shallows. The area is part of the natural park, so stay on the paths. Return your bike or scooter before the shop closes, buy a last bocadillo and check your ferry time.

🚲 Back to La Savina by bike or scooter 💵 Free, bocadillo ~€5-8 🕐 Return rentals before closing time (check your terms)
Evening

Ferry to Ibiza & the Airport

Take a fast ferry back to Ibiza, about 30 minutes, then the airport bus from the port (about €3.60, 20 to 30 minutes). Book the ferry ahead in summer, and leave Formentera three to four hours before your flight, because queues and delays happen. A taxi to the airport costs roughly €20 to €25 (check current).

⛴️ ~30 min by ferry, then ~20-30 min by bus 💵 Ferry ~€20-28 one way, bus ~€3.60 (check current) 🕐 Leave Formentera 3 to 4 hours before your flight

Formentera: what to know before you go

💶 Tipping, Menus & Coffee

  • Tipping is not expected in Spain, and a service charge is not added to the bill. Rounding up or leaving €1 to €2 for good service at a sit-down meal is plenty, and nothing is expected at beach kiosks.
  • A menú del día is a fixed-price weekday lunch of two courses and a drink, usually cheaper than ordering à la carte. Ask what is on offer, as choices on the island vary by season.
  • Coffee at the bar costs less than at a terrace table, and terraces at the beach and in the busiest squares often charge more. A café con leche is the standard coffee with milk.

🐟 Eating Like a Formenterer

  • Look for peix sec (sun-dried fish served with potatoes), bullit de peix (a fish stew finished with rice) and flaó (a sweet cheese tart flavoured with mint). Fresh grilled fish is sold by weight, so ask the price first.
  • Spanish meal times run late: lunch is usually 1:30 to 3:30 PM and dinner from about 8:30 PM. Many kitchens close between lunch and dinner, so a bocadillo from a bakery covers the gap.
  • Supermarkets in La Savina, Sant Francesc and Es Pujols sell bread, cheese, fruit and water at far lower prices than beach bars. Ask at your accommodation whether the tap water is drinkable.

🌊 Beaches, Nature & Nudism

  • The clear water depends on Posidonia seagrass meadows. Do not take sand, shells or the dried seagrass banks left on the shore, and never anchor a boat on the meadows. Take your rubbish with you.
  • Formentera is a protected landscape. Quads are banned in the Ses Salines natural park, fires and wild camping are not allowed, and the Consell de Formentera posts the current rules at the access points.
  • Naturism is common at Migjorn and parts of Ses Illetes and Llevant. It is accepted behaviour, but do not photograph other people, and keep to the textile areas if you prefer.

⚠️ Safety, Scams & Etiquette Warnings

  • Scooters are the main danger: gravel and sand on the roads, tourists unused to the bike, and heat. Wear your helmet and ride slowly. In an emergency call 112 (the single European number).
  • Photograph your rental vehicle all round before you leave the shop and keep the contract, since damage claims at drop-off are a common complaint. Read the excess and the deposit terms first.
  • Cameras at La Savina, Sant Francesc and Sant Ferran enforce the summer vehicle cap, and unauthorised vehicles are fined. Use a rental firm on the island, or check the current rules on the official formentera.eco site.
  • Do not leave bags or phones on the sand while you swim. For official advice before you go, check your government's travel pages, such as gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/spain or travel.state.gov.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Spanish & Catalan

  • "Hola" (hello), "Buenos días" (good morning; "Bon dia" in Catalan), "Buenas tardes" (good afternoon), "Adiós" (goodbye)
  • "Por favor" (please), "Gracias" (thank you; "Gràcies" in Catalan), "De nada" (you're welcome), "Perdón" (excuse me)
  • "Una mesa para dos, por favor" (a table for two, please), "La cuenta, por favor" (the bill, please)
  • "¿Habla inglés?" (do you speak English?), "¿Dónde está...?" (where is...?), "¿Cuánto cuesta?" (how much is it?)

💳 Money & Payments

  • Spain uses the euro. Cards and contactless work in most hotels, shops and restaurants, but carry some cash for small kiosks, bakeries and rental deposits. ATMs are in La Savina, Sant Francesc and Es Pujols, and are scarce at the beaches.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs and avoid standalone exchange booths. If a terminal offers to charge you in your home currency, choose euros.
  • The Balearic tourist tax (ecotasa) is charged per person per night by your accommodation, with the rate depending on category and season (check current). It is often paid on arrival or added to the final bill, so check what your booking includes.

Insider tips for Formentera

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🚲

Cycle into Ses Illetes for free

Cars pay €5 to €7 a day and motorbikes €3 to €5 at Ses Illetes in 2026, but pedestrians, bicycles and electric vehicles enter free. The ride from La Savina takes about 30 minutes and saves you the queue for parking.

🧺

Picnic from a supermarket

Beach bars charge a premium for a sandwich and a drink. Buy bread, cheese, fruit and water in La Savina or Sant Francesc, and keep a sit-down meal for dinner. A picnic lunch can come in at around €5 to €8.

📅

Travel in May, June or September

Ferry tickets, rooms and vehicle fees are lower outside July and August. Illetes car fees in 2026 rose from €5 to €7 through the peak weeks. The sea is warm from June, and crowds are thinner in the shoulder months.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🏖️

Walk away from the car park

Most visitors stop within a few minutes of the nearest car park or beach bar. Walk 10 to 15 minutes along Migjorn or the Trucadors spit and the sand empties quickly, even in high season.

🗼

Add La Mola on a spare afternoon

The La Mola plateau at the east end of the island has high cliffs and a lighthouse. The L2 bus reaches it, so you can go without a vehicle, and it is a good place to watch the evening light.

🥾

Use the quiet back roads

Formentera is small and mostly flat, with old tracks between the fields and stone walls. Ask the tourist office in La Savina or Sant Francesc for a walking and cycling map, and keep to marked routes in the natural park.

Travel services for Formentera

✈️ Flights

  • Formentera has no airport. Fly to Ibiza (IBZ), about 7 km from Ibiza town and the ferry terminal, then take the ferry to La Savina.
  • Direct flights from many European cities run mostly in the warmer months, and fares are highest in July and August. May, June, September and October are cheaper and quieter.
  • Allow time for the ferry on both days: the crossing is about 30 minutes, but queues and delays happen in summer.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Ibiza taxis run from the airport to the port for roughly €20 to €25, with a surcharge at night (check current).
  • On Formentera, taxis wait at La Savina port and at ranks in the main villages. Ask the price before you set off, and book ahead in July and August.
  • Rideshare apps are limited on the islands, so check the apps for current coverage before relying on them.

🚌 Public Transport & Tickets

  • Baleària, Trasmapi and Formentera Lines run fast ferries from Ibiza to La Savina in about 30 minutes. Fares are around €40 to €56 return (check current), and you can compare operators on their own sites.
  • The L1 bus runs from La Savina to Es Pujols, and the L2 runs from La Savina to La Mola via Sant Francesc, Es Pujols, Sant Ferran and Es Caló. The L3 tourist bus links La Savina and Ses Illetes (check current fares and times at busformentera.com).
  • In winter the service is cut back. In 2026-27 only lines L2 and N6 run until 30 April 2027, so check the summer timetable if you travel in May.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Es Pujols: the main resort, with the most hostals, apartments and restaurants, a good base for a budget-minded stay.
  • Sant Francesc: the central capital, with shops and restaurants, and easy riding to most beaches.
  • La Savina: next to the ferry, practical for arrival and departure, but with limited beaches and a lower-key evening scene.
  • Migjorn and Cala Saona: quieter, with hotels close to the sand, but you will need a bike or scooter for dinner.
  • There are no campsites on the island, and wild camping is not allowed.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • Spain is in the EU, so EU SIM holders roam at home rates. Other visitors can use an eSIM or a prepaid SIM, and most accommodation offers Wi-Fi.
  • Cards work in most places, but keep cash for small kiosks, bakeries and some rental deposits.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs in La Savina, Sant Francesc or Es Pujols and avoid standalone exchange booths.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Check your passport's Schengen rules before booking, including whether ETIAS applies on your travel dates.
  • Vehicle access to the island is capped from 1 June to 30 September (10,287 authorisations in 2026), and rental fleets are limited too. In 2025 the formentera.eco rates for visitors' own vehicles were €6 a day for cars in June and September, €9 in July and August. Check the 2027 rules, since the portal reopens on 20 April 2027.
  • Helmets are compulsory on scooters and motorbikes. For bicycles and e-bikes, helmets are required for under-16s and on roads between towns, and sensible everywhere. You do not need a car: bikes, scooters and buses cover this itinerary.
  • Book accommodation early for July and August, and avoid August if you can, since crowds and prices peak.
