Autumn Escapes

8 complete autumn itineraries: fall colour in the American Southwest and Asia, harvest season in Tuscany, autumn in New York and Beijing, and trekking season in Nepal, with prices per person.

Last updated: November 2026

Autumn is a good time to travel: summer crowds thin out, prices ease and many places look their best. This page collects eight short itineraries that suit September to November, each with the cost stated up front.

Prices are estimates per person for the whole stay, covering lodging, meals, local transport and activities. International flights are not included. Check the ideal season on each itinerary, because peak colour depends on the place and the altitude.

Eight autumn trips and who each one suits

These eight trips are grouped by when they are at their best rather than by where they are. They split into modern cities (Seoul and New York City), road trips (Tuscany, Utah and Zhangjiajie), mountain trips (Sedona and Nepal) and one old capital, Beijing.

Start with budget. Zhangjiajie ($95 per day), Beijing ($105) and Seoul ($120) are the cheapest per day, and Beijing has the lowest total at $300-520 per person. Nepal sits at $150 per day, Utah at $175, Tuscany at $215 and Sedona at $240. New York City shows $375 per day, but it is a two-day plan costing $650-850 per person, so the daily figure reflects a very short stay.

Then choose by style. Seoul is described as a walkable city, while Beijing fits the Forbidden City and the Great Wall into four days. Hikers have Nepal and Sedona, and drivers have Tuscany and Utah.

Timing: when autumn works and when it gets busy

September and October is the shared window for Seoul, Tuscany, Utah, Zhangjiajie and Beijing, and all five also list spring, so a missed autumn can be rebooked for April. Sedona and New York City stretch into November. Nepal is narrower, listed only for October and November, after the monsoon, when trekking views are usually clearest.

Public holidays matter. The Beijing page warns about the Golden Week holiday from 1 to 7 October, when attractions fill and tickets sell out days ahead. The same national holiday applies across China, and the Zhangjiajie page also mentions peak holiday weeks, so expect crowds there too. In Sedona the trip page notes that traffic on Oak Creek Canyon slows at weekends, especially when the trees change colour.

Pack layers. The Nepal page says mornings on the trail and at Poon Hill before sunrise are cold, while midday walking is warm, so you want several thin layers rather than one heavy coat.

Getting around and combining trips

Three of these trips depend on a rental car: Tuscany from Florence or Pisa, Utah from Las Vegas and Sedona from Phoenix. The pages advise booking weeks ahead. In Utah, the Zion Canyon Scenic Drive is closed to private cars while the shuttle runs, generally from March to November, and the shuttle is covered by the park pass, so check current arrangements.

Two pairings make sense. Utah and Sedona are both Southwest road trips from different airports, so price a flight into one and out of the other. Seoul and Beijing are neighbouring capitals that both list September and October, though you should compare the flight fare rather than assume it is cheap.

To stay on budget, treat New York City as a long weekend added to another trip rather than a standalone journey. Zhangjiajie at $380-570 per person and Beijing at $300-520 per person leave room in the budget for a second trip.

How to choose

  • Fall colour: Utah, Sedona, Seoul and Zhangjiajie, and New York City in the autumn. Utah, Sedona, Seoul, Zhangjiajie, New York City
  • Countryside and harvest: Tuscany by car, with hill towns, vineyards and long lunches. Tuscany
  • Clear autumn skies: Nepal, where October and November are the main trekking months, and Beijing, which is at its best from September to October. Nepal, Beijing

Frequently asked questions

How much does an autumn trip cost?

Our 8 itineraries range from about $300 to $1,500 per person for the whole trip, excluding flights.

Which autumn trip is the cheapest?

Beijing ($300-520), Zhangjiajie ($380-570), Seoul ($480-740) are the three lowest on this list, per person and excluding flights.

When is the best time to see autumn colour?

It depends on the place and the altitude, but late September to early November covers most of these destinations. Each itinerary lists its ideal season.

Are these trips also good in other seasons?

Many are. The ideal season on each page shows when the place works best, and several of these cities are also popular in spring.

Which autumn trips fit a long weekend?

New York City is built as a 48-hour trip, and Sedona and Beijing are four-day plans. The rest run five to seven days, so Seoul, Zhangjiajie, Tuscany, Nepal and Utah suit a full week away better than a quick break.

Is Nepal only worth visiting in October and November?

The trip page lists October to November as the ideal season, after the monsoon, when the air is usually clear. The plan includes Kathmandu temples, Pokhara and a Poon Hill trek, so clear weather matters most for the mountain views. Confirm conditions and trail details with your operator before you book.
