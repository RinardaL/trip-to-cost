4 Days in Kopaonik: A Budget Ski Trip in Serbia

Serbia's biggest ski resort on a small budget: the coach from Belgrade, a ski pass in dinars, easy blue runs, the Pančićev Vrh summit, pljeskavica with kajmak and an optional monastery.

4-day Kopaonik itinerary

How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Belgrade & the A1 minibus

Belgrade Nikola Tesla Airport (BEG) is about 290 km from Kopaonik, but it has far more flights than Niš. The A1 minibus runs to Slavija Square via the main railway station for about 400 RSD (about €3.50), paid in cash to the driver (check current). Winter coaches leave the Belgrade bus station, so match your landing time to the timetable.

🚌 ~30 min by A1 minibus into the city 💵 ~400 RSD (~€3.50) A1 minibus (check current) 🕐 Every 20 min by day, hourly after about 19:30 (check current)
Afternoon

The road to Kopaonik: coach, minibus or hire car

The Belgrade bus station (BAS) lists coaches at 07:00 and 08:45, about 5 hours, with a seasonal run from 25 December to 15 March. Fares are up to 2,750 RSD one way (about €23) or 4,090 to 4,250 RSD return (check current). After a later landing, book a minibus, or hire a car: about 3.5 hours, winter tyres required.

🚌 ~5 h by coach, ~3.5 h by car or minibus 💵 Coach ~2,750 RSD (~€23) one way, ~4,100-4,250 RSD (~€35-36) return (check current) 🕐 BAS lists 07:00 and 08:45 daily, so check the timetable first
Evening

Check in at Centar & a first pljeskavica

The resort centre sits on the Ravni Kopaonik plateau at about 1,770 m, with hotels and apartments a short walk from the lifts. If the ticket office is open, buy your chip card (RSD 500) tonight and collect rental boots to skip tomorrow's queue. Dinner: a pljeskavica, a spiced grilled patty, with kajmak and bread.

🚶 Everything in the centre is a short walk 💵 Pljeskavica ~580-1,790 RSD (~€5-15), beer ~320-550 RSD (check current) 🕐 Arrive by dusk, dinner from about 18:00
Morning

Ski pass, rental & the ski kindergarten

Buy passes at the official ticket office or webshop: 15% off in the November presale, 5% after. On the 2025/26 list an adult day pass was RSD 5,600 to 6,500 (about €48 to 55), a 2-day pass RSD 10,300 to 11,800 (check current). Ski hire is about €13 a day. Beginners: try the ski kindergarten.

🚶 Walk to the lifts from most centre hotels 💵 Day pass ~5,600-6,500 RSD (~€48-55), rental ~€13 a day (check current) 🕐 Reach the ticket office early, before the morning queue
Afternoon

Easy blues: Sunčana Dolina, Malo Jezero & Karaman Greben

Skiresort.info lists Sunčana Dolina, Malo Jezero, Krst, Karaman and Karaman Greben among the wide, easy runs, and about 70% of the terrain is blue or green. The Karaman Greben ropeway reaches the easy mid-mountain blues. Check live slope status on skijalistasrbije.rs, because the number of open runs changes through the season.

🚡 Chairlifts from the centre, 5 to 10 min per ride 💵 Included in your pass 🕐 Ski until the lifts close, then stop for a hot drink
Evening

Après-ski, night skiing & hot rakija

Après-ski here is informal: slope-side bars, kuvana rakija (hot spiced brandy) and ćevapi rather than clubs. Night skiing runs on Malo Jezero, Karaman Greben and Pančićev Vrh when the tracks are open, at about RSD 2,400 to 3,000 for a single night ticket (about €20 to 26, check current). A glass of rakija is about 340 to 580 RSD.

🚶 Bars and the night track are close to the centre 💵 Night ticket ~2,400-3,000 RSD (~€20-26), rakija ~340-580 RSD (check current) 🕐 Night skiing only when the tracks are open
Morning

Pančićev Vrh: the summit chairlift

Pančićev Vrh (2,017 m) is the highest point and has a four-seater chairlift. The resort site counts 21 blue, 9 red and 6 black runs across about 55 km, so intermediates have room to move. Go up first while the snow is firm. Wind can close the upper lifts, so check the status before you leave the centre.

🚡 Chairlift from the centre area, ~10 min to the top 💵 Included in your pass 🕐 First lifts, while the snow is firm
Afternoon

Suvo Rudište & the Brzeće gondola

Suvo Rudište is the peak that rings the plateau, with bare, open ground above the forest runs. Gobelja is the free-ride run, so stay on marked pistes unless you have a guide and avalanche gear. The Brzeće gondola climbs to Mali Karaman from the east side, and a single ride is RSD 1,550 (about €13, check current).

🚡 Lifts and runs link from the plateau 💵 Included in your pass, gondola single ride ~1,550 RSD (check current) 🕐 Leave time to ski back before the lifts close
Evening

Serbian dinner: pljeskavica, kajmak & rakija

Order like a local: pljeskavica or ten ćevapi with kajmak, a salty dairy spread, and bread. Resort prices were about 580 to 1,790 RSD for a pljeskavica (about €5 to 15) and 1,300 to 1,720 RSD for ten ćevapi (check current). Finish with a rakija and say "Živeli". Walk a few streets from the main square for lower prices.

🚶 Short walk in the centre 💵 ~€10-25 for a main and a drink (check current) 🕐 Busy 19:00-21:00, book ahead at weekends
Morning

Raška & Studenica Monastery (optional)

Studenica is a UNESCO monastery founded in the late 12th century, roughly an hour from the resort by car or taxi (check a route planner). Entry is usually free, and a guided tour was about 1,200 RSD per group (check current). Hours are roughly 8:00 to 16:00 or 17:00. Cover shoulders and knees. No car? Ski a last morning.

🚗 ~1 h by car or taxi (check a route planner) 💵 Entry usually free, tour ~1,200 RSD per group (check current) 🕐 Go early, allow 1.5 to 2 hours
Afternoon

Sopoćani, or lunch in Raška

Sopoćani is a 13th-century UNESCO monastery near Novi Pazar, known for its frescoes. One listing gives winter hours (November to March) as roughly 8:00 to 16:00 (check current). It adds about an hour, so pick Studenica or Sopoćani, not both. Raška has simple cafés for a cheap lunch before the long drive to Belgrade.

🚗 Short drive from Novi Pazar (check a route planner) 💵 Entry fee not confirmed, carry small cash (check current) 🕐 Winter hours ~8:00-16:00 (check current)
Evening

Back to Belgrade & the airport

Allow about 3.5 hours by car or about 5 hours by coach from Kopaonik to Belgrade, and reach BEG 2 to 3 hours before an international flight. The A1 minibus runs from Slavija to the airport. Winter weather can slow the mountain road. By coach, book a flight after about 21:00, and check the return times at bas.rs.

🚌 ~5 h by coach, ~3.5 h by car, then A1 to BEG 💵 Coach ~2,750 RSD one way, A1 ~400 RSD (check current) 🕐 Leave Kopaonik by early afternoon for an evening flight

Kopaonik: what to know before you go

💶 Tipping & Restaurant Habits

  • Tipping is not compulsory, but leaving about 10% or rounding up the bill is normal in restaurants and cafés. Service is rarely added to the bill, so check the receipt.
  • Say the amount you want to leave when you pay by card, because card terminals do not always have a tip option.
  • Ski cafés on the slopes charge more for coffee and tea than a bar in town, so a hot drink at the base is usually cheaper than one at altitude.

🍖 Eating & Drinking

  • Pljeskavica is a large grilled meat patty, ćevapi are small grilled sausages, and kajmak is a creamy, salty dairy spread served with bread. Soups, beans (prebranac) and stuffed peppers are common in mountain restaurants.
  • Rakija is fruit brandy, usually plum (šljivovica) or grape. At the resort you will also find kuvana rakija, a hot spiced version, and kuvano vino, mulled wine. Say "Živeli" when you clink glasses.
  • Bread arrives with most meals and is sometimes charged separately, so look at the menu if you want to avoid it.

🎿 Slope Etiquette & Ski Pass Rules

  • Single lift ride tickets are for sightseeing only, not skiing. Chip cards cost RSD 500 and a replacement for a lost card costs RSD 500 (check current).
  • Children aged 6 to 11 need a ticket, and children under 6 ski free with an adult on the beginner slopes (check the pass rules).
  • Students with an ISIC or European Youth Card get 10% off on the official list, and discounts do not combine (check current).

⚠️ Safety, Scams & Etiquette Warnings

  • Stay on marked pistes. Off-piste areas such as Gobelja are not patrolled like the groomed runs, so go with a guide and avalanche gear, and tell your hotel where you are going.
  • Mountain weather turns quickly: wind can close upper lifts, and fog cuts visibility. Check the resort status page and wear a helmet, which is advisable for all skiers.
  • In an emergency call 112, the single European emergency number that Serbia uses. Check your government travel advice for Serbia (for example the UK FCDO or the US State Department pages) before you book.
  • Agree a price before getting into an unmarked taxi, and book transfers in advance. Roads to Kopaonik can be icy, and winter tyres are legally required in winter, so confirm that a hire car has them.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Serbian

  • "Dobar dan" (DOH-bar dahn, good day), "Zdravo" (ZDRAH-voh, hi), "Doviđenja" (doh-vee-JEH-nyah, goodbye)
  • "Molim" (MOH-leem, please or you're welcome), "Hvala" (HVAH-lah, thank you), "Izvinite" (eez-VEE-nee-teh, excuse me)
  • "Jedno pivo, molim" (YED-noh PEE-voh, one beer, please), "Račun, molim" (RAH-choon, the bill, please)
  • "Govorite li engleski?" (do you speak English?), "Koliko košta?" (KOH-lee-koh KOSH-tah, how much is it?), "Živeli!" (cheers)

💳 Money & Payments

  • Serbia uses the dinar (RSD), at roughly 117 dinars to the euro. Prices on the mountain are in dinars, although some hotels quote in euros.
  • Cards work at ticket offices, hotels and most restaurants, but bring some cash for small cafés and tips. Withdraw dinars from a bank-branded ATM in Belgrade before heading up, as cash machines on the mountain are fewer (check current).
  • Avoid street exchange booths and choose a bank branch or a licensed exchange office. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your home currency, choose dinars.

Insider tips for Kopaonik

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎟️

Buy the pass online and early

The official webshop gives 15% off in the early-season presale (1 to 15 November) and about 5% for the rest of the season, on the 2025/26 rules (check current). Students with an ISIC card get 10% at the ticket office, but discounts do not combine.

🍞

Eat away from the slope cafés

Slope-side cafés charge noticeably more for coffee, tea and beer than shops and restaurants a short walk from the centre. Pack a sandwich for lunch, or eat a big breakfast and a late lunch in the village.

🏨

Stay in Brzeće or share an apartment

Beds in Brzeće, on the eastern side near the gondola, were listed from about $32 to $65 a night, against roughly $60 to $85 and up for apartments in Kopaonik (check current prices). Sharing an apartment among three or four people cuts the cost further.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

❄️

Try the season opening weekend

Ski Resorts of Serbia announced a season opening for 3 to 6 December 2026, with free skiing on 3 December and 30% off on 4 to 6 December. Only a few runs are open then, so check the conditions and treat it as a bargain, not a full ski week.

🚡

Ride the summit lift without skiing

A single sightseeing ride on the Pančićev Vrh or Karaman Greben lift was RSD 800 for adults on the 2025/26 list (check current). It gives wide views over the plateau, but it cannot be used for skiing.

⛪

Add a monastery before you fly

Studenica and Sopoćani are both UNESCO sites within a short drive of Raška and Novi Pazar. A quiet winter visit costs little and fits a rest morning, if you have a car or can share a taxi.

Travel services for Kopaonik

✈️ Flights

  • Belgrade Nikola Tesla (BEG) is the main airport, about 290 km and 3.5 hours by road from Kopaonik, and the one used by most visitors.
  • Niš (INI) is closer, about 125 km, and is reportedly served by Wizz Air and Ryanair (check current routes). Buses from Niš need a change, and a private transfer is the simplest option.
  • A private car transfer from Belgrade airport is quoted from about €280 per vehicle (check current), which is cheaper split four ways than it sounds.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Belgrade taxis are metered. Book from the airport desk or an app, and agree the fare before setting off if the car has no meter.
  • Ride-hailing apps such as CarGo operate in Serbia (check current), but they do not cover the mountain reliably.
  • Minibus and door-to-door kombi operators run Belgrade to Kopaonik, and prices of about €20 to 30 per person were listed in the past (check current).
  • Pre-book any airport pickup for Niš or Belgrade, because taxi availability in the mountains is limited.

🚌 Public Transport & Tickets

  • The coach from the Belgrade bus station (BAS) takes about 5 hours, with 07:00 and 08:45 departures listed for the 25 December to 15 March season (check current).
  • The A1 minibus connects Belgrade airport to Slavija Square for about 400 RSD, paid in cash (check current). Bus 72 also serves the airport, but fares are inconsistent across sources.
  • In the resort, a ski bus links Vikend naselje and Konaci (no timetable confirmed). Many hotels are within walking distance of the lifts.
  • Your ski pass is a chip card, which costs RSD 500 and works on the lifts, so keep it in a pocket away from your phone.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Kopaonik centre and Konaci: walking distance to the lifts and restaurants, the priciest and busiest base, with apartments listed from about $60 to $85 (check current).
  • Vikend naselje: quieter, with houses and apartments linked to the centre by the ski bus.
  • Brzeće: on the eastern side near the gondola, with some of the cheapest beds, but a drive or lift ride from the centre.
  • Raška: the valley town, a budget option with a bus link, about 28 km from the resort by road via Rudnica.
  • Parking is limited in the centre, so ask your hotel about it before you bring a car.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • Serbia is not in the EU, so EU roaming rules do not apply automatically. Check your operator, or use an eSIM or a prepaid SIM from a Serbian provider.
  • Wi-Fi is common in hotels and apartments, but mobile coverage can be patchy on the upper slopes.
  • Carry some dinars for small cafés, tips and the bus, and use bank-branded ATMs in Belgrade.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Check entry rules for your passport before booking. Many nationalities enter Serbia visa-free for short stays, but rules change.
  • Buy travel insurance that covers skiing, off-piste exclusions and mountain rescue.
  • Check lift status and prices on skijalistasrbije.rs before you travel, and buy your pass on the official webshop.
  • If you hire a car, winter tyres are legally required in winter (check current dates), and snow chains should be in the boot. Garage parking at the resort was RSD 200 an hour or 2,300 a day (check current).
