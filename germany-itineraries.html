Germany Itineraries and Trip Costs

4 complete day-by-day trips in Germany, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 4 Germany itineraries: Bavaria, Berlin, Nuremberg, Dresden. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $460 to $1,400 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Dresden at about $150 a day, and the most expensive is Bavaria at about $200 a day.

Trips run from 4 to 6 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
