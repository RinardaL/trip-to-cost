Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. A plain-language summary of what TripToCost does and doesn't do with your data.

The short version

TripToCost doesn't have user accounts, doesn't use cookies for tracking, and doesn't sell data to anyone, because it doesn't collect any in the first place beyond what's described below.

What's stored, and where

Everything below is stored using your browser's local storage, directly on your device: your saved trips, your packing checklist progress for each itinerary, your selected display currency, and any reviews you write (including the name you type in). None of this ever leaves your browser or is sent to any TripToCost server, and it's not visible to us or to other visitors in any way — a review you write only ever appears in your own browser. Clearing your browser's site data or using a different browser/device will reset all of it.

Third-party services

Pages on this site load a small number of external resources needed to render properly:

  • Destination photography served from Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash
  • Web fonts served from Google Fonts
  • The 3D globe on the homepage uses the Three.js library, loaded from a public CDN
  • Each destination page's live weather widget fetches current conditions from the Open-Meteo API, using only the destination's coordinates (no personal or location data about you is sent)

These providers may log standard technical request data (such as IP address) as part of normal web hosting. This is governed by their own respective privacy policies, not TripToCost's.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly.
