Autumn Escapes

8 complete autumn itineraries: fall colour in the American Southwest and Asia, harvest season in Tuscany and city breaks in Central Europe, with prices per person.

Autumn is a good time to travel: summer crowds thin out, prices ease and many places look their best. This page collects eight short itineraries that suit September to November, each with the cost stated up front.

Prices are estimates per person for the whole stay, covering lodging, meals, local transport and activities. International flights are not included. Check the ideal season on each itinerary, because peak colour depends on the place and the altitude.

How to choose

  • Fall colour: Utah, Sedona, Seoul and Zhangjiajie, and New York City in the autumn. Utah, Sedona, Seoul, Zhangjiajie, New York City
  • Countryside and harvest: Tuscany by car, with hill towns, vineyards and long lunches. Tuscany

Frequently asked questions

How much does an autumn trip cost?

Our 8 itineraries range from about $550 to $1,500 per person for the whole trip, excluding flights.

Which autumn trip is the cheapest?

Prague ($550-750), Budapest ($600-800), New York City ($650-850) are the three lowest on this list, per person and excluding flights.

When is the best time to see autumn colour?

It depends on the place and the altitude, but late September to early November covers most of these destinations. Each itinerary lists its ideal season.

Are these trips also good in other seasons?

Many are. The ideal season on each page shows when the place works best, and several of these cities are also popular in spring.
