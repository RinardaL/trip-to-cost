Spain Itineraries and Trip Costs

10 complete day-by-day trips in Spain, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 10 Spain itineraries: Barcelona, Seville, Canary Islands, Tenerife, Alicante, Gran Canaria, Málaga, Bardenas Reales, Formentera, Setenil de las Bodegas. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $270 to $1,600 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Alicante at about $95 a day, and the most expensive is Canary Islands at about $200 a day.

Trips run from 3 to 7 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
