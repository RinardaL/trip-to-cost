4 Days in Munich: Christmas Markets & the Harry Potter Market

The Marienplatz and Residenz markets, Tollwood and Nymphenburg, plus the free Harry Potter market on the Praterinsel (28 Nov to 6 Dec 2026 only), with a fallback for other dates.

Ideal SeasonLate Nov – Dec 24 (Christmas Markets)
Est. Budget$500-720
Duration4 Days
PaceModerate

Last updated: November 2026

4-day Munich itinerary

How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Munich Airport & the S-Bahn into town

Munich Airport (MUC) is about 30 km north-east of the centre. Take the S1 or S8 from the terminal station: the S8 reaches Marienplatz in about 38 minutes, the S1 in about 48, with trains roughly every 10 minutes combined. A single ticket to zones M to 5 costs about €13 to 15 (check current). Validate paper tickets first.

🚆 ~38 min by S8, ~48 min by S1 to Marienplatz 💵 ~€13-15 single to the airport zone, or a day ticket (check current) 🕐 Trains run about every 10 minutes (check current)
Afternoon

Marienplatz & the Christkindlmarkt

The Christkindlmarkt fills Marienplatz from 20 November to 24 December 2026: Monday to Saturday 10:00 to 21:00, Sunday 10:00 to 20:00, 10:00 to 14:00 on 24 December, and closed on 22 November. Entry is free. Mulled wine usually costs about €5 to 7 plus a mug deposit (check current). The Glockenspiel plays around 11:00 and 12:00 (check current).

🚇 S-Bahn or U3/U6 to Marienplatz 💵 Free entry, mulled wine ~€5-7 plus mug deposit (check current) 🕐 Open 10:00 to 21:00, closed 22 Nov, early close 24 Dec
Evening

Kripperlmarkt, Lebkuchen & a beer hall dinner

The Kripperlmarkt, a stall market for nativity figures, stands by the Alter Peter church and keeps the same dates and hours as the Christkindlmarkt. Then walk about 5 minutes to the Hofbräuhaus on the Platzl for a roast and a Maß: expect roughly €12 to 22 for a main and about €11 to 13 for a litre (check current).

🚶 ~5 min on foot from Marienplatz to the Hofbräuhaus 💵 ~€25-35 for dinner with a beer (check current) 🕐 Dinner from 18:00, book ahead at weekends
Morning

The Frauenkirche & the Viktualienmarkt

The Frauenkirche is free and open 8:00 to 20:00 except during services. The south tower costs €7.50 (Monday to Saturday 10:00 to 17:00, check current). Walk about 5 minutes to the Viktualienmarkt, open Monday to Saturday 8:00 to 20:00 and closed on Sundays, though each stall sets its own hours. A Leberkässemmel or a pretzel makes a cheap lunch.

🚶 ~5 min on foot between the two, both near Marienplatz 💵 Church free, tower €7.50, snacks ~€5-10 (check current) 🕐 Late morning, and note the market is closed on Sundays
Afternoon

Residenz Weihnachtsdorf & the medieval market

The Weihnachtsdorf in the Residenz Kaiserhof runs to 22 December 2026, daily 11:00 to 21:00, with free entry and more than 50 stalls (closed 22 November). Enter from Residenzstraße at Odeonsplatz. Then walk about 5 minutes to the medieval market on Wittelsbacherplatz: 20 November to 23 December, 11:00 to 21:00, free per listings (check current).

🚇 U3 to U6 to Odeonsplatz, then ~5 min on foot between the markets 💵 Free entry, crafts and mulled mead extra 🕐 11:00 to 21:00 daily, closed 22 Nov
Evening

Tollwood Winter Festival on the Theresienwiese

Tollwood runs 24 November to 23 December 2026 on the Theresienwiese. Entry to the grounds and the Markt der Ideen is free, and about 90% of events cost nothing, though some shows need tickets. It opens at 14:00 on weekdays and 11:00 at weekends, with the market closing around 22:30 to 23:30. Take the U4 or U5 to Theresienwiese.

🚇 U4 or U5 to Theresienwiese, ~10 min from Odeonsplatz 💵 Free entry, food ~€10-15 (check current) 🕐 From 14:00 on weekdays, 11:00 at weekends, to about 23:30
Morning

The Englischer Garten & the Chinese Tower market

The Englischer Garten is free and open all day. The Chinese Tower market is listed for 19 November to 23 December 2026, but only 2025 hours (about 11:30 to 20:30) are confirmed, so check current. Reach the tower by bus or a 30 to 40 minute walk from Odeonsplatz via the Hofgarten. The beer garden may be closed in winter.

🚌 Bus from Münchner Freiheit, or ~30-40 min on foot from Odeonsplatz 💵 Park free, mulled wine ~€5-7 (check current) 🕐 Late morning, market hours (check current)
Afternoon

The Deutsches Museum on the Isar

The Deutsches Museum, on an island in the Isar, opens daily 9:00 to 17:00 (last entry about 16:30). Adult tickets are about €16 to 17 (check current). Take tram 17 to Deutsches Museum or the S-Bahn to Isartor. Pick two halls, such as physics, aviation or musical instruments, and allow about 2.5 hours before walking on to the Praterinsel.

🚋 Tram 17 to Deutsches Museum, or S-Bahn to Isartor 💵 ~€16-17 adult ticket (check current) 🕐 Open 9:00 to 17:00, last entry ~16:30
Evening

The Harry Potter Christmas Market on the Praterinsel

This free HBO Max pop-up for its Harry Potter series runs only nine days, 28 November to 6 December 2026. Expect film-style photo backdrops, themed food and about ten stalls (check current). There are no tickets or time slots, but capacity is limited, so expect a queue. Outside those dates, use the Haidhausen market (listed to 24 December).

🚋 Tram 16 or 19 to Maxmonument, or U4/U5 to Lehel, then a short walk (check current) 💵 Free entry, themed food and drink extra 🕐 28 Nov 19:00-22:00; 29 Nov 12:00-22:00; 30 Nov-3 Dec 14:00-22:00; 4-6 Dec 12:00-22:00
Morning

Schloss Nymphenburg & its park

Take tram 17 from the main station to Schloss Nymphenburg, about 20 minutes (check current). In winter the palace alone costs €10 and the combination ticket €16, and the park is free. The palace courtyard market has no confirmed 2026 dates (check current), so the Rotkreuzplatz market (listed 20 November to 23 December, 11:00 to 20:30) is the backup.

🚋 Tram 17 from the main station, ~20 min (check current) 💵 Palace €10, combination ticket €16, park free (check current) 🕐 Winter palace hours are shorter (check current)
Afternoon

Sendlinger Tor market & last gifts

The market at Sendlinger Tor is listed for 20 November to 22 December 2026 (check current). It is about 10 minutes on foot from Marienplatz along Sendlinger Straße, a good street for gifts. Buy Lebkuchen and Christmas biscuits at the market or a bakery, and remember shops close on Sundays. Return your mug for the deposit before you leave.

🚶 ~10 min on foot from Marienplatz, or U1/U2/U3/U6 to Sendlinger Tor 💵 Lebkuchen and gifts ~€10-25 (check current) 🕐 Mid-afternoon, then collect your bags
Evening

Transfer to Munich Airport

Take the S8 or S1 from Marienplatz or the main station: the S8 is quicker from the east side of the centre. Allow about 40 to 50 minutes for the ride, plus 2 to 3 hours for check-in and security (check your airline). A day ticket for zones M to 5 stays valid until 6:00 the next morning.

🚆 ~38-48 min by S8 or S1 💵 ~€13-15 single, or your day ticket (check current) 🕐 Leave 3 to 3.5 hours before your flight

Munich: what to know before you go

💶 Tipping, Service & Mug Deposits

  • Service is included in German prices, but rounding up is normal: about 5 to 10% in restaurants and beer halls, and a euro or so for a drink. Tell the server the total you want to pay when they bring the bill ("Zehn Euro, bitte") instead of leaving coins on the table.
  • Most market stalls charge a deposit (Pfand) on the mug, often about €3 to 5, which you get back when you return it to a stall of the same market (check current). Keep the mug as a souvenir and you lose the deposit.
  • Taxi drivers expect a rounded-up fare or about 5 to 10%.

🍺 Beer Halls, Glühwein & Lebkuchen

  • Beer is served by the Maß (one litre) or the half-litre. Say "Prost" and look people in the eye when you clink glasses. Ask before taking an empty seat, and avoid tables marked "Reserviert" or "Stammtisch" (regulars' table).
  • Glühwein (mulled wine) is the standard market drink at roughly €5 to 7 a mug (check current). Kinderpunsch is the alcohol-free version, and Feuerzangenbowle is a stronger flaming rum punch.
  • Try the Bavarian classics: Weisswurst (traditionally a morning dish), Obatzda cheese, pretzels, Leberkässemmel and roast pork with dumplings. Pretzels brought to your table are charged, usually about €1 to 2.
  • Lebkuchen (spiced gingerbread) and Magenbrot (a spiced biscuit) are the market sweets. Nuremberg is the famous home of Lebkuchen, but Munich stalls sell it too.

🎄 Market Dates, Sundays & Closures

  • The Marienplatz Christkindlmarkt and the Residenz village close on Totensonntag (22 November 2026). Most markets end between 22 and 24 December, and Marienplatz closes at 14:00 on 24 December.
  • The Harry Potter market on the Praterinsel runs only 28 November to 6 December 2026, and its hours differ by day (see Day 3). If your dates fall outside it, swap in the Haidhausen market (listed 20 November to 24 December) and a walk along the Isar.
  • Shops, supermarkets and the Viktualienmarkt close on Sundays, so buy food and gifts on Saturday. Markets and museums stay open. Dates and hours come from city and tourism pages and can change.

⚠️ Safety, Scams & Etiquette Warnings

  • Pickpockets work crowded places: Marienplatz, the main station, the S-Bahn and busy market lanes, especially at weekends. Keep your phone and wallet in a front pocket or a zipped bag, and agree a meeting point in case you lose each other.
  • Ticket inspectors check for validated tickets on the S-Bahn, U-Bahn, trams and buses, and travelling without one risks a fine of about €60 (check current). Validate paper tickets at the blue machines before boarding.
  • Red-surfaced cycle lanes run along many pavements, and cyclists will not slow down. Stay off them. Germans also wait for the green pedestrian light even on empty streets.
  • Check your own government's travel advice for Germany before you go (for example the UK FCDO or the US State Department). The emergency number is 112, and the police number is 110. Pavements can be icy, so wear shoes with grip.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in German

  • "Grüß Gott" (hello, Bavarian), "Servus" (hi or bye, informal), "Guten Tag" (good day), "Tschüss" (bye)
  • "Bitte" (please, or you're welcome), "Danke" (thank you), "Entschuldigung" (excuse me)
  • "Einen Glühwein, bitte" (a mulled wine, please), "Eine Maß, bitte" (a litre of beer, please), "Prost" (cheers), "Zahlen, bitte" (the bill, please)
  • "Sprechen Sie Englisch?" (do you speak English?), "Wo ist...?" (where is...?), "Was kostet das?" (how much is it?)

💳 Money & Payments

  • Germany uses the euro. Cards and contactless work in most hotels, museums and larger restaurants, but many market stalls, small bakeries and some beer garden kiosks take cash only, so carry small notes and coins.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs and avoid standalone exchange booths and ATMs that charge high fees. If a terminal offers to charge you in your home currency, choose euros.
  • Public toilets often charge a small fee, so keep some coins. Tap water is safe to drink.

Insider tips for Munich

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎟️

Share a group day ticket

A single ticket in the city centre (Zone M) costs about €4.20, and a single day ticket €10.10. Groups of 2 to 5 can share a group day ticket: €19.70 for Zone M, or €32.60 for zones M to 5, which includes the airport. Day tickets run until 6:00 the next morning (check current fares).

🆓

Pick the free markets

Entry is free at the Marienplatz Christkindlmarkt, the Residenz village, Tollwood, the Harry Potter market and the Englischer Garten, so most of your spending goes on food. Mulled wine at about €5 to 7 a mug adds up: three rounds cost more than a day ticket.

🥨

Eat lunch at the stalls, dinner in the hall

Viktualienmarkt stalls and bakeries sell a Leberkässemmel, pretzel or sausage roll for a few euros, far cheaper than a sit-down lunch near Marienplatz. Keep the beer hall for dinner, and share a Brotzeit (snack platter) if the portions look big.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🚋

Ride the Christkindltram

The Christkindltram is a decorated tram that tours the city with a Christmas theme. The market organiser lists tickets at €5, sold on site or booked online (check current dates and times).

🌲

Try Haidhausen and the Chinese Tower

The Haidhausen market (listed 20 November to 24 December) sits across the Isar from the Praterinsel and is calmer than Marienplatz. The Chinese Tower market in the Englischer Garten is listed for 19 November to 23 December. Check hours for both.

⏰

Time the Harry Potter market

The pop-up runs only 28 November to 6 December 2026, and capacity is limited. Arrive at the weekday opening (14:00) to avoid the likeliest queues, since weekends and the opening evening (28 November from 19:00) are probably the busiest.

Travel services for Munich

✈️ Flights

  • Munich Airport (MUC) is the main international airport, about 30 km north-east of the centre, with an S-Bahn station under the terminal area. Memmingen (FMM) is a budget alternative, roughly 110 km away, but the transfer is long (check current).
  • The S8 takes about 38 minutes to Marienplatz and the S1 about 48. A single ticket to zones M to 5 costs about €13 to 15, and a group day ticket for zones M to 5 is €32.60 for 2 to 5 people (check current).
  • December is busy, so book flights and hotels early. Winter weather can delay flights, so leave a buffer on the way home.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Munich taxis are ivory or cream coloured, carry a roof sign and use a meter. A taxi from the airport costs several times the S-Bahn fare (check current).
  • Apps such as FreeNow book official taxis, and Uber also operates in Munich. Check the apps for current coverage and prices.
  • Taxi ranks stand outside the main station and near Marienplatz, but queues grow on market evenings, when the U-Bahn is usually quicker.

🚇 Public Transport & Tickets

  • The MVV network covers the S-Bahn, U-Bahn, trams and buses. A single ticket in Zone M costs €4.20, a short-trip ticket €2.10 and a ten-strip ticket €18.70 (check current fares).
  • A single day ticket for Zone M is €10.10, and a group day ticket for 2 to 5 people is €19.70. Day tickets run until 6:00 the next morning. Fares are due to rise on 1 January 2027.
  • Validate paper tickets at the blue machines on platforms or inside trams and buses before travel, or buy in the MVV or MVG app. Unvalidated tickets are treated as invalid.
  • Key lines: S-Bahn S1/S8 for the airport; U4/U5 for Odeonsplatz, the main station, Theresienwiese and Lehel; tram 16 or 19 for Maxmonument; tram 17 for the Deutsches Museum and Nymphenburg.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Altstadt (near Marienplatz): the most walkable base for the markets, but the priciest and the busiest in market season.
  • Maxvorstadt: close to the museums, the Residenz and the main station, with a mix of hotels and a student feel in the evenings.
  • Haidhausen: a village-like district with its own market, bars and the Ostbahnhof S-Bahn, and a short walk from the Praterinsel.
  • Near the main station: practical for airport trains and budget hotels, but the streets can be rough at night, so check recent reviews.
  • Book early for late November and December, because rates rise during the markets and Munich fills up on weekends.

📱 Connectivity & Cash

  • Germany is in the EU, so EU SIM holders roam at home rates. Other visitors can use an eSIM or a local prepaid SIM, and many hotels and cafés offer Wi-Fi.
  • Carry cash for market stalls, small bakeries and public toilets. Cards work at most hotels, museums and restaurants.
  • Use bank-branded ATMs and avoid standalone exchange booths.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Check your passport's Schengen rules before booking, including whether ETIAS applies on your travel dates (check current).
  • The Harry Potter market runs only 28 November to 6 December 2026, and it is run by HBO Max. This plan works on other dates by swapping in the Haidhausen market and an Isar walk. The Munich city site lists the dates, hours and free entry.
  • Most shops close on Sundays, and on 24 and 25 December. Most markets have finished by 24 December, and the Marienplatz market closes at 14:00 that day.
  • You do not need a car. The S-Bahn, U-Bahn and trams cover this itinerary, and the centre is compact and walkable.

Munich Christmas market 2026 dates

The Christkindlmarkt on Marienplatz runs 20 November to 24 December 2026. Monday to Saturday 10:00 to 21:00 and Sunday 10:00 to 20:00, with 10:00 to 14:00 on 24 December. It is closed on 22 November.
