6 Days in Bavaria: Castles, Alps & Beer Halls

Munich beer halls, the fairy-tale castle that inspired Disney, Germany's highest peak, and a turquoise alpine lake, a region-wide Bavaria road trip built around a Munich base and easy drives into the Alps.

Ideal SeasonMay – Jun, Sep
Est. Budget$1,000-1,400
Duration6 Days
PaceModerate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land at Munich Airport & ride the S-Bahn in

Skip the rental car for now, the S1 or S8 train runs straight from the terminal to the city center in about 45 minutes, and Munich's old town is easiest on foot.

🚆 ~45 min S-Bahn to the center 💵 ~€13 one-way ticket 🕐 Trains run every 20 min
Afternoon

Marienplatz & the Glockenspiel show

The Neues Rathaus's carillon acts out a 16th-century tournament and dance at 11 AM and noon (plus 5 PM in summer), with 32 figures and 43 bells.

🚶 Central square, easy to reach on foot 💵 Free to watch 🕐 Shows at 11 AM & noon
Evening

Dinner at the Hofbräuhaus

Munich's most famous beer hall since 1589, all long communal tables, liter steins, oompah music, and pretzels the size of dinner plates.

🚶 5 min walk from Marienplatz 💵 ~€30 dinner & a Maß of beer 🕐 Arrive by 7 PM for a table
Morning

Schloss Nymphenburg

The Wittelsbach family's summer palace, with a canal-lined park and the famous Gallery of Beauties, reachable by tram straight from the center.

🚋 ~20 min on Tram 17 💵 ~€10 palace entry 🕐 Opens 9 AM, quiet before the tour groups
Afternoon

BMW Museum & BMW Welt

The four-cylinder tower and futuristic BMW Welt showroom sit right next to the 1972 Olympic Park, a must for anyone into design or engineering.

🚇 U3 to Olympiazentrum 💵 ~€12 museum entry 🕐 Allow 2-3 hrs for both
Evening

Viktualienmarkt beer garden

This market square's communal beer garden rotates through Munich's six big breweries, and by custom, you're welcome to bring your own picnic as long as you buy your drinks there.

🚶 Central, near Marienplatz 💵 ~€25 dinner 🕐 Best in the early evening sun
Morning

Pick up the rental car & tour Neuschwanstein

King Ludwig II's unfinished 19th-century fantasy castle, the direct inspiration for Disney's Sleeping Beauty Castle. Entry is timed and pre-booked slots sell out weeks ahead in summer.

🚗 ~1 hr 40 min drive to Hohenschwangau 💵 ~€21 castle ticket 🕐 Book the earliest slot you can get
Afternoon

Hohenschwangau Castle

King Ludwig II's boyhood home sits on the opposite hill, a warmer, more lived-in yellow castle where he could watch Neuschwanstein being built from his window.

🚶 10 min walk from Neuschwanstein's base 💵 ~€21 castle ticket 🕐 Combo ticket saves time & money
Evening

Dinner in Füssen's old town

Germany's southernmost town has a compact, pastel-colored old town with a riverside monastery, a relaxed base for the night before the mountains.

🚗 ~10 min drive from Hohenschwangau 💵 ~€30 dinner 🕐 Sunset over the Lech river
Morning

Drive to Grainau & ride the Zugspitzbahn

The Eibsee cable car climbs nearly 2,000 meters in ten minutes to Germany's highest summit at 2,962m, with views into Austria on a clear day.

🚗 ~1 hr drive from Füssen 💵 ~€68 round-trip cable car 🕐 Go early, clouds build by midday
Afternoon

Partnachklamm gorge walk

A carved rock-wall gorge with waterfalls and tunnels, an easy 1-hour walk from central Garmisch that feels far wilder than the short approach suggests.

🚶 ~20 min walk from town 💵 ~€6 gorge entry 🕐 Mid-afternoon, cool inside the canyon
Evening

Dinner on Ludwigstraße

Garmisch's old-town street is lined with painted Lüftlmalerei house facades, a scenic backdrop for a schnitzel dinner after a day in the mountains.

🚶 Walkable old town 💵 ~€28 dinner 🕐 Golden hour on the painted facades
Morning

Drive to Berchtesgaden & the Königssee boat trip

Only quiet electric boats are allowed on this glacial lake to preserve its famous echo. The ride out to the red-domed St. Bartholomä chapel is the highlight of the whole trip for most visitors.

🚗 ~2 hr drive from Garmisch 💵 ~€24 round-trip boat 🕐 Book the early boat, it fills up
Afternoon

Eagle's Nest (Kehlsteinhaus)

A special bus climbs a one-lane mountain road to this former Nazi-era retreat at 1,834m, now a restaurant with the best panoramic view in the Berchtesgadener Land.

🚌 Shuttle bus only, no private cars 💵 ~€27 bus & elevator 🕐 Only open May – October
Evening

Dinner with a view of the Watzmann

Germany's second-highest mountain looms right over the town, a dramatic backdrop for a last alpine dinner of the trip.

🚶 Walkable in central Berchtesgaden 💵 ~€30 dinner 🕐 Alpenglow on the peak at sunset
Morning

Ferry to Herreninsel on the Chiemsee

Bavaria's largest lake sits conveniently on the drive back to Munich. Passenger ferries from Prien cross to Herreninsel in about 15 minutes.

🚗 ~1 hr 15 min drive from Berchtesgaden 💵 ~€10 round-trip ferry 🕐 Ferries run roughly hourly
Afternoon

Herrenchiemsee Palace & drive back to Munich

King Ludwig II's unfinished answer to Versailles sits on its own island, complete with a Hall of Mirrors. Return the ferry, then drive the last stretch back to Munich.

🚗 ~1 hr 15 min to Munich after the ferry 💵 ~€10 palace entry 🕐 Early afternoon, before the drive
Evening

Return the rental car & fly out

Munich Airport has rental return counters right at the terminal, so plan the final drive to land two to three hours before your flight.

🚗 Rental return at the terminal 💵 Rental already paid 🕐 Arrive 2-3 hrs before your flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service is included on the bill, but rounding up 5-10% for restaurants, beer halls, and taxis is standard and expected.
  • Say the total out loud when paying (e.g. "twenty-eight, please" on a €26 bill), rather than leaving cash on the table.
  • At the Hofbräuhaus and other big beer halls, tipping the server per round of drinks is the norm, not one lump sum at the end.

👗 Dress Code & Trachten

  • Everyday dress is casual and practical, layers matter more than style given the changeable Alpine-adjacent weather.
  • Lederhosen and dirndls (Tracht) are festive wear for Oktoberfest and village festivals, not costumes; visitors are welcome to wear them respectfully but shouldn't treat them as novelty items.
  • Cover shoulders and knees for the interiors of Neuschwanstein, Hohenschwangau, and any church visits on this route.

🚗 Getting Around: City Transit vs. the Alps

  • Munich's S-Bahn, U-Bahn, and trams cover the city and Nymphenburg easily, so there's no need for a car until Day 3.
  • Munich's Umweltzone (low-emission zone) requires a green Umweltplakette sticker to drive downtown; rental cars are issued one automatically.
  • Germany's Autobahn has no blanket speed limit on many rural stretches, but mountain roads to Berchtesgaden and Füssen are winding two-lanes, budget more time than the map suggests.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Pickpocketing is a real risk around Marienplatz, the Hofbräuhaus, and the Neuschwanstein ticket queue, the most crowded points on this route.
  • Neuschwanstein tickets are timed and non-transferable; arriving late to your slot can mean losing the tour entirely.
  • Königssee and Chiemsee are cold, deep alpine lakes, swim only at marked, supervised areas.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in German

  • Grüß Gott: the Bavarian hello, used instead of "Guten Tag"
  • Danke schön: thank you very much
  • Zahlen, bitte: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is used throughout Germany; cards and contactless are widely accepted, even at beer halls and museum ticket counters.
  • Keep some cash for smaller beer gardens, mountain hut snack stands, and parking meters in Füssen and Berchtesgaden.
  • Cable car and boat operators (Zugspitzbahn, Königssee) accept cards, but lines move faster with contactless ready.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🎟️

Book Neuschwanstein months ahead

Online tickets cost the same as day-of tickets but guarantee a slot, walk-up tickets in summer often sell out before 10 AM.

🚆

The Bayern-Ticket for a car-free Munich day

A regional day pass covers unlimited regional trains and city transit for up to five people, handy for Day 1 and 2 before you pick up the rental car.

🧺

Bring your own picnic to a Biergarten

At traditional beer gardens with communal tables, it's a protected Bavarian custom to bring your own food as long as you buy your drinks there.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🏞️

Alpsee over the Neuschwanstein crowds

The quiet lake behind Hohenschwangau has a walking path with a classic reflection view of Neuschwanstein and a fraction of the castle courtyard's foot traffic.

💧

Eibsee instead of the summit crowds

The turquoise lake at the base of the Zugspitzbahn is a scenic stop in its own right if the summit queue looks long.

⛪

Ramsau's postcard church

A short detour from Berchtesgaden, the little Ramsau church against the Reiteralpe peaks is one of Bavaria's most photographed, quieter scenes and almost never crowded.

✈️ Flights

  • Munich Airport (MUC) is the gateway for this route, well connected to major hubs in North America, Europe, and Asia.
  • The S-Bahn links the airport directly to the city center, so there's no need to rent a car on arrival day.
  • Book flights that land by early-to-mid afternoon to make the most of Day 1's Marienplatz and Glockenspiel timing.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Munich has metered taxis and limited Uber coverage; both work well for the city days before the rental car pickup.
  • Outside Munich, taxis are scarce, plan around the rental car and public transit rather than expecting a ride on demand.
  • A taxi is handy for a Hofbräuhaus night where nobody wants to worry about walking distance back to the hotel.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Essential for this itinerary from Day 3 onward: no train line covers Neuschwanstein, the Zugspitze valley, and Königssee on one convenient route.
  • Reserve an automatic transmission ahead of time, as with most of Europe, German rental fleets default to manual.
  • Unlike Austria or Switzerland, Germany doesn't require a toll vignette, but confirm your rental has the Umweltplakette sticker for Munich's low-emission zone.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • A central Munich hotel works for Days 1-2, everything is walkable or a short transit ride away.
  • Füssen (Day 3) and Garmisch-Partenkirchen (Day 4) both make convenient overnight bases before and after the mountain activities.
  • Berchtesgaden (Day 5) is worth a full night's stay rather than a day trip, given the drive time back to Munich.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • The Königssee boat to St. Bartholomä runs on quiet electric motors only, a Bavarian rule that keeps the lake's famous echo intact.
  • Chiemsee ferries from Prien to Herreninsel run roughly hourly and take about 15 minutes each way.
  • Both routes sell tickets at the dock, but arriving 20-30 minutes ahead avoids the longest queues in peak season.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Standard Schengen entry rules apply, so non-EU visitors should confirm visa requirements before booking flights.
  • The Bayern-Ticket regional rail pass is worth it for Days 1-2 if you'd rather delay the rental car pickup until Day 3.
  • Travel insurance is worth adding at this budget tier, given the timed castle tickets, cable car, and boat bookings all add up if a day gets disrupted.
