Our story

We got tired of 20 browser tabs too.

TripToCost exists because planning a great trip shouldn't take longer than the trip itself.

Last updated: November 2026

Why we built this

Planning a trip usually means juggling a dozen tabs: things to do, places to eat, what to pack, what is polite locally. TripToCost puts all of it in one place, so planning takes minutes instead of days.

Every guide has a day-by-day plan, a short culture and etiquette guide, a packing list for the season and a clear budget up front. Choose how many days you have, save the trips you like, and tick off your packing list as you go.

What is in every itinerary

TripToCost has 137 short itineraries, from 2 to 7 days, and most are 4 days. Each one starts with the airport arrival and the way into town, and then splits every day into morning, afternoon and evening. Each stop lists how to get there, roughly how long it takes and what it costs.

Around the plan sit the parts that usually send people to extra tabs: a culture and etiquette guide with tipping, food and safety notes, a packing checklist tuned to the season, a few money-saving tricks, and travel services such as transfers and local transport. Trips are also grouped into country and region guides and seasonal lists such as Christmas markets, winter trips and winter sun.

How we research the trips

Each itinerary is built from published sources rather than from reviews: the websites of transport operators, attractions, lift companies and national parks, city and tourism boards, and official government travel advice. We look for the price, the opening hours and the way to get there from the operator itself, and we prefer a source that states the season it applies to.

Where an official figure is not published yet, or two sources disagree, the page says so and marks the figure for you to confirm before you book. For example, ski lift prices for the coming winter are often published late, and Christmas market dates can change. We would rather show a flagged estimate than a confident number we could not check.

How we estimate costs

Every itinerary shows a cost per person for each trip length, from one day to the full plan. The low end is a careful budget and the high end a comfortable version, and both cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities. International flights are not included, because they depend on where you start. The cost calculator turns these ranges into a total for your group, and how we estimate costs explains what the labels mean.

Prices change with the season and the operator, so treat a budget as a planning figure and not a quote. Every page shows a Last updated date, and we change it only when the content changes.

Photos and credits

Most photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under their Creative Commons or public-domain licences. The credits page lists the photographer and licence of each one. A smaller number of photos come from Unsplash. If you spot a photo that does not match the place named beside it, tell us and we will replace it.

What we do not do

We do not sell trips, take bookings or ask for payment. You book your own flights, stays and tickets with whichever provider you prefer, and the guides are free to read, save and follow.

Corrections and contact

Prices, hours and timetables go out of date, and we would like to know when they do. If something on a page is wrong, or you want a destination covered, email triptocost@gmail.com with the page and what you found, and we will check it and update the guide.

What guides every itinerary

Built on four principles.

🗺️

Actually walkable

Every stop lists how to get there and roughly how long it takes, so a day is planned around real travel times and not just a list of attractions.

🌍

Source-based

Prices, hours and etiquette notes come from operator, tourism-board and official travel-advice pages, and anything we could not confirm is marked.

🎒

Season-specific

Packing lists are tuned to the actual climate and terrain of your trip dates, not a generic one-size-fits-all list.

💬

Honest about cost

Every guide states a real budget tier upfront: no surprise costs buried three days into the trip.
