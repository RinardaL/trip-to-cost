Italy Itineraries and Trip Costs

9 complete day-by-day trips in Italy, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 9 Italy itineraries: Amalfi Coast, Milan, Tuscany, Cortina d'Ampezzo, Val Gardena, Puglia, Rome, Livigno, Val Gardena. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $540 to $1,750 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Livigno at about $165 a day, and the most expensive is Cortina d'Ampezzo at about $275 a day.

Trips run from 4 to 7 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
