Christmas Markets & Festive Trips

Complete day-by-day itineraries for 14 Christmas destinations, from classic German and Alsace market towns to Lapland and Arctic Norway, with the price up front.

Most European Christmas markets open in the last week of November and close on or just before 24 December, and a few run into early January. Opening dates, hours and entry fees change every year, so each itinerary lists the dates we could confirm and marks the rest as "check current".

Every guide is built for a short trip: you land at the airport, get to the centre, see the main market in the evening and the sights by day. Prices are estimates per person for the whole stay, covering lodging, meals, local transport and activities. International flights are not included.

Frequently asked questions

When do Christmas markets open in 2026?

Most open in the last week of November and close around 24 December, and a few continue into January. Exact dates differ by city and are given on each itinerary where we could confirm them.

How much does a Christmas market trip cost?

Our 14 itineraries range from about $460 to $2,400 per person for the whole trip, depending on the city and the length. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities; flights are not included.

Which Christmas trip is the cheapest?

Dresden ($460-720), Prague ($460-730), Nuremberg ($480-740) are the three lowest on this list, per person and excluding flights.

How many days do I need?

Most of these itineraries are 4 or 5 days, enough for the main market, the sights and a day trip. Each page has a day picker with a price for every trip length from one day up to the full plan.

Can I use these guides outside December?

Mostly yes. Each itinerary lists its ideal season, and for most cities the day-by-day plan also works in other months when the markets are closed.
