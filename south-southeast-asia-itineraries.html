What does South and Southeast Asia cover here?

This hub groups seven trips across Nepal, Indonesia, Singapore, Laos and Thailand, with Thailand appearing three times, and each has a clear price per person up front. They differ mostly in landscape: Nepal is mountains and trekking, Bali is a mix of rice terraces and coast, Singapore is a modern city, Luang Prabang is a river town with waterfalls, and Chiang Mai is an old city of temples.

Luang Prabang is the cheapest by a distance, at $85 a day and $270-410 per person for four days. Bali follows at $120 a day, then Bangkok & Andaman Coast at $135. Nepal, Bangkok & Phuket and Chiang Mai are each $150 a day, and Singapore is the dearest at $195 a day.

Choose Bali or one of the Thailand beach trips for coast, Nepal if you want a short trek, Luang Prabang for the lowest cost, and Singapore for a compact city stay.

How do the seasons compare?

Most of these trips sit in the cooler, drier months. Luang Prabang runs from November to February, the Phuket trip from November to February, and the Andaman Coast trip from November to March. Nepal is narrower, October to November, which follows the end of the summer monsoon and suits the Poon Hill trek.

Chiang Mai is tied to the Yi Peng lantern festival in November. It follows the Lanna lunar calendar, so the dates shift every year, and you should confirm them before booking flights. Bali has the widest window, April to October, which broadly matches its dry season. Singapore can be visited in any month. The trip lists February to April as the driest stretch, and Singapore's National Environment Agency describes a drier phase of the Northeast Monsoon from about February to March. November is the month that works for six of the seven trips, with Bali the only exception.

How do you combine them and plan the details?

The two Thai beach trips overlap: both begin in Bangkok, then fly south, and both include a Phi Phi Islands day trip, so choose one rather than both. The Andaman Coast version adds floating markets and Railay Beach, while the Phuket version adds Old Phuket Town, Phang Nga Bay, a cooking class and a Muay Thai fight night.

The Singapore trip is built around hawker food, including a lunch at Maxwell Food Centre. UNESCO added Singapore's hawker culture to its intangible heritage list in 2020. In Nepal the Kathmandu to Pokhara leg can be a flight of about 25 minutes or a tourist bus, officially six to seven hours, though real journeys can run longer, so allow a buffer. The bus is the budget option if flights are full.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a trip to South and Southeast Asia cost?

Our 7 South & Southeast Asia itineraries cost from $270 to $1,100 per person for the whole trip, which is about $85 to $195 a day. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities, and exclude international flights.

Which South & Southeast Asia itinerary is the cheapest?

Luang Prabang ($270-410), Chiang Mai ($480-720), Singapore ($600-950) are the three lowest, per person and excluding flights.

How many days do I need in South and Southeast Asia?

Our South & Southeast Asia itineraries run from 4 to 7 days, and the most common length is 4 days. Each page has a day picker with a price for every length from one day up to the full plan.

When is the best time to visit South and Southeast Asia?

It depends on the trip, and each itinerary lists its ideal season. For example: Nepal, Oct – Nov; Bali, Apr – Oct; Singapore, Year-round (driest Feb – Apr). The table above shows the ideal season for every South & Southeast Asia trip.

Which trip is best for travelling alone?

Bali is the itinerary written for solo travellers, with a wellness focus, a stay in Ubud, beach days and a Nusa Penida day trip over seven days. Luang Prabang is also a good fit for a first solo trip: its old town is UNESCO-listed, the days are relaxed to moderate in pace, and at $85 a day it is the lowest daily figure in this hub.

What is the difference between the two Thailand beach trips?

Both start in Bangkok and fly south. The seven-day Andaman Coast trip adds floating markets, Railay Beach and a Phi Phi day trip, and costs $800-1,100 per person. The six-day Phuket trip adds a cooking class, a Muay Thai fight, Phang Nga Bay and Old Phuket Town, at $750-1,050 per person.
