Ultimate Road Trip Through Utah's National Parks

Zion, Bryce, Arches and Canyonlands, mapped mile by mile, with drive times, trail picks, and where to fuel up between parks.

Ideal SeasonApr – May, Sep – Oct
Est. Budget$950-1,500
Duration7 Days
PaceActive
How many days do you have?

Morning

Land in Las Vegas & pick up rental car

Las Vegas to Springdale is about 160 miles (260 km) via I-15 and Highway 9, so allow 2.5 to 3 hours. Reserve your rental early and check one-way fees if you return elsewhere. Utah is one hour ahead of Las Vegas, so adjust your watch. Fill up in St George or Hurricane, because services thin out near the park.

🚗 ~2.5 hr drive to Springdale 💵 ~$65/day rental 🕐 Book weeks ahead
Afternoon

Zion Canyon Visitor Center & shuttle

Zion Canyon Scenic Drive is closed to private cars for most of the year (generally March to November; check current). The free shuttle stops at nine points up to Temple of Sinawava, a round trip of about 80 minutes. Park in Springdale and use the town shuttle to the visitor centre, because the park lot is usually full by mid-morning.

🚐 free park shuttle 💵 Free w/ park pass 🕐 Ride before 10 AM
Evening

Riverside Walk & sunset at Canyon Junction Bridge

Riverside Walk is a paved path from the last shuttle stop, Temple of Sinawava, about 2.2 miles (3.6 km) round trip, with the Virgin River and hanging gardens beside it. For sunset, switch to Canyon Junction Bridge, shuttle stop 3, for the classic view of The Watchman. Check the last shuttle time, which leaves soon after dusk.

🚶 easy 2.2 mile trail 💵 $35/vehicle park pass 🕐 Sunset
Morning

Angels Landing (permit required)

Angels Landing is 5.4 miles (8.7 km) return from the Grotto, with the last half mile along exposed chains after Scout Lookout. A permit is needed for that final section, through the seasonal lottery or the day-before lottery on Recreation.gov, at $6 per application plus $3 per person if you win. Check trail status before applying.

🥾 ~4 hr round trip 💵 $6 + $3/person permit 🕐 Start by 7 AM
Afternoon

The Narrows

The Narrows hike starts beyond Riverside Walk, wading upstream in the Virgin River between canyon walls. Springdale shops rent water shoes, neoprene socks and a stick, and the river is closed when flow exceeds roughly 150 cubic feet per second or flash flood warnings are issued. Check the forecast at the visitor centre and turn back before afternoon storms.

🚐 shuttle to trailhead 💵 ~$20 gear rental 🕐 Before afternoon storms
Evening

Casual dinner in Springdale

Springdale has a compact main street of casual restaurants and brewpubs, mostly within walking distance of the free town shuttle. Expect mains near the badge price, and a little more in peak season. Book ahead in summer and eat early, because tomorrow's drive to Bryce takes about two hours and the mountain road is best enjoyed in daylight.

🚶 walkable in town 💵 ~$25 🕐 Early, ~6 PM
Morning

Zion–Mt. Carmel Highway

The Zion-Mt. Carmel Highway climbs through switchbacks and a 1.1-mile (1.8 km) tunnel opened in 1930 to the east entrance. Ordinary cars pass freely, and the one-way traffic control applies to oversized vehicles, which were barred from the route from June 2026 (check current). Stop at Checkerboard Mesa, and fuel up at Mt Carmel Junction.

🚗 ~2 hr drive 💵 Free 🕐 Mid-morning
Afternoon

Bryce Canyon Rim Trail

The paved stretch between Sunrise Point and Sunset Point is about 0.5 mile (0.8 km) and almost flat, while the whole Rim Trail is 5.5 miles (8.8 km) one way if you want more. The rim sits at around 8,000 feet, so walk slowly, drink water and carry a layer. The park shuttle runs seasonally to bring you back (check current).

🚶 5.5 mile trail one way 💵 $35/vehicle park pass 🕐 Late-afternoon light
Evening

Sunset at Inspiration Point

Inspiration Point has three tiers, and the upper one gives the widest view across the amphitheatre; the lower two need only a short climb. The amphitheatre faces east, so sunrise lights it more strongly, but sunset still colours the rock. Arrive early for parking, bring a jacket, and stay for the stars, as Bryce is an International Dark Sky Park.

🚗 short drive within park 💵 Free (in pass) 🕐 30 min before sunset
Morning

Sunrise at Bryce Point

Bryce Point faces east over the amphitheatre, so sunrise lights the hoodoos from the front. Arrive 30 minutes early and wrap up well, as nights are cold at altitude. The Navajo Loop starts at Sunset Point, a short drive away, and covers 1.3 miles (2.2 km). Ask at the visitor centre whether the Wall Street section is open.

🚶 ~2 km trail 💵 Free (in pass) 🕐 Sunrise
Afternoon

Scenic drive to Moab

From Bryce, take Highway 12 through Red Canyon and Torrey, then Highway 24 through Capitol Reef National Park, where the Fruita orchards and petroglyph panel make an easy stop, with no entrance station on that road. Allow about five hours with stops, a little more than the badge. Fuel up in Torrey or Hanksville.

🚗 ~5 hr drive 💵 Free 🕐 Depart by noon
Evening

Arrive in Moab

Moab's Main Street has restaurants and brewpubs within a few blocks of most hotels. Expect to pay near the badge price per meal, and book ahead at popular places, as spring and autumn are busy. Buy water and snacks for tomorrow before the shops close, and plan to reach the Arches entrance, about 5 miles north of town, by 7 AM.

🚶 walkable in town 💵 ~$25 dinner 🕐 Early dinner, ~6 PM
Morning

Early entry & Delicate Arch hike

Arches did not require timed-entry reservations in 2026, but the system has been used in recent peak seasons and may return, so check nps.gov before travelling. Delicate Arch is a 3-mile (4.8 km) round trip with a steep slickrock climb and no shade. Carry at least 2 litres of water and wear grippy shoes.

🥾 ~3 mile round trip 💵 $30/vehicle park pass 🕐 Before 8 AM
Afternoon

Devils Garden & the Windows Section

The Windows Section is a short drive off the main road, with an easy loop past North Window, South Window and Turret Arch. Devils Garden, at the end of the road, has Landscape Arch about 1.6 miles (2.6 km) round trip. Midday heat and full car parks are common in peak months, so carry water and be patient.

🚗 short drives between areas 💵 Free (in pass) 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Sunset at Balanced Rock

Balanced Rock is a 128-foot boulder perched on a pedestal, right beside the main park road, with a short 0.3-mile loop around its base. The rock turns a deep rust colour near sunset and the stop takes about 20 minutes. It is easy to reach, so you can still make dinner in Moab afterwards.

🚗 10 min from park exit 💵 Free (in pass) 🕐 Sunset
Morning

Mesa Arch at sunrise

The Mesa Arch trail is a flat 0.5-mile (0.8 km) loop from the car park, but the lot is small and photographers arrive an hour before sunrise to claim a spot. Allow roughly 45 to 50 minutes from Moab, including the road in the park. Wear layers, bring a headlamp, and stay back from the cliff edge.

🚗 ~45 min from Moab 💵 $30/vehicle park pass 🕐 Sunrise
Afternoon

Grand View Point & Green River Overlook

Grand View Point lies at the south end of the main road, about 12 miles from the visitor centre, with views over the White Rim and the Colorado and Green river canyons. Green River Overlook is nearer the entrance, and both are roadside. Bring plenty of water, as services in the district are limited.

🚗 short drives within park 💵 Free (in pass) 🕐 Late-afternoon light
Evening

Optional river rafting or jeep trail

Half-day trips on the calm stretch of the Colorado River near Moab usually last three to four hours, and rates vary by operator and season (check current). Jeep tours cost more. Book a day or two ahead in spring and autumn, and bring sun protection, a hat and a dry bag for your phone.

🚗 back to Moab 💵 ~$90 🕐 Book ahead
Morning

Easy trail or bike rental

Bike shops along Main Street rent mountain and road bikes, and full-day rates for good mountain bikes are often higher than the badge (check current). The paved Moab Canyon Pathway is gentle and mostly flat. Ride early, because summer heat builds quickly, and carry more water than you expect to need.

🚴 rentals in town 💵 ~$40+ bike rental (check current) 🕐 Early, before heat builds
Afternoon

Scenic drive back

From Moab, Salt Lake City is about 235 miles and 3.5 to 4 hours via US-191 and US-6, but Las Vegas is roughly seven hours via I-70 and I-15. Choose your direction by flight and leave by early afternoon. Fuel stops are widely spaced, and one-way rental drop fees can be steep.

🚗 ~4 hr to SLC, ~7 hr to Vegas 💵 Free 🕐 Depart early afternoon
Evening

Return rental car & fly home

Return the rental car with a full tank to avoid refuelling fees, and photograph it before drop-off. Allow two hours at the airport for domestic flights and three for international ones. Single-lane construction zones are common on US-191, I-70 and I-15 in summer, so leave a generous buffer for the drive.

🚗 airport drop-off 💵 rental already paid 🕐 Arrive 2 hrs early

💵 Tipping Etiquette

  • Standard US tipping applies at sit-down restaurants: 15–20% of the bill.
  • No tipping at park entrances, visitor centers, or shuttle drivers.
  • Tour guides (rafting, jeep trips): 10–15% is customary.

🥾 Dress Code

  • No formal dress rules: practical hiking layers are the norm everywhere.
  • Desert temperature swings hard between day and night: pack layers, not just one outfit.
  • Closed-toe shoes required on most maintained trails; sandals are fine in town only.

🎟️ Park Rules & Permits

  • Angels Landing requires a permit lottery: apply online the day before your hike.
  • Arches and Zion use timed-entry reservations in peak season (Apr–Oct): book weeks ahead.
  • Pets must be leashed and are barred from most backcountry trails.

⚠️ Safety Warnings

  • Flash flood risk in slot canyons and The Narrows: always check the forecast before entering.
  • Carry more water than feels necessary: desert heat dehydrates faster than it feels like.
  • Cell service is spotty between parks: download offline maps before you drive.

🗣️ Trail Talk

  • Trailhead: where a hike officially starts
  • Switchback: a zigzagging turn climbing a steep grade
  • Slot canyon: a narrow, deep canyon carved by water, beautiful but flood-prone

💳 Money & Payments

  • Cards accepted almost everywhere, including most park gift shops.
  • Keep some cash for small-town gas stations off the main highways.
  • Gas up in Springdale, Moab, or bigger towns: stations between parks are sparse and pricier.

Get there at first light, since by mid-morning it's a crowd, not a photo op.

🔥

A ranger-guided or permitted self-hike through Arches' maze section, with far fewer visitors than the main trails.

Right next to Canyonlands with a nearly identical canyon view, a fraction of the crowd.

✈️ Flights

  • Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) in Las Vegas is the nearest major airport for this route.
  • Pick up your rental car right at the airport and drive straight to Springdale, about 2.5 hours away.
  • Book the rental weeks ahead of peak season, SUVs sell out fast and airport counters run out first.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • This is a rental-car road trip, so taxis and rideshare barely factor in.
  • Uber and Lyft cover short in-town trips in Springdale and Moab, not park-to-park travel.
  • Skip rideshare between parks entirely, coverage is unreliable once you're off the highway.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Non-negotiable for this itinerary: no public transit connects Zion, Bryce, Arches, and Canyonlands.
  • Pick up at the Las Vegas airport rental counters and book early, since SUVs sell out in peak season.
  • Watch for flash flood warnings and sudden weather shifts on the desert highways between parks.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Springdale: right at Zion's entrance, walkable to the shuttle stop and the gear rental shops for the Narrows.
  • Bryce Canyon area: close to the Rim Trail and Sunrise/Sunset Points for the early starts this itinerary needs.
  • Moab: home base for both Arches and Canyonlands, with Main Street dinners and bike rentals for the last morning.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • No ferry or boat leg is needed, this itinerary is entirely inland desert.
  • The closest thing is the optional half-day Colorado River rafting trip out of Moab on day 6.
  • Book the rafting trip ahead if you want it, slots fill up fast during peak season.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Get the $80 America the Beautiful annual pass if visiting 3+ parks, cheaper than paying each $30-35 entrance fee separately.
  • Apply for the Angels Landing permit lottery the day before you plan to hike it.
  • Download offline maps before you leave cell coverage, service is spotty between parks.
