How do nine trips across South America and the Caribbean compare?

This hub is mostly South America: Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Brazil and Argentina. Two Caribbean islands, Curaçao and Puerto Rico, make up the rest. Most plans are built around one landmark or landscape, such as a mountain trek, a salt flat, a waterfall, a dune field or a coastline.

Cusco is the cheapest per day at $115, then Lençóis Maranhenses at $125, Curaçao at $145, Salar de Uyuni at $150, Rio de Janeiro and Iguazu Falls at $155, Machu Picchu at $170 and San Juan at $200. Marble Caves is the most expensive at $240 a day. The lowest totals are $350-550 per person for Cusco and $380-600 for Lençóis Maranhenses.

Beach lovers can choose Curaçao or Rio de Janeiro, and fort and old-town walkers San Juan. If landscape is the draw, pick between Iguazu Falls, Salar de Uyuni, Lençóis Maranhenses and Marble Caves. Inca sites are covered by Cusco and Machu Picchu.

Which months suit which trip?

No single month suits the whole hub. The Peru trips run from May to September and Lençóis Maranhenses from June to September. Salar de Uyuni and San Juan run from December to April, Marble Caves from November to March, and Rio de Janeiro from April to June and September to November.

Iguazu Falls lists only April and September, the narrowest window here, while Curaçao is year-round, with February to June the driest. Some trips follow water. The mirror effect at Uyuni needs a thin layer of water on the salt, which comes with the rainy season, so its months are the wet ones. The Lençóis lagoons form from rain and are fullest once the rains end, which is why its window starts in June.

Patagonia is in its southern summer from November to March, and the data marks December to February as the peak for the Marble Caves. The Peru window and the Bolivia window do not overlap, so you cannot pair those two on a single set of dates.

Combining trips and saving money

Cusco and Machu Picchu cover the same area, and both visit Pisac and Ollantaytambo, so pick one. Take Cusco for the city, a slow start and the Rainbow Mountain trek, and Machu Picchu if the citadel is the point. Both pages describe the flight from Lima as about one hour fifteen minutes.

Three trips touch Brazil, and the plans differ in character: Rio de Janeiro is a city, Iguazu Falls spans the Argentine and Brazilian sides, and Lençóis Maranhenses is a dune landscape explored by 4x4 and boat from Barreirinhas. The Lençóis page allows a transfer of 3.5 to 4 hours from São Luís airport to reach it.

Savings start with a cheaper daily rate, but also with booking early where a trip page warns about it: the Machu Picchu page says train seats sell out in peak season. A Marble Caves visit costs $800-1,100 per person, so it is the one to plan with the most care.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a trip to Latin America and the Caribbean cost?

Our 9 Latin America & Caribbean itineraries cost from $350 to $1,100 per person for the whole trip, which is about $115 to $240 a day. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities, and exclude international flights.

Which Latin America & Caribbean itinerary is the cheapest?

Cusco ($350-550), Lençóis Maranhenses ($380-600), Curaçao ($450-700) are the three lowest, per person and excluding flights.

How many days do I need in Latin America and the Caribbean?

Our Latin America & Caribbean itineraries run from 4 to 5 days, and the most common length is 4 days. Each page has a day picker with a price for every length from one day up to the full plan.

When is the best time to visit Latin America and the Caribbean?

It depends on the trip, and each itinerary lists its ideal season. For example: Curaçao, Year-round (Feb – Jun driest); San Juan, Dec – Apr; Lençóis Maranhenses, Jun – Sep. The table above shows the ideal season for every Latin America & Caribbean trip.

Which trips suit a beach holiday with some history?

San Juan pairs Old San Juan and its forts with Condado and Luquillo beach days, and Rio de Janeiro pairs Copacabana and Ipanema with Santa Teresa and Sugarloaf. Curaçao leans more toward snorkelling and west coast beaches, with Willemstad as the historic stop.

Is the Marble Caves trip a quick getaway?

It lasts four days, but the plan flies into Balmaceda via Santiago or Puerto Montt and opens with the Carretera Austral road to Puerto Río Tranquilo, so allow for that transfer. It is also the dearest per day in this hub at $240, so extra days are worth weighing.
