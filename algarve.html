4 Days in the Algarve, Portugal

Benagil Cave's circular skylight reached by kayak, the golden cliffs of Praia da Marinha, and a clifftop trail linking hidden coves to Lagos' dramatic grottoes: four days built around Portugal's most photographed coastline.

Ideal SeasonMay – Sep
Est. Budget$700-950
Duration4 Days
PaceRelaxed to Moderate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Arrival at Faro Airport & transfer to Albufeira

Faro Airport (FAO) is the Algarve's only international gateway, and a rental car or transfer covers the coast road to Albufeira in well under an hour.

🚗 ~35 min drive from Faro 💵 ~€35-45 taxi or transfer 🕐 Plan around your flight time
Afternoon

Check in & wander Albufeira's old town

Whitewashed lanes climb from the fishing beach past Praça dos Pescadores, with cliff-top viewpoints looking back over the harbor. Ease into the pace before the coastal days ahead.

🚶 Walkable in central Albufeira 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Mid-afternoon, easy first walk
Evening

First dinner & a cataplana

Cataplana, clams, fish, or mixed seafood steamed in a hinged copper pot, is the Algarve's signature dish. Pair it with a chilled vinho verde on an old-town terrace.

🍽️ Old town restaurants, walkable 💵 ~€20-25 dinner 🕐 Evening, around 7 to 8 PM
Morning

Kayak or boat tour to Benagil Cave

Benagil Cave's domed roof opens to a circular skylight above a sandy interior beach, but it cannot be reached on foot: a guided kayak, SUP, or small boat tour is the only way in. In recent years local authorities have restricted swimming or climbing into the cave itself for safety and to curb erosion, so tours now view and photograph it from the water rather than landing inside. Go early for calmer seas and thinner crowds.

🛶 ~2 hr guided kayak or boat tour 💵 ~€30-40 per person 🕐 Book the earliest morning slot
Afternoon

Praia da Marinha

Regularly ranked among the world's most beautiful beaches, Praia da Marinha's layered limestone cliffs, sea arches, and clear turquoise water are worth the whole afternoon. Bring a mask and fins for the rock pools at either end.

🚗 ~15 min drive from Benagil 💵 Free beach, ~€5-8 parking 🕐 Midday, arrive before the lots fill
Evening

Sunset at Algar Seco & dinner in Carvoeiro

A short drive brings you to Algar Seco's collapsed sea caves and stairway viewing platforms, one of the coast's best sunset spots, before a short walk into Carvoeiro for dinner.

🚗 ~10 min drive from Marinha 💵 ~€25-30 dinner 🕐 Sunset, about an hour before dusk
Morning

Hike the Seven Hanging Valleys Trail

The Percurso dos Sete Vales Suspensos traces the clifftop from Praia da Marinha to Vale Centeanes, dipping past sea stacks, blowholes, and Praia do Carvalho's stairway-only cove along the way.

🥾 ~2.5 to 3 hr, ~6 km one-way 💵 Free 🕐 Start mid-morning, bring water & sun protection
Afternoon

Wander Carvoeiro's old town

Once a working fishing village, Carvoeiro's tight lanes and stepped alleys are now lined with small cafés and shops, an easy, low-key counterpoint to the morning's hike.

🚶 Walkable in central Carvoeiro 💵 Free to wander, ~€10 lunch 🕐 Early afternoon
Evening

Sunset over the cliffs & dinner

Carvoeiro's clifftop promenade fills up around golden hour. Grab a table facing the water before the sun drops behind the headland.

🚶 Short walk from the town center 💵 ~€25 dinner 🕐 Golden hour, before dinner
Morning

Wander Lagos' old town

Lagos' walled old town, once a base for early Portuguese Atlantic voyages, is easy walking between Praça Gil Eanes and the marina, with plenty of cafés for a slow morning.

🚗 ~30 min drive from Carvoeiro 💵 Free to wander 🕐 Morning, before the day-trip crowds
Afternoon

Boat tour of Ponta da Piedade's cliffs & grottoes

Ponta da Piedade's ochre headland is riddled with sea arches, grottoes, and stacks. A short boat tour threads between them and past several of the smaller caves that dot this stretch of coast, a good comparison to Benagil.

⛴️ ~1 hr boat tour 💵 ~€20-25 per person 🕐 Early afternoon, calmer seas
Evening

Transfer to Faro Airport & departure

The drive back east retraces much of the coast road, with one last look at the cliffs before the terminal comes into view.

🚗 ~1 hr to the airport 💵 ~€50-60 return transfer 🕐 Leave 2 to 3 hrs before your flight

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Not obligatory: rounding up or leaving 5 to 10% at sit-down restaurants is appreciated for good service.
  • No tipping expected at cafés, bars, or counter service.
  • Boat, kayak, and tour guides welcome a small tip, but it's not required.

👗 Dress Code

  • Casual, light clothing works for most of the trip: swimwear under a cover-up by day, something a little smarter for old-town dinners.
  • Water shoes or reef sandals matter more here than regular sneakers: many cove and cave entries are over sharp, wet rock.
  • Bring a light windbreaker for boat and kayak trips, even in summer the water throws a cool spray.

🚗 Getting Around

  • The Algarve's beaches and villages are spread along the coast, so a rental car gives the most flexibility for reaching Benagil, Marinha, Carvoeiro, and Lagos in one trip.
  • Local buses connect the main towns but run infrequently, better suited to a single day trip than a packed four-day itinerary.
  • Albufeira, Carvoeiro, and Lagos are each easily walkable once you're in town, so a car mainly matters for getting between them.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Benagil Cave cannot be entered on foot, and local authorities now actively discourage and, in places, restrict swimming or climbing into the cave itself due to rockfall risk and erosion. View it from a licensed kayak, SUP, or boat tour instead.
  • Clifftop paths on the Seven Hanging Valleys trail run close to unfenced edges that are actively eroding: stay well back from the rim, especially in wind.
  • Boat and kayak tours to Benagil are weather and tide dependent, and can be cancelled or rerouted on rougher days: build a buffer into your schedule rather than a single fixed slot.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in Portuguese

  • Bom dia: good morning / hello
  • Obrigado / Obrigada: thank you (said by men / women respectively)
  • A conta, por favor: the bill, please

💳 Money & Payments

  • The euro is used throughout, the same as mainland Portugal.
  • Cards and contactless are accepted almost everywhere in Albufeira, Carvoeiro, and Lagos.
  • Carry some cash for smaller kayak and boat operators, beach parking, and market stalls.

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

🛶

Book Benagil by kayak, not speedboat

A guided kayak or SUP tour costs a fraction of a private speedboat charter and gets you just as close to the cave's mouth.

🍽️

Eat where the fishing boats come in

Cataplana and grilled fish cost noticeably less a street back from the beachfront terraces in Albufeira and Carvoeiro.

🚗

Rent a car for the whole trip

One multi-day rental beats stacking individual taxis and tours once you're covering Benagil, Marinha, Carvoeiro, and Lagos.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🏖️

Praia do Carvalho

A tiny beach reached through a hand-cut tunnel in the rock, right along the Seven Hanging Valleys trail and often much quieter than Marinha.

🌅

Early or late for Benagil

The first and last boat slots of the day see far fewer kayaks stacked outside the cave, and the light is better for photos.

🐟

Ferragudo

A quiet fishing village across the estuary from Portimão, largely skipped by day-trippers heading straight for Benagil.

✈️ Flights

  • Faro Airport (FAO) is the Algarve's only international gateway, roughly 30 to 45 minutes from Albufeira, Lagoa, and Carvoeiro by road.
  • Summer is peak season with far more direct flights from Northern Europe and the UK, but also higher fares: book well ahead for a May to September trip.
  • Build a little buffer into tight connections, arrivals cluster heavily in summer mornings.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Uber and Bolt operate around Albufeira, Lagos, and Faro, but coverage thins out fast toward Carvoeiro and Benagil.
  • Regular taxis are easy to book in advance for airport transfers or a single evening out.
  • Agree a fare or confirm the meter before longer trips outside the main towns.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • Genuinely useful for this itinerary: Benagil, Praia da Marinha, Carvoeiro, and Lagos are spread across roughly 30 km of coastline, awkward to link by taxi or bus.
  • Parking at the smaller beaches fills early in summer, arrive before mid-morning or expect a walk from an overflow lot.
  • Automatic transmission is more common here than in mainland Portugal's interior, but still worth booking ahead in peak season.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Albufeira old town: puts Day 1's evening stroll and dinner right outside the door, with the widest range of restaurants and nightlife.
  • Lagoa or Carvoeiro: closer to Benagil and Praia da Marinha, a quieter base for Days 2 and 3.
  • Lagos: a good final-night base for Day 4, with an easier morning start before the drive back to Faro.

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Book the Benagil kayak or boat tour, and the Ponta da Piedade grotto tour, several days ahead in peak summer: popular morning slots sell out.
  • Both trips depend on calm seas and manageable tides, so operators may shift departure times the night before, keep contact details handy.
  • A combined multi-cave boat tour comparing Benagil with the smaller grottoes along the coast is a good add-on if you have a spare afternoon.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • The Algarve is Portuguese and EU territory, so standard Schengen entry rules apply for the whole trip.
  • Travel insurance that covers water sports is worth checking given the kayaking and boat tours on Days 2 and 4.
  • May to September brings the calmest seas for reaching Benagil Cave: rougher autumn and winter swells can cancel water-based tours entirely.
