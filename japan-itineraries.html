Japan Itineraries and Trip Costs

5 complete day-by-day trips in Japan, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 5 Japan itineraries: Tokyo & Kyoto, Hakone, Fuji Five Lakes, Yoshino, Niseko. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $350 to $1,900 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Yoshino at about $150 a day, and the most expensive is Tokyo & Kyoto at about $235 a day.

Trips run from 3 to 7 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a trip to Japan cost?

Our 5 Japan itineraries cost from $350 to $1,900 per person for the whole trip, which is about $150 to $235 a day. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities, and exclude international flights.

Which Japan itinerary is the cheapest?

Yoshino ($350-540), Hakone ($460-740), Fuji Five Lakes ($480-720) are the three lowest, per person and excluding flights.

How many days do I need in Japan?

Our Japan itineraries run from 3 to 7 days, and the most common length is 3 days. Each page has a day picker with a price for every length from one day up to the full plan.

When is the best time to visit Japan?
