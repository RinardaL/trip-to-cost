4 Days in Megève: Christmas Lights, Sleigh Rides & Gourmet Dining

Christmas lights on the village square, a horse-drawn sleigh, gentle slopes and a spa hour or a celebrated table each evening, in the Alps' most low-key luxury village.

Ideal SeasonDec – Mar (Christmas & New Year peak)
Est. Budget$1,900-2,600
Duration4 Days
PaceRelaxed to Moderate
How many days do you have?

Morning

Private or shared transfer from Geneva

Megève has no commercial airport, so the trip starts with a 1.5-hour drive from Geneva through the Arve valley. French school holidays run from 19 December 2026 to 4 January 2027 (check current), and Saturday is the usual changeover day, so book the transfer early and allow extra time on the road.

🚐 ~1.5 hr transfer from Geneva Airport 💵 ~€50 shared shuttle, or ~€140 private transfer for up to 4 🕐 Book a midday transfer to arrive with daylight, and reserve early in the Christmas weeks
Afternoon

Check in and a first walk through Place de l'Église

Megève's historic core has been closed to through traffic for decades, so the streets around the church square suit an easy first stroll. The tourist board lists the 2026 tree lighting for 5 December. Saint-Jean-Baptiste, a church founded around 1085, is free and open daily outside services (check current).

🚶 Flat walk through the pedestrian center 💵 Free to explore, church entry free 🕐 Mid-afternoon once you've checked in, then again after dark when the tree and facades are lit
Evening

Fondue or raclette at a chalet restaurant

A first dinner of melted Beaufort cheese or raclette is a local tradition, best with Savoie wine. A French Christmas Eve table usually runs to foie gras, roast capon or guinea fowl, a Savoie cheese board and a bûche de Noël, so ask whether restaurants serve a set menu on 24 December (check current).

🚶 Most restaurants are a short walk from the center 💵 ~€40-50 for a fondue or raclette dinner 🕐 Early evening, and reserve before you arrive in the Christmas weeks
Morning

Cable car up to Rochebrune and a first run

The Téléphérique de Rochebrune lifts you from the village centre to wide, gently pitched slopes for learners and families. The 2026-27 listing shows partial opening on 5-6 and 12-18 December and general opening from 19 December to 4 April 2027, daily 9:00 to 17:00, snow permitting. Christmas weeks are busiest, so ride early.

🚡 ~8 min cable car from the village center 💵 ~€58.50 adult 4-hour pass from 19 Dec (€65 day pass), ~€65 group ski lesson 🕐 Morning, while the beginner runs are freshly groomed
Midday

A long lunch on a sun terrace

Megève's mountain restaurants are built for lingering: deck chairs and blankets on a snow-covered terrace, a plate of tartiflette and a view over the valley. In the holiday weeks the terraces fill early, so reserve a table the day before and ask whether your restaurant is open on 24 and 25 December (check current).

🚶 On the mountain, a short ski or walk from the lift 💵 ~€40-55 per person 🕐 Midday, while the terrace is still in the sun, with a booking in the Christmas weeks
Afternoon

Gondola to Mont d'Arbois for the Mont Blanc panorama

The Télécabine du Mont d'Arbois climbs to a wide viewing terrace facing the entire Mont Blanc massif, with no technical step needed, then an easy blue run or the same gondola takes you back down. December days are short and the lifts stop at 17:00, so go up in early afternoon.

🚡 ~15 min gondola from the village 💵 Included on the Évasion Mont-Blanc lift pass 🕐 Early to mid-afternoon, for the clearest light on the massif before the lifts close
Morning

A horse-drawn sleigh ride through the decorated village

Horse-drawn carriages and sleighs are a long-running Megève sight. Les traîneaux de Megève is listed on the village square from 15 December to 15 April, daily 10:30 to 19:30, with carriage rides around the pedestrian village centre. The listing gives no fares, so ask the driver for the route and price before you board.

🛷 Short ride around the village center, from the village square or by phone 💵 Ask the price before boarding; the 2024 listing says cash only, +50% after 20:00 🕐 Late morning, from 10:30 when the stand opens
Midday

Browsing Rue Charles Feige, Rue Ambroise Martin & the Christmas Village

The boutiques on Megève's two main shopping streets, from ski-wear ateliers to jewellers, reflect its quietly upscale identity. The Village de Noël on the rink esplanade is listed for 21 to 31 December 2026, closed on 25 December. It is a children's village with 45-minute elf workshops (€5, booked online), not a crafts market (check current).

🚶 Flat walk through the shopping streets to the Christmas Village by the rink 💵 Free to browse; elf workshops €5 per child 🕐 Village open 10:00-12:00 and 15:30-18:45 (2026 listing)
Evening

A reserved table at one of Megève's celebrated restaurants

Megève punches well above its size for fine dining, led by Emmanuel Renaut's three-Michelin-star Flocons de Sel, which lists its winter reopening for 4 December 2026 and closes on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Reserve first for Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, when some tables may run a special menu (check current).

🚕 Short taxi or walk depending on the address 💵 ~€120-320 per person depending on the restaurant 🕐 Reserve weeks ahead, and for 24 December and New Year's Eve as soon as your dates are fixed
Morning

A slow morning at the hotel spa

With the skiing and sleigh ride behind you, a spa morning with sauna, steam room or a wood-fired hot tub is a fitting way to ease into the last day. Spa slots fill up in the Christmas weeks, so book treatments when you book the room.

🚶 Most hotel spas are on-site 💵 ~€70-100 spa day pass where not already included 🕐 Morning, before the spa fills up with other departing guests
Midday

A few laps on the Place de la Résistance rink

The open-air village-centre rink is an easy last hour outdoors. The 2026-27 season runs from 5 December to 7 March 2027, daily 13:30 to 19:30 from 19 December, closing at 19:00 on 24 December and opening at 14:00 on 25 December and 1 January. It is cancelled in bad weather.

🚶 In the village center, no transport needed 💵 ~€10.50: €6 entry plus €4.50 skate rental 🕐 Opens at 13:30 (14:00 on 25 Dec), so make it the first stop after a late lunch
Afternoon

Last coffee and the transfer back to Geneva

One more espresso on a village terrace, then the same 1.5-hour drive back through the Arve valley to Geneva. At the end of the holiday period the road and Geneva Airport can be busy, so check traffic and allow extra time.

🚐 ~1.5 hr transfer to Geneva Airport 💵 ~€50 shared shuttle, or ~€140 private transfer 🕐 Leave at least 4 hrs before an international flight, more at holiday changeover weekends

💶 Tipping Etiquette

  • Service is included by law in French restaurant and hotel bills (service compris), so tipping isn't obligatory the way it is in the US.
  • Rounding up or leaving a few euros for excellent service is appreciated, especially at Michelin-tier tables, but never demanded.
  • Ski instructors and mountain-restaurant staff don't expect tips beyond the usual French rounding-up gesture.

👗 Dress Code

  • Megève's identity is chic mountain, not backcountry: technical layers are fine on the slopes, but village dinners and bars lean toward polished apres-ski style, think good boots and a nice coat over a battered puffer. Pack one dressier outfit for a Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve dinner.
  • Layer for fast-changing alpine weather regardless: a moisture-wicking base, an insulating mid-layer, and a windproof or waterproof shell.
  • Even a casual lunch on a mountain-restaurant terrace expects a slightly tidier look than typical ski-resort cafeteria fare elsewhere.
  • A sleigh ride, the 24 December parade and the open-air rink are all outdoors in the cold, so keep a warm hat, gloves and an extra layer in your day bag.

🛷 Getting Around the Village

  • The historic center around Place de l'Église is largely pedestrian, easy to cross end-to-end on foot in about 15 minutes.
  • Horse-drawn carriages and sleighs wait on the village square from 15 December to 15 April (2024 listing, check current), alongside a free local shuttle bus connecting the center to the ski lifts.
  • Both Rochebrune and Mont d'Arbois, Megève's two main ski areas, have lift stations within a short walk of the village center.
  • On 24 December the Père Noël parade leaves Place du Village at 17:30 and runs to 19:00 (free, 2026 listing), with Père Noël in a horse-drawn carriage, so expect a crowd on the square at that hour.

⚠️ Safety & Etiquette Warnings

  • Restaurant tables, especially at well-known addresses, fill up weeks ahead in ski season, and 24 December, 25 December and New Year's Eve are the dates to book first, before you land, not after you arrive.
  • Sun exposure on the slopes is stronger than it looks even on a gentle beginner run, sunscreen and eye protection matter regardless of skill level.
  • Lifts occasionally pause for high wind, build slack into any plans that hinge on the Mont d'Arbois cable car specifically. The open-air rink also closes in bad weather.
  • Check opening hours for 24 and 25 December and 1 January before you land: shops, restaurants and the rink all change hours around the holidays.

🗣️ Crucial Phrases in French

  • Bonjour: hello
  • Merci beaucoup: thank you very much
  • L'addition, s'il vous plaît: the bill, please
  • Une table réservée au nom de...: a table reserved under the name of..., worth knowing given how far ahead the best tables book
  • Joyeux Noël: Merry Christmas
  • Bonnes fêtes: happy holidays, the usual greeting in the weeks around Christmas and New Year

💳 Money & Payments

  • France uses the euro (€); cards and contactless payments are accepted almost everywhere, including lift-ticket counters and mountain restaurants.
  • Megève sits at the upscale end of French ski resorts, on par with or above Switzerland's grand-hotel towns, budget accordingly for dining and lodging.
  • Many top restaurants require a credit card to hold a reservation and may charge a fee for late cancellations.
  • Christmas and New Year are peak weeks: on the 2026-27 listing the adult lift day pass is €65 from 19 December to 20 March, against €52 on 12-18 December and from 21 March. Lodging is also priciest in these weeks, so book early (check current).

💰 Money-Saving Hacks

📅

Arrive 12 to 18 December

On the 2026-27 listing the adult day pass is €52 from 12 to 18 December against €65 from 19 December, and the village tree is already lit (5 December). Only part of the ski area is open, and the Christmas Village and the 24 December parade have not started yet.

🎟️

Compare the 4-hour pass with the day pass

In the peak weeks the adult 4-hour pass is €58.50 against €65 for the day, so the saving is small. It pays off mainly for a short morning session on Rochebrune or Mont d'Arbois.

🍽️

Take the fixed-price weekday lunch menu

Many mountain-restaurant terraces run a set-price weekday lunch menu that comes in well under ordering à la carte, without sacrificing the view.

🏨

Book a half-board hotel rate

Given how much a Michelin-caliber dinner runs here, a half-board hotel package takes the pressure off booking every single meal out.

📸 Off-the-Beaten-Path Secrets

🚡

Take the Jaillet gondola for a quieter panorama

The smaller Télécabine du Jaillet gets a fraction of the Mont d'Arbois crowds for a similarly sweeping view over the village and the Mont Blanc massif.

⛷️

Try the valley's cross-country trails

Groomed Nordic ski loops thread the valley floor below the main slopes, a quiet, lift-line-free alternative for an easy morning on skis.

🎄

Photograph the lit bell tower after dark

The tourist board points to the illuminated bell tower and the traditional facades around the village tree as the best photo spots once the lights are on.

⛪

Walk up to the Calvaire chapels

A short path above the village climbs past a row of 18th-century chapels to a rooftop view over Megève, often nearly empty even in high season.

✈️ Flights

  • Geneva Airport (GVA) is the closest major gateway, about 70 km and roughly 1.5 hours by road from Megève. The Christmas holiday weeks (19 December 2026 to 4 January 2027 for French schools, check current) are the busiest, so book flights and transfers early.
  • Chambéry Airport is closer in a straight line for some winter charters, but Geneva has far more scheduled flights and remains the practical choice for most visitors.
  • Megève has its own small altiport used for private aviation, but it isn't a commercial airport.

🚕 Taxis & Rideshare

  • Taxis and pre-booked private transfers run the Geneva-to-Megève route directly; shared shuttles cost less but add stops along the way.
  • Within the village, horse-drawn carriages and sleighs (on the village square 15 December to 15 April, cash only on the 2024 listing) plus a free local shuttle bus cover most short trips.
  • Ride-hailing apps have limited coverage in this part of the Alps; hotels and the taxi rank near the center are the more reliable option.

🚗 Rental Cars

  • A car isn't necessary for this itinerary, the village center, both ski areas, and most restaurants sit within walking distance or a short shuttle ride.
  • Renting does help for day trips beyond Megève, toward Annecy or Chamonix, if extending the stay.
  • Winter tires or snow chains are required in Haute-Savoie from 1 November to 31 March under the Loi Montagne (check the current prefectural order), so confirm your rental is equipped for a Christmas trip.

🏨 Hotels & Where to Stay

  • Village center, around Place de l'Église: closest to restaurants, boutiques, and the pedestrian streets on this itinerary.
  • Near the Rochebrune or Mont d'Arbois lift stations: shortest walk to the slopes each morning.
  • Just outside the center: often the address for Megève's grand luxury chalets and five-star hotels, a short taxi or shuttle from everything else.
  • Christmas and New Year weeks sell out first: book several months ahead and ask about minimum-stay rules (check current).

⛴️ Boat Trips

  • Megève is a landlocked mountain village at over 1,100 meters elevation, so boat trips simply aren't part of this itinerary.
  • The nearest lake excursions are around Annecy or Lake Geneva, both over an hour away, better suited to a separate add-on trip.
  • Lac de Javen, the small alpine lake above the village, is a summer photo stop, not a boating destination, and is typically snowed over in ski season.

🧳 Other Essentials

  • Reserve restaurant tables, especially any Michelin-starred address, well before arrival, and for 24 December and New Year's Eve as soon as your dates are fixed, as Megève's best tables go early.
  • France is part of the Schengen area, so some non-EU travelers should still double-check passport and visa rules before booking.
  • Travel insurance covering ski activities and lift disruptions is worth adding given the Day 2 mountain excursions.
  • Check hours for 24 and 25 December before you land: the rink closes at 19:00 on 24 December and opens at 14:00 on 25 December on the 2026-27 listing, and the Christmas Village is closed on 25 December.

How much does a 4-day trip to Megève cost?

About $1,900-2,600 per person for 4 days, roughly $475-650 a day, excluding international flights. The estimate covers activities, local transport and meals, and real costs vary with the season and your spending habits.

Per day, Megève averages about $565, which puts it in the pricier third of the 121 trips on TripToCost, ranked by average cost per day.
