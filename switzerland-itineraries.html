Switzerland Itineraries and Trip Costs

5 complete day-by-day trips in Switzerland, with the price per person up front.

TripToCost has 5 Switzerland itineraries: Zermatt, Grindelwald, Lauterbrunnen, St. Moritz, Interlaken. Each is a complete short trip planned day by day from your airport arrival, with the price shown up front.

Budgets run from $850 to $2,600 per person for the whole trip, excluding international flights. The cheapest per day is Lauterbrunnen at about $360 a day, and the most expensive is St. Moritz at about $565 a day.

Trips run from 3 to 4 days. Prices cover lodging, meals, local transport and activities for two people sharing a room, and every page has a day picker with a price for each trip length.
